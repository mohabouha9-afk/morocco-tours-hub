Frequently Asked Questions

Is Morocco safe for tourists?

Yes. Morocco is Africa’s most visited country and is safe for tourists, families and solo women. Watch for pickpockets in busy medinas; on our private Morocco tours, your English-speaking driver-guide is with you every day.

When is the best time to visit Morocco and the Sahara Desert?

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are the best time to visit Morocco and the Sahara Desert. Winter brings sunny days but cold desert nights, while July and August pass 40 °C inland, ideal for the coast.

Do I need a visa to visit Morocco?

No visa is needed for US, UK, EU, Canadian, Australian and New Zealand citizens: you get 90 days free on arrival. Bring an undamaged passport valid for 6 months; other nationalities apply online at acces-maroc.ma.

How far is the Sahara Desert from Marrakech?

Merzouga’s Erg Chebbi dunes are about 560 km (9 hours) from Marrakech, so our Sahara desert tour takes 3 days via Aït Ben Haddou and the Dades Valley, with a camel trek and a desert camp. Agafay is under an hour away.

What is included in your private Morocco tours?

Your own air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking driver, fuel, hotel pick-up, accommodation with breakfast and, on desert tours, dinners, a camel trek and a camp night. Your quote lists everything: what we agree on is what you pay.

How do I book, and do I pay everything upfront?

No. Message us your dates and we’ll send a tailor-made itinerary with a fixed price. A 20–30% deposit by bank transfer secures your dates; the balance is paid on arrival in cash (EUR, USD or MAD) or by card.

What currency is used in Morocco, and can I pay by card?

Morocco uses the dirham (MAD), a closed currency you get on arrival from ATMs or exchange offices. Cards work in hotels and larger restaurants, but souks and small cafés are cash only. Your tour balance can be paid in cash or by card.
