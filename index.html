Private Morocco Tours

Best Morocco Tours & Desert Trips

Morocco is a country best discovered at your own pace. That is why we offer private Morocco tours designed around your dates, interests, and the way you like to travel. Whether you want to spend more time in the cities, enjoy the mountains, discover local villages, or simply take things slowly in the desert, we can adapt the journey to you.

Our Morocco tours take you beyond the main sights, from the lively streets and souks of Marrakech and the historic medina of Fes to the Atlas Mountains, old kasbahs, peaceful valleys, and the Atlantic coast. Along the way, our local drivers and guides are there to help you discover Morocco, share their knowledge, and make the journey comfortable and enjoyable.

For many travelers, the highlight is the Sahara Desert. When you reach Merzouga, you can ride a camel through the dunes of Erg Chebbi as the sun goes down, then spend the night in a desert camp. After a traditional dinner and an evening around the campfire, you can sit outside and enjoy the quiet desert sky. In the morning, wake up early to watch the sunrise over the dunes before continuing your journey.

Whether you are planning a Morocco desert tour from Marrakech, a trip from Fes or Casablanca, a family holiday, a honeymoon, or a longer journey around the country, we are happy to help you build an itinerary that feels right for you.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your Morocco tours private or in a group?

All our tours are private by default. You travel only with your own party, in a private vehicle with your own driver-guide, and the itinerary is yours to shape. If you'd prefer to share and save, just ask us about small-group options.

Is Morocco safe to travel to?

Yes. Morocco is one of the most welcoming and safe countries in the region for travellers, including families and solo women. Your private driver-guide is with you throughout, and we're reachable on WhatsApp before and during your trip.

Can I customise my itinerary?

Absolutely — every tour on this site is a starting point. We can shorten or extend it, add cities, change the pace, upgrade the hotels or design a completely new route around your dates and interests.

How do I book, and do I pay everything upfront?

Just send us a message with your dates and group size. We'll confirm availability and a tailored quote, then hold your tour with a small deposit. The balance is usually settled at the start of your trip — no need to pay it all upfront.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer for your deposit, and the remaining balance can be paid in cash (EUR, USD or MAD) or by card on arrival. We'll confirm the exact options when you book.

When is the best time to visit Morocco?

Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are ideal, with warm days and comfortable desert nights. Winter suits the imperial cities and the Sahara by day, while summer is best on the Atlantic coast around Essaouira.
