Tour Overview

Discover the best of Morocco on this 13-day private tour from Marrakech to Casablanca, combining the country's historic cities, Atlantic coast, High Atlas Mountains and Sahara Desert. The journey begins in Marrakech, where you explore the famous medina, souks, palaces and gardens before travelling to the Atlantic coast and the charming town of Essaouira.

From the coast, cross the High Atlas Mountains and continue through Aït Ben Haddou, Ouarzazate and the Dades Valley before reaching the golden dunes of Merzouga. Enjoy a camel ride, sunset over the dunes and a night in a private Sahara desert camp. The following days take you through the Middle Atlas Mountains to Fes, then north to the blue-painted streets of Chefchaouen.

The final part of the journey includes Meknes, Volubilis, Rabat and Casablanca, giving you a wonderful overview of Morocco's history, landscapes and culture.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Arrival in Marrakech

Welcome to Morocco! Upon arrival at Marrakech airport, your private driver will meet you and transfer you to your hotel or traditional riad in the heart of Marrakech. After check-in, you can relax after your journey or begin discovering the atmosphere of the Red City independently.

Depending on your arrival time, you may take a gentle walk through the nearby streets and experience the lively atmosphere around Jemaa el-Fna. In the evening, enjoy your first Moroccan dinner and settle into your accommodation.

If you arrive early, we can also arrange a short introduction to Marrakech with your private guide. Your first night is spent in Marrakech, preparing for the exciting journey ahead.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 2

Full-Day Marrakech Guided Tour

Today you will discover Marrakech with a private local guide. Explore the historic Medina and its narrow streets, traditional souks and important monuments. Visit the Koutoubia Mosque from the outside, the Saadian Tombs and the Bahia Palace, known for its beautiful courtyards and Moroccan architecture.

Continue through the colorful souks where local artisans work with leather, wood, metal and textiles. You will also have time to enjoy the lively atmosphere of Jemaa el-Fna Square. Depending on your interests, the itinerary can include the Majorelle Garden or other places of interest.

Your guide will help you understand the history and traditions behind the city's landmarks while leaving enough free time for shopping, photography and enjoying a Moroccan lunch.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 3

Marrakech → Essaouira

After breakfast, leave Marrakech and travel towards the Atlantic coast to Essaouira. The drive takes you through changing landscapes and rural areas before reaching this charming fortified coastal town. Along the way, you may see Morocco's famous argan trees and, depending on the season and conditions, goats climbing the branches.

Arrive in Essaouira and begin exploring the old town at your own pace. Walk through the historic medina, browse the artisan shops and visit the lively fishing port. Essaouira is known for its relaxed atmosphere, traditional architecture and ocean views.

You can enjoy fresh seafood at the port or take a walk along the beach before returning to your riad. The combination of Marrakech and Essaouira is one of Morocco's classic land-and-sea experiences.

~190 km Essaouira Breakfast
Day 4

Essaouira → Marrakech → High Atlas → Aït Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate → Dades Valley

Today begins a spectacular journey towards southern Morocco. Leave Essaouira and travel back towards Marrakech before crossing the High Atlas Mountains through the Tizi n'Tichka pass. Stop along the way to admire mountain villages and panoramic landscapes.

Continue towards the UNESCO-listed ksar of Aït Ben Haddou, one of Morocco's most impressive examples of traditional earthen architecture. After exploring the kasbah, continue to Ouarzazate, often called the gateway to the desert.

The journey then continues through the Valley of Roses and into the Dades Valley. As the landscape changes from mountains to rocky desert scenery, you will begin to experience the dramatic south of Morocco. Enjoy dinner and a relaxing evening at your hotel.

~530 km Dades Valley Breakfast & Dinner
Day 5

Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Erfoud → Rissani → Merzouga

After breakfast, continue towards the Sahara Desert. Drive through the dramatic Todra Gorge, where towering cliffs rise on both sides of the road. Continue through the oasis landscapes around Tinghir and onward to Erfoud, famous for fossils and stone craftsmanship.

You will then reach Rissani, an important historic trading town and gateway to the Tafilalet region. From here, the landscape gradually becomes more arid as you approach Merzouga and the Erg Chebbi dunes.

In the late afternoon, leave your vehicle and begin your camel trek into the desert. Ride across the golden dunes while watching the changing colors of the landscape. Stop to enjoy the sunset before continuing to your private desert camp for dinner and an evening of traditional music.

~300 km Private Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & Dinner
Day 6

Full-Day Sahara Desert Experience in Merzouga

Wake early to watch the sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes before breakfast. Today is dedicated to discovering the Sahara around Merzouga without changing hotels. Travel by private 4×4 through the desert and visit a nomadic family, where you can learn about traditional desert life and enjoy Moroccan tea.

Continue to Khamlia village to experience Gnawa music before visiting areas known for fossils and desert landscapes. Depending on the season, you may also visit the seasonal lake near Merzouga. There is time for sandboarding on the dunes or simply relaxing and taking photographs.

Return to your accommodation in Merzouga for a peaceful afternoon. Spending extra time in the area allows you to experience more than simply arriving for a camel ride and leaving the next morning. The Sahara is one of Morocco's signature experiences.

~40 km Merzouga Breakfast & Dinner
Day 7

Merzouga → Ziz Valley → Midelt → Azrou → Ifrane → Fes

After breakfast, leave the Sahara and begin travelling north towards Fes. The route passes through the spectacular Ziz Valley, where green palm groves contrast with the surrounding rocky landscapes.

Continue through the Middle Atlas Mountains towards Midelt, an important agricultural town known for its apple production. After lunch, continue towards Azrou and its cedar forests, where you may have the opportunity to see Barbary macaques. The landscape then changes again as you reach Ifrane, known for its European-style architecture and mountain setting.

Continue to Fes, arriving in the late afternoon or evening. Check into your traditional riad and relax after the journey. This day offers an impressive transition from the Sahara's dry landscapes to the greener mountains of northern Morocco.

~470 km Fes Breakfast
Day 8

Full-Day Guided Tour of Fes

Today you will explore Fes with a private local guide. Fes is one of Morocco's most important cultural and historic cities, with a medina famous for its narrow streets, traditional workshops and centuries-old architecture.

Visit the Royal Palace gates, the Jewish Quarter, the famous Al Quaraouiyine area, the Bou Inania Madrasa and the Nejjarine Museum area. Walk through the souks and discover traditional crafts including leatherwork, metalwork, ceramics and textiles. You will also visit the famous tanneries from a suitable viewpoint.

Your guide will explain the history and traditions of the city while helping you navigate the maze-like medina. After the guided visit, enjoy some free time to explore, shop or relax at your riad.

Fes Breakfast
Day 9

Fes → Volubilis → Meknes → Chefchaouen

Leave Fes after breakfast and travel towards northern Morocco. Your first stop is Volubilis, an ancient Roman archaeological site where you can see impressive mosaics, columns and remains of Roman buildings.

Continue to Meknes, one of Morocco's imperial cities. Visit important landmarks such as Bab Mansour, the old medina and other historic areas before continuing north. The journey then takes you towards the Rif Mountains and Chefchaouen.

As you approach the town, the landscape becomes greener and more mountainous. Arrive in Chefchaouen and check into your riad. In the evening, take a relaxed walk through the famous blue-painted streets and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of the old town. Chefchaouen's location in the Rif Mountains makes it particularly attractive for walking and photography.

~300 km Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 10

Full Day in Chefchaouen

Enjoy a slower day in Chefchaouen. After breakfast, explore the charming old medina on foot, where blue-painted buildings, small squares and traditional shops create the town's distinctive character.

Visit the Kasbah and its gardens, then continue through the narrow streets towards the main square, Plaza Uta el-Hammam. You can enjoy a traditional Moroccan lunch before spending the afternoon at your own pace. If you enjoy walking, optional hikes in the surrounding Rif Mountains can be arranged depending on your interests and fitness level.

Alternatively, simply enjoy a café with views over the old town, shop for local handicrafts or photograph the blue alleyways. Keeping a full day here gives you time to appreciate Chefchaouen without rushing through it as a short stop between destinations.

Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 11

Chefchaouen → Rabat

After breakfast, depart Chefchaouen and travel south towards Rabat, Morocco's capital city. The journey takes you through changing landscapes before reaching the Atlantic coast.

In Rabat, visit the historic Kasbah of the Udayas, stroll through its atmospheric streets and enjoy views towards the Bou Regreg River and Atlantic Ocean. Continue to the Hassan Tower and the Mausoleum of Mohammed V, two of the city's most recognizable landmarks.

Depending on your arrival time and interests, there may also be time to visit the ancient Chellah site or enjoy a relaxed walk through the city. Rabat offers a quieter and more elegant atmosphere compared with some of Morocco's busier destinations. After sightseeing, check into your hotel and enjoy a comfortable evening in the capital.

~250 km Rabat Breakfast
Day 12

Rabat → Casablanca

Today, continue along the Atlantic coast towards Casablanca, Morocco's largest city and economic center. After arrival, visit the impressive Hassan II Mosque, one of the city's most important landmarks. Take time to admire its architecture and its dramatic location overlooking the Atlantic Ocean.

Continue with a panoramic tour of Casablanca, including the Corniche and central areas of the city. Depending on your interests, you can also visit the Habous Quarter, known for its traditional Moroccan architecture, small shops and cafés.

The afternoon is kept relatively relaxed so you can enjoy your final evening in Morocco. Your driver will then take you to your hotel for your last night of the tour.

~90 km Casablanca Breakfast
Day 13

Casablanca → Departure

Your Moroccan adventure comes to an end today. After breakfast, you will have some free time depending on your flight schedule. If your departure is later in the day, you can enjoy a final walk, do some shopping or relax at your hotel.

At the appropriate time, your private driver will transfer you to Casablanca airport for your departure flight. We will make sure the transfer is planned according to your flight time, allowing you to finish your journey comfortably and without unnecessary stress.

End of tour: Casablanca Breakfast

FAQ

Is this a private Morocco tour?

Yes. This is a private tour exclusively for your group. You will not share the vehicle or desert transportation with other travelers.

Can we customize the itinerary?

Yes. The itinerary can be adjusted according to your travel dates, interests, hotel category and preferred pace.

Is the Sahara camp private?

Yes. We can arrange a private desert tent with a private bathroom and comfortable facilities, depending on the accommodation category selected.

How long is the camel ride?

The camel ride is generally around 1 to 1.5 hours, depending on the timing and desert conditions. It is planned around sunset so you can enjoy the dunes before reaching camp.

Is the tour suitable for families?

Yes. The tour is suitable for families, couples and friends. We can adapt the daily schedule and activities for families travelling with children.

Can we add more nights in Marrakech or the Sahara?

Yes. Extra nights can be added in Marrakech, Essaouira, Merzouga, Fes or Chefchaouen depending on your travel plans.

Does the tour include flights?

No. International and domestic flights are not included, but we can plan the itinerary around your flight arrival and departure times.
