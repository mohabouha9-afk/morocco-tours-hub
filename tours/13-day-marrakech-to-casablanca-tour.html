Overview

Thirteen days from Marrakech to Casablanca at an easy pace, with time to settle in each place. Two nights in Marrakech with a guided day in the Red City, then two real nights by the ocean in Essaouira, not a rushed day trip.

Straight from the coast over the High Atlas to Aït Ben Haddou, through the Dades and Todra gorges to two nights by the dunes of Erg Chebbi, then Fes, Meknes and the blue town of Chefchaouen, a night in Rabat and the Hassan II Mosque on the last morning. Your own car and driver-guide all the way.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Arrival in Marrakech

    Your driver meets you at Marrakech airport at any hour, or at your hotel, and walks you to your riad in the medina. If you land early, a first evening on Jemaa el-Fnaa, when the music and food stalls come alive.

    • About 20 minutes from the airport
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast from day 2
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Marrakech: a guided day in the Red City

    With a local Marrakech guide: the Koutoubia, the painted cedar ceilings of the Bahia Palace, the Ben Youssef Madrasa and the souks. In the afternoon, the Majorelle Garden or the riad pool, as you like.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Marrakech → Essaouira, by the Atlantic

    West across the argan country to Essaouira, the walled port on the ocean. An afternoon on the Skala ramparts and their bronze cannons, the blue fishing boats in the harbour and grilled fish for lunch.

    • About 190 km, about 2.5–3 hours on the road
    • Night: Essaouira
    • Breakfast
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Essaouira: a day by the ocean

    No driving: the Mellah and the woodworkers under the ramparts, the long beach and the trade winds, a slow lunch in the medina. The easy day before the mountains and the desert.

    • No driving unless you ask
    • Night: Essaouira
    • Breakfast
  5. 5
    Day 5

    Essaouira → Tizi n’Tichka → Aït Ben Haddou

    Straight from the coast to the High Atlas, passing Marrakech on the ring road, then over the Tizi n’Tichka pass, 2,260 m, past Berber villages to Aït Ben Haddou for a sunset walk up through the ksar and a night beside it.

    • About 380 km, about 6.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Aït Ben Haddou
    • Breakfast & dinner
  6. 6
    Day 6

    Aït Ben Haddou → Skoura → Valley of Roses → Dades Gorge

    The Road of a Thousand Kasbahs: Ouarzazate, the palm oasis of Skoura and its kasbahs, the rose villages of Kelaat M’Gouna, then up the Dades Gorge among red cliffs and the rock formations called the Monkey Fingers.

    • About 180 km, about 3.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Dades Valley
    • Breakfast & dinner
  7. 7
    Day 7

    Dades → Todra Gorge → Merzouga

    A walk between the 300 m walls of the Todra Gorge in the cool of the morning, then east along the Jebel Saghro to Erg Chebbi. A camel ride into the dunes for sunset, dinner and drums by the fire in the camp.

    • About 320 km, about 5.5 hours on the road
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Breakfast & dinner
  8. 8
    Day 8

    A full day around Merzouga by 4×4

    Sunrise and sandboarding, then a day with no long road: tea with a nomad family, the Gnawa musicians of Khamlia and the market town of Rissani. A hot shower and a calm night in a hotel by the dunes.

    • 4×4 day around Merzouga
    • Night: Merzouga hotel
    • Breakfast & dinner
  9. 9
    Day 9

    Merzouga → Ziz Valley → Midelt → Ifrane → Fes

    North through the palm gorges of the Ziz Valley, lunch in Midelt, then over the Middle Atlas through the cedar forest with its Barbary macaques and the alpine town of Ifrane, to your riad in the Fes medina.

    • About 470 km, about 8 hours on the road
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  10. 10
    Day 10

    Fes: a guided day in the medina

    With a local Fes guide: the Bou Inania Madrasa, the Nejjarine fountain, the coppersmiths of Place Seffarine and a rooftop above the Chouara tanneries. Shops only if you ask.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  11. 11
    Day 11

    Fes → Meknes → Volubilis → Chefchaouen

    Meknes, the imperial city of Moulay Ismail, with the great gate of Bab Mansour and his royal granaries, then the Roman town of Volubilis and its mosaics, and north into the green Rif hills to Chefchaouen, the blue town.

    • About 290 km, about 5.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Chefchaouen
    • Breakfast
  12. 12
    Day 12

    Chefchaouen → Rabat

    A slow morning in the blue lanes before the day visitors arrive, then down to the Atlantic and Rabat, the capital: the Hassan Tower, the royal Mausoleum and the blue-and-white lanes of the Kasbah of the Oudayas at sunset over the ocean.

    • About 250 km, about 4 hours on the road
    • Night: Rabat
    • Breakfast
  13. 13
    Day 13

    Rabat → Hassan II Mosque → Casablanca, end of the tour

    Down the coast to Casablanca for a guided visit inside the Hassan II Mosque, built over the Atlantic, with its 210 m minaret. Then the airport, the train station or your hotel, with plenty of time for your flight.

    • About 120 km, about 1.5 hours on the motorway
    • Ends at Casablanca airport or your hotel
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

Why two nights in Essaouira instead of a day trip?

A day trip means about six hours in the car for a few hours in town. With two nights you see Essaouira in the quiet morning and evening, after the day visitors leave, and the drive on to the mountains starts fresh.

Is the day from Essaouira to Aït Ben Haddou long?

Yes, about six and a half hours with stops: we pass Marrakech on the ring road and cross the High Atlas. It saves you from going back to Marrakech for a night, and you arrive at Aït Ben Haddou for sunset.

Which days are the long drives?

Day 5, Essaouira to Aït Ben Haddou, and day 9, Merzouga to Fes, about eight hours with stops. Days 2, 4, 8 and 10 have little or no driving, and the last day is short.

Why a night in Rabat?

It turns the last day into an easy morning. Chefchaouen to Casablanca in one go is about five hours; with a night in Rabat you see the capital at sunset and reach the mosque and the airport fresh.

What if our last day is a Friday?

The Hassan II Mosque is closed to visitors on Fridays. We then see it from outside and walk the Corniche, or you add a night in Casablanca and visit it on Saturday morning.
