Tour Overview

Experience the best of southern Morocco on our 3-Day Morocco Student Tour from Marrakech to Fes via Merzouga. Designed for university students, Erasmus groups and young travelers, this affordable adventure combines Morocco's mountains, ancient kasbahs, dramatic valleys and the Sahara Desert in one exciting journey.

Starting in Marrakech, travel across the High Atlas Mountains and visit the famous Aït Ben Haddou Kasbah before continuing through Ouarzazate and the Dades Valley. On the second day, discover Todra Gorge, Erfoud and Rissani before reaching Merzouga and the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi. Enjoy a camel trek at sunset and spend the night at a desert camp with dinner and traditional music.

On the final day, travel north through the Ziz Valley, Midelt, Azrou and Ifrane before arriving in Fes. This one-way route means your student group can experience the Sahara while continuing directly to Morocco's historic cultural capital.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Marrakech → High Atlas → Aït Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate → Dades Valley

Your Morocco student tour from Marrakech to Fes begins with an early morning departure from Marrakech. Travel through the spectacular High Atlas Mountains, crossing the Tizi n'Tichka Pass and stopping at scenic viewpoints along the way. Continue toward the UNESCO-listed Aït Ben Haddou, one of Morocco's most famous fortified villages. Explore its narrow streets, traditional earthen buildings and panoramic viewpoints before continuing toward Ouarzazate.

After a break for lunch, drive through the Skoura area and the beautiful Valley of Roses before reaching the Dades Valley. The route offers plenty of opportunities for photographs and short stops. In the afternoon, arrive at your hotel or riad in Dades, where your student group can relax and enjoy dinner together.

~360 km Dades Valley Dinner
Day 2

Dades → Todra Gorge → Erfoud → Rissani → Merzouga Sahara

After breakfast, continue east toward the Sahara Desert. Drive through the spectacular landscapes of the Dades region before reaching Todra Gorge, famous for its enormous limestone canyon walls. Enjoy time to walk through the gorge and take photographs before continuing toward Erfoud, known for fossils and dates. Continue to Rissani, an important historic market town and traditional gateway to the Sahara.

In the afternoon, arrive in Merzouga and meet your camel guides. Ride camels across the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi as the sun begins to set. After reaching your desert camp, enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner and spend the evening around the campfire with your fellow students. Enjoy Berber music, drums and the spectacular night sky before sleeping in your Sahara camp.

~300 km Sahara Desert Camp, Merzouga Breakfast & Dinner
Day 3

Merzouga → Ziz Valley → Midelt → Azrou → Ifrane → Fes

Wake early to watch the Sahara sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes. After breakfast, return from the desert camp and begin the journey north toward Fes. The route follows the spectacular Ziz Valley and continues through Errachidia toward Midelt, with beautiful mountain scenery along the way.

Continue through the Middle Atlas Mountains toward Azrou, where you may have time for a short stop in the cedar forest. The journey then continues to Ifrane, often called the "Switzerland of Morocco" because of its alpine-style architecture and mountain setting. From Ifrane, continue toward Fes, arriving in the late afternoon or evening depending on traffic and stops. Your 3-Day Morocco Student Tour from Marrakech to Fes via Merzouga ends with drop-off at your Fes accommodation.

~470 km End of tour: Fes Breakfast

Why Choose This Tour?

Student-Friendly: This tour is designed for university students, Erasmus groups, youth travelers and student associations, with a focus on affordability and shared experiences.

Marrakech to Fes Without Backtracking: Instead of returning to Marrakech after visiting Merzouga, your group continues north and finishes in Fes, making the journey more practical for students traveling onward.

Sahara Adventure: Enjoy one of Morocco's most memorable experiences: camel trekking across Erg Chebbi, sleeping in a desert camp and watching the sunrise over the dunes.

Culture & Nature: The route combines Amazigh villages, kasbahs, mountain landscapes, desert scenery, oasis valleys and historic cities.

Great for Erasmus Groups: The itinerary works particularly well for students traveling together who want a social experience while discovering Morocco's landscapes and culture.

FAQ

Is this tour suitable for Erasmus students?

Yes. The itinerary is particularly suitable for Erasmus students, university groups, youth organizations and student associations.

Does the tour finish in Fes?

Yes. Unlike a Marrakech–Merzouga–Marrakech tour, this itinerary is one-way and finishes in Fes.

Is the tour shared or private?

It can be organized as a shared student group tour or as a private trip exclusively for your university or student group.

How many students can join?

We can organize transportation according to your group size. Larger student groups can be accommodated with suitable vehicles and arrangements.

Is the camel ride included?

Yes. A camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes at sunset is included.

Is sandboarding included?

Yes, sandboarding can be enjoyed at the Sahara camp.

Can we add quad biking?

Yes. Quad biking can be arranged as an optional activity in Merzouga.

Is this tour physically difficult?

The tour does not require special physical fitness. However, students should be prepared for long driving days, particularly on Day 3 from Merzouga to Fes.

Can we stay longer in Fes?

Yes. Your group can arrange additional nights in Fes after the tour, depending on your travel plans.
