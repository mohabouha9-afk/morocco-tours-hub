Overview

Essaouira is the easy-going opposite of Marrakech: a walled port on the Atlantic, with a UNESCO-listed medina, blue fishing boats and a sea wind that keeps it cool even in summer.

It is 2.5 to 3 hours each way on a flat road, so a private car makes the difference: no coach timetable, no built-in shop stops, and about five hours in town instead of the usual four.

Programme

  1. 1
    08:00

    Pick-up in Marrakech

    Your driver meets you at your hotel, or at the nearest gate if your riad is in the medina. We confirm the exact time and place on WhatsApp the day before.

  2. 2
    09:15

    Coffee stop and argan country

    A break for coffee halfway, then the argan forest, where goats sometimes climb the trees. The whole drive is about 190 km on a flat road, 2.5 to 3 hours.

  3. 3
    10:45

    The medina and the ramparts

    Your guide walks you through the UNESCO-listed medina and the old Jewish quarter, then up onto the Skala ramparts, with their bronze cannons facing the Atlantic.

  4. 4
    12:30

    Lunch at the port

    Choose your fish at the harbour grills and have it cooked on the spot, or eat in a medina café. Around €10–15 a person (not included); the price is agreed first.

  5. 5
    14:00

    Free time by the sea

    Watch the blue boats come in, walk the long beach, browse the thuya-wood workshops, or ride a camel or horse on the sand (extra).

  6. 6
    16:00

    Back to Marrakech

    About 3 hours back with a coffee stop, at your hotel or riad around 19:00, or straight to a restaurant if you go out for dinner.

Good to know

  • This is a suggestion: tell us your preferences
  • The road is flat: good news if you get car-sick
  • The medina is car-free: about 2–3 km on foot, mostly flat
  • The main streets suit strollers; the ramparts have steps
  • Weekends are busier; Tuesday to Thursday is calmer
  • Agree prices before you order and check the bill

Costs on the day

  • Grilled fish at the port: around €10–15 per person, priced by weight before cooking
  • Mint tea or fresh juice: about 10–25 MAD
  • Skala du Port and museums: small entry fees
  • Camel, horse or quad on the beach: about an hour, price agreed on the spot
  • Tips: up to you
  • Cash: bring some dirhams; small cafés and grills rarely take cards

Frequently Asked Questions

How much time will we have in Essaouira?

About five hours, where most shared tours give you four after a shop stop. Want more? Leave at 07:00, or stay for the sunset and come back later in the evening.

Why not just take the bus?

The bus takes about 3 hours from the station on fixed times, so with taxis at both ends a day trip leaves little time by the sea. With a private car you leave from your door, stop when you like and return when you are ready.

Are the goats in the argan trees real?

Goats do climb argan trees for the fruit, but along the main road herders often place them there and ask for a tip for photos. We stop only if you want to, and the tip is up to you.

Is it windy? Can we swim?

The Atlantic wind blows most afternoons, strongest from June to August, so bring a light jacket. The long beach is better for walks, kitesurfing and rides than for sunbathing; on calm days a swim is possible.

Where should we eat?

At the port grills you pick fresh fish, sardines or prawns and they cook them while you wait: agree the price by weight before they start. Your guide also knows calm medina cafés away from the busy square.
