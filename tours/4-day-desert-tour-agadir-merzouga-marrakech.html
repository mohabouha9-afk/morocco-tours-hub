Tour Overview

This 4-day private desert tour from Agadir to Merzouga and Marrakech takes you from Morocco's Atlantic coast through the landscapes of southern Morocco to the golden dunes of the Sahara. Along the way, you will travel through traditional towns, rocky mountain scenery, palm-filled valleys and historic kasbahs.

Your journey begins in Agadir and continues through Taroudant and Taliouine to Ouarzazate, where you will spend your first night. On the second day, travel through Alnif and the desert landscapes of southern Morocco before reaching Merzouga. Here, you will enjoy a camel ride across the Erg Chebbi dunes and spend the night in a Sahara desert camp.

The following day, leave Merzouga and travel through Rissani, Erfoud and Todra Gorge toward the Dades Valley. On your final day, visit Aït Ben Haddou and cross the High Atlas Mountains before arriving in Marrakech.

With your own private driver and comfortable vehicle, you can enjoy the changing scenery and stop for photographs and short breaks along the way.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Agadir → Taroudant → Taliouine → Ouarzazate

Your private driver will pick you up from your hotel or riad in Agadir in the morning. Leaving the Atlantic coast behind, you will travel through the Souss Valley toward Taroudant, a traditional Moroccan town surrounded by historic walls. Enjoy a short stop to explore the town and take photographs before continuing inland.

The journey takes you toward Taliouine, a region known for saffron cultivation. Along the way, you will pass small villages, agricultural areas and the rocky landscapes of southern Morocco. Your driver can arrange stops for lunch, refreshments and photographs whenever time allows.

In the afternoon, continue toward Ouarzazate, where the scenery becomes increasingly dry and mountainous. Upon arrival, check into your hotel or riad and enjoy some free time to relax after the journey.

Depending on your arrival time, you may have the opportunity to take a short walk around Ouarzazate before dinner.

~370 km Ouarzazate Dinner
Day 2

Ouarzazate → Agdz → Nkob → Alnif → Merzouga

After breakfast, leave Ouarzazate and travel south toward Agdz, where the landscapes of the Anti-Atlas Mountains and the palm groves of the Draa Valley create a beautiful setting for the journey. Continue through traditional villages and rocky desert scenery toward Nkob, a small town known for its old kasbahs.

The road then takes you through Alnif, where you can stop for lunch before continuing toward the Sahara. As you travel farther east, the landscape becomes more open, with dry plains, distant mountains and small desert settlements along the way.

In the afternoon, arrive in Merzouga at the edge of the Erg Chebbi dunes. After a short rest, meet your camel guide and begin your ride across the golden sand. Stop to watch the sunset before continuing to your desert camp.

In the evening, enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner and music around the campfire. Spend some time beneath the desert sky before retiring to your private tent.

~360 km Sahara desert camp, Merzouga Breakfast & Dinner
Day 3

Merzouga → Rissani → Erfoud → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley

Wake up early to watch the sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes. After breakfast at the desert camp, return to Merzouga by camel or 4×4 and meet your driver for the journey toward the Dades Valley.

Travel through Rissani, a traditional town known for its local market, before continuing toward Erfoud. Along the way, you will pass desert landscapes, small villages and palm groves as you leave the Sahara behind.

Continue toward Tinghir and Todra Gorge, where you will have time to walk through the canyon and admire its towering rock walls. Enjoy lunch in the area before continuing through the surrounding valleys toward Dades.

In the afternoon, arrive in the Dades Valley. Depending on your arrival time, your driver can take you to a viewpoint overlooking the winding mountain roads and unusual rock formations.

Afterwards, check into your hotel or riad and enjoy dinner in the peaceful surroundings of the valley.

~300 km Dades Valley Breakfast & Dinner
Day 4

Dades Valley → Valley of Roses → Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → Marrakech

After breakfast, leave the Dades Valley and travel through the Valley of Roses toward Skoura and Ouarzazate. Along the way, you will pass small villages, palm groves and traditional kasbahs, with opportunities to stop for photographs.

Continue toward Aït Ben Haddou, a historic fortified village known for its traditional earthen buildings and narrow passages. Take time to walk through the old ksar, explore its streets and enjoy the views over the surrounding valley.

After lunch, begin the journey across the High Atlas Mountains toward Marrakech. The road follows the Tizi n'Tichka Pass, offering views of mountain villages, valleys and changing landscapes along the way. Your driver can arrange short stops at viewpoints when road conditions and time allow. Arrive in Marrakech in the evening, where your driver will drop you off at your hotel, riad or another agreed location in the city.

~360 km End of tour: Marrakech Breakfast

FAQ

Where does the 4-day desert tour start and end?

The tour starts at your hotel or riad in Agadir and ends at your accommodation in Marrakech. It is a one-way private tour, so you do not need to return to Agadir after visiting the Sahara.

Is this a private tour?

Yes. You will travel in your own private vehicle with a professional driver. The tour does not include unrelated travelers or shared group transportation.

How many nights will we spend in the Sahara?

You will spend one night in a Sahara desert camp in Merzouga. The other two nights are in Ouarzazate and the Dades Valley.

Is the camel ride included?

Yes. A sunset camel ride through the Erg Chebbi dunes is included. If you prefer not to ride a camel, a 4×4 transfer to the desert camp can be arranged instead.

Can we customize the itinerary?

Yes. We can adjust the accommodation category, sightseeing stops and optional activities according to your travel plans. The tour can also be extended if you would like more time in Merzouga or along the route.
