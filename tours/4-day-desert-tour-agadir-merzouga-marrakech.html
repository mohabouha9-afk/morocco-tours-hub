Overview

From the beaches of Agadir to the dunes of the Sahara and on to Marrakech, without ever driving the same road twice. Out through the saffron villages of the Anti-Atlas and the palm groves of the Draa Valley, back through the Todra and Dades gorges and the kasbahs of the south.

A sunset camel ride and a night in a camp in the Erg Chebbi dunes, Aït Ben Haddou on the last morning, and days of five to seven hours on the road with plenty of stops. Your own car and driver-guide all the way, and no shop stops you didn’t ask for.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Agadir → Taroudant → Taliouine → Ouarzazate

    Pick-up at your hotel in Agadir around 08:00. Inland through the argan groves of the Souss Valley to Taroudant and a short walk by its red ramparts, then up into the Anti-Atlas to Taliouine, the saffron country, purple with flowers in late October and November. Ouarzazate in the afternoon, with time to walk into the Taourirt Kasbah before dinner if you like.

    • About 390 km, about 6.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Ouarzazate
    • Dinner & breakfast
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Ouarzazate → Agdz → Draa Valley → N’Kob → Merzouga

    South over the Tizi n’Tinifift pass to Agdz and the long palm groves of the Draa Valley, then east by the quiet desert road through N’Kob, the village of forty-five kasbahs, and Alnif. The dunes of Erg Chebbi appear in the afternoon: leave your big bags in the car and ride a camel into the sand for sunset. Dinner, drums by the fire and a night in the camp.

    • About 365 km, about 6.5 hours on the road
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Breakfast & dinner
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Merzouga → Rissani → Erfoud → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley

    Sunrise and sandboarding on the dunes, breakfast, and back by camel or 4×4. Through Rissani, the old caravan town, and Erfoud with its fossil workshops, to the palm grove of Tinghir and a walk between the 300 m walls of the Todra Gorge. On to the Dades Valley for a quiet evening among the red cliffs.

    • About 270 km, about 5 hours on the road
    • Night: Dades Valley
    • Breakfast & dinner
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Dades → Valley of Roses → Aït Ben Haddou → Tizi n’Tichka → Marrakech

    Down the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs, through the Valley of Roses and the Skoura palm grove, to Aït Ben Haddou and a walk up through the ksar to its old granary. Then over the High Atlas by the Tizi n’Tichka pass, 2,260 m, and down to Marrakech in the late afternoon, at your hotel or riad.

    • About 320 km, about 6 hours on the road
    • Drop-off at your Marrakech hotel or riad
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is each day on the road?

About 390 km on day 1, 365 km on day 2, 270 km on day 3 and 320 km on day 4: five to seven hours a day with stops for photos, coffee and lunch. Agadir to Merzouga is about 10 hours by road, so splitting it keeps every day easy.

Why out by the Draa Valley and back by the gorges?

So you never drive the same road twice. The southern road by the Draa palm groves and N’Kob is quiet and leads you straight to the dunes; the way back takes in the Todra and Dades gorges and Aït Ben Haddou.

Can the tour end back in Agadir?

Marrakech is about 3 hours from Agadir by motorway, so we can drive you back the next day. Or we turn the route into a 5-day trip that ends on the coast; tell us when you book.

What do we do in Ouarzazate on the first evening?

You arrive in the afternoon, in time to walk into the Taourirt Kasbah, the old home of the Glaoui family, or to see the outside of the Atlas film studios. Or simply rest by the pool after the drive.

When is the best time to go?

Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are the most pleasant. Summer days in the desert pass 40 °C, so we start early; winter nights in the camp and the mountains are cold, and blankets are provided.
