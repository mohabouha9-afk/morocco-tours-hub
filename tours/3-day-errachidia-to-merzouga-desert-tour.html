Overview

The quickest way into the Sahara: fly to Errachidia and you are at the Merzouga dunes about two hours later. Three days in your own car with your own driver-guide, and almost all of it spent in the desert instead of on the road.

A first night in a hotel by the dunes, then a whole day around Merzouga with a nomad family and the Gnawa musicians of Khamlia, a camel ride into Erg Chebbi for sunset and a night in the camp. We meet every flight, at any hour, and you can finish back at the airport or carry on to Fes or Marrakech. No groups and no shop stops you didn’t ask for.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Errachidia airport → Ziz palm grove → Erfoud → Merzouga

    Your driver-guide meets you in the arrivals hall with your name on a sign, whatever time your flight lands; we follow your flight, so a delay is no problem. South along the palm groves of the Ziz Valley and through Erfoud to Merzouga, about two hours. Dinner and a quiet night in a hotel at the edge of the dunes, so you wake up rested, right next to the Sahara.

    • About 125 km, about 2 hours on the road
    • Night: Merzouga hotel
    • Dinner & breakfast
  2. 2
    Day 2

    A full day around Merzouga, sunset camel ride and the camp

    Sunrise from the dunes if you like, then a day around Merzouga by 4×4 at your pace: tea with a nomad family in their tent, Gnawa music in the village of Khamlia, the black stony desert and, after a wet winter, the seasonal lake of Dayet Srij. In the late afternoon, leave your big bags at the hotel, take a small bag and ride a camel into the Erg Chebbi dunes for sunset. Dinner in the camp, music around the fire and a sky full of stars.

    • 4×4 tour around Merzouga
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Breakfast & dinner
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Merzouga → Rissani → Erfoud → Errachidia

    Wake for sunrise on the dunes and try the sandboards, then breakfast at the camp and back by camel or 4×4. In Rissani, the old caravan town, walk through the market (busiest on Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday), then see how fossils are cut and polished in Erfoud. North along the Ziz palm groves to Errachidia: we drop you at the airport in good time for your flight, or at your hotel. Prefer to go on? We can end the tour in Fes or Marrakech instead.

    • About 135 km, about 2.5 hours on the road
    • Drop-off at Errachidia airport or hotel
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Merzouga from Errachidia airport?

About 125 km, or around two hours by road through the Ziz palm groves and Erfoud. It is the closest airport to the Erg Chebbi dunes, which is why a short trip from here leaves so much time for the desert.

Which flights go to Errachidia?

Royal Air Maroc flies from Casablanca and Rabat, Ryanair from Marrakech, and Transavia from Paris-Orly in season. Most flights land in the evening; we meet every flight, at any hour.

What if my flight lands late at night or is delayed?

No problem. Your driver-guide follows your flight and waits in the arrivals hall with your name on a sign, then drives you to your hotel in Merzouga. That is why the first night is in a hotel and the camp comes on day 2.

Can we end the tour in Fes or Marrakech instead?

Yes. On day 3 we can drive you on to Fes over the Middle Atlas (about 8 hours) or to Marrakech by the Todra Gorge and the High Atlas (about 9 hours). Tell us when you book.

What is the desert camp like?

A private Berber tent with real beds in the Erg Chebbi dunes, with dinner and breakfast at the camp. All three camps in our table have a private bathroom in the tent; see the Accommodations section above.

How do I book and pay?

  1. Send your request with the form or on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay a 20–30% deposit by bank transfer to confirm; the rest is paid on arrival in cash (EUR, USD or MAD) or by card.

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before the tour.
