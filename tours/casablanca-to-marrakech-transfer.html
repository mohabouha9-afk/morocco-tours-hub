Overview

Most long-haul flights to Morocco land in Casablanca, and most travellers want to be in Marrakech. Your driver meets you in arrivals at Mohammed V airport, or at your Casablanca hotel, and takes you straight to Marrakech on the motorway. No train change at Casa Voyageurs, no haggling for a taxi after a long flight.

The same car runs the other way: from your riad or hotel in Marrakech to Casablanca, or straight to the airport for your flight home, timed so you arrive with hours to spare.

How It Works

  1. 1
    Before you travel

    Send your flight or address

    Your flight number and landing time, or your pick-up address and time. The evening before, you get your driver’s name and number on WhatsApp.

  2. 2
    At Mohammed V airport

    Your name on a sign

    Your driver waits in the arrivals hall after customs. We track your flight, so a late landing or a long queue at passport control costs nothing extra.

  3. 3
    In Casablanca

    Or pick-up at your door

    From your hotel, the port or Casa Voyageurs station. Want to see the Hassan II Mosque first? We stop there before the motorway (see Extras).

  4. 4
    On the A7

    About three hours of motorway

    A smooth toll motorway all the way, with no mountain bends. Coffee or a toilet stop at a motorway rest area whenever you ask.

  5. 5
    Arriving in Marrakech

    To your hotel or riad door

    Cars cannot enter the medina lanes, so your driver parks at the nearest gate and helps you with the bags to the riad door.

  6. 6
    The other way

    Marrakech to Casablanca or your flight

    Pick-up at your riad gate or hotel. For a flight from Casablanca, we usually leave Marrakech about 6 hours before departure, so you reach the airport 3 hours ahead.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if my flight is late or we land at night?

Your driver still waits. We follow your flight, so the pick-up moves with the real landing time, and we meet every flight, day or night. A night arrival means a quiet motorway: you are in Marrakech about three hours later.

Can we stop at the Hassan II Mosque on the way?

Yes. The guided visit inside takes about an hour at set times (fewer on Fridays and in Ramadan), and the ticket is 140 MAD. Your luggage stays locked in the car with the driver. For more of the city, add our Casablanca city tour before the drive.

We fly home from Casablanca. When do we leave Marrakech?

Usually about 6 hours before departure: around 3 hours on the road with a short stop, and 3 hours at the airport for check-in and security. For a morning flight we leave Marrakech in the night, so tell us your flight time and we plan it with you.

Is three hours in the car tiring?

It is an easy drive: a straight, flat motorway with rest areas along the way. The car is air-conditioned, there is water on board, and you stop whenever you want.

Can you also drive us to Rabat, Fes or Essaouira?

Yes, we drive between any two cities in Morocco. Tell us your route, or turn the drive into a trip with one of our tours from Casablanca.

How do I book and pay?

  1. Send your request with the form or on WhatsApp: date, flight or pick-up place, and destination.
  2. Pay a small deposit to confirm; the rest is paid to your driver, in cash or by card.
