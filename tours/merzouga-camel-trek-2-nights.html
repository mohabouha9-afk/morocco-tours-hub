Overview

Most visitors ride an hour into the dunes and back. On this trek you keep going: a first night in a desert camp, then a whole day by camel through the heart of Erg Chebbi, lunch and tea with a nomad family, and a second night in a small nomad camp far from any road.

The same camel guide walks with you all three days. We meet you in Merzouga, keep your car and big bags with us, and agree everything before you arrive: no fees on the way.

Programme

  1. 1
    Day 1, afternoon

    Meet us in Merzouga

    About 1.5 hours before sunset: around 16:30 in winter, 19:00 in summer. Your car and big bags stay with us; take a small soft bag for the camel. We confirm the time on WhatsApp the day before.

  2. 2
    Day 1, sunset

    Ride to the desert camp

    About 1.5 hours by camel, with a stop on a high dune for the sunset. Mint tea in camp, dinner, drums by the fire and your own tent for the night.

  3. 3
    Day 2, morning

    Deeper into the dunes

    Sunrise from the dunes, breakfast, then about 2 hours by camel through the heart of Erg Chebbi. Walk beside your camel whenever you want to stretch your legs.

  4. 4
    Day 2, midday

    Lunch with a nomad family

    Lunch and mint tea at a nomad family’s tent, then a long rest in the shade through the hottest hours.

  5. 5
    Day 2, evening

    The nomad camp

    About 1 hour more to a small, simple nomad camp. Sunset from the dunes, dinner by the fire and a sky full of stars, with no lights for miles.

  6. 6
    Day 3, morning

    Sunrise and back

    Sunrise, breakfast and about 1.5 to 2 hours by camel back to Merzouga. Take a shower in our room before you drive on; back around 10:30.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do we ride each day?

About 1.5 hours on day 1, about 3 hours on day 2 split by a long lunch break, and 1.5 to 2 hours on day 3. Your guide sets the pace, and you can walk beside your camel whenever you like.

How simple is the nomad camp?

Simple on purpose: Berber tents, mattresses and thick blankets, dinner by the fire and a shared toilet, but no showers or electricity. If you would rather have comfort both nights, sleep at your first camp again.

What do we do with our luggage?

Leave it with us in Merzouga with your car. Take one small soft bag with a warm layer, a toothbrush and your camera: it rides on the camel. You shower in our room when you come back.

When is the best time for the trek?

October to April is the most pleasant. In summer we start early and rest through the midday heat; in winter the days are mild but the nights are cold.

Can children do the trek?

Older children who are happy on a camel usually love it. With little ones we suggest one night at the desert camp instead, or a 4×4 between the camps.

How do I book and pay?

  1. Send your request with the form or on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay a small deposit to confirm; the rest is paid on the day, in cash or by card.
