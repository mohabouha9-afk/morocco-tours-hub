Tour Overview

Discover the incredible diversity of Morocco on this 14-Day Morocco Tour from Tangier to Tangier, a complete private journey combining the country's northern cities, Atlantic coast, imperial heritage, High Atlas Mountains and Sahara Desert.

Your adventure begins in Tangier, continues through Rabat and Casablanca, and then heads south to the colorful streets of Marrakech and the relaxed Atlantic atmosphere of Essaouira. From Marrakech, travel across the High Atlas Mountains and through the dramatic landscapes of southern Morocco before reaching Merzouga and the Erg Chebbi dunes.

After your Sahara experience, continue north through the Ziz Valley and Middle Atlas Mountains to Fes, then discover the blue-painted streets of Chefchaouen before returning to Tangier. The itinerary combines cultural sightseeing with scenic road journeys and time to relax, making it an excellent two-week introduction to Morocco.

"More than a trip... It's a story to tell!"

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Tangier: Arrival & City Tour

Welcome to Morocco! Your 14-Day Morocco Tour from Tangier to Tangier begins with a private pickup from Tangier Ibn Battouta Airport, ferry port or your accommodation. Settle into your hotel before beginning an introduction to this fascinating northern city. Depending on your arrival time, explore the historic Kasbah, the old medina and the Grand Socco.

Tangier has a unique atmosphere influenced by its position between the Mediterranean Sea and Atlantic Ocean and its historic connections with Europe. Visit Cap Spartel, where the Atlantic and Mediterranean meet, and continue to the nearby Caves of Hercules if time allows. In the evening, enjoy your first Moroccan dinner and relax at your hotel. Your driver will also discuss the itinerary with you so you can adjust stops and activities according to your interests.

Tangier No meals
Day 2

Tangier → Asilah → Rabat

After breakfast, leave Tangier and travel south along Morocco's Atlantic coastline toward Rabat, the country's capital. Before continuing, make a stop in Asilah, a charming fortified coastal town famous for its whitewashed buildings, artistic atmosphere and historic medina. Take time to walk along the old walls and enjoy views of the Atlantic Ocean.

Continue toward Rabat, arriving in the afternoon. Explore some of the capital's most important landmarks, including the Hassan Tower, the Mausoleum of Mohammed V and the Kasbah of the Udayas overlooking the Bou Regreg River. If time permits, enjoy a walk through the historic medina before checking into your accommodation. Rabat offers a calmer atmosphere than many larger Moroccan cities and provides an excellent introduction to the country's history, royal heritage and modern capital.

~250 km Rabat Breakfast
Day 3

Rabat → Casablanca → Marrakech

After breakfast, depart Rabat and continue south to Casablanca, Morocco's largest city and economic capital. Begin your visit with the impressive Hassan II Mosque, dramatically positioned beside the Atlantic Ocean. Depending on the opening schedule, a guided interior visit can be arranged. Continue through the city to see the Corniche, Mohammed V Square and other notable areas before enjoying lunch.

In the afternoon, continue toward Marrakech, traveling through the changing landscapes of central Morocco. Arrive in the Red City later in the day and check into your riad or hotel. The evening is free to relax or take your first walk around Jemaa el-Fnaa, Marrakech's famous main square, where food stalls, performers and local activity create a lively atmosphere after sunset.

~330 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 4

Guided Tour of Marrakech

Today is dedicated to discovering Marrakech, one of Morocco's most famous imperial cities. After breakfast, meet your local guide and explore the historic medina. Visit the beautiful Bahia Palace, the Saadian Tombs and the Koutoubia Mosque from outside. Continue through the colorful souks, where you can see artisans working with leather, metal, wood and textiles. Your guide will explain the history and traditions behind the city's famous markets.

Later, visit the lively Jemaa el-Fnaa and enjoy time for lunch in the medina. In the afternoon, you may choose to visit the Majorelle Garden and the surrounding modern district or simply relax at your riad. The evening is free to enjoy Marrakech at your own pace, perhaps returning to Jemaa el-Fnaa after sunset when the square becomes especially lively.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 5

Marrakech → Essaouira

Leave Marrakech after breakfast and drive west toward the Atlantic coast to Essaouira, a journey of approximately three hours. Along the way, travel through the landscapes of Morocco's argan-growing region. If conditions allow, stop to see traditional argan production and learn how local cooperatives make argan oil.

Continue to Essaouira, a relaxed coastal city known for its white-and-blue architecture, historic ramparts, fishing harbor and artistic atmosphere. After checking into your accommodation, explore the old medina and walk along the Skala de la Ville, where historic cannons overlook the Atlantic. Visit the fishing port and enjoy fresh seafood if desired. The rest of the afternoon is free for shopping, photography or a walk along the beach. Essaouira provides a welcome change of pace after the busy streets of Marrakech.

~190 km Essaouira Breakfast
Day 6

Essaouira: Free Day

Enjoy a relaxed day exploring Essaouira at your own pace. After breakfast, you can wander through the UNESCO-listed medina, discover small art galleries and artisan workshops, or return to the harbor to watch local fishermen bring in their daily catch. Walk along the Atlantic beach or enjoy a traditional Moroccan lunch overlooking the ocean.

For travelers interested in local culture, Essaouira is known for its musical traditions, crafts and strong connection to the sea. You can also visit the historic ramparts for panoramic views of the coastline. The day is intentionally flexible, giving you time to slow down and enjoy the coastal atmosphere without following a strict sightseeing schedule. In the evening, return to your riad or hotel and prepare for the following day's journey toward the High Atlas Mountains and the Sahara region.

Essaouira Breakfast
Day 7

Essaouira → Marrakech → High Atlas → Aït Ben Haddou → Dades Valley

Today begins your journey into southern Morocco. Leave Essaouira after breakfast and return toward Marrakech before continuing across the spectacular High Atlas Mountains. Cross the Tizi n'Tichka Pass, stopping at viewpoints to admire the mountain scenery and traditional Amazigh villages.

Continue toward Aït Ben Haddou, Morocco's famous fortified ksar and UNESCO World Heritage site. Explore its narrow lanes and traditional earthen architecture before continuing toward Ouarzazate. The journey then follows the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs toward the Dades Valley. Along the way, enjoy scenic views of palm oases, villages and rugged mountain landscapes. Arrive in the Dades Valley in the afternoon or evening and check into your hotel. After dinner, relax and enjoy the peaceful surroundings before continuing toward the Sahara tomorrow.

~530 km Dades Valley Breakfast & Dinner
Day 8

Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Erfoud → Rissani → Merzouga

After breakfast, continue toward the Sahara Desert. Drive through the spectacular landscapes of the Dades region before reaching Todra Gorge, where towering limestone cliffs create one of southern Morocco's most impressive natural landscapes. Enjoy a walk through the gorge before continuing toward Erfoud, known for fossils and dates. Continue through the desert landscapes to Rissani, an important historic trading town and gateway to the Sahara.

In the afternoon, arrive in Merzouga, where the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi rise dramatically from the surrounding landscape. Leave your vehicle and begin a camel trek into the dunes. Ride through the Sahara as the sun begins to set, reaching your desert camp in the evening. Enjoy a traditional dinner, Berber music and a peaceful night beneath the stars.

~300 km Sahara Desert Camp, Merzouga Breakfast & Dinner
Day 9

Merzouga: Sahara 4×4 Experience

Wake early to watch the sunrise over Erg Chebbi before returning to the camp for breakfast. Today is dedicated to discovering the Sahara region at a relaxed pace. Travel by 4×4 around the dunes and visit a traditional nomadic family, learning about life in the desert. Continue to Khamlia village, known for its Gnawa music, and enjoy a traditional musical performance.

Depending on your interests, visit seasonal desert lakes, old mining areas or panoramic viewpoints over the dunes. Return to Merzouga for lunch and free time. You may choose to try quad biking across the dunes as an optional activity or simply relax at your hotel or camp. In the afternoon, enjoy another beautiful Sahara sunset. Spending a second night in the Merzouga area allows you to experience the desert without immediately having to continue a long road journey.

~40 km Merzouga Breakfast & Dinner
Day 10

Merzouga → Ziz Valley → Midelt → Azrou → Ifrane → Fes

After breakfast, leave Merzouga and travel north toward Fes. The route follows the spectacular Ziz Valley and continues through Errachidia before reaching Midelt, known for its apple-growing region. Continue into the Middle Atlas Mountains, where the scenery changes dramatically from desert landscapes to forests and mountain valleys.

Stop around Azrou to see the famous cedar forests, where Barbary macaques can sometimes be spotted. Continue to Ifrane, a distinctive mountain town known for its alpine-style architecture and cool climate. After a short visit, continue toward Fes. Arrive in the late afternoon or evening and check into your riad in or near the historic medina. After several days in the desert and mountains, enjoy a relaxing evening in Fes and prepare for tomorrow's guided exploration of one of Morocco's most fascinating historic cities.

~470 km Fes Breakfast
Day 11

Guided Tour of Fes

Today you will explore Fes, Morocco's spiritual and cultural capital. After breakfast, meet your local guide and begin your walking tour through the ancient Fes el-Bali medina, one of the country's most remarkable historic areas. Wander through narrow streets filled with traditional workshops, markets and historic buildings.

Visit important landmarks such as the Bou Inania Madrasa, Nejjarine Fountain and the famous Chouara Tanneries, viewed from one of the surrounding terraces. Your guide will explain the history of the city's religious schools, crafts and traditional neighborhoods. Enjoy lunch in the medina before continuing your exploration. The afternoon can be adapted to your interests, whether you want to focus on architecture, shopping, photography or local crafts. Return to your riad in the evening and enjoy some free time in Fes.

Fes Breakfast
Day 12

Fes → Volubilis → Chefchaouen

Leave Fes after breakfast and travel toward the Rif Mountains. Along the way, visit the ancient Roman ruins of Volubilis, where you can see remarkably preserved mosaics, columns and remains of Roman-era buildings. Continue to Chefchaouen, the famous blue-painted town nestled in the Rif Mountains.

After arrival, explore the picturesque medina with its blue-washed streets, small squares, artisan shops and cafés. Walk toward the Kasbah and enjoy views over the town from the surrounding hills. Chefchaouen has a very different atmosphere from Morocco's larger imperial cities, making it a relaxing final stop before returning to Tangier. Enjoy dinner at a local restaurant or your accommodation and take an evening walk through the beautifully illuminated blue streets.

~200 km Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 13

Chefchaouen → Tangier

After breakfast, enjoy a relaxed morning in Chefchaouen. There is time for photographs, shopping for local handicrafts or enjoying Moroccan mint tea in one of the medina's small cafés. Later, depart for Tangier, traveling through the Rif Mountains.

On arrival, continue with a sightseeing tour if desired, including the Kasbah, Grand Socco, Petit Socco and the historic areas of the city. You can also visit Cap Spartel, where the Atlantic Ocean and Mediterranean Sea meet, or the nearby Caves of Hercules. Afterward, check into your hotel and enjoy your final evening in Morocco. This is a good opportunity to have a farewell dinner and reflect on the landscapes and cities you have experienced during your journey from northern Morocco to the Sahara and Atlantic coast.

~115 km Tangier Breakfast
Day 14

Tangier: Departure

Your 14-Day Morocco Tour from Tangier to Tangier comes to an end today. After breakfast, enjoy some free time depending on your departure schedule. If your flight or ferry is later in the day, you can take a final walk through Tangier's medina, enjoy coffee or Moroccan mint tea, or do some last-minute shopping.

At the agreed time, your private driver will transfer you to Tangier Ibn Battouta Airport, Tangier ferry port or another location in the city. Your journey has taken you through Morocco's northern coastline, Rabat and Casablanca, Marrakech and Essaouira, the High Atlas Mountains, Aït Ben Haddou, Dades Valley, Todra Gorge, the Sahara Desert, the Middle Atlas Mountains, Fes and Chefchaouen. We hope you leave Morocco with wonderful memories and a desire to return and discover more.

End of tour: Tangier Breakfast

FAQ

Can this tour start and finish in Tangier?

Yes. This itinerary is specifically designed as a Tangier-to-Tangier private Morocco tour.

Does the tour include Casablanca and Rabat?

Yes. You will visit both Rabat and Casablanca before continuing south toward Marrakech.

Is Essaouira included?

Yes. The itinerary includes two nights in Essaouira, giving you time to experience the Atlantic coast at a relaxed pace.

How many nights are spent in the Sahara?

The itinerary includes two nights in the Merzouga area, with one night in the Sahara Desert Camp and a second night in Merzouga.

Is the camel ride included?

Yes. A sunset camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes is included.

Can we add quad biking?

Yes. Quad biking can be arranged in Merzouga as an optional activity.

Are Marrakech and Fes guided tours included?

Yes. Local guided visits in Marrakech and Fes are included in this itinerary.

Can the itinerary be customized?

Absolutely. This is a private tour, so the route, accommodation level, activities and pace can be adjusted according to your preferences.
