Overview

Two weeks around the whole of Morocco, starting and ending at the Strait of Gibraltar. Down the Atlantic by Asilah, Rabat, the Hassan II Mosque and the oyster lagoon of Oualidia to two nights in Essaouira, then two nights in Marrakech with a guided day in the Red City.

Over the High Atlas to Aït Ben Haddou, through the Dades and Todra gorges to two nights by the dunes of Erg Chebbi, then north to Fes and the blue town of Chefchaouen. A full loop that never uses the same road twice, with your own car and driver-guide all the way.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Tangier → Asilah → Rabat

    Your driver-guide meets you off the ferry at the port, at Tanger Med, at the airport or at your hotel. Down the Atlantic to Asilah, a white seaside medina painted with murals, for lunch by the ramparts, then on to Rabat, the capital, for the evening.

    • About 250 km, about 3 hours on the motorway
    • Night: Rabat
    • Breakfast from day 2
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Rabat → Casablanca → El Jadida → Oualidia

    The Kasbah of the Oudayas and the Hassan Tower in the morning light, then the Hassan II Mosque in Casablanca, built over the ocean. South along the coast to the Portuguese cistern of El Jadida and the calm lagoon of Oualidia, famous for its oysters.

    • About 310 km, about 4 hours on the road
    • Night: Oualidia
    • Breakfast
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Oualidia → Safi → Essaouira

    A quiet morning on the lagoon, then the coast road past the potters’ hill of Safi and the argan country to Essaouira, the walled port on the ocean, in time for a first walk on the Skala ramparts at sunset.

    • About 190 km, about 3 hours on the road
    • Night: Essaouira
    • Breakfast
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Essaouira: a day by the ocean

    No driving: the blue fishing boats in the harbour, grilled fish for lunch, the Mellah and the woodworkers under the ramparts, and the long beach. The trade winds make it a fresh, easy day after the road.

    • No driving unless you ask
    • Night: Essaouira
    • Breakfast
  5. 5
    Day 5

    Essaouira → Marrakech

    East across the argan plains to Marrakech, your riad in the medina by lunchtime and a free afternoon. Jemaa el-Fnaa comes alive after dark with music and food stalls.

    • About 190 km, about 2.5–3 hours on the road
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  6. 6
    Day 6

    Marrakech: a guided day in the Red City

    With a local Marrakech guide: the Koutoubia, the painted ceilings of the Bahia Palace, the Ben Youssef Madrasa and the souks. In the afternoon, the Majorelle Garden or the riad pool, as you like.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  7. 7
    Day 7

    Marrakech → Tizi n’Tichka → Aït Ben Haddou

    Over the High Atlas by the Tizi n’Tichka pass, 2,260 m, past Berber villages clinging to the slopes, to Aït Ben Haddou. An afternoon walk up through the ksar and a sunset from the old granary, then a night beside it.

    • About 190 km, about 4 hours on the road
    • Night: Aït Ben Haddou
    • Breakfast & dinner
  8. 8
    Day 8

    Aït Ben Haddou → Skoura → Valley of Roses → Dades Gorge

    The Road of a Thousand Kasbahs: the palm oasis of Skoura and its kasbahs, the rose villages of Kelaat M’Gouna, then up the Dades Gorge among red cliffs and the rock formations called the Monkey Fingers.

    • About 180 km, about 3.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Dades Valley
    • Breakfast & dinner
  9. 9
    Day 9

    Dades → Todra Gorge → Merzouga

    A walk between the 300 m walls of the Todra Gorge in the cool of the morning, then east along the Jebel Saghro to Erg Chebbi. A camel ride into the dunes for sunset, dinner and drums by the fire in the camp.

    • About 320 km, about 5.5 hours on the road
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Breakfast & dinner
  10. 10
    Day 10

    A full day around Merzouga by 4×4

    Sunrise and sandboarding, then a day with no long road: tea with a nomad family, the Gnawa musicians of Khamlia and the market town of Rissani. A hot shower and a calm night in a hotel by the dunes.

    • 4×4 day around Merzouga
    • Night: Merzouga hotel
    • Breakfast & dinner
  11. 11
    Day 11

    Merzouga → Ziz Valley → Midelt → Ifrane → Fes

    North through the palm gorges of the Ziz Valley, lunch in Midelt, then over the Middle Atlas through the cedar forest with its Barbary macaques and the alpine town of Ifrane, to your riad in the Fes medina.

    • About 470 km, about 8 hours on the road
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  12. 12
    Day 12

    Fes: a guided day in the medina

    With a local Fes guide: the Bou Inania Madrasa, the Nejjarine fountain, the coppersmiths of Place Seffarine and a rooftop above the Chouara tanneries. Shops only if you ask.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  13. 13
    Day 13

    Fes → Volubilis → Chefchaouen

    The Roman town of Volubilis, with its mosaics and triumphal arch, then north into the green Rif hills to Chefchaouen, the blue town, for an evening in its lanes and sunset from the Spanish Mosque.

    • About 265 km, about 5 hours on the road
    • Night: Chefchaouen
    • Breakfast
  14. 14
    Day 14

    Chefchaouen → Tetouan → Tangier, end of the tour

    A last morning in the blue lanes, then down through Tetouan to Tangier: the city port for the Tarifa ferry, Tanger Med for Algeciras, the airport or your hotel. A short day, so there is time to spare for your crossing.

    • About 125 km, about 2.5 hours on the road
    • Ends at Tangier port, Tanger Med or the airport
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

What if our return ferry is cancelled by the wind?

The Levante wind sometimes stops the Tarifa ferries for a few hours or a day. That is why the last day is short: we watch the forecast with you and can switch you to Tanger Med and the larger Algeciras ships, or plan a later sailing.

Why go down the coast first?

Going south by Rabat, Oualidia and Essaouira means you reach the ocean without driving Marrakech to Essaouira and back. The loop then crosses the Atlas, the desert and the north, so no road is driven twice.

Can we bring our own car on the ferry instead?

Many people do, but a private driver means no Moroccan insurance papers, no navigating medinas and no long desert days at the wheel. Leave your car at the port in Spain and walk on, and we meet you on the Tangier side.

Which days are the long drives?

Day 11, Merzouga to Fes, about eight hours with stops, and day 9 to the dunes, about five and a half. Days 4, 6, 10 and 12 have little or no driving, and the last day to Tangier is short.

Is Oualidia worth the night?

Yes, if you like slow places. The lagoon is calm and beautiful at high tide, the oysters and fish are the best on the coast, and it breaks the long drive to Essaouira. At low tide the water draws back over the sand flats.
