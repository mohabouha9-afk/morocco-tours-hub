Overview

Three of Morocco's most historic sites in one day from Fes. Volubilis is the country's best-preserved Roman city, a UNESCO site with stunning mosaics still in situ. Moulay Idriss is the holy hill town where Morocco's first Islamic dynasty began. Meknes, the Versailles of Morocco, was the 17th-century capital of Sultan Moulay Ismail.

Your driver-guide picks you up at your Fes riad or hotel, guides you through Volubilis and Meknes, and gives you free time in Moulay Idriss and for lunch in Meknes. No shopping stops unless you ask.

    The drive is about 80 km through the rolling hills of the Saïs plain. You arrive around 10:00 and start the guided walk.

    About 2 hours with your guide: the Decumanus Maximus, the Capitol, the Basilica, the Arch of Caracalla, and the famous mosaics (Orpheus, Bacchus, Diana, the Four Seasons). Entrance about 100 MAD.

  4. 4
    12:30

    Moulay Idriss Zerhoun

    A short 5 km drive to the holy town. Your guide shows you the main square and explains the history of Morocco's first Islamic dynasty. Non-Muslims cannot enter the mausoleum but can visit the town. Free time for coffee and photos (about 45 minutes).

  5. 5
    13:30

    Lunch in Meknes

    Drive 25 km to Meknes (about 30 minutes). Your guide suggests a few local places near Place El-Hedim; you choose where to eat (around €10–15).

  6. 6
    14:30

    Meknes — Imperial City

    Guided walk of about 2.5 hours: Bab Mansour gate, Place El-Hedim, the Mausoleum of Moulay Ismail (non-Muslims can enter the courtyard), the Heri es-Souani granaries and stables.

  7. 7
    17:00

    Depart Meknes for Fes

    About 60 km and 1 hour back to Fes on the motorway.

  8. 8
    18:00

    Drop-off at your hotel, riad or the station.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Volubilis from Fes?

About 80 km, 1.5 hours on a good road through the Saïs plain. The round trip is about 2.5 hours of driving, so the day is relaxed compared to Chefchaouen.

Is Volubilis worth the visit?

Yes. It is Morocco's best-preserved Roman site and a UNESCO World Heritage site. The mosaics are still in their original floors, and the Capitol, Basilica and Arch of Caracalla are standing. Two hours with a guide brings it to life.

Can we enter the Moulay Idriss mausoleum?

No. Non-Muslims cannot enter the mausoleum of Moulay Idriss I, but you can visit the town, the main square and the panoramic viewpoint. The same applies to the tomb chamber of Moulay Ismail in Meknes (courtyard is open).

How much walking is there at Volubilis?

About 2 hours on uneven Roman paving at a gentle pace. The site slopes gently and the stones are irregular. Wear comfortable closed shoes.

What are the entrance fees?

Volubilis about 100 MAD per adult (children 7–13 about 50 MAD). Meknes: a small fee at the door of the Heri es-Souani granaries. The Mausoleum of Moulay Ismail courtyard is free. The Moulay Idriss mausoleum is not open to non-Muslims.
