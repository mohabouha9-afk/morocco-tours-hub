Overview

The Ourika Valley is the closest green escape from Marrakech. The road follows a mountain river past Berber villages and terraced fields up to Setti Fatma, where cafés set their tables in the water and seven waterfalls step down the mountain.

It is also one of the busiest day trips from the city, so a private day makes the difference: we set off early, your own guide walks with you to the falls, and you choose where to eat and how far to climb.

Programme

  1. 1
    09:00

    Pick-up in Marrakech

    Your driver meets you at your hotel, or at the nearest gate if your riad is in the medina. We confirm the exact time and place on WhatsApp the day before.

  2. 2
    09:30

    Up the Ourika river road

    About 65 km south, past Tnine Ourika, clay villages and terraced fields along the river. No other pick-ups and no group to wait for; we stop for photos whenever you ask.

  3. 3
    10:45

    Setti Fatma waterfalls

    Your guide walks with you to the first and biggest of the seven falls, 20–40 minutes up a steep, rocky path. Climb on to the quieter falls, or relax by the river instead.

  4. 4
    12:30

    Lunch in the river

    You choose the café, with tables set right in the water: tagine, grilled meat, salads and mint tea. Around €10 a person (not included); your guide checks prices before you order.

  5. 5
    14:30

    Free time by the water

    Cool your feet in the river, take a slow mint tea, or stop at a village and a viewpoint on the way down. The afternoon moves at your pace.

  6. 6
    16:00

    Back to Marrakech

    About an hour and a half back to your hotel or riad, or straight to a restaurant in Guéliz or near Jemaa el-Fna if you go out for dinner.

Good to know

  • Wear shoes with grip: sandals and flip-flops slip on the wet rocks
  • Not possible with wheelchairs or strollers; tell us about knees, heights or small children
  • Sundays, summer and Moroccan holidays are busy; weekdays are calmer
  • After storms the river rises fast: if one is forecast, we change the plan or the date
  • No toilets on the trail: use the café before you start
  • The road has bends: ask for the front seat if you get car-sick

Costs on the day

  • Lunch in the river: around €10 per person (about 80–130 MAD), depending on what you eat
  • Mint tea or a soft drink: about 15–30 MAD; ask the price before you order
  • Waterfall guide and parking: already paid, nothing to hand over
  • Tips: up to you
  • Cash: bring some dirhams; the valley cafés rarely take cards

Frequently Asked Questions

Will anyone at the waterfalls ask us for money?

Not for guiding. Men in Setti Fatma offer to lead visitors up and ask for a fee afterwards, but your licensed guide is already in the price and walks with you, and we pay the parking. A polite “no, thank you” is all you need.

How hard is the walk to the waterfalls?

The first fall is 20–40 minutes up a steep path of wet, polished rock, where you sometimes use your hands. Most people manage it in shoes with grip. Above it the path becomes a real scramble, so small children and anyone wary of heights stop at the first fall, or skip the walk and stay by the river.

Is Ourika a tourist trap?

Setti Fatma is popular: busy on Sundays and in summer, with stalls along the path. What spoils the day on cheap tours is the shop stops, the set-menu restaurant and the rush. With us there are none of those: we go early, you choose where to eat, and you decide how long to stay.

Ourika Valley or Ouzoud Falls?

Ourika is the easy day: 1 to 1.5 hours away, green, with lunch in the river. Ouzoud has far bigger falls but is about 2.5–3 hours each way. If you have one free day and want to rest from the city, choose Ourika.

Can we swim in the river?

A cold dip, yes: the pools are small, rocky and fed by mountain snow. Stay where you can stand, never jump in, and keep out when the water turns brown. Your guide shows you the safe spots.

How do I book, and what if a storm is forecast?

  1. Send your request with the form or on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay a small deposit to confirm; the rest is paid on the day, in cash or by card.

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before. If storms make the river unsafe, we tell you and you choose a new date or a refund.
