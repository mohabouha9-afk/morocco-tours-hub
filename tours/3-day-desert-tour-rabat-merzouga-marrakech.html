Tour Overview

This 3-day private desert tour from Rabat to Merzouga and Marrakech takes you from Morocco's capital to the golden dunes of the Sahara before finishing in Marrakech. Along the way, you will cross the Middle Atlas Mountains, travel through palm-filled valleys, visit traditional towns and discover some of southern Morocco's most impressive landscapes.

Your first day takes you from Rabat to Merzouga, where you will enjoy a sunset camel ride and spend the night in a Sahara desert camp. On the second day, travel through Rissani and Todra Gorge before reaching the Dades Valley. The final day takes you through Ouarzazate and Aït Ben Haddou, then across the High Atlas Mountains to Marrakech.

This is a private tour with your own driver and air-conditioned vehicle. The first day involves a particularly long drive, so an early departure from Rabat is essential. The itinerary is designed for travelers who have three days available and want to experience the Sahara while traveling from Rabat to Marrakech.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Rabat → Ifrane → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

Your private driver will pick you up from your hotel or riad in Rabat early in the morning. Leave the capital behind and travel toward the Middle Atlas Mountains, passing through changing countryside and mountain landscapes on the way to Ifrane.

Continue toward Midelt, where you can stop for lunch before traveling south through the Ziz Valley. Along the way, enjoy views of rocky mountains, traditional villages and palm groves as the landscape gradually becomes drier.

In the late afternoon or evening, arrive in Merzouga at the edge of the Erg Chebbi dunes. If you reach the desert before sunset, meet your camel guide and ride across the sand toward your camp. After arrival, enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner and an evening beneath the Sahara sky.

Please note: Rabat to Merzouga is a very long driving day. Arrival before sunset cannot be guaranteed, particularly during winter. If you arrive after dark, a 4×4 transfer to the camp can be arranged instead of the sunset camel ride.

~560 km Sahara desert camp, Merzouga Dinner
Day 2

Merzouga → Rissani → Erfoud → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley

Wake up early to watch the sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes. After breakfast at the desert camp, return to Merzouga by camel or 4×4 and meet your driver for the journey toward the Dades Valley.

Travel through Rissani, a traditional town known for its local market, before continuing toward Erfoud. As you leave the desert behind, the road passes through small villages, palm groves and rocky landscapes.

Continue toward Tinghir and Todra Gorge, where you will have time to walk between the canyon's towering rock walls and enjoy the scenery. After lunch, travel through the surrounding valleys toward Dades.

In the afternoon, arrive at your hotel or riad in the Dades Valley. Depending on your arrival time, you may have the opportunity to visit a viewpoint overlooking the valley's winding roads and unusual rock formations.

Enjoy dinner and a relaxing evening at your accommodation.

~300 km Dades Valley Breakfast & Dinner
Day 3

Dades Valley → Valley of Roses → Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → Marrakech

After breakfast, leave the Dades Valley and travel through the Valley of Roses toward Skoura and Ouarzazate. Along the way, you will pass traditional villages, palm groves and old kasbahs, with opportunities to stop for photographs.

Continue toward Aït Ben Haddou, a historic fortified village built from traditional earthen materials. Take time to explore its narrow passages, walk among the old buildings and enjoy the views over the surrounding landscape.

After lunch, begin the journey across the High Atlas Mountains toward Marrakech. The road follows the Tizi n'Tichka Pass, with mountain scenery and small villages along the way. Your driver can arrange short stops at viewpoints when conditions allow.

Arrive in Marrakech in the evening, where your driver will drop you off at your hotel, riad or another agreed location in the city.

~360 km End of tour: Marrakech Breakfast

FAQ

Is three days enough to travel from Rabat to Merzouga and Marrakech?

Yes, the route can be completed in three days, but it includes long driving distances. The first day from Rabat to Merzouga is particularly demanding. If you have an additional day available, a four-day itinerary would allow more time for sightseeing and a more relaxed arrival in the desert.

Is the tour private?

Yes. You will travel in your own private vehicle with a professional driver. The tour does not include unrelated travelers or shared group transportation.

Will we arrive in Merzouga in time for sunset?

A sunset arrival cannot be guaranteed on the first day because of the long journey from Rabat. Your driver will arrange an early departure, but the arrival time will depend on road conditions, stops and the season. If you arrive after sunset, you can travel to the camp by 4×4.

Can we still enjoy a camel ride if we arrive late?

Yes. If you arrive too late for a sunset camel ride, it may be possible to arrange a camel ride the following morning, depending on your preferred schedule and the time needed to reach the Dades Valley.

Where does the tour end in Marrakech?

Your driver will drop you off at your hotel, riad or another agreed location in Marrakech on the evening of Day 3.
