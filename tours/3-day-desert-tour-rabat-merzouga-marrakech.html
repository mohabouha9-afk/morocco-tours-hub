Overview

From the capital to the Sahara and on to Marrakech, without driving the same road twice. Across the cedar forests of the Middle Atlas, where Barbary macaques watch from the trees, through Ifrane and Midelt and down the palm gorges of the Ziz Valley to the dunes of Erg Chebbi, for a sunset camel ride and a night in the camp.

Then the great road of the south: the Todra Gorge, an evening in the Dades Valley, the kasbahs of the Valley of Roses and Aït Ben Haddou, and over the High Atlas to Marrakech. Your own car and driver-guide all the way, and no shop stops you didn’t ask for.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Rabat → Ifrane → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

    Pick-up at your hotel or riad in Rabat around 06:30. East into the Middle Atlas to Ifrane, the alpine town of red roofs, and the cedar forest where Barbary macaques live by the road. Lunch in Midelt under the Jebel Ayachi, then down through the palm gorges of the Ziz Valley to Erfoud. At the dunes your big bags stay in the car and you ride a camel into the sand for sunset, then dinner, drums by the fire and a night in the camp.

    • About 600 km, about 9 hours on the road
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Dinner & breakfast
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Merzouga → Rissani → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley

    Sunrise and sandboarding on the dunes, breakfast, and back by camel or 4×4. Through Rissani, the old caravan town, and along the Jebel Saghro to the long palm grove of Tinghir and a walk between the 300 m walls of the Todra Gorge. On to the Dades Valley for a quiet evening among the red cliffs and kasbahs.

    • About 270 km, about 5 hours on the road
    • Night: Dades Valley
    • Breakfast & dinner
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Dades → Valley of Roses → Aït Ben Haddou → Tizi n’Tichka → Marrakech

    Down the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs, through the Valley of Roses and the Skoura palm grove, past Ouarzazate to Aït Ben Haddou and a walk up through the ksar to its old granary. Then over the High Atlas by the Tizi n’Tichka pass, 2,260 m, and down to Marrakech in the late afternoon, at your hotel, riad or the airport.

    • About 320 km, about 6 hours on the road
    • Drop-off at your Marrakech hotel or the airport
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the drive from Rabat to Merzouga?

About 600 km, nine hours with stops for coffee, the cedar forest and lunch in Midelt. That is why we leave early: you reach the dunes in time for the sunset camel ride. Days 2 and 3 are about five and six hours.

Will we reach the dunes in time for sunset?

Yes, with a 06:30 start. In winter, when the sun sets early, we may leave a little earlier; we confirm the time on WhatsApp the day before. If you prefer a calmer first day, we can add a night in Midelt.

Can you pick us up in Casablanca or Meknes?

Yes. Casablanca adds about an hour on the first morning, so we start earlier; Meknes is on the way, close to Ifrane. Send us your hotel name when you book.

Will we see the Barbary macaques?

Usually, yes. They live in the cedar forest near Azrou, and families often sit by the road. We stop so you can watch them; please don’t feed them, it makes them sick and aggressive.

Can you drop us at Marrakech airport?

Yes. We reach Marrakech in the late afternoon, so an evening or next-day flight works well. Send us your flight time when you book.

How do I book and pay?

  1. Send your request with the form or on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay a 20–30% deposit by bank transfer to confirm; the rest is paid on arrival in cash (EUR, USD or MAD) or by card.

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before the tour.
