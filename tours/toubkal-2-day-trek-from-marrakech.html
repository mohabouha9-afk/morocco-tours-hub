Overview

Stand on the summit of Jebel Toubkal, at 4,167 m the highest peak in North Africa, with the whole High Atlas below you. Day one climbs from Imlil through Berber villages to the mountain refuge; day two starts before dawn for the summit, then walks all the way down.

It is a hard trek, not a climb: no ropes or skills, but long days and thin air. Your mountain guide sets a steady pace, a mule carries your bags and a cook feeds you, so all you carry is a day pack.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1 · 08:00

    Marrakech to Imlil

    Pick-up at your hotel and about 1.5 hours by car to Imlil (1,740 m), where you meet your guide, the mule and the cook. Big luggage stays in your Marrakech hotel or in Imlil.

  2. 2
    Day 1 · 10:00

    Up the Mizane valley

    Through Aremd and its walnut terraces, then along the valley to the shrine of Sidi Chamharouch for a cooked lunch, about 2.5 hours.

  3. 3
    Day 1 · afternoon

    To the Toubkal refuge

    About 3 more hours up to the refuge at 3,207 m: in all 5–6 hours and 12 km today. Tea, a hot dinner and an early night in a shared room.

  4. 4
    Day 2 · about 05:00

    The summit

    Breakfast by head torch, then about 3 hours up the south cirque, steep scree and rock with no climbing, to 4,167 m and the view over the whole Atlas.

  5. 5
    Day 2 · mid-morning

    Back to the refuge

    Down the same path in 2–3 hours for lunch at the refuge and a rest; your bags go down with the mule.

  6. 6
    Day 2 · afternoon

    Down to Imlil and Marrakech

    About 4 hours down the valley to Imlil, 10–12 hours of walking today in all, then the car back to your Marrakech hotel in the evening.

Good to know

  • This is a suggestion: tell us your preferences
  • The refuge has shared rooms and bathrooms; hot water is limited
  • The summit is cold and windy even in summer: bring a warm jacket, hat and gloves
  • At 4,167 m the air is thin: walk slowly and tell your guide about headaches
  • November to April there is snow: crampons and mountain experience needed
  • Bring boots you have walked in and a head torch

Costs on the day

  • Sleeping bag: about €2.50 a day to rent in Imlil
  • Winter gear: crampons about €5 and an ice axe about €3 a day
  • Drinks: soft drinks and snacks sold at the refuge
  • Tips: up to you, for the guide, cook and muleteer
  • Cash: bring dirhams, there is no cash machine on the trail

Frequently Asked Questions

How fit do I need to be?

Fit enough to walk uphill for 5–6 hours one day and 10–12 hours the next. No climbing skills are needed, but regular hiking helps a lot; if you are unsure, the 3-day version is easier.

Will I get altitude sickness?

Some people feel headaches or tiredness above 3,000 m. Your guide keeps a slow pace and watches the group; if it gets worse, you turn back, which is the only real cure. A night in Imlil first (3-day version) helps.

Do we need a guide?

Yes. A guide is required on the Toubkal trail, and yours is included: a local professional mountain guide who stays with you from Imlil to the summit and back.

What is the refuge like?

A stone mountain hut with shared rooms of bunk beds, shared bathrooms, a heated dining room and hot meals. Hot water is limited, and a sleeping bag or liner is useful.

Can we do it in winter?

From about November to April the summit is under snow and ice, so crampons, an ice axe and some mountain experience are needed. We check conditions before your date; mid-April to mid-November is easiest.
