Tour Overview

Discover the best of Morocco on this 10-day private tour from Marrakech to Casablanca, combining the country's most famous cities, spectacular mountain landscapes and the golden dunes of the Sahara Desert.

Start with two nights in Marrakech, giving you enough time to experience the Red City's historic medina, colorful souks, palaces and gardens without rushing. Then cross the High Atlas Mountains to Aït Ben Haddou, Ouarzazate and the Dades Valley before continuing to the Sahara at Merzouga.

Enjoy a sunset camel trek, a private desert camp and a full day exploring the Erg Chebbi region. Continue north through the Middle Atlas Mountains to Fes, spend time discovering its ancient medina and then travel to the famous blue city of Chefchaouen. The final part of the journey takes you through Rabat to Casablanca, ending beside the Atlantic Ocean at the impressive Hassan II Mosque.

"More than a trip... It's a story to tell!"

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Arrival in Marrakech

Welcome to Marrakech, Morocco's famous Red City. Your private driver will meet you at Marrakech airport and transfer you to your hotel or traditional riad in the heart of the city.

Depending on your arrival time, enjoy a relaxed introduction to Marrakech. You can take your first walk through the historic medina, explore the nearby souks or visit the lively Jemaa el-Fna square.

The evening is free to enjoy traditional Moroccan food and experience the atmosphere of the old city. If you arrive early, you may also have time to visit the Koutoubia Mosque from outside or explore some of the surrounding streets.

Keeping the first day flexible allows you to settle in after your flight without a busy schedule.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 2

Full-Day Marrakech Guided Tour

After breakfast, meet your local licensed guide and spend a full day discovering Marrakech. Begin in the historic medina and explore some of the city's most important landmarks, including Bahia Palace, Koutoubia Mosque and the traditional souks.

Walk through the narrow streets of the old city and discover traditional Moroccan craftsmanship, including leather, carpets, pottery and handmade products.

Continue to the Mellah, the historic Jewish quarter, before visiting another of Marrakech's famous attractions. Depending on your interests, you can include the Majorelle area, gardens or additional historical sites.

Enjoy lunch at a traditional Moroccan restaurant before continuing your exploration.

In the late afternoon, return to your riad or enjoy free time in Jemaa el-Fna. Having two nights in Marrakech gives you the opportunity to experience the city properly before beginning the desert journey.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 3

Marrakech → High Atlas → Aït Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate → Dades Valley

After breakfast, leave Marrakech and travel south through the spectacular High Atlas Mountains. Cross the Tizi n'Tichka Pass, stopping at scenic viewpoints along the way.

Continue to Aït Ben Haddou, one of Morocco's most famous historic ksars. Explore the narrow streets and traditional earthen buildings before continuing to Ouarzazate.

After lunch, travel through the Valley of Roses and along the scenic Road of a Thousand Kasbahs towards the Dades Valley. Along the route, enjoy views of palm groves, Berber villages and dramatic mountain formations.

Arrive in the Dades Valley in the afternoon and visit the famous rock formations and winding road viewpoints before checking into your hotel or kasbah.

~360 km Dades Valley Breakfast & Dinner
Day 4

Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Erfoud → Rissani → Merzouga

After breakfast, continue towards the Sahara Desert. Drive through the Dades Valley and reach the impressive Todra Gorge, where towering canyon walls surround the valley.

Continue towards Erfoud, a town known for fossils and desert products, before reaching Rissani. This historic town was once an important caravan center and remains an important gateway to the Sahara.

From Rissani, continue towards Merzouga and the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi.

In the late afternoon, leave your vehicle and begin your camel trek into the desert. Ride through the dunes while watching the colors of the Sahara change as the sun sets.

Arrive at your private desert camp and enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner, Berber music and an evening beneath the stars.

~300 km Private Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & Dinner
Day 5

Full-Day Merzouga Sahara Desert Experience

Wake early to watch the Sahara sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes. After breakfast, begin a private 4×4 excursion to discover the different landscapes and communities around Merzouga.

Visit a local nomadic family and learn about their traditional way of life in the desert. Continue to Khamlia village, known for its Gnawa musical heritage, before exploring local fossil areas and the surrounding desert.

Depending on the season and weather conditions, you can also visit the seasonal lake near Merzouga. There will be time for sandboarding or simply enjoying the dunes.

After lunch, return to your hotel or riad in Merzouga and enjoy a relaxed afternoon.

Having a full day in the Sahara means you are not simply passing through the desert—you have time to experience its landscapes, culture and people.

~40 km Merzouga Breakfast & Dinner
Day 6

Merzouga → Ziz Valley → Midelt → Azrou → Ifrane → Fes

After breakfast, leave the Sahara and begin your journey north. Travel through the spectacular Ziz Valley, with its palm-filled oasis and dramatic cliffs.

Continue towards Midelt, known for its apple orchards and location between the High and Middle Atlas Mountains. After lunch, drive through the Middle Atlas towards Azrou and its famous cedar forests.

Depending on the season and conditions, you may see Barbary macaques around the cedar trees.

Continue to Ifrane, a mountain town known for its distinctive architecture and alpine atmosphere, before continuing towards Fes.

Arrive in Fes in the evening and check into your traditional riad or hotel. Relax after the journey and prepare for the following day's guided exploration of the ancient medina.

~470 km Fes Breakfast & Dinner
Day 7

Full-Day Fes Guided Tour

Today is dedicated to discovering Fes, Morocco's cultural and spiritual capital. After breakfast, meet your private local guide and begin exploring the historic Fes el-Bali medina.

Walk through the maze of narrow streets and discover traditional souks, artisan workshops, historic madrasas and beautiful riads. Visit the famous Chouara Tanneries and explore important areas of the ancient medina.

Your guide will explain the history, architecture and traditions that make Fes unique. Continue to the Mellah, the historic Jewish quarter, and visit a panoramic viewpoint overlooking the old city.

You will have time for a traditional Moroccan lunch and can explore local artisan shops selling leather, ceramics, carpets, textiles and other handmade products.

The afternoon can be adapted to your interests, with time to relax or continue exploring the medina.

Fes Breakfast
Day 8

Fes → Chefchaouen

After breakfast, leave Fes and travel north towards the Rif Mountains and the famous Blue City of Chefchaouen.

The scenery becomes increasingly mountainous as you approach Chefchaouen. After checking into your riad, begin exploring the old medina.

Walk through the blue-painted streets and discover small squares, traditional houses, artisan shops and local cafés. Visit the historic Kasbah in the main square and, if you wish, walk towards the Spanish Mosque viewpoint for panoramic views over Chefchaouen and the surrounding Rif Mountains.

The afternoon and evening are intentionally relaxed so you can enjoy the unique atmosphere of the town without rushing.

Chefchaouen is a completely different experience from Marrakech and Fes, offering a quieter mountain setting and a slower pace.

~200 km Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 9

Chefchaouen → Rabat → Casablanca

After breakfast, leave Chefchaouen and travel south towards Rabat, Morocco's capital city.

Upon arrival, explore the historic Kasbah of the Udayas, the Hassan Tower and the Mausoleum of Mohammed V. Enjoy lunch in Rabat before continuing towards Casablanca.

Arrive in Casablanca in the afternoon and check into your hotel. Depending on your schedule, enjoy some free time along the Corniche or in the city center.

Casablanca provides a modern contrast to the historic cities visited during the tour. It is Morocco's largest city and an important center of business, culture and Atlantic life.

Spend the evening relaxing before your final day in Morocco.

~340 km Casablanca Breakfast
Day 10

Casablanca → Hassan II Mosque → Departure

After breakfast, begin your final morning in Casablanca with a visit to the impressive Hassan II Mosque, dramatically located beside the Atlantic Ocean.

Depending on your departure time and the mosque's visiting schedule, you can visit the interior with a guided tour or admire the mosque from outside.

Continue through central Casablanca or enjoy a final walk along the Corniche before your private driver transfers you to Casablanca Mohammed V International Airport, your hotel or another location in the city.

Your 10-day private Morocco tour from Marrakech to Casablanca ends here, after experiencing Marrakech, the High Atlas, Aït Ben Haddou, Dades, Todra, Merzouga, Fes, Chefchaouen, Rabat and Casablanca.

End of tour: Casablanca Breakfast

FAQ

Is this a private tour?

Yes. This is a completely private tour with your own vehicle and private driver.

Does the tour include the Sahara Desert?

Yes. The itinerary includes Merzouga and Erg Chebbi, a sunset camel trek, a private desert camp and a full day exploring the Sahara region.

How many nights do we spend in the Sahara?

The standard itinerary includes one night in the desert camp and one additional night in Merzouga.

Can we spend two nights in the desert camp?

Yes, this can be arranged depending on availability and the type of Sahara experience you prefer.

Can we add Essaouira?

Yes. If you want to include Essaouira, we can modify the itinerary or extend the tour to 11 days.

Can we visit Volubilis and Meknes?

Yes. We can add Volubilis and Meknes, especially if you prefer to extend the tour by one day.

Can we end the tour in Marrakech instead?

Yes. The itinerary can be changed to start and end in Marrakech or to finish in Fes, Tangier or another Moroccan city.
