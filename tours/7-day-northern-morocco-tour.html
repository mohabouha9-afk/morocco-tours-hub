Tour Overview

Discover the beauty, history and culture of northern Morocco on this unforgettable 7-day private Northern Morocco tour. From the coastal charm of Tangier to the blue-painted streets of Chefchaouen, this journey introduces you to some of the country's most fascinating destinations.

Explore Tangier, where the Atlantic Ocean meets the Mediterranean Sea, before discovering the peaceful Rif Mountains and the enchanting Blue City of Chefchaouen. Continue to the ancient Roman ruins of Volubilis and the historic imperial cities of Meknes and Fes.

Along the way, enjoy authentic Moroccan cuisine, traditional architecture, colorful souks and beautiful mountain and countryside landscapes. With private transportation and a flexible itinerary, this tour allows you to discover northern Morocco comfortably while enjoying plenty of time in each destination.

"More than a trip... It's a story to tell!"

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Tangier: Arrival & City Tour

Your 7-day Northern Morocco tour begins with a private pick-up from Tangier Ibn Battouta Airport, ferry port, train station or your hotel. Meet your private driver and begin discovering this fascinating northern city.

Explore the historic Kasbah and wander through Tangier's old medina, where narrow streets, traditional houses, small shops and local cafés create an authentic Moroccan atmosphere. Continue to the Grand Socco and Petit Socco before discovering some of the city's historic neighborhoods.

In the afternoon, travel towards Cap Spartel, one of Tangier's most spectacular viewpoints, where the Atlantic Ocean and Mediterranean Sea meet. You can also visit the nearby Hercules Caves and enjoy views over the coastline.

Return to Tangier in the late afternoon and enjoy free time to relax, explore the city or enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner.

~60 km Tangier Breakfast
Day 2

Tangier → Asilah → Larache → Tangier

After breakfast, enjoy a day exploring Morocco's beautiful Atlantic coast. Travel south from Tangier to Asilah, a charming seaside town famous for its whitewashed buildings, artistic atmosphere and historic medina.

Walk through the peaceful old town, discover colorful murals and explore the impressive Portuguese walls overlooking the Atlantic Ocean. Enjoy some free time for lunch and relaxation beside the sea.

Continue towards Larache, another coastal town with a rich history and relaxed atmosphere. Explore its central square, traditional streets and coastal viewpoints before returning towards Tangier.

Depending on your interests, you can spend the afternoon relaxing at your hotel or enjoying more free time in Tangier's medina and waterfront.

This day offers a slower pace and a chance to experience northern Morocco beyond its major tourist destinations, combining coastal scenery, local culture and charming historic towns.

~180 km Tangier Breakfast
Day 3

Tangier → Chefchaouen

After breakfast, depart Tangier and travel through the beautiful Rif Mountains towards Chefchaouen. The journey offers spectacular views of green valleys, mountain villages and rolling hills.

Arrive in Chefchaouen, famously known as Morocco's Blue Pearl. After checking into your traditional riad, begin exploring the enchanting old medina.

Wander through narrow blue-painted streets and discover colorful doors, traditional houses, artisan workshops and small local shops. Visit Plaza Uta el-Hammam, the lively heart of the medina, and enjoy a traditional Moroccan lunch at one of the nearby restaurants.

In the late afternoon, walk towards the Spanish Mosque for breathtaking panoramic views across Chefchaouen and the surrounding mountains. Sunset is particularly beautiful from this viewpoint.

Return to your riad for a relaxing evening and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of this unique mountain town.

~120 km Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 4

Chefchaouen → Akchour → Chefchaouen

After breakfast, enjoy a day discovering the natural beauty surrounding Chefchaouen. Travel into the Rif Mountains towards Akchour, a spectacular area known for its waterfalls, rivers, rock formations and scenic walking trails.

Depending on your preferences and fitness level, you can enjoy a nature walk through the valley, follow part of the hiking trails or simply relax beside the river and appreciate the peaceful mountain scenery.

The area offers excellent opportunities for photography and is a wonderful contrast to the historic cities visited during the tour.

After spending time in nature, return to Chefchaouen in the afternoon. Enjoy free time to explore more of the Blue City, revisit your favorite streets, browse local handicrafts or relax at a traditional café in Plaza Uta el-Hammam.

Spend another peaceful evening in Chefchaouen and enjoy dinner at a local restaurant.

~70 km Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 5

Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Moulay Idriss → Meknes → Fes

After breakfast, leave Chefchaouen and continue towards the historical heart of Morocco. Your first stop is Volubilis, one of the country's most important archaeological sites and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Explore the ancient Roman ruins and admire the impressive columns, ancient streets and beautifully preserved mosaics. Continue to Moulay Idriss, a picturesque hilltop town and an important spiritual destination in Morocco.

Enjoy a short walk through its atmospheric streets and admire panoramic views over the surrounding countryside.

Continue to Meknes, one of Morocco's four imperial cities. Explore the historic medina and admire the magnificent Bab el-Mansour gate. Discover the city's royal heritage and enjoy free time for lunch.

In the afternoon, continue to Fes. Arrive in the evening and check into your traditional riad, where you can relax after a fascinating day of history and culture.

~240 km Fes Breakfast
Day 6

Fes: Full-Day Guided City Tour

Today is dedicated to discovering Fes, Morocco's cultural and spiritual capital. After breakfast, meet your local licensed guide and begin exploring Fes el-Bali, the historic UNESCO-listed medina.

Walk through its maze of narrow streets and colorful souks while discovering traditional artisan workshops, historic buildings and beautiful architectural details.

Visit important landmarks including the Bou Inania Madrasa, Nejjarine Fountain, the Al Quaraouiyine area and the famous Chouara Tanneries. Learn about the city's long history and its importance as a center of education, craftsmanship and Moroccan culture.

After lunch, continue towards the Mellah, the historic Jewish quarter, before visiting one of the panoramic viewpoints overlooking the ancient medina.

Return to your riad in the evening with free time to relax or enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner.

Fes Breakfast
Day 7

Fes: Departure

After breakfast, enjoy a relaxed final morning in Fes. Depending on your departure time, you can have some free time to explore the surrounding area, visit additional shops in the medina or purchase traditional Moroccan souvenirs.

Fes is particularly famous for its craftsmanship, including leather goods, ceramics, textiles, carpets and traditional metalwork, making it an excellent place to find authentic souvenirs.

At the agreed time, your private driver will transfer you to Fes-Saïss Airport, the train station or another departure point according to your travel arrangements.

Your 7-day Northern Morocco tour comes to an end after an unforgettable journey through Tangier, Asilah, Larache, Chefchaouen, the Rif Mountains, Volubilis, Meknes and Fes.

End of tour: Fes Breakfast
