Tour Overview

Discover the very best of Morocco on this 12-Day Grand Morocco Tour from Tangier to Marrakech, an exceptional journey combining Morocco's northern cities, imperial heritage, spectacular landscapes and the legendary Sahara Desert. Starting in Tangier, you will explore the blue-painted streets of Chefchaouen, the Roman ruins of Volubilis, the imperial city of Meknes and the historic medina of Fes.

The journey then continues south through the Middle Atlas Mountains, Ifrane, Azrou and the dramatic Ziz Valley before reaching Merzouga and the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi. Enjoy a camel trek, Sahara sunset and a night in a desert camp before continuing through Todra Gorge, Dades Valley, Ouarzazate and Aït Ben Haddou.

Your Moroccan adventure ends in Marrakech, where you will discover colorful souks, historic palaces, beautiful gardens and the famous Jemaa el-Fna square.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Tangier: Arrival & City Tour

Welcome to Morocco! Your 12-day Morocco tour begins in Tangier, the fascinating northern gateway where the Mediterranean Sea meets the Atlantic Ocean. After meeting your private driver at the airport, port or hotel, begin exploring this historic city. Tangier has long attracted travelers, artists and writers because of its unique international character and strategic location.

Visit the atmospheric Kasbah, wander through the old medina and explore the Grand Socco, one of the city's lively gathering places. Continue toward the coast to admire spectacular views across the Strait of Gibraltar. You can also visit Cap Spartel, where the Atlantic and Mediterranean are traditionally considered to meet, followed by the nearby Hercules Caves.

After sightseeing, transfer to your hotel or riad and enjoy your first evening in Morocco. Take time to relax, explore the surrounding streets or enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner.

~60 km Tangier Breakfast
Day 2

Tangier → Chefchaouen

After breakfast, leave Tangier and travel toward the spectacular Rif Mountains to Chefchaouen, one of Morocco's most beautiful and photographed destinations. Known as the "Blue Pearl of Morocco," Chefchaouen is famous for its blue-painted houses, narrow streets, flower-filled corners and peaceful mountain atmosphere.

Upon arrival, check into your traditional riad before exploring the medina on foot. Wander through its charming lanes and discover small artisan shops selling handmade textiles, leather goods, pottery and local crafts. Continue toward Plaza Uta el-Hammam, the heart of the old town, where you can relax at a café and watch everyday Moroccan life unfold.

For wonderful panoramic views, walk toward the Spanish Mosque area around sunset. The surrounding Rif Mountains provide a beautiful backdrop as the blue city changes color in the evening light. Enjoy a relaxed dinner and your first night in Chefchaouen.

~120 km Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 3

Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Moulay Idriss → Meknes → Fes

Today, your Grand Morocco tour continues toward the imperial cities of northern Morocco. Leave Chefchaouen after breakfast and drive through beautiful countryside toward the ancient Roman ruins of Volubilis. Explore the archaeological site, where you can admire remarkably preserved mosaics, columns, temples and ancient streets.

Continue to the holy town of Moulay Idriss, built dramatically across two hills and named after Morocco's first Islamic ruler. Enjoy a short visit and admire the town's unique architecture and surrounding landscapes.

Continue to Meknes, one of Morocco's four imperial cities. Discover highlights including Bab Mansour, the monumental gateway, the historic medina and the former royal granaries and stables.

In the afternoon, continue to Fes, Morocco's spiritual and cultural capital. After checking into your riad, enjoy a relaxing evening and perhaps explore the area around your accommodation.

~240 km Fes Breakfast
Day 4

Fes: Guided Sightseeing Tour

Today is dedicated entirely to discovering Fes, one of the most fascinating cities in Morocco. After breakfast, meet your local licensed guide and begin a walking tour through the historic medina of Fes el-Bali, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the largest pedestrian areas in the world.

Explore the maze of narrow streets filled with craftsmen, traditional shops, colorful markets and historic buildings. Visit important landmarks such as the Bou Inania Madrasa, Al Quaraouiyine area, Nejjarine Fountain and the famous traditional leather tanneries.

Your guide will help you understand the history, architecture and traditions behind the city rather than simply showing you the sights. You will also have time to discover local handicrafts, including leather, ceramics, textiles and traditional Moroccan products.

In the afternoon, visit the newer part of Fes and enjoy panoramic views over the ancient medina. The evening is free for you to relax at your riad or enjoy dinner in the historic city.

Fes Breakfast
Day 5

Fes → Ifrane → Azrou → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

Today begins your journey toward the Sahara Desert. Leave Fes after breakfast and travel through the scenic Middle Atlas Mountains. Your first stop is Ifrane, often called the "Switzerland of Morocco" because of its European-style architecture, clean streets and alpine atmosphere.

Continue through the cedar forests near Azrou, where you may see Barbary macaques among the trees. The road then climbs and descends through spectacular mountain landscapes before reaching Midelt, known for its apple production and dramatic surroundings.

Continue toward the Sahara through the Ziz Valley, where winding roads reveal impressive views of palm-filled oases and rocky cliffs. As you approach Merzouga, the landscape gradually changes from mountains and valleys to the vast golden dunes of Erg Chebbi.

In the late afternoon, begin your camel trek across the dunes. Reach your desert camp in time to watch the sun set over the Sahara.

~470 km Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & dinner
Day 6

Merzouga: Sahara Desert Experience

Wake early to experience one of the highlights of your 12-day Morocco tour: sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes. Watch the first rays of sunlight illuminate the desert before returning to camp for breakfast.

Afterward, return to Merzouga and explore the surrounding desert region. You can visit a traditional village and learn more about the local Saharan communities and their music, traditions and way of life. Depending on your interests, you may also visit seasonal nomadic settlements or enjoy a peaceful walk through the nearby desert landscapes.

The afternoon is free to relax at your accommodation or participate in optional desert activities such as 4×4 dune exploration, quad biking or sandboarding.

As evening approaches, enjoy another opportunity to admire the vast Sahara landscape and its changing colors. The clear desert sky is particularly beautiful after sunset, offering excellent conditions for stargazing.

~40 km Merzouga Breakfast & dinner
Day 7

Merzouga → Rissani → Erfoud → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley

After breakfast, leave Merzouga and continue through the fascinating landscapes of southeastern Morocco. Your first stop is Rissani, an important historic trading town and gateway to the Sahara. If the local market is operating, you can explore its lively souks and discover traditional products from the surrounding region.

Continue to Erfoud, famous for its fossils and marble workshops. The road then leads toward the spectacular Todra Valley. Stop at Todra Gorge, where enormous limestone cliffs rise dramatically on both sides of the narrow valley. Take a walk through the gorge and enjoy the impressive natural scenery.

Continue through the palm-filled oasis and Berber villages toward the Dades Valley. The landscape changes constantly, with dramatic rock formations, agricultural terraces and winding mountain roads.

Arrive at your hotel or riad in the Dades Valley in the evening and enjoy a relaxing dinner surrounded by the peaceful atmosphere of southern Morocco.

~300 km Dades Valley Breakfast
Day 8

Dades Valley → Valley of Roses → Skoura → Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → Marrakech

After breakfast, continue west through some of Morocco's most scenic landscapes. Drive through the Valley of Roses, famous for its rose cultivation and traditional rose-based products. In season, the area is particularly colorful and fragrant.

Continue toward Skoura, a beautiful oasis filled with palm groves and historic kasbahs. The journey then takes you to Ouarzazate, often called the gateway to the Sahara and southern Morocco. You can visit the Taourirt Kasbah and learn about the region's history and cinematic importance.

Continue to Aït Ben Haddou, Morocco's most famous fortified ksar and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Walk through its narrow lanes and climb toward the upper section for beautiful views across the surrounding landscape. The site has also appeared in numerous international film and television productions.

After exploring Aït Ben Haddou, continue across the High Atlas Mountains toward Marrakech and check into your riad for the evening.

~360 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 9

Marrakech: Guided City Tour

Today, discover the vibrant Red City of Marrakech with a local licensed guide. Begin with the historic medina and explore some of the city's most important monuments and cultural sites.

Visit the impressive Koutoubia Mosque, the Saadian Tombs and the beautiful Bahia Palace, known for its intricate decoration, courtyards and traditional Moroccan architecture. Continue through the colorful souks, where artisans create and sell leather goods, lamps, carpets, spices, ceramics and other Moroccan handicrafts.

You will also have time to explore the narrow streets of the medina and discover hidden courtyards and traditional shops. Later, visit the famous Jemaa el-Fna square, the lively heart of Marrakech.

The afternoon can be customized according to your interests. You may choose to visit the Majorelle Garden, relax at your riad or enjoy traditional Moroccan cuisine.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 10

Marrakech → Essaouira → Marrakech

Today, enjoy a relaxing excursion from Marrakech to the Atlantic coast and the charming seaside town of Essaouira. The journey takes you through the Moroccan countryside, with opportunities to see the famous argan trees and, depending on the season, local goats climbing their branches.

Upon arrival in Essaouira, explore the historic medina, protected by impressive stone walls and known for its relaxed atmosphere. Walk through the colorful fishing harbor, see traditional blue fishing boats and explore the lively market.

Continue through the medina to discover artisan workshops specializing in wood carving, textiles, jewelry and local crafts. Enjoy fresh seafood or other Moroccan dishes at one of the town's restaurants before taking a peaceful walk along the beach.

Essaouira offers a wonderful contrast to the busy atmosphere of Marrakech, making it an excellent addition to your Morocco private tour. In the late afternoon, return to Marrakech and enjoy a free evening.

~380 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 11

Marrakech: Free Day to Explore

Enjoy a flexible final day in Marrakech. After breakfast, the day is yours to experience the city at your own pace. You can return to the medina to shop for Moroccan souvenirs, visit places you may have missed during your guided tour or simply relax at your riad.

For travelers interested in Moroccan culture, this is an excellent opportunity to take a traditional cooking class, visit local artisan workshops or enjoy a Moroccan hammam experience. Alternatively, you can spend time in the souks searching for carpets, spices, leather products, lanterns and handmade crafts.

If you prefer a more peaceful day, relax beside your hotel or riad pool and enjoy the atmosphere of Marrakech.

In the evening, return to Jemaa el-Fna for one final experience of the city's lively atmosphere. Enjoy a traditional dinner and reflect on the incredible journey from northern Morocco and Chefchaouen through Fes and the Sahara Desert to Marrakech.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 12

Marrakech: Departure

Your 12-Day Grand Morocco Tour via Tangier & Chefchaouen comes to an end today. Depending on your flight schedule, enjoy your final breakfast at your hotel or riad and take some time for a final walk through Marrakech.

Your private driver will meet you at your accommodation and transfer you comfortably to Marrakech Menara Airport or your preferred departure point.

If your departure is later in the day, the morning can be customized according to your schedule. You may have additional time for shopping, relaxing at your accommodation or enjoying one last Moroccan coffee in the medina.

From the Mediterranean atmosphere of Tangier and the blue streets of Chefchaouen to the imperial heritage of Fes, the golden dunes of Merzouga and the colorful streets of Marrakech, this journey offers an extensive introduction to Morocco's landscapes, history and culture. We say goodbye with wonderful memories of Morocco and hope to welcome you again for another Moroccan adventure.

End of tour: Marrakech Breakfast

Why Choose This Tour?

This 12-Day Grand Morocco Tour from Tangier to Marrakech is designed for travelers who want to experience Morocco beyond the main cities. Instead of rushing from one destination to another, you will have time to discover northern Morocco, imperial cities, mountain landscapes, the Sahara Desert and southern Morocco.

Because this is a private Morocco tour, your journey can be adapted to your interests, pace and travel style. You can stop for photographs, spend more time in places you love and enjoy a more personal experience with your private driver.

From Chefchaouen's blue medina to the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi and the colorful souks of Marrakech, this itinerary brings together some of the most iconic experiences in Morocco in one carefully designed journey.
