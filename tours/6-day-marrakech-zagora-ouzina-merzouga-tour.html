Overview

Six days from Marrakech into the deep south, in your own 4×4 with your own driver-guide. You follow the palm groves of the Draa Valley to Zagora, then leave the road behind: the old Paris–Dakar rally tracks take you through the oases of Sidi Ali and Ramlia to the ochre dunes of Ouzina, where you spend a quiet night far from any crowd.

From Ouzina it is a short way to Merzouga: a full day around the tall dunes of Erg Chebbi, a camel ride at sunset and a night in the camp. You come back through the Todra Gorge, the Dades Valley and Aït Ben Haddou. Because the tour is private, you stop when you want, eat where you like and never stop at a shop you didn’t ask for.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Marrakech → Tizi n’Tichka → Ouarzazate → Draa Valley → Zagora

    Pick-up at 07:00 at your hotel, or at the nearest gate if your riad is in the medina. Over the High Atlas by the Tizi n’Tichka pass (2,260 m) to Ouarzazate for lunch, then south to Agdz and along the Draa Valley, a long ribbon of date palms and earthen kasbahs, to Zagora for dinner.

    • About 360 km, about 7 hours on the road
    • Night: Zagora
    • Dinner & breakfast
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Zagora → Paris–Dakar track → Sidi Ali → Ramlia → Ouzina

    Today the road ends. Your 4×4 heads east on the old Paris–Dakar rally tracks, across stony plains and dry riverbeds and past the clay pan of Lake Maider, to the oasis of Sidi Ali. Lunch at a simple stop on the way, then through the palm oasis of Ramlia to Ouzina, where ochre dunes rise behind the village. Sunset from the dunes, dinner and a sky full of stars.

    • About 170 km, mostly desert track, 6 to 7 hours
    • Night: Ouzina
    • Breakfast & dinner
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Ouzina → Taouz → Khamlia → Merzouga

    A morning walk on the dunes of Ouzina, then north on desert tracks to Taouz, known for its rock carvings, and Khamlia, the village of the Gnawa musicians. You reach Merzouga by early afternoon, with time to rest at your hotel or climb the first dunes of Erg Chebbi.

    • About 60 km, desert track then a quiet road, about 3 hours
    • Night: Merzouga hotel
    • Breakfast & dinner
  4. 4
    Day 4

    A full day around Merzouga

    Sunrise over Erg Chebbi, then a day around Merzouga by 4×4 at your pace: tea with a nomad family, the lakes of the old mining pits and, after a wet winter, the seasonal lake. In the late afternoon a camel takes you into the dunes for sunset; dinner in the camp and music around the fire.

    • 4×4 day around Merzouga
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Breakfast & dinner
  5. 5
    Day 5

    Merzouga → Rissani → Erfoud → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley

    Sunrise and the sandboards before breakfast. Then Rissani, the old caravan town and its market, the fossil workshops of Erfoud and a walk on the flat riverbed of the Todra Gorge, between rock walls about 300 m high. On through the Tinghir palm grove to the Dades Valley for dinner.

    • About 280 km, about 6 hours on the road
    • Night: Dades Valley
    • Dinner & breakfast
  6. 6
    Day 6

    Dades Valley → Valley of Roses → Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → Marrakech

    Along the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs and the Valley of Roses to Ouarzazate, then Aït Ben Haddou: your driver-guide walks you up through the ksar to the old granary at the top. Lunch nearby, then back over the High Atlas to your hotel in Marrakech in the early evening.

    • About 320 km, about 7 hours on the road
    • In Marrakech in the early evening
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

How rough is the off-road day?

Day 2 is about 170 km, mostly on desert tracks: stony plains, dry riverbeds and some sand, 6 to 7 hours with stops. It is bumpy in places but safe in a 4×4 with a driver who knows the tracks. There is no phone signal for long stretches, so your driver carries water and plans each stop.

Why a night in Ouzina, not straight to Merzouga?

The whole crossing from Zagora to Merzouga is about 230 km of track, too much for one comfortable day; desert specialists split it the same way. Ouzina gives you quiet ochre dunes and a starry night before the busier Erg Chebbi.

How are the dunes of Ouzina different from Merzouga?

The dunes of Ouzina are smaller and ochre-red, with only a few guesthouses around them. Erg Chebbi at Merzouga has the tallest dunes in Morocco, up to about 150 m, and many camps. On this tour you get both: the quiet one first, then the classic giant dunes.

Is it safe so close to the Algerian border?

Yes. Ouzina and Taouz are quiet villages, and visitors travel here normally with a local driver. You pass a few police checkpoints on the way, so keep your passport within reach.

When is the best time for this tour?

Spring (March to May) and autumn (late September to November) are best: mild days and clear skies for the crossing. Summer is very hot in the deep south, and winter nights in Ouzina and the camp drop close to freezing. After heavy rain, your driver may choose another track.
