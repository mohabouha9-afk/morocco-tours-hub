Overview

Drive a buggy over the stony Agafay hills with your partner, a friend or your child beside you, following your guide on the desert tracks to a Berber village and the viewpoints over the High Atlas, 40 minutes from Marrakech.

Choose one hour on the tracks, or two hours with mint tea in a Berber village and the view over the Lalla Takerkoust lake on clear days. Roll bars, seat belts and helmets keep everyone safe, and family buggies take up to four. Your buggy is yours alone, never shared with strangers.

Programme

  1. 1
    09:00 or 15:00

    Pick-up in Marrakech

    Your driver meets you at your hotel, or at the nearest gate if your riad is in the medina. We confirm the time on WhatsApp the day before.

  2. 2
    40 minutes later

    Helmets and briefing

    At the buggy base: helmets, goggles and a scarf for the dust, seat belts done, then a short briefing and a few slow turns until the driver feels at ease.

  3. 3
    On the tracks

    Over the Agafay hills

    Your guide drives in front on the desert tracks, at a pace that suits you, with stops for photos and the view of the Atlas.

  4. 4
    2-hour ride

    Mint tea in a Berber village

    On the longer ride, mint tea with a family in a Berber village, then a viewpoint over the Lalla Takerkoust lake on clear days.

  5. 5
    After the drive

    Back to Marrakech

    Wash off the dust, then your driver takes you back to your hotel, about 3 hours after pick-up for the 1-hour ride, 4 hours for the 2-hour ride.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can drive the buggy?

Drivers need to be 18 or older with a valid driving licence; bring it with you. No off-road experience is needed: the briefing comes first and your guide sets the pace.

How much time do we really drive?

The full hour or two hours you book, on the tracks. The pick-up, drive and briefing come on top, so plan about 3 hours door to door for the 1-hour ride and 4 hours for the 2-hour ride.

Buggy or quad: which should we choose?

A buggy has seats, seat belts and a roll bar, and you ride side by side, so it suits couples and families. A quad gives everyone their own machine and feels more like riding a bike.

Can children come?

Yes, from 6 years old as passengers, with a seat belt and a helmet. With two children, a 4-seat family buggy keeps everyone together.

Is it dusty?

Yes, the tracks are dry most of the year. We give you goggles and a scarf; wear clothes you don’t mind getting dusty and keep your camera in a bag.

How do I book and pay?

  1. Send your request with the form or on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay a small deposit to confirm; the rest is paid on the day, in cash or by card.
