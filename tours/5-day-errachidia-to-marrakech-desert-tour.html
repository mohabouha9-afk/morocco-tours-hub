Overview

Land in Errachidia and two hours later you are at the Merzouga dunes. Two nights by Erg Chebbi, one in a hotel and one in a camp, give you a full day in the desert: a nomad family, the Gnawa music of Khamlia and a camel ride into the dunes for sunset.

Then the road to Marrakech, taken slowly over three days: a night at the Todra Gorge, the bends of the Dades Gorge, the kasbahs of Skoura and a night beside the ksar of Aït Ben Haddou, so you see the busiest sights in their quiet hours. Your own car and driver-guide all the way, every flight met at any hour, and no shop stops you didn’t ask for.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Errachidia airport → Ziz Valley → Erfoud → Merzouga

    Your driver-guide waits in arrivals with your name on a sign, at whatever hour you land; we follow your flight, so a delay changes nothing. The road runs south past the palm groves of the Ziz Valley and through Erfoud, the fossil town, and the first dunes of Erg Chebbi appear two hours later. Dinner and a good night’s sleep in a hotel at the foot of the dunes.

    • About 125 km, about 2 hours on the road
    • Night: Merzouga hotel
    • Dinner & breakfast
  2. 2
    Day 2

    A day around Merzouga, sunset camel ride and the camp

    A whole day in the desert by 4×4, at your pace: mint tea with a nomad family in their tent, the Gnawa musicians of Khamlia, the black stony plains and, after a wet winter, the lake of Dayet Srij with its birds. In the late afternoon your big bags stay in the car and you ride a camel into the dunes for sunset. Dinner in the camp, drums by the fire and the stars.

    • 4×4 day around Merzouga
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Breakfast & dinner
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Merzouga → Rissani → Alnif → Tinghir → Todra Gorge

    Sunrise and sandboarding on the dunes, breakfast, and back by camel or 4×4. West through Rissani, the old caravan town with its big market, and along the Jebel Saghro to Alnif, where trilobite fossils come out of the hills, and the long palm grove of Tinghir. You sleep at the Todra Gorge and walk between its 300 m walls in the late afternoon, when the day visitors have left.

    • About 220 km, about 4.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Todra Gorge
    • Breakfast & dinner
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Todra Gorge → Dades Gorge → Valley of Roses → Skoura → Aït Ben Haddou

    Up the Dades Gorge past the Monkey Fingers rocks to the hairpin bends of Tissadrine, then down the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs: the Valley of Roses at Kelaat M’Gouna and the Skoura palm grove, where Kasbah Amridil, the kasbah once printed on the 50-dirham note, is open to visit. Past Ouarzazate to Aït Ben Haddou, where you sleep beside the ksar and see it turn red at sunset.

    • About 275 km, about 5.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Aït Ben Haddou
    • Breakfast & dinner
  5. 5
    Day 5

    Aït Ben Haddou → Tizi n’Tichka → Marrakech

    An early walk up through the ksar to the old granary, before the day groups from Marrakech arrive. Then over the High Atlas by the Tizi n’Tichka pass, 2,260 m, with stops at the viewpoints and the Berber villages, and down to Marrakech in the early afternoon. We drop you at your hotel or riad, or at Marrakech airport for an evening flight.

    • About 175 km, about 3.5 hours on the road
    • Drop-off at your Marrakech hotel or the airport
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

How much driving is there each day?

About 2 hours on day 1 and a 4×4 day around Merzouga on day 2, then about 4.5, 5.5 and 3.5 hours on days 3 to 5, with stops. The 560 km from the desert to Marrakech is split over three days.

Can we end the tour in Fes instead of Marrakech?

Yes. From Merzouga the road can go north instead, through the Ziz Valley, Midelt and the cedar forests of the Middle Atlas, to Fes. We rework days 3 to 5 so you keep your time in the dunes.

Can we go on to Chefchaouen, Tangier, Rabat or Casablanca?

Yes, with the same car and driver-guide. From Fes the trip can go on to Chefchaouen and Tangier; from Marrakech to Casablanca and Rabat on the coast. Tell us how many days you have and we plan it.

Why sleep at Aït Ben Haddou and not in Ouarzazate?

The day groups from Marrakech reach the ksar around midday. Sleeping next to it, you see it at sunset and walk it early in the morning, when the lanes are quiet. If you prefer Ouarzazate, 30 minutes away, we can change it.

Can you drop us at Marrakech airport on the last day?

Yes. We leave Aït Ben Haddou after breakfast and reach Marrakech in the early afternoon, so an evening flight works well. For an earlier flight we leave at sunrise; send us your flight time when you book.
