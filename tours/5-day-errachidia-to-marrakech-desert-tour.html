Tour Overview

Discover the landscapes of southern Morocco on this 5-Day Merzouga Desert Tour from Errachidia to Marrakech. This private journey combines the Sahara Desert with the Ziz Valley, Erfoud, Rissani, Erg Chebbi, Todra Gorge, Dades Valley, Ouarzazate, Aït Ben Haddou and the High Atlas Mountains.

Starting in Errachidia, you travel south toward the golden dunes of Merzouga and Erg Chebbi, one of Morocco's main gateways to the Sahara. The route then continues west through the spectacular valleys and kasbahs of southeastern Morocco before crossing the High Atlas to Marrakech.

With five days, you can enjoy the desert at a relaxed pace instead of treating the journey as a simple transfer. You will have time for a camel trek, Sahara sunset and sunrise, a desert 4×4 excursion, local villages, the Todra Gorge, Dades Valley, Aït Ben Haddou and the scenic road to Marrakech.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Errachidia → Ziz Valley → Erfoud → Rissani → Merzouga

Your 5-day Morocco desert tour from Errachidia begins with a private pickup from Errachidia Airport or your hotel. Travel through the spectacular Ziz Valley, where the mountains open to reveal an extensive oasis filled with palm trees. Stop at a panoramic viewpoint to enjoy the scenery and take photographs.

Continue to Erfoud, known for its fossils and traditional stone workshops. You can visit a local workshop to learn how fossilized materials are transformed into decorative products.

Continue toward Rissani, an important historic town in the Tafilalet region. Depending on the day, you may have time to explore its traditional souk and enjoy lunch before continuing toward Merzouga.

In the afternoon, the landscape gradually changes as the first golden dunes of Erg Chebbi appear. Begin your camel trek into the Sahara Desert, riding across the dunes while watching the sunset.

Arrive at your private desert camp for Moroccan tea, dinner, traditional music and a night beneath the stars.

~130 km Private Sahara Desert Camp Dinner
Day 2

Merzouga: Full-Day Sahara Desert Exploration

Wake early to experience a beautiful Sahara sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes. After breakfast at the camp, return to Merzouga by camel or 4×4 and begin a full-day exploration of the desert region.

Your private 4×4 excursion takes you to some of the most interesting places around Erg Chebbi. Visit the Hassi Labied oasis and, depending on seasonal conditions, Lake Dayet Srij, a natural area that can attract migratory birds.

Continue to Khamlia village, famous for its Gnawa music and cultural traditions. You can also visit a local nomadic family and share tea while learning about traditional desert life.

After lunch, continue through the desert landscapes and rocky plateaus surrounding the dunes. There is also time for optional activities such as quad biking and sandboarding.

Return to your Merzouga accommodation in the afternoon. Enjoy a relaxed evening and another opportunity to admire the changing colors of the dunes.

~40 km Merzouga Desert Hotel/Riad Breakfast & Dinner
Day 3

Merzouga → Rissani → Erfoud → Todra Gorge → Tinghir

After breakfast, leave Merzouga and travel north through Rissani and Erfoud before continuing toward the dramatic landscapes of the Todra Valley.

Along the way, you can stop for photographs and discover the changing scenery from desert plains to mountain landscapes. Continue toward Tinghir, a beautiful oasis town surrounded by palm groves and dramatic cliffs.

The highlight of the afternoon is Todra Gorge, one of southern Morocco's most impressive natural landscapes. The gorge is surrounded by towering limestone walls and offers a wonderful opportunity for a short walk and photography.

After exploring the gorge, continue to your accommodation in the Tinghir or Todra Gorge area.

The evening is free to relax at your hotel and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of the oasis region.

~300 km Tinghir / Todra Gorge Breakfast & Dinner
Day 4

Tinghir → Dades Valley → Rose Valley → Skoura → Ouarzazate

After breakfast, continue through the spectacular Dades Valley, one of the most scenic areas of southern Morocco. The route passes through Berber villages, palm groves, kasbahs and dramatic red-rock landscapes.

Stop at some of the famous viewpoints overlooking the Dades Valley and continue along the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs, where traditional earthen architecture can be seen throughout the region.

Continue toward the Valley of the Roses, known for its rose cultivation and local products. Depending on the season, you may see rose fields and visit a small cooperative.

The journey then continues toward Skoura, a beautiful palm oasis known for its historic kasbahs. If time permits, visit the famous Kasbah Amridil before continuing to Ouarzazate.

Ouarzazate is often known as the gateway to the Sahara and is an important stop on southern Morocco's tourism route.

~170 km Ouarzazate Breakfast & Dinner
Day 5

Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → High Atlas Mountains → Marrakech

After breakfast, travel toward Aït Ben Haddou, one of Morocco's most famous fortified villages. Explore the historic ksar and walk through its narrow lanes while learning about the traditional architecture of southern Morocco.

Aït Ben Haddou is also internationally recognized as a filming location and is one of the most iconic cultural sites along the route between Ouarzazate and Marrakech.

Continue toward the High Atlas Mountains, crossing the spectacular Tizi n'Tichka Pass. Along the way, stop at scenic viewpoints to photograph the mountain landscapes and Berber villages.

The road then descends toward the Marrakech plain. Depending on traffic and stops, you will arrive in Marrakech in the afternoon or early evening.

Your private driver will drop you at your hotel, riad or another agreed location in Marrakech.

~200 km End of tour: Marrakech Breakfast

Why Choose This Tour?

This itinerary is ideal for travelers who want to combine the Sahara Desert with the best landscapes of southern Morocco without rushing from one destination to another.

The five-day format gives you enough time to enjoy two different sides of the journey: the desert experience around Merzouga and the spectacular route west through Todra Gorge, Dades Valley, Ouarzazate and Aït Ben Haddou.

It is also a practical route because you start near the Sahara in Errachidia and finish in Marrakech, avoiding the need to return to your starting point.

Most importantly, this is a private and customizable tour, so the daily schedule can be adjusted around your interests, accommodation preferences and flight or hotel arrangements.

FAQ

Is 5 days enough for an Errachidia to Marrakech desert tour?

Yes. Five days is an excellent length for this route because it allows you to enjoy Merzouga and the Sahara before continuing through Todra Gorge, Dades Valley, Ouarzazate and Aït Ben Haddou to Marrakech.

Where do we sleep during the tour?

You spend one night in a private Sahara Desert Camp, one night in the Merzouga area, one night in Todra/Tinghir and one night in Ouarzazate.

Is the camel trek included?

Yes. The itinerary includes a camel trek through the Erg Chebbi dunes, normally timed around sunset.

Can we spend two nights in the desert?

Yes. If you prefer a deeper Sahara experience, we can customize the itinerary to include two nights at a desert camp or combine one camp night with a second night at a desert hotel.

Can we do quad biking in Merzouga?

Yes. Quad biking can be added as an optional activity in the Merzouga region.

Is Aït Ben Haddou included?

Yes. Aït Ben Haddou is included on Day 5 before crossing the High Atlas Mountains toward Marrakech.

Can families take this tour?

Yes. The five-day format is suitable for families because the route is divided into manageable sections and includes time to relax in Merzouga and the southern valleys.

Is this a private tour?

Yes. This is a private Morocco desert tour, meaning the vehicle, driver and itinerary are exclusively for your group.

Can we change the accommodation category?

Yes. We can arrange Standard, Mid-Range or Luxury accommodation depending on your budget and preferences.

Can the tour start from Errachidia Airport?

Yes. We can pick you up directly from Errachidia Airport (ERH) or from your hotel in Errachidia.

Can we add Marrakech sightseeing?

Yes. If you have additional time in Marrakech, we can extend the trip with a guided city tour or additional nights.
