Overview

Ride a horse through the palm grove on the edge of Marrakech, on soft sandy tracks between the palms and small earth villages, in the warm light before sunset or the cool calm of the morning.

No riding experience needed: a calm horse matched to you, a helmet and a short lesson first, and a full hour in the saddle at your own pace, 15 to 20 minutes from your hotel.

Programme

  1. 1
    Pick-up

    Pick-up at your hotel

    About 2 hours before sunset (around 15:30 in winter, 17:30 in summer) or at 09:00 for the morning ride. We confirm your time on WhatsApp the day before; in the medina we meet you at the nearest gate.

  2. 2
    15–20 min

    Out to the stables

    A short drive north of the medina to the stables in the palm grove, straight there with no shop on the way.

  3. 3
    Before the ride

    Meet your horse

    We match each rider to a horse by level and size, fit a helmet, and the horse guide shows you how to sit, hold the reins, stop and turn.

  4. 4
    One hour

    Through the palms

    A full hour on sandy tracks between the palms and earth villages. Beginners start led on a rope, then walk and trot; confident riders can canter.

  5. 5
    After the ride

    Mint tea at the stables

    Mint tea in the shade while the horses rest, with time to say goodbye to them and look at the photos your guide took on the way.

  6. 6
    About 3 h after pick-up

    Back to your hotel

    Back at your hotel or riad about 3 hours after pick-up, or we drop you at a restaurant or at Jemaa el-Fna if your evening goes on.

Frequently Asked Questions

I have never ridden a horse. Is that OK?

Yes. Most of our riders are beginners: you get a calm horse, a short lesson before you start, and the horse guide leads you on a rope until you feel ready. The pace follows the least experienced rider in your group.

Can children ride?

Yes, from about 6 years old on a gentle horse led on foot by the horse guide; from about 10 most children ride on their own. Tell us their ages and we bring the right horses and small helmets.

Are the horses well cared for?

We work with one stable we know: the horses are fed and shod properly, rest between rides and never go out in the midday heat. Each rider gets a horse that suits their weight, so tell us if anyone is over about 100 kg.

What should we wear?

Long trousers (jeans or cotton, not shorts or slippery leggings) and closed shoes with a small heel, no sandals. Add sunglasses, sun cream and a scarf for the dust; we provide the helmet.

Palmeraie or Agafay: where is it better to ride?

The Palmeraie is flat, shaded by palms and only 15 to 20 minutes away, which makes it the best choice for beginners and families. Agafay is a stony desert with views of the Atlas, about 45 minutes away; ask for it if you are a confident rider.
