Overview

Six days from the beaches of the south to the souks of Marrakech, for travellers who land in Agadir. Two nights in Taghazout, the surf village up the coast, with a morning swim in the rock pools of Paradise Valley, then the coast road by Imsouane to Essaouira’s white and blue medina.

Two nights in Marrakech to finish, with a local guide for the palaces, tombs and souks of the medina and a free afternoon of your own. Your own vehicle and driver-guide from the arrivals hall to your flight home, with short drives and stops wherever you like.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Agadir airport → Agadir Oufella → Taghazout

    Your driver-guide meets you at Agadir airport with your name on a sign, at whatever hour you land. In daylight, a stop on the Agadir Oufella hill for the view over the bay, then up the coast to your hotel in Taghazout, the surf village, for a first evening by the sea.

    • About 60 km, about 1 hour on the road
    • Night: Taghazout
    • Breakfast from day 2
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Taghazout: Paradise Valley and the bay

    An early drive into the hills to Paradise Valley, a palm gorge with turquoise rock pools for a swim before the heat. The afternoon back in Taghazout at your pace: a first surf lesson in the bay, the cliff path to the next beach or a café on the sand.

    • About 70 km there and back
    • Night: Taghazout
    • Breakfast
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Taghazout → Imsouane → Essaouira

    North on the coast road through argan hills to Imsouane, a fishing village with one of Africa’s longest waves, for a fish lunch by the bay. In Essaouira by mid-afternoon: the white and blue medina, the ramparts of the Skala and the blue boats of the port at sunset.

    • About 170 km, about 3 hours on the road
    • Night: Essaouira
    • Breakfast
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Essaouira → Marrakech

    A slow morning in Essaouira: the old Mellah, the woodworkers under the ramparts, a coffee by the port. Then inland across the argan plains to Marrakech, in time for your first evening on Jemaa el-Fnaa as the food stalls light up.

    • About 180 km, about 3 hours on the road
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  5. 5
    Day 5

    Marrakech: a guided day in the medina

    With a local Marrakech guide: the Koutoubia, the painted ceilings of the Bahia Palace, the Saadian Tombs, the souks and the Ben Youssef Madrasa, with no shop stops unless you ask. The afternoon is free for the Majorelle Garden, a hammam or a rooftop café.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  6. 6
    Day 6

    Marrakech → the airport

    A free morning for a last walk in the souks, then your driver-guide takes you to Marrakech airport, or to a hotel in the city, at the time of your flight.

    • Riad to Marrakech airport, about 20 minutes
    • Ends at Marrakech airport or your hotel
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we stay in Agadir instead of Taghazout?

Yes. Agadir has the long sandy beach, big hotels and the promenade; Taghazout is a small surf village 20 minutes up the coast, with more charm and Paradise Valley closer by. Tell us which you prefer and we book the first two nights there.

What if our flight lands late at night?

Your driver-guide follows your flight and waits in the arrivals hall with your name on a sign, at any hour, at no extra cost. On a late arrival we drive straight to the hotel and keep Agadir Oufella for another time.

How long are the drives?

Short: about an hour from the airport to Taghazout, about 3 hours on the coast road to Essaouira with a lunch stop in Imsouane, and about 3 hours from Essaouira to Marrakech. Day 2 and day 5 have almost no driving.

Will the Marrakech guide take us to shops?

No. Your local guide shows you the monuments and the souks and explains what you see, but never takes you into a shop unless you ask. If you want carpets or argan oil, tell the guide and they will show you honest places.

We fly home early on day 6. Is that a problem?

No. We leave your riad at the time your flight needs; Marrakech airport is about 20 minutes away. With an afternoon flight you have the morning free for a last walk in the souks.
