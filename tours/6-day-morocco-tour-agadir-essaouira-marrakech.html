Tour Overview

Enjoy six days exploring Morocco's Atlantic coast and Marrakech on a private tour that combines beach time, local culture and city sightseeing. You will spend two nights in Agadir, one night in the coastal town of Essaouira and two nights in Marrakech before your airport transfer on the final day.

The itinerary gives you time to enjoy Agadir at your own pace, explore Essaouira's historic medina and fishing port, and discover the gardens, palaces and traditional souks of Marrakech. With a private driver and comfortable vehicle, you can travel between destinations without rushing and enjoy stops along the way.

"More than a trip... It's a story to tell!"

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Arrival in Agadir → Airport Transfer & Free Time

Upon arrival at Agadir Airport, your private driver will welcome you and transfer you to your hotel. After check-in, you will have free time to relax and settle into your accommodation.

Depending on your arrival time, you can take a walk along Agadir's beachfront promenade, enjoy a coffee overlooking the Atlantic Ocean or explore the cafés and restaurants near the marina. If you prefer a quieter afternoon, you can relax at your hotel or spend some time on the beach.

Agadir is a pleasant place to begin your Morocco holiday, especially after a long flight. There is no fixed sightseeing schedule today, so you can enjoy the city at your own pace.

In the evening, choose a local restaurant for dinner or enjoy a relaxed meal at your hotel before returning to your room.

~25 km Agadir —
Day 2

Agadir Sightseeing → Kasbah Oufella → Souk El Had → Beach

After breakfast, begin your day exploring Agadir with your private driver. Start with a visit to the Kasbah Oufella viewpoint, where you can enjoy panoramic views of the city, the fishing port and the Atlantic coastline.

Continue to Souk El Had, one of Agadir's main traditional markets. Walk through its different sections and browse stalls selling spices, fresh produce, clothing, household goods and local handicrafts. Your driver can arrange a convenient meeting point and give you time to explore.

After lunch, return toward the coast and enjoy a free afternoon. You may choose to relax on the beach, walk along the promenade or spend time at the marina. Optional activities can also be arranged according to your interests.

In the evening, return to your hotel and enjoy your second night in Agadir.

~30 km Agadir Breakfast
Day 3

Agadir → Atlantic Coast → Essaouira

After breakfast, leave Agadir and travel north along the Atlantic coast toward Essaouira. The journey passes through coastal landscapes and small villages, with opportunities to stop for photographs or refreshments along the way.

Upon arrival in Essaouira, check into your hotel or riad before heading out to explore the town. Walk through the historic medina, where narrow streets are lined with small shops, art galleries and traditional workshops. Continue toward the fishing port to see the blue boats and watch the activity around the harbour.

You can also walk along the old ramparts and enjoy views of the Atlantic Ocean. For lunch, try fresh seafood at a local restaurant or choose one of the cafés inside the medina.

The evening is free to enjoy Essaouira's relaxed atmosphere before returning to your accommodation.

~175 km Essaouira Breakfast
Day 4

Essaouira → Marrakech

Enjoy breakfast at your accommodation before leaving Essaouira for Marrakech. If you would like, you can spend a little more time in the medina or take a morning walk near the beach before departure.

The drive to Marrakech takes you inland through the countryside. Along the way, you may see argan trees and traditional villages. Your driver can arrange a short stop for refreshments before continuing to the city.

Upon arrival in Marrakech, check into your hotel or riad and take some time to relax. Later in the afternoon, you can begin exploring the medina and its lively streets.

Visit Jemaa el-Fna square, where food stalls, street performers and local vendors gather throughout the day and evening. You can also enjoy dinner at a restaurant overlooking the square before returning to your accommodation.

~190 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 5

Marrakech Guided Walking Tour → Palaces, Souks & Medina

After breakfast, meet your local guide for a walking tour of Marrakech. Begin with some of the city's historic landmarks, including Bahia Palace, where you can admire traditional Moroccan architecture, decorated courtyards and peaceful gardens.

Continue through the old medina, passing narrow streets, local workshops and traditional markets. Your guide will introduce you to the different areas of the souks, where artisans sell leather goods, lanterns, carpets, spices and other handmade products.

Depending on your interests and the time available, the tour can also include the Saadian Tombs and an exterior visit to the Koutoubia Mosque.

After the guided tour, enjoy lunch and spend the afternoon at your leisure. You may wish to visit a garden, enjoy a traditional hammam or return to the souks for shopping.

~12 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 6

Marrakech → Airport Transfer

Enjoy breakfast at your hotel or riad in Marrakech. Depending on your flight departure time, you may have some free time for a final walk, a coffee or last-minute shopping.

Your private driver will collect you from your accommodation at the agreed time and transfer you directly to Marrakech Menara Airport. The pickup will be arranged according to your flight schedule, allowing sufficient time for check-in and airport procedures.

Your six-day private Morocco tour comes to an end at Marrakech Airport.

~10 km End of tour: Marrakech Menara Airport Breakfast

FAQ

Where does this tour start and end?

It starts with pickup from Agadir Airport and ends with a drop-off at Marrakech Menara Airport, so it works well if you fly into Agadir and out of Marrakech. We can also arrange it in reverse.

Is this a private tour?

Yes. You travel with your own driver and vehicle throughout, and the pace and stops can be adjusted to suit you. Day 5 in Marrakech also includes a local walking guide.

How much is free time versus sightseeing?

The tour is relaxed by design: Agadir and Essaouira are mostly at your own pace with a driver on hand, while Marrakech includes a guided walking tour of the palaces, souks and medina.

Is this tour suitable for a beach holiday?

Yes. With two nights in Agadir and a night in Essaouira, there is plenty of time for the beach and the coast before the culture and sightseeing of Marrakech.

Can the itinerary be customized?

Yes. We can add nights, change hotels, include extra activities or reverse the route to start in Marrakech and finish in Agadir — just tell us your plans.
