Overview

The Three Valleys day trip is a loop through the High Atlas foothills south of Marrakech: mint tea in a Berber house in the Ourika valley, the villages and weekly souks of the Asni valley, the high Kik plateau facing Toubkal, and Lake Lalla Takerkoust below the mountains.

It is the day for seeing a lot without long walks: about four hours on the road in short drives, never the same road twice, and each stop as long as you like. Your private 4×4 handles the plateau track, and your local professional guide explains village life as you go.

Programme

  1. 1
    08:30

    Pick-up in Marrakech

    Your driver meets you at your hotel, or at the nearest gate if you stay in a medina riad. We confirm the time on WhatsApp the day before.

  2. 2
    09:10

    Tea in an Ourika valley house

    About 40 minutes south, a Berber family opens its home in the lower Ourika valley: mint tea, bread from the clay oven and a look at how the house works.

  3. 3
    10:15

    Tahanaout and the Asni valley

    Across to Tahanaout and up to Asni, about 45 minutes. On a Tuesday (Tahanaout) or a Saturday (Asni) you walk through the weekly souk; other days, the village and its views.

  4. 4
    12:30

    Lunch in the Asni valley

    Tagine or couscous with a Berber family or at a country restaurant facing the mountains. You pay on the spot, around €10 per person.

  5. 5
    14:00

    Over the Kik plateau

    Back through the Moulay Brahim gorge and up on to the plateau by 4×4: the Toubkal massif on one side, the lake far below on the other. Stop for photos whenever you like.

  6. 6
    15:30

    Lake Lalla Takerkoust

    Mint tea by the water or a camel ride on the shore, then 45–55 minutes back to Marrakech, at your hotel around 17:30.

Good to know

  • This is a suggestion: tell us your preferences
  • Weekly souks: Tuesday in Tahanaout, Saturday in Asni, Sunday at Lalla Takerkoust
  • Little walking: short strolls on uneven ground, fine for most ages
  • Bring a warm layer: the plateau is windy and cooler than Marrakech
  • Dress modestly in the villages: shoulders and knees covered
  • The roads have bends: ask for the front seat if you get car-sick

Costs on the day

  • Lunch in the Asni valley: around €10 per person (about 100 MAD), depending on what you eat
  • Mint tea or a soft drink: about 10–25 MAD
  • Camel ride at the lake: optional; the price and the length are agreed before you get on
  • Tips: up to you, including a thank-you for the Berber family
  • Cash: bring some dirhams; the souks and village cafés rarely take cards

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the three valleys?

Ourika, Asni below Toubkal, and the N’Fis valley around Lake Lalla Takerkoust, joined by the Kik plateau. Tours sell different mixes under the same name; ours is one loop, so you never drive the same road twice.

Is there a waterfall on this trip?

No, that is what makes room for the plateau and the lake. If waterfalls matter most, take the Ourika Valley day trip to Setti Fatma, or the Ouzoud day trip for Morocco’s highest falls.

How long is the camel ride?

As long as you agree before you get on, usually 20–30 minutes along the shore. It is optional and paid to the camel man on the spot; your guide makes sure the price is fair.

Is it a lot of time in the car?

About four hours in all, but never much more than an hour at a stretch, with a stop after every drive. If you get car-sick, take the front seat: the plateau road has bends.

Will we stop at shops on the way?

No. The Berber house is a family home, not a shop, and nobody expects you to buy. Your guide is included all day, so you never pay a village guide either.

How do I book, and what if it rains?

  1. Send your request with the form or on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay a small deposit to confirm; the rest is paid on the day, in cash or by card.

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before. After heavy rain we skip the plateau track and drive back on the paved road; if the weather is really bad, you choose a new date or a refund.
