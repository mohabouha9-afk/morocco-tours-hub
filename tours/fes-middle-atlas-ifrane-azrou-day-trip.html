Overview

An easy, green day out of Fes and into the cool Middle Atlas. You climb through Imouzzer to the lake of Dayet Aoua, walk the tidy, Swiss-looking streets of Ifrane at 1,665 m, then go into the great cedar forest near Azrou, where wild Barbary macaques live among the trees.

The drives are short, about 40 minutes at most between stops, so it is a relaxed day for families and anyone who has had enough of the medina. Your driver and a local professional guide stay with you all day, and you are back in Fes in the late afternoon.

Programme

  1. 1
    09:00

    Pick-up in Fes

    Your driver and guide meet you at your hotel, or at the nearest gate if you stay in a medina riad. We confirm the time on WhatsApp the day before.

  2. 2
    09:40

    Imouzzer Kandar

    A 40-minute climb (38 km) onto the first Middle Atlas plateau. Coffee stop with a wide view back over the Saïs plain and Fes.

  3. 3
    10:15

    Dayet Aoua lake

    15 minutes on, a short walk on the shore of this tree-lined lake. Birds in spring; the water level depends on the year’s rain.

  4. 4
    11:00

    Ifrane

    25 minutes to the "Switzerland of Morocco": sloping roofs, gardens and clean air at 1,665 m. About an hour to walk the centre and see the stone lion.

  5. 5
    12:30

    The cedar forest and the macaques

    20 minutes into the forest near the Gouraud cedar. About an hour walking under the Atlas cedars, where wild Barbary macaques come close. Watch, but please don’t feed them.

  6. 6
    13:45

    Lunch in Azrou

    15 minutes down to the market town of Azrou. Your guide suggests a local place (grilled meat, tagine or trout, around €10); then a short look at the town.

  7. 7
    15:30

    Back to Fes

    About 1.5 hours (83 km) back via El Hajeb. You are at your hotel or riad around 17:00, or dropped at a restaurant if you go out for dinner.

Good to know

  • Ifrane is about 10 °C cooler than Fes: bring a warm layer, even in summer
  • December to March can bring snow: we check the roads and adjust the plan
  • Forest paths are easy but can be muddy: closed shoes are best
  • Keep food out of sight near the macaques, they grab bags
  • No shopping stops unless you ask

Costs on the day

  • Lunch in Azrou: around €10 per person
  • Coffee or mint tea on the way: around 15–20 MAD
  • Entrance fees: none, the town, lake and forest are free
  • Tips for your driver and guide: up to you
  • Cash is best for small payments on the way

Frequently Asked Questions

Will we really see the macaques?

Almost always. The macaques live in the cedar forest near the Gouraud cedar all year and are used to people. They are wild animals, so it can never be promised, but your guide knows where the troops usually are.

Can we feed the monkeys?

Please don’t, even if sellers offer peanuts. The Barbary macaque is an endangered species; feeding makes them sick and aggressive. You can watch them from a few metres, and they often come close on their own.

Is it worth it in winter?

Yes, and many people love it: the cedars under snow are beautiful. Bring warm clothes. If heavy snow closes a road, we change the order or the stops, and tell you before you leave.

Is it a good day for children?

One of the best from Fes: short drives, easy walks and the monkeys. We bring child seats on request.

Do we need a guide in the forest?

A local professional guide is always included, so you never pay anyone else.
