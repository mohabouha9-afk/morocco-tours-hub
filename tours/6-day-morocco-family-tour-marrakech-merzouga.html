Overview

Six days from Marrakech to the Sahara, paced for families. Over the High Atlas to the kasbah of Aït Ben Haddou, through the palm oases of Skoura and the Dades Valley, and into the Todra Gorge, with short drives on the way out and a stop whenever the children need one.

Then the best part: a sunset camel ride and a night in a desert camp, and a whole family day in the dunes with sandboarding, music in Khamlia and tea with a nomad family. Your own vehicle and driver-guide, child seats on request, and a hotel with a pool in Erfoud for the last night out.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Marrakech → Tizi n’Tichka → Aït Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate

    Your driver-guide collects the family at your Marrakech riad or hotel in the morning and climbs into the High Atlas by the Tizi n’Tichka pass, with stops for the views and for little legs. Aït Ben Haddou, the earthen ksar on its hill, to walk up through the old village, then a short drive to your hotel in Ouarzazate.

    • About 200 km, about 4.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Ouarzazate
    • Breakfast from day 2
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Ouarzazate → Skoura → Valley of Roses → Dades

    The Taourirt Kasbah in Ouarzazate if you like, then the palm oasis of Skoura and the Valley of Roses, stopping wherever the children want. An easy afternoon at your hotel in the Dades Valley, with time to rest or take a short walk.

    • About 150 km, about 3 hours on the road
    • Night: Dades Valley
    • Breakfast
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Dades → Todra Gorge → Merzouga

    A short walk between the high red walls of the Todra Gorge, then east through Erfoud and Rissani to the dunes. In the late afternoon, a gentle camel ride of about an hour into the Erg Chebbi dunes for the sunset (little ones ride with a parent, or go to camp by 4×4), then dinner, drums and stars at the desert camp.

    • About 270 km, about 5 hours on the road
    • Night: Desert camp
    • Breakfast and dinner
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Merzouga: a family day in the desert

    Sunrise over the dunes and a go at sandboarding, then a 4×4 day around Merzouga: Gnawa music in the village of Khamlia, tea with a nomad family, and the seasonal lake of Dayet Srij when it has water. Quad bikes can be added for older children. Back to your hotel in Merzouga.

    • 4×4 day around the dunes
    • Night: Merzouga hotel
    • Breakfast
  5. 5
    Day 5

    Merzouga → Rissani → Erfoud → Ziz Valley

    An easy day: the old caravan town of Rissani and its market, the fossils of Erfoud, and a viewpoint over the palm-filled gorge of the Ziz Valley. Afternoon free at your hotel in Erfoud, with time for the pool.

    • About 90 km, an easy day on the road
    • Night: Erfoud
    • Breakfast
  6. 6
    Day 6

    Erfoud → High Atlas → Marrakech

    The long road home, west by Tinghir and Ouarzazate and back over the High Atlas, with as many stops as the family needs: breakfast early, lunch on the way, ice cream breaks. Back at your Marrakech riad or hotel in the evening.

    • About 510 km, about 9.5 hours with stops
    • Ends at your Marrakech hotel or riad
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tour suitable for young children?

Yes. It is private and paced for families, with stops whenever the children need them, a pool at the Erfoud hotels and child seats on request. The drives are short on the way out; day 6 is the long one, and we break it up with plenty of stops.

Can small children ride the camels?

Yes. The camels walk slowly and a camel guide leads them; little ones ride with a parent. If a child is unsure, part of the family can go to the camp by 4×4 and meet the others there.

Is the desert camp safe and comfortable for kids?

Yes. You sleep in a private tent with real beds and blankets, with dinner, music around the fire and the stars. Nights are cold in winter, so pack warm layers, and tell us if you want a camp with a private bathroom in the tent.

Can children try quad biking?

Quad bikes can be added in Merzouga for older children: drivers from 16, passengers from about 8, and buggies for younger kids with a parent. Helmets and a guide are provided, and the price is agreed before you ride.

Is the last day too long for children?

It is about 510 km and 9 to 10 hours with stops, the only long day. If you prefer, we can add a night in Ouarzazate and split the drive in two; tell us when you book.
