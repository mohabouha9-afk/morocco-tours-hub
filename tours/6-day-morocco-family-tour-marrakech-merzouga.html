Tour Overview

Enjoy an unforgettable 6-Day Morocco Family Tour from Marrakech to Merzouga, combining Moroccan culture, mountain landscapes, ancient kasbahs and the Sahara Desert.

This private Morocco family vacation is designed at a comfortable pace, with plenty of opportunities for breaks, photographs and family-friendly experiences. Travel across the High Atlas Mountains, visit the famous Aït Ben Haddou, explore the Dades Valley and Todra Gorge, and continue toward Merzouga and the Sahara.

The highlight of this family desert tour in Morocco is your time in Merzouga. Ride camels across the dunes at sunset, spend the night in a comfortable desert camp, enjoy traditional dinner and music, and wake up to the sunrise over the Sahara.

With private transportation throughout the journey, the itinerary can be adjusted around your family's needs, children's ages and preferred pace.

"More than a trip... It's a story to tell!"

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Marrakech → High Atlas Mountains → Aït Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate

Your Morocco family tour begins in Marrakech. Meet your private driver in the morning and leave the city behind as you travel through the High Atlas Mountains. Cross the Tizi n'Tichka pass and enjoy panoramic views of mountain villages and valleys. There will be opportunities to stop for photographs and refreshments along the way.

Continue to Aït Ben Haddou, the famous fortified ksar and one of southern Morocco's most impressive historic sites. Explore its narrow lanes and traditional earthen buildings before continuing to Ouarzazate.

After checking into your hotel, enjoy a relaxed evening. Families can have dinner at the hotel and rest after the journey.

~≈200 km km Ouarzazate Breakfast
Day 2

Ouarzazate → Skoura → Valley of Roses → Dades Valley

After breakfast, continue your family trip through Morocco toward the Dades Valley. Before leaving Ouarzazate, you can visit the historic Kasbah of Taourirt or simply begin the journey toward Skoura.

Stop in the Skoura Oasis, where palm groves and traditional kasbahs create a beautiful landscape. Continue through the Valley of Roses toward the Dades Valley, making family-friendly stops whenever you wish.

In the afternoon, arrive at your hotel in the Dades Valley. The rest of the day is free for the family to relax, enjoy the surroundings, or take a short walk.

The slower afternoon gives children time to rest before continuing toward the Sahara the following day.

~≈160 km km Dades Valley Breakfast
Day 3

Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Erfoud → Merzouga

Today your Morocco family desert tour continues toward the Sahara. After breakfast, drive toward Tinghir and stop at the spectacular Todra Gorge. Take a short walk between the towering cliffs and enjoy the dramatic mountain scenery.

Continue toward Erfoud, known for its fossils and dates, before reaching Rissani. From Rissani, the landscape gradually changes as you approach the dunes of Merzouga.

In the afternoon, arrive in Merzouga and prepare for one of the most exciting experiences of your Morocco family vacation: a camel trek into the Sahara.

Ride through the dunes as the sun begins to set, then arrive at your desert camp for dinner, traditional music and an evening under the stars.

~≈260 km km Private Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & Dinner
Day 4

Merzouga: Family Desert Adventure

Wake early to watch the sunrise over the Sahara before breakfast. Today is dedicated to enjoying Merzouga without rushing.

Your private 4×4 excursion takes you around the desert region. Visit Khamlia, where you can experience traditional Gnawa music, and meet a local nomadic family to learn about life in the Sahara.

Depending on seasonal conditions, you can also visit Lake Dayet Srij. Children can enjoy the changing landscapes and open desert environment, while parents can take time to relax and photograph the scenery.

Optional activities such as sandboarding or quad biking can be arranged depending on the children's ages and your family's interests.

Return to your accommodation in the afternoon for a relaxed evening.

~≈40 km km Merzouga Breakfast
Day 5

Merzouga → Rissani → Ziz Valley → Erfoud

After breakfast, leave Merzouga and begin your journey north through the Tafilalet region. Stop in Rissani, a historic town with an important connection to the region's caravan routes and oasis culture.

Continue toward Erfoud before following the road toward the Ziz Valley. Stop at a panoramic viewpoint to admire the contrast between the rocky mountains and the green palm-filled oasis below.

This is a more relaxed travel day, giving your family time to enjoy the scenery without too many scheduled activities.

Arrive at your accommodation in the afternoon and enjoy free time. Families can relax at the hotel, enjoy dinner and prepare for the final journey the following day.

~≈120 km km Erfoud / Ziz Valley Breakfast
Day 6

Erfoud → Ziz Valley → High Atlas Mountains → Marrakech

After breakfast, begin the return journey toward Marrakech. Travel through the changing landscapes of southeastern Morocco and cross the High Atlas Mountains once again.

Enjoy several stops along the route for photographs, refreshments and family breaks. The private nature of the tour means you do not have to follow a rigid group schedule.

Depending on your family's energy and arrival time, you can stop for lunch along the way before continuing toward Marrakech.

Arrive in Marrakech in the afternoon or evening. Your driver will drop you at your hotel, riad or another agreed location.

Your 6-Day Morocco Family Tour from Marrakech to Merzouga ends with wonderful memories of the Atlas Mountains, ancient kasbahs, the Dades Valley and your Sahara Desert adventure.

~≈460 km km End of tour Breakfast

FAQ

Is this tour suitable for young children?

Yes. The itinerary is private and paced for families, with regular breaks, flexible stops and driving days that can be adjusted to your children's ages and needs.

Is the camel trek safe for kids?

The sunset camel trek is gentle and short. Younger children can ride with a parent, or skip the camels and travel to the camp by 4×4 — just let us know what suits your family.

What is the desert camp like for families?

You stay in a comfortable private Sahara camp with proper beds and private tents, a traditional dinner and music around the fire, and a sunrise over the dunes.

Can we adjust the driving days?

Absolutely. Because the tour is private, we can shorten days, add stops or change the pace so the trip never feels rushed for the family.

Are child car seats available?

We can arrange child seats on request — just tell us your children's ages when you book.

Can the itinerary be customized?

Yes. The route, accommodation level and activities can all be tailored to your family's interests and travel style.
