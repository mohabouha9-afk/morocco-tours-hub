Overview

See the most photographed garden in Marrakech before the crowds, then three royal sites of the old Kasbah quarter: the painted Bahia Palace, the vast ruins of El Badi and the Saadian Tombs.

Majorelle tickets sell out in timed slots, so we buy yours for the first one. Your own car takes you across town, your guide plans each stop around the queues, and you are back for lunch.

Highlights

  • Majorelle Garden: the cobalt-blue villa, cactus garden and bamboo paths, before the crowds
  • Pierre Bergé Museum of Berber Arts: jewellery, carpets and costumes, inside the garden
  • Bahia Palace: painted cedar ceilings and the great marble courtyard
  • El Badi Palace: the 16th-century sultan’s palace, its sunken gardens and nesting storks
  • Saadian Tombs: the Hall of Twelve Columns, carved cedar and marble
  • Kasbah quarter: the Kasbah Mosque and the lanes between the royal sites

Programme

  1. 1
    07:45

    Pick-up at your hotel or riad

    Your driver and local guide meet you; in a riad, at the nearest gate. We confirm the time on WhatsApp the day before and send your Majorelle ticket.

  2. 2
    08:00

    Majorelle Garden

    The first timed slot, when the light is soft and the paths are quiet. About 1 hour 15 minutes for the garden and the Berber Museum inside it.

  3. 3
    09:30

    Across town to the Kasbah

    A 10 to 20-minute drive to the gate nearest the Bahia Palace, while your guide tells you about the sultans who built the three sites you will see next.

  4. 4
    09:50

    Bahia Palace

    About 50 minutes in painted cedar rooms and tiled courtyards, before the tour groups arrive around 11:00.

  5. 5
    10:50

    El Badi Palace

    Walk the walls of the 16th-century palace, its sunken orange gardens and the storks’ nests, with a view over the medina from the terrace.

  6. 6
    11:50

    Saadian Tombs, then back

    Your guide times the narrow entrance queue; inside is the Hall of Twelve Columns. Back at your hotel by about 12:45, or dropped at Jemaa el-Fnaa.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Majorelle Garden worth it?

Yes, at the right time. It is small, about an hour, and later in the day the paths get crowded. At 08:00 it is calm, and the Berber Museum inside makes the visit.

Do we need tickets in advance?

Yes. Majorelle tickets are sold online only, in timed slots, and they often sell out the same day; there is no desk at the gate. We buy your tickets when you confirm and send them on WhatsApp; you repay the ticket price on the day.

Should we add the YSL Museum?

If you love fashion, yes: allow about an hour. It opens at 10:00, so we start a little later and reorder the palaces around it.

How much walking is it?

About 3 km over the morning, on flat paths and courtyards. The car takes you from Majorelle to the Kasbah quarter, and there are benches at every site.

Can we combine it with the medina tour?

Yes, for a full day. The medina tour also visits the Bahia, so on that day we swap it here for the Dar El Bacha museum and its coffee house.

  2. Pay a small deposit to confirm; the rest is paid on the day, in cash or by card.
