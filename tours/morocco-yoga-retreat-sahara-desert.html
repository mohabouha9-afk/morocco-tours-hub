Tour Overview

Reconnect with yourself on a unique 9-day Morocco yoga retreat from Marrakech to the Sahara Desert. This private desert yoga retreat in Morocco combines yoga, meditation, nature, Moroccan culture, and the peaceful landscapes of Merzouga.

Travel from Marrakech through the High Atlas Mountains, Aït Ben Haddou, Ouarzazate, Dades Valley, and Todra Gorge before reaching the Sahara. The highlight of this Morocco wellness retreat is three nights in Merzouga, giving you plenty of time to slow down and enjoy your yoga practice without rushing.

Practice yoga in the Sahara Desert at sunrise and sunset, meditate among the dunes, enjoy camel trekking, meet local communities, and experience the silence of the desert.

Whether you are an experienced practitioner or looking for a relaxing yoga holiday in Morocco, this private retreat can be adapted to your preferred pace and yoga style.

"More than a trip... It's a story to tell!"

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Marrakech → High Atlas Mountains → Aït Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate

Your Morocco yoga retreat begins in Marrakech. Meet your private driver and travel through the High Atlas Mountains, crossing the Tizi n'Tichka pass. Enjoy panoramic mountain views before reaching Aït Ben Haddou, one of Morocco's most famous historic ksars.

Take time to explore its traditional architecture and surrounding landscapes before continuing to Ouarzazate. After checking into your hotel, enjoy a relaxing evening and, depending on your arrival time, an optional gentle yoga or meditation session. This first day introduces you to the slower rhythm of your Morocco wellness retreat.

~≈200 km km Ouarzazate Breakfast
Day 2

Ouarzazate → Skoura → Valley of Roses → Dades Valley

After breakfast, continue toward the Dades Valley through some of southern Morocco's most beautiful landscapes. Stop in the Skoura Oasis, surrounded by palm trees and traditional kasbahs, before continuing through the Valley of Roses.

Arrive in the Dades Valley in the afternoon and take time to relax at your accommodation. The peaceful surroundings make this an ideal place for a gentle yoga and meditation session. Stretch after the day's journey, breathe deeply, and enjoy the mountain scenery around you. Your yoga retreat in Morocco is designed to balance sightseeing with time to slow down and reconnect with yourself.

~≈160 km km Dades Valley Breakfast
Day 3

Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Erfoud → Merzouga

Begin the day with a peaceful morning practice before continuing toward Merzouga. Travel through Tinghir and visit the spectacular Todra Gorge, where dramatic cliffs surround the valley. After exploring the gorge, continue through Erfoud and Rissani toward the Sahara.

In the afternoon, arrive in Merzouga and settle into your desert accommodation. This marks the beginning of the main Sahara yoga retreat experience. Depending on the time of arrival, enjoy a first yoga session surrounded by the desert landscape. As the sun sets, take a few quiet moments for meditation before enjoying a traditional Moroccan dinner.

~≈260 km km Merzouga Breakfast
Day 4

Merzouga: Sunrise Yoga & Desert Wellness

Wake early for a sunrise yoga session in the Sahara Desert. Practice gentle movements and breathing exercises while watching the first light spread across the dunes. After breakfast, enjoy a relaxed morning with time for reading, walking, meditation, or personal yoga practice.

Later, explore the surroundings of Merzouga before returning for another wellness session. In the evening, enjoy a sunset yoga experience among the dunes. Spending several nights in Merzouga allows your body and mind to adapt to the peaceful rhythm of the desert instead of rushing through a traditional one-night desert experience.

~Desert / at leisure km Merzouga Breakfast
Day 5

Merzouga: Sahara 4×4, Yoga & Meditation

Today combines desert exploration with your Merzouga yoga retreat experience. After your morning yoga session and breakfast, explore the desert by private 4×4. Visit Khamlia and experience traditional Gnawa music, meet a local nomadic family, and discover the landscapes around Merzouga. You may also visit Lake Dayet Srij when seasonal conditions allow.

Return to your accommodation for free time and relaxation. In the afternoon, enjoy a gentle yoga or meditation session. As evening approaches, practice sunset yoga in the Sahara before dinner. The day is intentionally flexible, giving you time to enjoy both adventure and wellness.

~≈40 km km Merzouga Breakfast
Day 6

Merzouga: Camel Trek & Sahara Desert Camp

Begin the morning with yoga, breathing exercises, or meditation. After breakfast, enjoy free time before preparing for your camel trek. In the afternoon, ride camels through the golden dunes of Merzouga while watching the changing colors of the desert.

Reach your Sahara desert camp in time for sunset. After settling into your tent, enjoy dinner and traditional music around the campfire. Depending on your yoga program, an evening meditation session can be arranged beneath the stars. Sleeping in the Sahara adds another dimension to your desert yoga retreat in Morocco, allowing you to experience the quiet of the dunes from sunset until sunrise.

~Camel trek km Private Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & Dinner
Day 7

Merzouga: Full Wellness Day in the Sahara

Today is dedicated to relaxation and your Sahara yoga retreat experience. Start with a sunrise meditation and yoga practice before breakfast. The rest of the morning is free for personal practice, walking, reading, photography, or simply enjoying the silence of the dunes.

Optional activities can be arranged for those who want more adventure. Later, return to your wellness routine with another yoga session focused on gentle movement, stretching, and breathing. End the day with sunset meditation as the desert changes color around you. With three nights in Merzouga, this retreat gives you genuine time to disconnect from your normal routine and enjoy the natural rhythm of the Sahara.

~At leisure km Merzouga Breakfast
Day 8

Merzouga → Rissani → Ziz Valley → Erfoud

After your final morning yoga practice in Merzouga, leave the Sahara and travel through Rissani toward Erfoud. Continue through the spectacular Ziz Valley, stopping at panoramic viewpoints to admire the palm groves and dramatic landscapes.

This is a slower travel day, allowing you to reflect on your Morocco yoga retreat experience. Enjoy free time after arriving at your accommodation and take the opportunity for personal meditation or relaxation. The desert may be behind you, but the peaceful rhythm of the retreat continues as you prepare for your final journey back toward Marrakech.

~≈120 km km Erfoud / Ziz Valley Breakfast
Day 9

Erfoud → High Atlas Mountains → Marrakech

After breakfast, begin your journey back toward Marrakech. Travel through the changing landscapes of southern Morocco and cross the High Atlas Mountains once again. Enjoy the scenery and make stops along the way for photographs and refreshments.

Arrive in Marrakech in the afternoon or evening, depending on road conditions and your preferred pace. Your 9-day Morocco yoga retreat ends with your private transfer to your hotel or Marrakech airport. You leave Morocco with memories of mountain landscapes, ancient kasbahs, golden Sahara dunes, meditation, yoga, and quiet moments of connection in the desert.

~≈460 km km End of tour Breakfast

FAQ

Do I need yoga experience to join this retreat?

No. The program can be adapted to all levels, from complete beginners to experienced practitioners, and the pace is flexible.

Is this a private retreat?

Yes. This is a fully private journey for you, your partner, your friends or your own yoga group — not a fixed shared departure.

Who leads the yoga sessions?

Sessions are arranged as part of your program and adapted to your group. If you prefer, you are welcome to bring your own private instructor, whose fees are not included.

How much time is spent in the Sahara?

You spend three nights in the Merzouga desert region, including a night in a private Sahara camp — genuine time to slow down rather than a single rushed desert night.

When do the yoga sessions take place?

Practices are planned around sunrise and sunset whenever conditions allow, with meditation, breathwork and free time for personal practice through the day.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothing for yoga, layers for cool desert mornings and evenings, sun protection, and your own mat if you prefer — mats can also be arranged on request.

Can the itinerary be customized?

Absolutely. The route, pace, yoga style and accommodation level can all be adapted to your preferences.
