Overview

One week from Marrakech to Fes, with time to enjoy each place instead of racing past it. Over the High Atlas to a night beside the ksar of Aït Ben Haddou, through the kasbahs of Skoura and the Valley of Roses, and up the Dades and Todra gorges to the dunes of Erg Chebbi.

Two nights by the dunes, one in a camp after a sunset camel ride and one in a hotel after a whole day in the desert. Then north through the Ziz Valley, a night in Midelt and the cedar forests of the Middle Atlas to Fes, for a guided day in the oldest medina of Morocco. Your own car and driver-guide all the way, and no shop stops you didn’t ask for.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Marrakech → Tizi n’Tichka → Aït Ben Haddou

    Pick-up at your hotel or riad in Marrakech around 09:00, no early start. Up into the High Atlas by the Tizi n’Tichka pass, 2,260 m, with stops at the viewpoints and the Berber villages, and down to Aït Ben Haddou in the early afternoon. Time to walk up through the ksar to its old granary and watch it turn red at sunset from your terrace.

    • About 190 km, about 4 hours on the road
    • Night: Aït Ben Haddou
    • Dinner & breakfast
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Aït Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate → Skoura → Valley of Roses → Dades Gorge

    Past the Taourirt Kasbah in Ouarzazate to the Skoura palm grove and Kasbah Amridil, then along the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs through the Valley of Roses at Kelaat M’Gouna. An easy afternoon up the Dades Gorge, past the Monkey Fingers rocks to the hairpin bends of Tissadrine.

    • About 180 km, about 4 hours on the road
    • Night: Dades Valley
    • Breakfast & dinner
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Dades → Todra Gorge → Erfoud → Merzouga

    A morning walk between the 300 m walls of the Todra Gorge, before the midday groups. East along the oasis road to Erfoud and past Rissani to the dunes of Erg Chebbi. Your big bags stay in the car and you ride a camel into the sand for sunset, then dinner, drums by the fire and a night in the camp.

    • About 320 km, about 6.5 hours on the road
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Breakfast & dinner
  4. 4
    Day 4

    A full day around Merzouga by 4×4

    Sunrise and sandboarding on the dunes, breakfast, and back by camel or 4×4. A whole day in the desert at your pace: mint tea with a nomad family, the Gnawa musicians of Khamlia, the black stony plains, the lake of Dayet Srij after a wet winter and the market of Rissani. A hot shower and a calm night in a hotel at the foot of the dunes.

    • 4×4 day around Merzouga
    • Night: Merzouga hotel
    • Breakfast & dinner
  5. 5
    Day 5

    Merzouga → Erfoud → Ziz Valley → Midelt

    North past the fossil workshops of Erfoud and up the Ziz Valley, with stops above its palm gorges and the old ksour along the river. Over the Tizi n’Talghemt pass to Midelt, the apple town at the foot of Jebel Ayachi, for a quiet afternoon and a night in the cool air of the Atlas.

    • About 260 km, about 4.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Midelt
    • Breakfast & dinner
  6. 6
    Day 6

    Midelt → Azrou cedar forest → Ifrane → Fes

    Across the Middle Atlas plateau to the cedar forest of Azrou, where Barbary macaques sit by the road, and coffee in Ifrane, the alpine town of red roofs. Down to Fes in the early afternoon: check in to your riad in the medina and take a first walk to the Bab Bou Jeloud, the Blue Gate, at dusk.

    • About 205 km, about 4 hours on the road
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  7. 7
    Day 7

    Fes: a guided day in the medina

    With a local Fes guide: the Bou Inania Madrasa, the Nejjarine fountain and woodwork museum, the coppersmiths of Place Seffarine, the Al-Attarine Madrasa and a rooftop above the Chouara tanneries. Shops only if you ask. In the evening the tour ends at your riad, or we take you to Fes airport or the train station.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Ends at your riad, Fes airport or the train
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

How much driving is there each day?

About 4 hours on days 1 and 2, 6.5 hours on day 3, a 4×4 day in the desert on day 4, then about 4.5 and 4 hours on days 5 and 6, with stops. No day starts before breakfast and no day is a marathon.

Why a night in Midelt instead of driving straight to Fes?

Merzouga to Fes is about 470 km in one go. Stopping in Midelt splits it into two easy halves, so you arrive in Fes in the early afternoon, rested, with time for a first walk in the medina.

What do we see in Fes?

A full day on foot with a local guide: the Bou Inania and Al-Attarine madrasas, the Nejjarine fountain, the coppersmiths of Place Seffarine and the Chouara tanneries from a rooftop. The route can be shaped around what you like.

Will we be taken to shops in the medina?

Only if you ask. The tanneries are seen from a leather shop’s terrace, which is free; you may look inside, but nobody expects you to buy.

Can the tour end at Fes airport or the train station?

Yes. On day 7 we take you to Fes airport or the train after the medina day, or the next morning if you keep your riad one more night.
