Overview

Twelve days from Agadir and back, one big loop through Morocco that never drives the same road twice. Inland first, to Aït Ben Haddou, the Dades and Todra gorges and two nights by the dunes of Erg Chebbi, a camp after a sunset camel ride and a hotel after a day with the nomads.

Then north over the Middle Atlas to Fes, with a guided day in its medina, the Roman ruins of Volubilis, the blue town of Chefchaouen and Rabat by the ocean. The Hassan II Mosque, a guided day in Marrakech, a night in Essaouira and the coast road home. Your own car and driver-guide all the way, and no shop stops you didn’t ask for.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Agadir → Taroudant → Taliouine → Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou

    Pick-up at your Agadir hotel around 07:30. Inland through the argan country to Taroudant and its golden ramparts, up into the saffron fields of Taliouine and over the Anti-Atlas to Ouarzazate. In the late afternoon, Aït Ben Haddou, the earthen ksar on its hill, glowing at sunset from your guesthouse.

    • About 410 km, about 7 hours on the road
    • Night: Aït Ben Haddou
    • Dinner
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Aït Ben Haddou → Skoura → Valley of Roses → Dades Gorge

    An early walk up through the ksar to its old granary before the day visitors arrive. An easy day east along the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs: the palm oasis of Skoura, the Valley of Roses, then the red cliffs and the hairpin bends of the Dades Gorge for the evening.

    • About 175 km, about 4 hours on the road
    • Night: Dades Valley
    • Breakfast & dinner
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Dades → Todra Gorge → Erfoud → Merzouga

    To the palm grove of Tinghir and a walk between the 300 m walls of the Todra Gorge, then across the stony plains past Erfoud to the dunes of Erg Chebbi. Your big bags stay in the car and you ride a camel into the sand for sunset. Dinner, drums by the fire and a night in the camp.

    • About 315 km, about 6 hours on the road
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Breakfast & dinner
  4. 4
    Day 4

    A full day around Merzouga by 4×4

    Sunrise and sandboarding on the dunes, then a whole day in the desert by 4×4: mint tea with a nomad family in their tent, the Gnawa musicians of Khamlia, the market town of Rissani and the lake of Dayet Srij after a wet winter. A hot shower and a quiet night in a hotel at the foot of the dunes.

    • 4×4 day around Merzouga
    • Night: Merzouga hotel
    • Breakfast & dinner
  5. 5
    Day 5

    Merzouga → Ziz Valley → Midelt → Ifrane → Fes

    North up the palm gorges of the Ziz Valley, lunch in Midelt under the snowy Jbel Ayachi, then the cedar forest of Azrou with its Barbary macaques and the alpine town of Ifrane. Into Fes in the evening, to your riad in the medina.

    • About 470 km, about 8 hours on the road
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  6. 6
    Day 6

    Fes: a guided day in the medina

    With a local Fes guide: the Bou Inania Madrasa, the Nejjarine fountain, the coppersmiths of Place Seffarine, the Al-Attarine Madrasa, the outside of the Al-Qarawiyyin and a rooftop above the Chouara tanneries. Shops only if you ask; the evening is yours.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  7. 7
    Day 7

    Fes → Volubilis → Chefchaouen

    West to Volubilis, the Roman town on the plain, with its mosaics and triumphal arch, then north into the green hills of the Rif to Chefchaouen, the blue town. The late afternoon in its lanes and the Outa el-Hammam square, and sunset from the Spanish Mosque.

    • About 260 km, about 5 hours on the road
    • Night: Chefchaouen
    • Breakfast
  8. 8
    Day 8

    Chefchaouen → Rabat

    An early walk to the Ras el-Maa spring while the blue lanes are quiet. South through the farmland of the Gharb to Rabat, the capital, for the blue and white Kasbah des Oudayas above the ocean at sunset, the Hassan Tower and the Mausoleum of Mohammed V.

    • About 250 km, about 4 hours on the road
    • Night: Rabat
    • Breakfast
  9. 9
    Day 9

    Rabat → Hassan II Mosque, Casablanca → Marrakech

    Down the coast to Casablanca for a guided visit inside the Hassan II Mosque, built over the ocean (Saturday to Thursday). Then the motorway south to Marrakech, at your riad in the afternoon, with the evening free for the food stalls of Jemaa el-Fnaa.

    • About 330 km, about 4.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  10. 10
    Day 10

    Marrakech: a guided day in the medina

    With a local Marrakech guide: the Koutoubia, the painted ceilings of the Bahia Palace, the Mellah, the souks and the Ben Youssef Madrasa. The afternoon is free for a hammam, the Majorelle Garden or a rooftop café.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  11. 11
    Day 11

    Marrakech → Essaouira

    West across the argan plains to Essaouira on the Atlantic. The afternoon in its white and blue medina, the ramparts of the Skala with their old cannons, the fishing port and its blue boats, and grilled fish by the harbour if you like.

    • About 180 km, about 3 hours on the road
    • Night: Essaouira
    • Breakfast
  12. 12
    Day 12

    Essaouira → the coast road → Agadir

    A free morning by the sea, then south on the coast road past the argan trees, the surf beaches of Imsouane and Taghazout and the cliffs above the ocean. In Agadir by mid-afternoon, at your hotel or Agadir airport.

    • About 175 km, about 3 hours on the road
    • Ends at your Agadir hotel or the airport
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the tour go to the desert first?

Agadir is the gateway to the south, so the Sahara is closer from here than the north. Going inland first and coming home down the Atlantic makes a loop: no road is driven twice, and the last day is a 3-hour coast drive instead of 550 km from Casablanca.

Which days are long drives?

Day 1, Agadir to Aït Ben Haddou, about 7 hours, and day 5, Merzouga to Fes, about 8 hours, both with stops for photos, tea and lunch. Most other days are 3 to 5 hours, and you stop whenever you want.

We are staying in Taghazout or Tamraght. Can you pick us up there?

Yes. We pick you up at any hotel or surf camp around Agadir, Taghazout and Tamraght, or at Agadir airport, and bring you back there on day 12. Tell us the address when you book.

Can we see the Hassan II Mosque on any day?

The mosque opens for guided visits from Saturday to Thursday. If day 9 falls on a Friday, we see it from the outside and the ocean promenade, or move the visit by changing a day earlier in the tour.

Can we fly home from Marrakech instead?

Yes. We can end on day 10 after the guided day, at Marrakech airport, or keep the night in Essaouira and finish at Essaouira or Marrakech airport. The loop is a suggestion: tell us how your flights work.
