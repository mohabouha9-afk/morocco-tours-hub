Overview

Agafay is a stone desert of pale rolling hills, about 40 minutes from Marrakech, with the High Atlas on the horizon. It gives you the feeling of the desert without the long drive to the Sahara.

Spend the day at a desert camp: ride a camel or a quad through the hills, relax by the pool, have lunch and stay for the sunset before your driver takes you back to the city.

Highlights

  • Views of the High Atlas over the hills
  • Camel or quad ride
  • Lunch at a desert camp
  • Pool time at the camp
  • Sunset over Agafay
  • Only 40 minutes from Marrakech

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Agafay a sand desert?

No, it is a stone desert of rolling hills. For big sand dunes you need Merzouga or Erg Chigaga, on one of our desert tours.

Can we stay for dinner?

Yes, you can add our camel ride and dinner show and return later in the evening.

Is it suitable for families?

Yes. The drive is short and the camp is comfortable, so it is easy with children.
