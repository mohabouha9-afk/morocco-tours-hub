Tour Overview

The Marrakech Agafay Desert Day Trip is a full-day private Morocco tour that travels Marrakech → Agafay → Marrakech. You'll spend your time exploring the souks, palaces and gardens of Marrakech and experiencing the stony Agafay Desert.

A full-day private excursion with your own driver, returning to your Marrakech hotel in the evening. Like every Morocco tour we offer, it is 100% private — just for your group — and can be tailored to your dates, pace, hotels and budget.

Day 1

Marrakech → Agafay Desert

Your driver collects you from your Marrakech hotel in the morning for the scenic drive to Agafay Desert. In the stony Agafay Desert, just outside Marrakech, rolling desert hills open up beneath the High Atlas — perfect for a camel or quad ride, lunch in a desert camp and a spectacular sunset. You'll have free time to explore at your own pace and to enjoy lunch (own account) before the return drive to Marrakech, arriving back in the late afternoon or evening. You travel privately with your own driver, so you can stop for photos and refreshments whenever something catches your eye. The drive itself is part of the experience, with ever-changing Moroccan scenery unfolding on both sides of the road. It's an easy, relaxed day out from the city and a great way to see a different side of Morocco without changing hotels.

~80 km Return to Marrakech in the evening Lunch (own account)
