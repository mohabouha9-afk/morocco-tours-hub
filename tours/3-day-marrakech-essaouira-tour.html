Overview

Three days for the two faces of Morocco’s south: the Red City and the white port on the Atlantic. A day in the Marrakech medina with a local guide, then west across the argan plains to Essaouira, with time to wander its lanes and watch the sun set from the ramparts.

Sleeping in Essaouira is the secret: you get the quiet morning port, with the blue boats coming in and the fish sold on the quay, before the day visitors arrive. Your own vehicle and driver-guide, and back in Marrakech by mid-afternoon on day 3.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Marrakech: a guided day in the medina

    Your driver-guide meets you at your riad in the morning with a local Marrakech guide: the Koutoubia, the painted ceilings of the Bahia Palace, the Saadian Tombs, the souks and the Ben Youssef Madrasa, with no shop stops unless you ask. The evening on Jemaa el-Fnaa as the food stalls light up.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast from day 2
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Marrakech → Essaouira

    West across the argan plains to the Atlantic, with a coffee stop on the way. The afternoon is yours in Essaouira’s white and blue medina and its woodworkers’ lanes, then the ramparts of the Skala for the sun setting over the ocean and grilled fish by the harbour.

    • About 180 km, about 3 hours on the road
    • Night: Essaouira
    • Breakfast
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Essaouira → Marrakech

    The best hour in Essaouira: the port in the early morning as the blue boats come in and the fish is sold on the quay, then the old Mellah and a coffee on the square before the day visitors arrive. Back in Marrakech by mid-afternoon, at your hotel or the airport.

    • About 180 km, about 3 hours on the road
    • Ends at your Marrakech hotel or the airport
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

Why sleep in Essaouira instead of a day trip?

A day trip gives you about 5 hours in town between two 3-hour drives. Sleeping there adds the sunset on the ramparts and the early morning port, the two best moments in Essaouira, and the day drives feel easy.

Is the medina tour full of shop stops?

No. Your local guide shows you the monuments and the souks and explains what you see, but never takes you into a shop unless you ask. If you want carpets or argan oil, tell the guide and they will show you honest places.

Will we see goats in the argan trees?

Sometimes, but many of the goats you see by the road are placed there for photos and the owners ask for money. We stop only if you want to; there is no fee from us and no argan shop stop unless you ask.

Is Essaouira good for swimming?

Essaouira is famous for its wind, which is great for kitesurfing and long beach walks but makes the water cool and choppy. Come for the old town, the port and the light, not for a swim.

Can we start or end at Marrakech airport?

Yes. We can meet your flight the evening before day 1, or drop you at Marrakech airport on day 3 instead of your hotel; we are back by mid-afternoon, so an evening flight works well.
