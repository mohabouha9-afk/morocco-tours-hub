Overview

From Tarifa, Morocco is about an hour away by ferry. We meet you at the Tangier Ville port exit and spend the day with you: the medina and the Kasbah on foot with a local guide, lunch where you like, then the coast road to Cap Spartel, where the Atlantic meets the Mediterranean, and the Caves of Hercules. You are back at the port in good time for your ferry.

It is just your party, not a coach group: no waiting for others, no fixed souk stop, and no shopping stops unless you ask. Your driver-guide plans the day around your ferry times, keeps the touts at a distance and stops wherever you want.

Programme

  1. 1
    10:00

    Meet at Tangier Ville port

    Take the morning ferry from Tarifa (about 1 hour). Your passport is stamped on board, so join the police queue as soon as you sail. Your driver-guide waits at the port exit with a name sign.

  2. 2
    10:15

    Medina and Kasbah on foot

    About 2 hours with a local guide: the Grand and Petit Socco, the souks, the American Legation (entry optional) and up to the Kasbah for the view over the Strait.

  3. 3
    12:30

    Lunch in Tangier

    Your guide suggests a few places, from fresh fish to a tagine; you choose where to eat (around €10–15).

  4. 4
    14:00

    Cap Spartel

    About 25 minutes west along the coast to the lighthouse on the north-west tip of Africa, and the viewpoint where the Atlantic and the Mediterranean meet.

  5. 5
    14:45

    Caves of Hercules and the beach

    Ten minutes down the Atlantic coast: the sea caves with their Africa-shaped opening, then a walk or a short camel ride on the beach.

  6. 6
    16:30

    Back to the port

    About 20 minutes back to Tangier Ville. We plan to have you at the port at least 1 hour before your ferry; later sailings mean more time in Tangier.

Good to know

  • Bring your passport: most European, UK, US, Canadian and Australian travellers need no visa
  • Morocco is on GMT+0 all year: Spain is 1 hour ahead in winter, 2 hours in summer
  • Ferry times are local: check the time on both sides of the Strait
  • Storms and strong east wind can stop the ferries: we keep an eye on the forecast with you
  • The medina has slopes and stairs: wear comfortable shoes
  • No shopping stops unless you ask

Costs on the day

  • Kasbah Museum: about 20 MAD per adult (closed on Tuesdays, optional)
  • American Legation: about 20 MAD per adult (closed on Sundays, optional)
  • Caves of Hercules: a small fee at the entrance
  • All other sites are free: the medina, the Grand and Petit Socco, the Kasbah square and Cap Spartel
  • Lunch: around €10–15 per person, depending on what you eat
  • Camel ride: paid to the camel owner; your guide helps you agree the price first
  • Tips: up to you
  • Cash: bring some dirhams; small payments are easier in cash

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I book the ferry myself?

Yes. Buy a return ticket Tarifa ⇄ Tangier Ville with the ferry company (Baleària runs the route) and send us your sailing times. We plan the day around them and tell you if a later return gives you more to see.

What if the ferry is late or cancelled?

Your driver follows your sailing and waits if it is late. If storms or strong wind cancel the crossing, you move your date for free or cancel and get your deposit back.

Is Tangier safe? Will people hassle us?

Tangier is a lively, safe city by day. Unofficial “guides” wait at the port, but with your own guide beside you they leave you alone; a polite “no, thank you” is enough.

Can we start from a hotel in Tangier instead?

Yes. If you sleep in Tangier, we pick you up at your hotel or the nearest gate to your riad, and the day can run longer, with no ferry to catch.

How do I book and pay?

  1. Send your request with the form or on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay a small deposit to confirm; the rest is paid on the day, in cash or by card.

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before your trip.
