Overview

Sixteen days from Casablanca and back, the whole country from the Strait of Gibraltar to the Sahara without a road driven twice. Rabat and whitewashed Asilah, Tangier and Cap Spartel, two nights in the blue town of Chefchaouen, the Roman ruins of Volubilis, and Fes with a guided day in its medina.

Then two nights by the dunes of Erg Chebbi, the Todra and Dades gorges, a sunset night at Aït Ben Haddou, Marrakech with a local guide, two slow days in Essaouira and a night on the Oualidia lagoon before the coast road home. Your own car and driver-guide all the way, and no shop stops you didn’t ask for.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Casablanca: arrival and the Hassan II Mosque

    Your driver-guide meets you in arrivals at Casablanca airport with your name on a sign, at whatever hour you land, and we follow your flight. If you arrive in time, a guided visit inside the Hassan II Mosque on the ocean and a walk on the Corniche; if not, rest and see it in the morning.

    • Airport to the city, about 45 minutes
    • Night: Casablanca
    • Breakfast from day 2
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Casablanca → Rabat → Asilah → Tangier

    Up the coast to Rabat for the blue and white Kasbah des Oudayas and the Hassan Tower, then north to Asilah, a whitewashed fishing town with murals on its walls and ramparts on the sea. Tangier by late afternoon, a city on the Strait with Spain in view.

    • About 340 km, about 5 hours on the road
    • Night: Tangier
    • Breakfast
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Tangier → Cap Spartel → Tetouan → Chefchaouen

    The Kasbah and the old medina of Tangier, then Cap Spartel, where the Atlantic meets the Mediterranean, and the Caves of Hercules. Lunch in Tetouan’s white medina, then into the green Rif hills to Chefchaouen, the blue town, for the evening.

    • About 170 km, about 3.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Chefchaouen
    • Breakfast
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Chefchaouen: a free day in the blue town

    A day at your own pace in the lanes of blue houses, the Outa el-Hammam square, the Kasbah garden and the Ras el-Maa spring, with sunset from the Spanish Mosque. Or a guided hike to the Akchour waterfalls in the Rif, on request.

    • Free day; Akchour on request
    • Night: Chefchaouen
    • Breakfast
  5. 5
    Day 5

    Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Meknes → Fes

    South through the Rif to Volubilis, the Roman town on the plain, with its mosaics and triumphal arch, then Meknes for the great gate of Bab Mansour and Place el-Hedim. Your riad in the Fes medina by late afternoon.

    • About 260 km, about 5 hours on the road
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  6. 6
    Day 6

    Fes: a guided day in the medina

    With a local Fes guide: the Bou Inania Madrasa, the Nejjarine fountain, the coppersmiths of Place Seffarine, the Al-Attarine Madrasa, the outside of the Al-Qarawiyyin and a rooftop above the Chouara tanneries. Shops only if you ask.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  7. 7
    Day 7

    Fes → Ifrane → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

    South over the Middle Atlas around 07:30: Ifrane and the cedar forest of Azrou with its Barbary macaques, lunch in Midelt, then the palm gorges of the Ziz Valley to the dunes of Erg Chebbi. Your big bags stay in the car and you ride a camel into the sand for sunset. Dinner, drums by the fire and a night in the camp.

    • About 470 km, about 8 hours on the road
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Breakfast & dinner
  8. 8
    Day 8

    A full day around Merzouga by 4×4

    Sunrise and sandboarding on the dunes, then a whole day in the desert by 4×4: mint tea with a nomad family in their tent, the Gnawa musicians of Khamlia, the black stony plains and the lake of Dayet Srij after a wet winter. A hot shower and a quiet night in a hotel at the foot of the dunes.

    • 4×4 day around Merzouga
    • Night: Merzouga hotel
    • Breakfast & dinner
  9. 9
    Day 9

    Merzouga → Rissani → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley

    Through Rissani, the old caravan town with its market, and along the Jebel Saghro to the palm grove of Tinghir and a walk between the 300 m walls of the Todra Gorge. On to the Dades Valley for an evening among the red cliffs and kasbahs.

    • About 270 km, about 5 hours on the road
    • Night: Dades Valley
    • Breakfast & dinner
  10. 10
    Day 10

    Dades → Valley of Roses → Skoura → Aït Ben Haddou

    An easy day west on the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs: the Valley of Roses, the palm oasis of Skoura and its Kasbah Amridil, and Ouarzazate. In the late afternoon, Aït Ben Haddou, the earthen ksar on its hill, glowing at sunset from your guesthouse.

    • About 175 km, about 4 hours on the road
    • Night: Aït Ben Haddou
    • Breakfast & dinner
  11. 11
    Day 11

    Aït Ben Haddou → Tizi n’Tichka → Marrakech

    An early walk up through the ksar to its old granary before the day visitors arrive. Over the High Atlas by the Tizi n’Tichka pass, 2,260 m, past Berber villages and walnut terraces, and down to your riad in Marrakech by early afternoon.

    • About 190 km, about 4 hours on the road
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  12. 12
    Day 12

    Marrakech: a guided day in the medina

    With a local Marrakech guide: the Koutoubia, the painted ceilings of the Bahia Palace, the Mellah, the souks and the Ben Youssef Madrasa. The afternoon is free for a hammam, the Majorelle Garden or a rooftop café, and the evening for the food stalls of Jemaa el-Fnaa.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  13. 13
    Day 13

    Marrakech → Essaouira

    West across the argan plains to Essaouira on the Atlantic. The afternoon in its white and blue medina, the ramparts of the Skala with their old cannons and the fishing port with its blue boats, and grilled fish by the harbour if you like.

    • About 180 km, about 3 hours on the road
    • Night: Essaouira
    • Breakfast
  14. 14
    Day 14

    Essaouira: a free day by the sea

    A day with no driving: the Mellah and the Bayt Dakira house of memory, the woodworkers under the ramparts, a long walk on the beach, a surf or kite lesson, or a camel ride along the dunes south of town. The evening on a rooftop as the sun sets over the ocean.

    • Free day
    • Night: Essaouira
    • Breakfast
  15. 15
    Day 15

    Essaouira → Safi → Oualidia

    North on the coast road past the argan trees and the cliffs to Safi, the pottery town, and its hill of potters’ kilns. In the afternoon, Oualidia, a quiet lagoon behind the ocean famous for its oysters, for a walk on the sand, a boat out on the water and a seafood dinner.

    • About 190 km, about 3 hours on the road
    • Night: Oualidia
    • Breakfast
  16. 16
    Day 16

    Oualidia → El Jadida → Casablanca airport

    A slow morning by the lagoon, then up the coast to El Jadida and the old Portuguese cistern, its vaulted hall reflected in a sheet of water. On to Casablanca airport, or your hotel in the city, by early afternoon.

    • About 225 km, about 3 hours on the road
    • Ends at Casablanca airport or your hotel
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 16 days too long for Morocco?

For a trip that wants the whole country, no. Two nights in Chefchaouen, Fes, the desert, Marrakech and Essaouira give you free time in each place, and three short days near the end (Aït Ben Haddou, the High Atlas, the coast) keep it from feeling like a race.

What does this tour add to the 14-day Best of Morocco?

Two things: the north coast, with Asilah, a night in Tangier and Cap Spartel where the Atlantic meets the Mediterranean, and a night at Aït Ben Haddou so you see the ksar at sunset and early morning, without the day visitors.

Which days are long drives?

Day 7, Fes to Merzouga, about 8 hours, with stops for photos, tea and lunch. Days 2, 5 and 9 are about 5 hours; every other day is 4 hours or less, or no driving at all.

Can we end in Tangier for the ferry to Spain?

Yes. We can run the loop the other way round, or finish in Tangier after the north instead of Casablanca; tell us how you travel and we rebuild the order around your ferry or flight.

What time can we fly home on day 16?

We reach Casablanca airport by early afternoon, so any flight from about 15:00 works. For a morning flight, we spend the last night in Casablanca instead of Oualidia.
