Tour Overview

Discover Morocco from north to south on this 16-Day Complete Morocco Tour from Casablanca, an extensive private journey designed to showcase the country's most fascinating cities, landscapes, cultural sites and natural wonders.

Your adventure begins with your first night in Casablanca, followed by Morocco's capital Rabat and the vibrant northern city of Tangier. From Tangier, travel through the Rif Mountains to the beautiful blue streets of Chefchaouen before continuing to the historic imperial city of Fes.

The journey then heads south through the Middle Atlas Mountains to the spectacular Sahara Desert. Experience the golden Erg Chebbi dunes, enjoy a camel trek at sunset, spend a night in a private desert camp and explore the desert region by 4×4. Continue through Rissani, Erfoud, Todra Gorge, Dades Valley, the Valley of Roses and Ouarzazate before visiting the UNESCO-listed Aït Ben Haddou and crossing the High Atlas Mountains to Marrakech. The final part of the journey takes you to Essaouira before returning to Casablanca.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Casablanca: Arrival

Welcome to Morocco! Upon arrival at Casablanca Mohammed V International Airport, your private driver will meet you and transfer you to your hotel in the city. Since this is your first night in Casablanca, you can enjoy a relaxed introduction to Morocco after your flight. Depending on your arrival time, you may visit the spectacular Hassan II Mosque, one of Morocco's most famous landmarks, beautifully positioned beside the Atlantic Ocean. You can also take a walk along the Corniche or enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner. Your first evening is intentionally relaxed so you can recover from your journey before beginning the road trip through Morocco. Your driver will also explain the following day's schedule and answer any questions you may have.

Casablanca Breakfast
Day 2

Casablanca → Rabat → Tangier

After breakfast, begin your journey north along Morocco's Atlantic coast. Before leaving Casablanca, you can visit any attractions you did not see on arrival. Continue to Rabat, Morocco's capital and one of its four imperial cities. Visit the Hassan Tower, the Mausoleum of Mohammed V and the Kasbah of the Udayas overlooking the Bou Regreg River and Atlantic Ocean. After exploring Rabat, continue toward Tangier, located at the meeting point of the Atlantic Ocean and Mediterranean Sea. Upon arrival, discover some of the city's highlights, including the historic Medina and Kasbah. If time permits, visit Cap Spartel, where the Atlantic and Mediterranean meet, and the nearby Hercules Caves. Enjoy the evening in Tangier before checking into your accommodation.

~340 km Tangier Breakfast
Day 3

Tangier → Chefchaouen

Enjoy breakfast before continuing your Moroccan adventure toward the Rif Mountains. Depending on your interests, you can spend some additional time in Tangier during the morning before departing for Chefchaouen. The drive takes you through beautiful mountain landscapes and traditional villages before arriving in the famous blue city. After checking into your riad, begin exploring Chefchaouen's charming Medina. Walk through narrow streets surrounded by blue-painted walls, colorful doors and small artisan shops. Plaza Uta el-Hammam is the heart of the old town and a pleasant place to stop for Moroccan tea or lunch. In the afternoon, you can walk toward the Spanish Mosque for panoramic views over Chefchaouen and the surrounding Rif Mountains. Enjoy a relaxed evening in this peaceful mountain town.

~120 km Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 4

Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Meknes → Fes

After breakfast, leave Chefchaouen and travel toward the historic heart of Morocco. Your first stop is Volubilis, one of the country's most important archaeological sites. Explore the impressive Roman ruins, including ancient columns, mosaics and the remains of Roman villas. Continue to Moulay Idriss, a sacred pilgrimage town built across the hills, before heading to Meknes. This former imperial city is known for its impressive architecture and historic monuments. Visit Bab Mansour, one of Morocco's most spectacular gateways, and explore the old Medina. After your visit, continue toward Fes. Arrive in the late afternoon and check into your traditional riad. The evening is free to relax or take a short walk around the area surrounding your accommodation.

~240 km Fes Breakfast
Day 5

Fes: Guided City Tour

Today you will discover Fes with a private local guide. Begin your exploration in Fes el-Bali, the ancient Medina and one of the largest pedestrian historic areas in the world. Walk through its maze of narrow streets and discover traditional souks, historic mosques, madrasas and artisan workshops. Visit important landmarks such as the Bou Inania Madrasa, Nejjarine Fountain and the famous leather tanneries. Your guide will explain the history, architecture and traditions of the city while introducing you to local craftsmanship. Continue to the Mellah, the historic Jewish Quarter, and enjoy panoramic views of the old Medina. Fes is regarded as Morocco's spiritual and intellectual capital, and its ancient streets provide a fascinating window into Moroccan history. After the guided tour, enjoy free time for shopping or relaxing at your riad.

Fes Breakfast
Day 6

Fes → Ifrane → Azrou → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

After breakfast, leave Fes and travel south through the spectacular Middle Atlas Mountains toward the Sahara Desert. Stop in Ifrane, a beautiful mountain town known for its distinctive European-style architecture. Continue to the cedar forests near Azrou, where you may see Barbary macaques among the trees. The journey continues toward Midelt, surrounded by dramatic mountain scenery and known for its apples and minerals. After lunch, cross the landscapes toward the Ziz Valley, stopping at a panoramic viewpoint overlooking the palm-filled valley. As you travel farther south, the scenery becomes increasingly dry and desert-like. By late afternoon, arrive in Merzouga, where the golden Erg Chebbi dunes rise dramatically from the surrounding landscape. Begin your camel trek into the dunes and watch the Sahara sunset before reaching your private desert camp.

~470 km Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & dinner
Day 7

Merzouga: Full Sahara Desert Experience

Wake early to watch the sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes before enjoying breakfast at the camp. After returning to Merzouga, begin a private 4×4 excursion around the Sahara region. Visit a local nomadic family and learn about their traditional lifestyle and how communities survive in the desert. Continue to Khamlia village, known for Gnawa music and its cultural heritage. You will also have the opportunity to visit fossil-rich areas and discover the geological history of the region. Depending on conditions, you may visit the seasonal lake near Merzouga, where desert birds and other wildlife can sometimes be seen. There will also be time for sandboarding and photography among the dunes. In the afternoon, relax at your accommodation and enjoy another peaceful evening surrounded by the Sahara landscape.

~40 km Merzouga Breakfast & dinner
Day 8

Merzouga → Rissani → Erfoud → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley

After breakfast, leave Merzouga and continue west through the dramatic landscapes of southeastern Morocco. Stop in Rissani, an important historic trading town and a great place to experience a traditional Moroccan souk when the market is operating. Continue toward Erfoud, famous for fossils and dates, before traveling toward the Todra Gorge. The road takes you through beautiful oasis landscapes and traditional villages. At Todra Gorge, take a walk between the enormous limestone cliffs and enjoy one of southern Morocco's most impressive natural settings. Continue through the palm-filled oasis toward the Dades Valley. Along the way, you will see changing landscapes of rocky plateaus, mountains and cultivated valleys. Arrive at your hotel or riad in the Dades Valley and enjoy a relaxing evening.

~300 km Dades Valley Breakfast
Day 9

Dades Valley → Valley of Roses → Skoura → Ouarzazate

After breakfast, continue through the beautiful landscapes of southern Morocco. Travel through the Valley of Roses, famous for its rose gardens and traditional rose-based products. During the flowering season, the valley becomes particularly colorful and fragrant. Continue toward Skoura, a peaceful oasis surrounded by thousands of palm trees and known for its historic kasbahs. Enjoy time to appreciate the traditional architecture before continuing toward Ouarzazate. Known as the gateway to the Sahara, Ouarzazate is also famous for its film studios and historic Taourirt Kasbah. Depending on your interests, you can visit one of these attractions before checking into your accommodation. Spend the evening relaxing and enjoying the atmosphere of this important southern Moroccan city.

~120 km Ouarzazate Breakfast
Day 10

Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → High Atlas Mountains → Marrakech

Today you continue toward Marrakech through some of Morocco's most spectacular landscapes. Begin with a visit to Aït Ben Haddou, the famous fortified village and UNESCO World Heritage Site. Walk through its narrow passages and explore the traditional earthen buildings built into the hillside. After visiting the kasbah, continue across the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka Pass. Along the route, your private driver can stop at scenic viewpoints overlooking Berber villages, valleys and rugged mountain landscapes. The road gradually descends toward Marrakech as the landscape changes from high mountains to fertile plains. Arrive in the Red City in the afternoon and check into your riad or hotel. The evening is free to explore Jemaa el-Fna or simply relax after the day's journey.

~200 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 11

Marrakech: Guided City Tour

Today explore Marrakech with a private local guide. Begin in the historic Medina and discover some of the city's most important landmarks, including the Bahia Palace, Saadian Tombs and Koutoubia Mosque. Continue through the lively souks, where local artisans produce carpets, leather goods, ceramics, metalwork and other traditional crafts. Visit Jemaa el-Fna and learn about the history and cultural importance of this famous square. Depending on your interests, you can also visit the Mellah or the beautiful Majorelle Garden. Your guide will help you understand Marrakech's history while taking you through its most interesting neighborhoods. After the guided visit, enjoy free time to shop, relax at your riad or discover the city independently. In the evening, experience the lively atmosphere of Marrakech.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 12

Marrakech → Essaouira

After breakfast, leave Marrakech and travel toward Morocco's Atlantic coast. The route passes through the countryside and the famous argan-growing region. Along the way, you may have the chance to see goats climbing into argan trees. Continue to Essaouira, a relaxed coastal city known for its historic Medina, fishing harbor and artistic atmosphere. After checking into your riad, explore the old city and walk through its narrow streets filled with handicraft shops and local cafés. Visit the Skala ramparts and the fishing port, where colorful boats line the harbor. Enjoy fresh seafood for lunch if you wish before taking a walk along the Atlantic beach. Essaouira provides a pleasant change from Morocco's inland cities, with its ocean breeze, relaxed pace and strong cultural character.

~190 km Essaouira Breakfast
Day 13

Essaouira → Marrakech

Enjoy a relaxed morning in Essaouira before returning to Marrakech. You can spend your morning exploring parts of the Medina that you may have missed, shopping for handmade souvenirs or enjoying Moroccan tea at a rooftop café. You may also take a final walk along the beach and enjoy the Atlantic Ocean views. Later, meet your private driver and begin the journey back to Marrakech. The drive takes you through the beautiful landscapes of southwestern Morocco and the argan-growing region. Upon arrival in Marrakech, check into your accommodation and enjoy the rest of the day at leisure. This is another opportunity to visit Jemaa el-Fna, explore the souks or simply relax at your riad after the journey.

~190 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 14

Marrakech → Casablanca

After breakfast, begin your journey back toward Casablanca. Depending on your interests, there is an option to stop along the way or continue directly to the city. Upon arrival in Casablanca, you can revisit the Hassan II Mosque, walk along the Corniche or explore the Old Medina. Casablanca provides a modern contrast to the traditional cities and desert landscapes experienced throughout your journey. Spend the afternoon at your own pace and enjoy your final evening in Morocco. Your private driver will make sure you arrive comfortably at your hotel and will confirm your airport transfer for the following day.

~240 km Casablanca Breakfast
Day 15

Casablanca: Free Day

Enjoy a relaxed final full day in Casablanca. After many days of traveling through Morocco, this free day allows you to slow down and enjoy the city without a fixed itinerary. You can visit the Hassan II Mosque if you have not already done so, explore the Old Medina, walk along the Corniche or enjoy Moroccan cuisine at a local restaurant. Casablanca also offers modern shopping areas for anyone looking for last-minute souvenirs. Your private driver can assist with additional sightseeing if you would like to visit other parts of the city. In the evening, enjoy a final Moroccan dinner and take some time to reflect on your journey through Tangier, Chefchaouen, Fes, the Sahara Desert, southern Morocco, Marrakech and Essaouira.

Casablanca Breakfast
Day 16

Casablanca: Departure

Today marks the end of your 16-Day Complete Morocco Tour. Depending on your flight time, enjoy your final breakfast at the hotel and some free time in Casablanca. At the agreed time, your private driver will meet you and transfer you comfortably to Casablanca Mohammed V International Airport. We recommend allowing sufficient time for the journey and airport check-in. If your departure is later in the day, we can adjust your morning schedule to allow additional sightseeing or shopping. Your journey has taken you from Morocco's Atlantic coast and northern cities through ancient imperial destinations, the Middle Atlas Mountains, the golden dunes of the Sahara, historic kasbahs and the colorful streets of Marrakech and Essaouira. We hope you leave Morocco with wonderful memories and a desire to return.

End of tour: Casablanca Breakfast
