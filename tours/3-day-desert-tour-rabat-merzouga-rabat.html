Overview

Three days from Rabat to the Sahara and back. Over the Middle Atlas and down the Ziz Valley to the dunes of Erg Chebbi, for a sunset camel ride, a night in the camp and a morning with the nomads and the Gnawa musicians of Khamlia.

The way home is a different trip: the cedar forest of Azrou and its macaques, the imperial city of Meknes and the Roman ruins of Volubilis, before you are back in Rabat in the evening. Your own car and driver-guide all the way, and no shop stops you didn’t ask for.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Rabat → Ifrane → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

    Pick-up at your hotel or riad in Rabat around 06:30. Up onto the Middle Atlas for coffee in Ifrane, the town of red roofs and lawns, then across the high plateau to Midelt for lunch, with the snowy ridge of Jebel Ayachi behind it. South through the palm gorges of the Ziz Valley to the dunes of Erg Chebbi, where you leave your big bags in the car and ride a camel into the sand for sunset. Dinner, drums by the fire and a night in the camp.

    • About 600 km, about 9 hours on the road
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Dinner & breakfast
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Merzouga → Khamlia → Erfoud → Ziz Valley → Midelt

    Wake up for the sunrise over the dunes and try sandboarding before breakfast, then back by camel or 4×4. A morning around Merzouga by 4×4: mint tea with a nomad family and the Gnawa musicians of Khamlia. After lunch, north past the fossil workshops of Erfoud and up the Ziz Valley, with stops above its palm gorges, to a quiet night in Midelt, the apple town of the Atlas.

    • About 260 km, about 4.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Midelt
    • Breakfast & dinner
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Midelt → Azrou → Meknes → Volubilis → Rabat

    Over the Col du Zad to the cedar forest of Azrou, where Barbary macaques sit by the road. On to Meknes, the city of Moulay Ismail, for the great gate of Bab Mansour, Place el-Hedim and lunch in the old town. In the afternoon, a walk through Volubilis, the Roman town on the plain, with its mosaics and triumphal arch, then back to Rabat in the early evening.

    • About 390 km, about 6.5 hours on the road
    • Drop-off at your hotel or riad in Rabat
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

Is three days enough to go from Rabat to the Sahara and back?

Yes, with an early start. Day 1 is the long one, about nine hours with stops, so you reach the dunes for sunset. The way back is split with a night in Midelt, so days 2 and 3 are about 4.5 and 6.5 hours.

Why come back by Meknes and Volubilis?

So the last day is more than a drive. Meknes has the great gate of Bab Mansour and an old town that sees few tourists; Volubilis is the best Roman site in Morocco. Both sit on the road home to Rabat.

Do we need a guide at Volubilis?

Not to walk around, and your driver-guide shows you the main mosaics. Official guides wait at the entrance if you want a full tour; you pay them there.

Can you pick us up in Casablanca?

Yes. Casablanca adds about an hour each way, so we start a little earlier on day 1 and drop you back in the evening on day 3.

Is it a good trip for children?

Yes. The camel ride is gentle, the camp has proper beds and dinner, and the stops break up the drive. We bring child seats on request; tell us the ages when you book.

How do I book and pay?

  2. Pay a 20–30% deposit by bank transfer to confirm; the rest is paid on arrival in cash (EUR, USD or MAD) or by card.

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before the tour.
