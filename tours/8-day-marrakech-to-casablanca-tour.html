Tour Overview

Experience the highlights of Morocco on this 8-day private tour from Marrakech to Casablanca, combining the country's colorful imperial cities, dramatic mountain landscapes and the golden dunes of the Sahara Desert.

Your journey begins in Marrakech before crossing the High Atlas Mountains to discover Aït Ben Haddou, Ouarzazate and the Dades Valley. Continue through the Todra Gorge and Erfoud to reach Merzouga, where you will experience a sunset camel trek and spend the night in a private Sahara Desert camp.

From the desert, travel north through the Middle Atlas Mountains to historic Fes, then continue to the famous blue-painted town of Chefchaouen. Your final days take you through Rabat before ending in Casablanca, where you can visit the impressive Hassan II Mosque. This private itinerary allows you to experience a remarkable variety of Morocco in just eight days.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Marrakech: Arrival & the Red City

Welcome to Marrakech, one of Morocco's most fascinating cities. Your private driver will meet you at Marrakech airport and transfer you to your hotel or traditional riad.

Depending on your arrival time, begin discovering the famous Marrakech Medina. Walk through the colorful souks and explore the lively streets surrounding Jemaa el-Fna, the city's main square. You can also visit the Koutoubia Mosque from the outside and enjoy your first taste of traditional Moroccan architecture and culture.

If you arrive early enough, you can visit Bahia Palace or one of Marrakech's famous gardens. Otherwise, enjoy a relaxed evening and traditional Moroccan dinner at your riad.

This first day is kept flexible so that your itinerary can be adapted to your flight arrival time.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 2

Marrakech → High Atlas → Aït Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate → Dades Valley

After breakfast, leave Marrakech and begin your journey south through the spectacular High Atlas Mountains. Cross the Tizi n'Tichka Pass, stopping at panoramic viewpoints to enjoy the mountain scenery and take photographs.

Continue towards the famous Aït Ben Haddou, a historic fortified ksar built from earthen architecture. Explore its narrow lanes and traditional houses before continuing to Ouarzazate, often known as the gateway to the Sahara.

From Ouarzazate, travel through the Valley of Roses and along the scenic Road of a Thousand Kasbahs towards the Dades Valley. The landscape changes dramatically as you enter the valley, with colorful rock formations, palm groves and traditional Berber villages.

Arrive at your hotel or kasbah-style accommodation and enjoy a relaxing evening.

~360 km Dades Valley Breakfast & Dinner
Day 3

Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Erfoud → Rissani → Merzouga

After breakfast, continue towards the Sahara Desert. Begin with a drive through the Dades Valley before reaching the spectacular Todra Gorge, where towering canyon walls surround the river.

After exploring the gorge, continue through the desert landscapes towards Erfoud, famous for fossils and local stone craftsmanship. Continue to Rissani, an important historic market town and former caravan center.

From Rissani, the road leads towards Merzouga and the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi.

In the late afternoon, leave your vehicle and begin a camel trek into the Sahara. Ride across the dunes as the sun begins to set, arriving at your private desert camp.

Enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner followed by Berber music around the campfire and a peaceful evening beneath the stars.

~300 km Private Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & Dinner
Day 4

Merzouga → Sahara Experience → Ziz Valley → Midelt

Wake early to watch the Sahara sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes. After breakfast, enjoy a private 4×4 excursion around the Merzouga desert.

Visit a local nomadic family and learn about traditional desert life before continuing to Khamlia village, known for its Gnawa music. Depending on the season and conditions, you can also visit local fossil sites and the seasonal lake near Merzouga.

After lunch, leave the Sahara and travel north through the spectacular Ziz Valley. Continue towards Midelt through changing desert and mountain landscapes.

This day combines the cultural and natural highlights of the Sahara with the beautiful scenery of Morocco's Middle Atlas region.

Arrive in Midelt in the evening and relax at your hotel.

~310 km Midelt Breakfast & Dinner
Day 5

Midelt → Azrou → Ifrane → Fes

After breakfast, continue north through the Middle Atlas Mountains. Stop near Azrou to explore the cedar forests, where Barbary macaques can sometimes be seen among the trees.

Continue to Ifrane, a mountain town known for its European-style architecture and cool climate. After a short stop, continue towards Fes through the fertile landscapes of the Saïss region.

Arrive in Fes, Morocco's cultural and spiritual capital, in the afternoon. Check into your traditional riad or hotel and enjoy some free time.

If you arrive early enough, you can take a short walk around the medina or enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner.

The following day will be dedicated entirely to exploring the historic city with a local guide.

~200 km Fes Breakfast
Day 6

Fes → Chefchaouen

Start the morning with a private guided tour of Fes, exploring the historic Fes el-Bali medina. Discover traditional souks, artisan workshops, beautiful madrasas and historic streets.

Visit the famous Chouara Tanneries and explore important areas of the ancient medina. Your local guide will introduce you to the history, architecture and craftsmanship that make Fes one of Morocco's most fascinating cities.

After the tour and lunch, leave Fes and travel north towards the Rif Mountains and Chefchaouen.

Arrive in the famous Blue City in the afternoon. Check into your riad and spend the rest of the day exploring its blue-painted streets, small squares and local artisan shops.

In the evening, enjoy dinner in the medina and take in the peaceful mountain atmosphere.

~200 km Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 7

Chefchaouen → Rabat

Enjoy breakfast before having some free time to explore Chefchaouen. Walk through the beautiful blue streets, visit the historic Kasbah in Outa el-Hammam square or walk towards the Spanish Mosque viewpoint for panoramic views over the town.

Later, leave Chefchaouen and travel south towards Rabat, Morocco's capital city.

Upon arrival, explore the Kasbah of the Udayas, the Hassan Tower and the Mausoleum of Mohammed V. Rabat offers an interesting combination of historic monuments, elegant neighborhoods and Atlantic coastal scenery.

After sightseeing, check into your hotel and enjoy a relaxed evening in the capital.

~250 km Rabat Breakfast
Day 8

Rabat → Casablanca → Departure

After breakfast, leave Rabat and travel to Casablanca, Morocco's largest city and economic capital.

Upon arrival, visit the impressive Hassan II Mosque, dramatically positioned beside the Atlantic Ocean. Depending on your departure schedule and the mosque's visiting hours, you can visit the interior with a guided tour or admire the building from outside.

Continue through central Casablanca and, if time allows, take a short walk along the Corniche.

Your private driver will then transfer you to Casablanca Mohammed V International Airport, your hotel or another location in the city.

Your 8-day private Morocco tour from Marrakech to Casablanca ends here after an incredible journey through the High Atlas, Sahara Desert, Fes, Chefchaouen, Rabat and Casablanca.

~90 km End of tour: Casablanca Breakfast

Why Choose This Tour?

Because the tour is completely private, you can travel at your own pace, make photo stops whenever you wish and customize the accommodation and activities according to your preferences.

It is an excellent choice for first-time visitors who want to experience Morocco's cities, mountains, culture and Sahara Desert in one journey.

FAQ

Is this an entirely private Morocco tour?

Yes. You will have your own private vehicle and private driver throughout the tour.

Does the tour include a Sahara Desert camp?

Yes. The itinerary includes a night in a private Sahara Desert camp in Merzouga with a camel trek and dinner.

Can we have two nights in Merzouga?

Yes. If you prefer a slower Sahara experience, we can modify the itinerary to include two nights in Merzouga.

Can we add Volubilis and Meknes?

Yes, although the itinerary is already quite full. We recommend adding one additional day if you want enough time to properly visit Volubilis and Meknes.

Can we end the tour in Marrakech instead of Casablanca?

Yes. The itinerary can be customized according to your international flight arrangements.

Is the tour suitable for families?

Yes. The tour is private and can be adapted for families with children, including shorter stops and a more relaxed daily schedule.
