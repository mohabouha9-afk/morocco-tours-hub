Tour Overview

Discover the best of northern, central, and southern Morocco on this unforgettable 8-Day Morocco Tour from Tangier. This private journey combines the charm of Morocco's northern cities with the imperial heritage of Fes, the spectacular landscapes of the Middle Atlas, the golden dunes of the Sahara Desert, and the vibrant atmosphere of Marrakech.

Begin your adventure in Tangier before exploring the blue-painted streets of Chefchaouen. Continue to the ancient Roman ruins of Volubilis and the imperial city of Meknes before discovering the fascinating medina of Fes.

From Fes, travel south through Ifrane, the cedar forests of Azrou, Midelt, and the spectacular Ziz Valley toward Merzouga. Experience a camel trek across the Erg Chebbi dunes, enjoy a Sahara sunset, and spend an unforgettable night in a desert camp. The journey continues through Todra Gorge, Dades Valley, Ouarzazate, Aït Ben Haddou, and the High Atlas Mountains before ending in Marrakech.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Tangier: Arrival & City Tour

Your 8-Day Morocco Tour from Tangier begins with a private pickup from Tangier Airport, ferry port, hotel, or riad. Depending on your arrival time, begin exploring this fascinating northern Moroccan city, historically known as a meeting point between Africa and Europe.

Visit the Kasbah and its atmospheric old medina, where whitewashed buildings, traditional cafés, and narrow streets create a distinctive Moroccan atmosphere. Continue to the Grand Socco and enjoy time to discover the city at a relaxed pace.

You can also visit Cap Spartel, the dramatic point where the Atlantic Ocean meets the Mediterranean Sea, and the nearby Hercules Caves. Enjoy panoramic coastal views and plenty of opportunities for photographs.

After sightseeing, check into your hotel or riad. The remainder of the evening is free to relax, explore the surroundings, or enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner.

~60 km Tangier Breakfast
Day 2

Tangier → Chefchaouen

After breakfast, leave Tangier and travel through the beautiful Rif Mountains toward Chefchaouen, one of Morocco's most picturesque destinations.

The journey offers beautiful views of green hills, valleys, and traditional mountain villages. Arrive in Chefchaouen, famous for its blue-painted buildings and peaceful atmosphere.

After checking into your riad, begin exploring the historic medina on foot. Wander through narrow blue streets, discover colorful doors and small artisan shops, and stop at one of the local cafés for traditional Moroccan mint tea.

Visit Plaza Uta el-Hammam, the lively heart of the old town, and explore the surrounding streets at your own pace. If you enjoy photography, Chefchaouen provides endless opportunities to capture its unique architecture and mountain scenery.

Later, climb toward the Spanish Mosque viewpoint or another panoramic location to enjoy beautiful views over the blue city, especially around sunset.

~120 km Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 3

Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Moulay Idriss → Meknes → Fes

After breakfast, leave Chefchaouen and continue toward the historic heart of Morocco. Your first major stop is Volubilis, an ancient Roman archaeological site surrounded by beautiful countryside.

Explore the impressive ruins and admire the remains of Roman villas, columns, mosaics, and ancient streets. The site offers a fascinating glimpse into Morocco's Roman past.

Continue to Moulay Idriss, one of Morocco's most important pilgrimage towns. Enjoy time to discover its whitewashed streets and panoramic viewpoints before continuing to Meknes.

Explore this former imperial capital and discover landmarks such as Bab Mansour, the old medina, and the historic royal granaries, depending on opening times.

After sightseeing, continue toward Fes. Arrive in the late afternoon and check into your traditional riad or hotel. The evening is free to relax and enjoy your first night in Fes.

~240 km Fes Breakfast
Day 4

Fes → Ifrane → Azrou → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

After breakfast, begin your journey toward the Sahara Desert. Travel through the Middle Atlas Mountains and stop in Ifrane, a charming mountain town known for its alpine-style architecture and cool climate.

Continue through the cedar forests near Azrou, where you may have the opportunity to see Barbary macaques. The landscape gradually changes as you travel toward Midelt, a town famous for its apples and mountain scenery.

Continue across the Atlas landscapes toward the Ziz Valley. Stop at a panoramic viewpoint to admire the dramatic contrast between the green palm oasis and the surrounding rocky desert landscape.

Travel onward through Erfoud and Rissani before arriving in Merzouga. In the late afternoon, begin your camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes. Ride across the golden sands while watching the sunset transform the desert landscape.

Arrive at your desert camp, enjoy a traditional dinner, listen to local music, and spend an unforgettable evening beneath the stars.

~470 km Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & dinner
Day 5

Merzouga: Sahara Desert Experience

Wake early to experience the spectacular Sahara sunrise. Watch the first rays of sunlight illuminate the Erg Chebbi dunes before returning to camp for breakfast.

Today is dedicated to discovering the Merzouga region and experiencing its desert culture. Depending on your interests, explore the surrounding dunes by private 4×4 and visit nearby communities to learn more about local traditions and daily life.

You may visit a Gnawa music community, discover traditional desert handicrafts, or explore the seasonal lake near Merzouga when conditions allow. For adventure lovers, sandboarding on the dunes is another enjoyable option.

You will also have time to relax and appreciate the peaceful atmosphere of the Sahara. In the afternoon, return toward the dunes to enjoy another spectacular sunset.

As night falls, enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner at your accommodation and listen to local music around the campfire. The clear desert sky provides the perfect setting for stargazing before another peaceful night in Merzouga.

~40 km Merzouga Breakfast & dinner
Day 6

Merzouga → Rissani → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley

After breakfast, leave Merzouga and continue toward the dramatic landscapes of southeastern Morocco. Begin with Rissani, an historic trading town and former caravan center.

If the local market is open, explore its traditional souk, where you can see local products, dates, spices, handicrafts, and everyday Moroccan life.

Continue through Erfoud and toward Tinghir. As the landscape changes, you will pass palm-filled oases, traditional villages, and rugged mountain scenery.

One of today's highlights is Todra Gorge, where enormous limestone cliffs rise dramatically on either side of the narrow canyon. Enjoy a walk through the gorge and take time for photographs.

Continue toward the Dades Valley, famous for its spectacular rock formations, winding roads, palm groves, and traditional Berber villages. Arrive at your hotel or riad in the afternoon and enjoy a relaxing evening surrounded by the beautiful scenery of southern Morocco.

~300 km Dades Valley Breakfast
Day 7

Dades Valley → Valley of Roses → Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → Marrakech

After breakfast, continue your journey through the spectacular landscapes of southern Morocco. Drive through the Valley of Roses, famous for its rose fields and traditional cosmetic products.

Continue to Skoura, a beautiful oasis surrounded by palm trees and historic kasbahs, before reaching Ouarzazate. Known as the gateway to the Sahara, Ouarzazate is also famous for its film studios and kasbahs.

Continue to Aït Ben Haddou, one of Morocco's most famous fortified villages and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Explore the ancient earthen buildings, narrow pathways, and panoramic viewpoints overlooking the surrounding landscape.

After lunch, begin the spectacular drive across the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka Pass. Enjoy breathtaking views of mountain valleys and traditional Berber villages along the way.

Descend toward Marrakech and arrive in the evening. Check into your hotel or riad and relax after an incredible day of sightseeing.

~360 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 8

Marrakech: Guided Tour & Departure

After breakfast, enjoy a guided exploration of Marrakech before your departure. Meet your local guide and discover the historic medina, one of Morocco's most fascinating cultural areas.

Visit the beautiful Bahia Palace, the Saadian Tombs, and the exterior of the famous Koutoubia Mosque. Continue through the colorful souks, where artisans sell carpets, leather goods, ceramics, spices, lanterns, and traditional handicrafts.

Explore Jemaa el-Fnaa, the famous central square, and enjoy time for shopping, photography, or a traditional Moroccan mint tea.

Depending on your departure time, you may have some free time to relax or explore more of Marrakech independently.

At the appropriate time, your private driver will transfer you to Marrakech Menara Airport or your preferred departure location. Your 8-Day Morocco Tour from Tangier ends with unforgettable memories of northern Morocco, the imperial cities, the Sahara Desert, ancient kasbahs, the Atlas Mountains, and Marrakech.
