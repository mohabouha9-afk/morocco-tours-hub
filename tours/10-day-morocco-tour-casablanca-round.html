Overview

Fly into Casablanca and out of Casablanca, with the whole of Morocco in between. Rabat and the blue town of Chefchaouen, the Roman ruins of Volubilis and Meknes, then two nights in Fes with a guided day in its medina.

Two nights by the dunes of Erg Chebbi, a camp after a sunset camel ride and a hotel after a day with the nomads, the Todra and Dades gorges and Aït Ben Haddou, two nights in Marrakech, and an easy motorway drive back to your flight. Riads inside the medinas, your own car and driver-guide all the way, and no shop stops you didn’t ask for.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Casablanca: arrival and the Hassan II Mosque

    Met in arrivals at Casablanca airport with your name on a sign, whatever time you land; we follow your flight. If there is time, a guided visit inside the Hassan II Mosque on the ocean and the Corniche at sunset; if not, it waits for the morning. Night in Casablanca.

    • Airport to the city, about 45 minutes
    • Night: Casablanca
    • Breakfast from day 2
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Casablanca → Rabat → Chefchaouen

    Rabat on the way north: the Kasbah des Oudayas above the river mouth, the Hassan Tower and the Mausoleum of Mohammed V. Then into the green Rif hills to Chefchaouen, with the evening for its blue lanes and sunset from the Spanish Mosque.

    • About 340 km, about 5.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Chefchaouen
    • Breakfast
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Meknes → Fes

    A morning walk in the quiet medina of Chefchaouen before the day visitors arrive, then south to the Roman mosaics of Volubilis and Meknes, with the great gate of Bab Mansour. Your riad in the Fes medina by late afternoon.

    • About 260 km, about 5 hours on the road
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Fes: a guided day in the medina

    With a local Fes guide: the Bou Inania Madrasa, the Nejjarine fountain, the coppersmiths of Place Seffarine, the Al-Attarine Madrasa and a rooftop above the Chouara tanneries. Shops only if you ask, and an evening free in the old town.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  5. 5
    Day 5

    Fes → Ifrane → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

    South over the Middle Atlas around 07:30, with stops in Ifrane, the cedar forest where Barbary macaques sit by the road, and Midelt for lunch. Down the palm gorges of the Ziz Valley to the dunes of Erg Chebbi and a camel ride into the sand for sunset. Dinner, drums by the fire and a night in the camp.

    • About 470 km, about 8 hours on the road
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Breakfast & dinner
  6. 6
    Day 6

    A full day around Merzouga by 4×4

    Sunrise and sandboarding, breakfast, and back by camel or 4×4. A day in the desert with no long road: tea with a nomad family, the Gnawa musicians of Khamlia, the black stony plains and the Rissani market. A hot shower and a calm night at a hotel by the dunes.

    • 4×4 day around Merzouga
    • Night: Merzouga hotel
    • Breakfast & dinner
  7. 7
    Day 7

    Merzouga → Rissani → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley

    West along the Jebel Saghro to the palm grove of Tinghir and a walk between the 300 m walls of the Todra Gorge, then on to the Dades Valley for a quiet evening among the red cliffs.

    • About 270 km, about 5 hours on the road
    • Night: Dades Valley
    • Breakfast & dinner
  8. 8
    Day 8

    Dades → Valley of Roses → Aït Ben Haddou → Tizi n’Tichka → Marrakech

    Down the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs through the Valley of Roses and Skoura to Aït Ben Haddou and a walk up through the ksar. Over the High Atlas by the Tizi n’Tichka pass, 2,260 m, and down to your riad in Marrakech in the late afternoon.

    • About 320 km, about 6 hours on the road
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  9. 9
    Day 9

    Marrakech: a guided day in the medina

    With a local guide: the Koutoubia, the painted ceilings of the Bahia Palace, the Mellah, the souks and the Ben Youssef Madrasa. The afternoon is free for the gardens, a hammam or Jemaa el-Fnaa at sunset.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  10. 10
    Day 10

    Marrakech → Casablanca airport

    A free morning in Marrakech, then about 2.5 hours on the motorway back to Casablanca. We drop you at Mohammed V airport in good time for your flight, or at a hotel in the city if you fly the next day.

    • About 230 km, about 2.5 hours on the motorway
    • Ends at Casablanca airport or your hotel
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

How much driving is there?

Days 2, 3, 7 and 8 are five to six hours with stops, day 5 to the desert is the long one, about eight hours, and the last day is 2.5 hours on the motorway. Days 1, 4, 6 and 9 have little or no driving, so the trip never feels like a race.

What time do we reach Casablanca airport on the last day?

Whenever your flight needs. Marrakech to Mohammed V airport is about 2.5 hours on the motorway, so for an afternoon or evening flight you keep the morning in Marrakech; for an early flight we leave at dawn or you sleep in Casablanca.

Why only one night in Chefchaouen?

To keep two nights in Fes, the desert and Marrakech in ten days. You arrive in the afternoon, see the sunset from the Spanish Mosque and walk the quiet lanes the next morning. A second night is easy to add.

Are local guides included?

Yes, in the Fes and Marrakech medinas, where they make the biggest difference. In Rabat, Meknes and Volubilis your driver-guide shows you around, and the Hassan II Mosque has its own guides on the visit.

Can we add Essaouira at the end?

Yes. Essaouira is about 2.5 hours from Marrakech and 4 hours from Casablanca, so a night by the sea fits well before the drive back. Tell us when you book.
