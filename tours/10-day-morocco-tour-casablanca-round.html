Tour Overview

Experience the best of Morocco on this unforgettable 10-day private Morocco tour from Casablanca to Casablanca, combining imperial cities, the famous Blue City of Chefchaouen, the cultural treasures of Fes, the golden Sahara Desert and the spectacular landscapes of southern Morocco.

Your journey begins in Casablanca before traveling to Morocco's capital, Rabat, and then north to the enchanting blue streets of Chefchaouen. Continue to Volubilis and Meknes before discovering Fes with a local licensed guide.

From Fes, cross the Middle Atlas Mountains and continue through the Ziz Valley toward Merzouga and the Erg Chebbi dunes. Enjoy a camel trek, Sahara sunset and sunrise, and a memorable desert camp experience. Continue through Rissani, Erfoud, Todra Gorge and Dades Valley before discovering Ouarzazate and Aït Ben Haddou. Cross the High Atlas Mountains to Marrakech, explore the Red City with a local guide, and finally return to Casablanca for your departure.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Casablanca → Rabat

Your Moroccan adventure begins with pickup at Casablanca airport or your accommodation. Start by discovering Casablanca, Morocco's largest city and economic capital. Visit the spectacular Hassan II Mosque, dramatically positioned beside the Atlantic Ocean, and enjoy time along the Corniche. Depending on your arrival time, you can also explore the Habous Quarter and Place Mohammed V before continuing to Rabat. Morocco's elegant capital offers a fascinating combination of royal history, Andalusian architecture and coastal charm. Visit the historic Kasbah of the Udayas, the Hassan Tower and the Mausoleum of Mohammed V. Your private driver will allow time for photographs and relaxed sightseeing throughout the day. In the afternoon, check into your comfortable hotel or traditional riad. Enjoy a relaxing evening and your first taste of Moroccan hospitality.

~100 km Rabat Breakfast
Day 2

Rabat → Chefchaouen

After breakfast, leave Rabat and travel north toward the beautiful Rif Mountains and the famous Blue City of Chefchaouen. The journey takes you through peaceful countryside before reaching one of Morocco's most picturesque destinations. Upon arrival, explore Chefchaouen's historic medina, famous for its blue-painted walls, narrow streets, colorful doors and traditional architecture. Visit Plaza Uta el-Hammam and explore the historic Kasbah before wandering through the artisan shops and atmospheric lanes. You will have plenty of time for photographs and to enjoy a traditional Moroccan tea at one of the local cafés. For panoramic views of the city and surrounding Rif Mountains, walk toward the Spanish Mosque in the late afternoon. Chefchaouen offers a peaceful atmosphere that makes it an ideal place to slow down and enjoy Morocco.

~200 km Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 3

Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Moulay Idriss → Meknes → Fes

After breakfast, depart Chefchaouen and continue toward Fes, with several fascinating historical stops along the way. Your first visit is Volubilis, one of Morocco's most important archaeological sites. Explore the ancient Roman ruins, impressive columns, historic buildings and beautifully preserved mosaics while learning about the region's Roman past. Continue to Moulay Idriss, a picturesque town built across green hills and an important pilgrimage destination. Afterwards, travel to Meknes, one of Morocco's four imperial cities. Explore the magnificent Bab Mansour gate, the old medina and historic monuments connected with Sultan Moulay Ismail. Continue to Fes in the afternoon and check into your traditional riad. You can spend the evening relaxing or taking a short walk around the surroundings of the ancient medina. Fes will be one of the cultural highlights of your Moroccan journey.

~240 km Fes Breakfast
Day 4

Fes: Full-Day Guided City Tour

Today is dedicated to discovering Fes with a local licensed guide. Begin in Fes el-Bali, the ancient medina and one of the world's most fascinating historic urban areas. Walk through its maze of narrow streets and discover traditional souks, historic madrasas, artisan workshops and centuries-old architecture. Visit important landmarks including the Bou Inania Madrasa, Al-Attarine Madrasa, Nejjarine Fountain and the famous Chouara Tanneries. Your guide will explain the history, culture and traditions that have shaped Fes over the centuries. Explore traditional workshops where craftsmen continue producing leather goods, pottery, carpets, metalwork and other Moroccan handicrafts. Enjoy lunch at a traditional restaurant before continuing your sightseeing. In the afternoon, visit the area around the Royal Palace and enjoy panoramic views over the ancient city. Return to your riad and enjoy a relaxing evening in Fes.

Fes Breakfast
Day 5

Fes → Ifrane → Azrou → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

After breakfast, begin your journey toward the Sahara Desert. Leave Fes and cross the beautiful Middle Atlas Mountains. Stop in Ifrane, a charming mountain town known for its distinctive architecture and cool climate, before continuing through the cedar forests around Azrou. Depending on local conditions, you may have the opportunity to see Barbary macaques. Continue toward Midelt, surrounded by impressive mountain scenery and known for its agricultural produce. From Midelt, travel through changing landscapes toward the spectacular Ziz Valley. Stop at a panoramic viewpoint to admire the lush palm-filled oasis surrounded by rugged mountains. Continue toward Merzouga as the scenery gradually transforms into the vast Sahara. Upon arrival, begin your camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes. Ride through the golden sands while watching the sunset before reaching your desert camp. Enjoy a traditional dinner, local music and an unforgettable evening beneath the stars.

~470 km Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & dinner
Day 6

Merzouga → Rissani → Erfoud

Wake early to experience the spectacular sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes. After breakfast, enjoy a morning exploring the Merzouga region before continuing your journey. Travel by private 4×4 around the desert and discover the unique landscapes surrounding the dunes. Visit a traditional nomadic community and learn about life in the Sahara, then continue to Khamlia village, famous for its Gnawa music and African cultural heritage. Depending on local conditions, you can also visit Lake Dayet Srij. After lunch, leave the desert area and travel toward Rissani, a historic trading town and former gateway between the Sahara and northern Morocco. Explore its traditional souk if it is open and discover local products such as dates, spices and handicrafts. Continue to Erfoud, famous for fossils and fossilized marble products. Enjoy a relaxing evening after your desert adventure.

~90 km Erfoud Breakfast
Day 7

Erfoud → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley → Ouarzazate

After breakfast, continue your journey through the spectacular landscapes of southern Morocco. Travel from Erfoud toward Tinghir and visit the impressive Todra Gorge, where enormous limestone cliffs rise dramatically on both sides of the narrow valley. Enjoy a walk through the gorge and take photographs of this spectacular natural setting. Continue through the green palm-filled oasis and toward the Dades Valley. Along the route, admire traditional villages, ancient kasbahs and dramatic rock formations. Continue through the scenic Valley of Roses before reaching Ouarzazate, often called the Gateway to the Sahara. Ouarzazate is famous for its kasbahs, film studios and beautiful desert landscapes. After arriving, check into your hotel or riad and enjoy a relaxing evening. This part of the journey provides a wonderful contrast to the Sahara, with lush oases, mountain valleys and traditional Berber architecture.

~300 km Ouarzazate Breakfast
Day 8

Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → High Atlas Mountains → Marrakech

After breakfast, visit the famous Aït Ben Haddou, one of Morocco's most remarkable fortified villages and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Explore its narrow lanes, traditional earthen buildings and panoramic viewpoints while learning about the history of this ancient settlement. After your visit, begin the scenic journey toward Marrakech. Cross the spectacular High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka Pass, one of Morocco's most beautiful mountain roads. Stop along the way to admire panoramic views of the valleys, mountains and traditional Berber villages. The landscape changes dramatically as you descend from the mountains toward the plains surrounding Marrakech. Arrive in the Red City in the afternoon or evening and check into your traditional riad. Enjoy some free time to relax or take an evening walk around the historic medina. Marrakech offers a completely different atmosphere, filled with colorful souks, lively squares and beautiful traditional architecture.

~200 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 9

Marrakech → Casablanca

After breakfast, explore Marrakech with a local licensed guide. Begin in the historic medina and discover famous landmarks such as the Bahia Palace, Saadian Tombs and Koutoubia Mosque. Walk through the colorful souks and discover traditional Moroccan crafts, including carpets, leather goods, lamps, spices and textiles. Visit the legendary Jemaa el-Fnaa square, the lively heart of Marrakech, and experience its unique atmosphere. Your guide will introduce you to the history, culture and architecture of the Red City. After lunch, continue your journey toward Casablanca. The drive takes you across the Moroccan plains toward the Atlantic coast. Upon arrival in Casablanca, check into your hotel and enjoy a relaxing final evening. Depending on your schedule, you can enjoy a final walk along the Corniche or spend the evening relaxing before your departure.

~240 km Casablanca Breakfast
Day 10

Casablanca: Departure

Enjoy your final morning in Morocco at your own pace. Depending on your departure time, you may have time for breakfast at your hotel, a final visit to the city, shopping for souvenirs or a relaxing walk along the Atlantic coast. If you missed the Hassan II Mosque on the first day because of your arrival time, additional sightseeing can be arranged according to your flight schedule. Your private driver will transfer you to Casablanca airport at the appropriate time. Your 10-day private Morocco tour from Casablanca to Casablanca comes to an end after an incredible journey through Morocco's imperial cities, Rif Mountains, ancient Roman history, Sahara Desert, dramatic valleys, High Atlas Mountains and vibrant Marrakech. From the blue streets of Chefchaouen to the golden dunes of Merzouga and the colorful souks of Marrakech, this journey offers a complete introduction to Morocco and its extraordinary cultural and natural diversity.

End of tour: Casablanca Breakfast
