Tour Overview

This Special 5-Day Morocco Student Desert Tour from Marrakech is designed for student groups who want to experience the Sahara while keeping their arrival and departure days simple. The program begins with pickup directly from Marrakech Airport and an overnight stay in Marrakech.

The following morning, the group starts the journey across the High Atlas Mountains toward Merzouga and the Erg Chebbi dunes. Students will spend two nights in the Merzouga area, including a Sahara desert camp experience, a camel ride and a full day exploring the desert.

The program includes time to discover the villages and landscapes around Erg Chebbi, experience local culture and enjoy activities on the dunes. On Day 4, the group travels back to Marrakech, followed by a final airport transfer on Day 5.

This is a private student tour, making it suitable for school groups, university groups and groups of friends travelling together.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Arrival at Marrakech Airport → Transfer to Hotel

Welcome to Morocco! Upon arrival at Marrakech Menara Airport, our driver will meet your student group and assist with the transfer to your accommodation in Marrakech.

After check-in, students will have time to relax after the flight and prepare for the desert journey. Depending on your arrival time, the group can enjoy some free time in Marrakech.

If you arrive early enough, you can take a walk around Jemaa el-Fna and experience the lively atmosphere of the old medina. Students can see local food stalls, shops and everyday life around one of Marrakech's busiest areas.

There is no fixed sightseeing program on the arrival day, making the schedule flexible according to your flight time. In the evening, return to your accommodation for your first night in Morocco, and your driver will confirm the following morning's departure time for Merzouga.

~15 km Marrakech —
Day 2

Marrakech → High Atlas Mountains → Ouarzazate → Merzouga Desert

After an early breakfast, leave Marrakech for the journey toward the Sahara. Cross the High Atlas Mountains, enjoying views of mountain valleys, traditional villages and changing landscapes along the way.

Continue through the Ouarzazate area and travel east toward the desert. This is a long driving day, but there will be stops for lunch, refreshments, photographs and short breaks along the route.

As you travel farther south and east, the green mountain scenery gradually changes into rocky desert landscapes. Continue toward Erfoud and Rissani before reaching Merzouga and the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi.

Depending on the arrival time, students will meet their camel guides and ride across the dunes toward the desert camp. If the group arrives after sunset, transportation to the camp can instead be arranged by 4×4.

Enjoy dinner at the camp followed by traditional music under the desert sky.

~560 km Merzouga desert camp Breakfast & Dinner
Day 3

Full-Day Merzouga Desert Experience → 4×4 Tour → Khamlia → Erg Chebbi

Wake up early to watch the sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes before enjoying breakfast at the camp. Today is dedicated entirely to exploring Merzouga and the surrounding Sahara landscapes.

Begin a private 4×4 excursion around the dunes, travelling along desert tracks and visiting different areas around Erg Chebbi. Stop in Khamlia village, where students can listen to traditional Gnawa music and learn about local culture.

Depending on local conditions, the excursion can also include a visit with a nomadic family and stops at panoramic locations around the desert. The experience gives students an opportunity to learn about everyday life and traditions in this part of Morocco.

After lunch, enjoy free time near the dunes. Students can try sandboarding, take photographs or simply relax. Optional quad biking can also be arranged.

~40 km Hotel or riad in Merzouga Breakfast
Day 4

Merzouga → Rissani → Ouarzazate → High Atlas Mountains → Marrakech

After an early breakfast, leave Merzouga and begin the return journey to Marrakech. Travel through Rissani and the landscapes of southern Morocco before continuing west toward Ouarzazate.

This is another long travel day, so several stops can be made along the route for refreshments, lunch and photographs. The journey gives students another opportunity to see how Morocco's landscapes change between the Sahara, rocky valleys and mountain regions.

Continue through the Ouarzazate area before crossing the High Atlas Mountains toward Marrakech. Panoramic stops can be made when time and road conditions allow.

Arrive in Marrakech in the evening and check into your accommodation. After the long journey from the Sahara, the group can relax at the hotel or enjoy a final evening in the city, depending on the arrival time. Your driver will confirm the airport pickup time for the following day.

~560 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 5

Marrakech → Airport Transfer

Enjoy breakfast at your hotel or riad before preparing for your departure from Morocco.

Your final morning will depend on the scheduled flight time. If your flight leaves later in the day, students can enjoy some free time in Marrakech for last-minute shopping, a walk through the medina or simply relaxing at the accommodation.

At the agreed time, our driver will meet the group and provide a private transfer to Marrakech Menara Airport. We recommend planning the transfer with enough time for airport check-in and security procedures.

Upon arrival at the airport, your Special Morocco Student Desert Tour comes to an end after five days combining Marrakech, the High Atlas Mountains and the Sahara Desert at Merzouga.

~15 km End of tour: Marrakech Airport Breakfast

FAQ

Is this tour suitable for student groups?

Yes. This tour is designed for student groups, university groups and groups of young travelers who want to combine Marrakech with a Sahara Desert experience.

How many nights do students spend in Merzouga?

The group spends two nights in the Merzouga area: one night at a Sahara desert camp and one night in a hotel or riad.

Is the camel ride included?

Yes. A camel ride through the Erg Chebbi dunes is included. A 4×4 transfer can be arranged for students who prefer not to ride a camel.

What activities are included in Merzouga?

The program includes a camel experience, sandboarding and a private 4×4 desert excursion, including Khamlia and other stops around Erg Chebbi.

Is Marrakech to Merzouga a long drive?

Yes. Marrakech to Merzouga is a long travel day, particularly when completed in one day. The group will leave early and make several breaks along the route. For groups wanting a more relaxed itinerary, the desert journey can be extended with an overnight stop in the Dades Valley.

Can the program be customized for a school or university?

Yes. The itinerary can be adjusted according to group size, flight times, budget, accommodation requirements and educational needs.
