Why Start Your Tour in Agadir?

Agadir, Morocco's relaxed Atlantic beach city, is a great starting point if you are holidaying on the coast but want to experience the Sahara. Tours from Agadir head inland through Taroudant, the Anti-Atlas and the saffron region of Taliouine to Aït Ben Haddou, the Dades Valley, Todra Gorge and the golden dunes of Merzouga. Every tour below begins in Agadir and is 100% private and customizable around your stay.
