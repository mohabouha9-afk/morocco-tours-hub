Overview

Six days in and around Marrakech without ever packing a bag. You stay in the same riad all week: a day in the medina with a local guide, then a different world each day, from the green Ourika Valley in the High Atlas to the Ouzoud Waterfalls and the kasbah of Aït Ben Haddou.

Each evening you are back in the Red City for dinner, with your own vehicle and driver-guide every day and a local guide at each site. Swap any day out for another trip, or keep it free.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Arrival in Marrakech

    Your driver-guide meets you at Marrakech airport with your name on a sign, at whatever hour you land, and takes you to your riad. If you arrive early, a first walk to Jemaa el-Fnaa as the food stalls light up; otherwise the evening is yours to settle in.

    • Airport to your riad, about 20 minutes
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast from day 2
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Marrakech: a guided day in the medina

    With a local Marrakech guide: the Koutoubia, the painted ceilings of the Bahia Palace, the old Mellah, the souks and a garden, with no shop stops unless you ask. The Majorelle Garden can be added in the afternoon.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Ourika Valley in the High Atlas

    Into the mountains by 09:00, along the river through Berber villages, then a guided walk up to the first waterfalls of Setti Fatma, with mint tea in a Berber house and lunch by the water. Back at your riad around 17:30.

    • About 130 km there and back
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Ouzoud Waterfalls

    An 08:00 start for one of the highest waterfalls in Morocco, through olive groves in the Middle Atlas foothills. A local guide walks you down to the foot of the falls, past the Barbary macaques, with lunch facing the water and a boat ride if you like. Back around 18:00.

    • About 300 km there and back
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  5. 5
    Day 5

    Aït Ben Haddou and Ouarzazate

    The big day out: over the High Atlas by the Tizi n’Tichka pass at 2,260 m to Aït Ben Haddou, about two hours in the old ksar with a local guide, up to the granary at the top. Lunch facing the kasbah, then Ouarzazate for the film studios or the Taourirt Kasbah. Back around 19:30.

    • About 380 km there and back
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  6. 6
    Day 6

    Free morning and departure

    A slow last morning for the souks or a coffee on a rooftop, then your driver-guide takes you to Marrakech airport, or anywhere in the city, at the time of your flight.

    • Riad to Marrakech airport, about 20 minutes
    • Ends at Marrakech airport or your hotel
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

Why stay in one hotel all week?

No packing, no check-ins, and the same room every night: easier with children and more restful for everyone. Most of the best places around Marrakech are an easy day out, so you lose nothing by coming back each evening.

Can we change the day trips?

Yes. Swap any day for another day trip, such as Essaouira, the Agafay desert or Imlil, or keep a day free for the pool and the souks. Tell us when you book and we send you the new plan.

Is the Aït Ben Haddou day too long?

It is the longest: about 3.5 hours each way over the High Atlas, out at 07:30 and back around 19:30, with stops whenever you like. If that is too much, we can drop Ouarzazate, or you can sleep a night at Aït Ben Haddou.

How hard are the walks at Ourika and Ouzoud?

At Ourika the path to the first waterfalls takes 30–40 minutes over wet rocks; at Ouzoud a stepped path leads down to the falls and back up. Your local guide sets the pace, and you can stop at the viewpoints instead.

Is this tour good with children?

Yes, it is the easiest way to see Morocco with children: one room, short days except day 5, rock pools and monkeys, and child seats on request. Tell us their ages and we adapt each day.

How do I book and pay?

  2. Pay a 20–30% deposit by bank transfer to confirm; the rest is paid on arrival in cash (EUR, USD or MAD) or by card.

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before the tour.
