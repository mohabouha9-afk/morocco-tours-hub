Overview

Fly to Errachidia and you are at the Merzouga dunes two hours later. Two nights by Erg Chebbi, one in a hotel and one in a camp, with a whole day in the desert, a nomad family, the Gnawa musicians of Khamlia and a camel ride into the dunes for sunset.

Then the road turns west to the Todra Gorge, where you sleep at the foot of the cliffs and walk the gorge in the quiet hours, and comes back to the airport by the oasis villages of Tinejdad and Goulmima, never on the same road twice. Your own car, your own driver-guide, every flight met at any hour, and no shop stops you didn’t ask for.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Errachidia airport → Ziz palm grove → Erfoud → Merzouga

    Your driver-guide meets you in the arrivals hall with your name on a sign, whatever time your flight lands; we follow your flight, so a delay is no problem. South along the palm groves of the Ziz Valley and through Erfoud, where the fossil workshops line the road, to Merzouga, about two hours. Dinner and a quiet night in a hotel at the edge of the dunes.

    • About 125 km, about 2 hours on the road
    • Night: Merzouga hotel
    • Dinner & breakfast
  2. 2
    Day 2

    A full day around Merzouga, sunset camel ride and the camp

    A day around Merzouga by 4×4 at your pace: tea with a nomad family in their tent, Gnawa music in the village of Khamlia, the black stony desert and, after a wet winter, the seasonal lake of Dayet Srij. In the late afternoon, leave your big bags in the car, take a small bag and ride a camel into the Erg Chebbi dunes for sunset. Dinner in the camp, music around the fire and a sky full of stars.

    • 4×4 tour around Merzouga
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Breakfast & dinner
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Merzouga → Rissani → Alnif → Tinghir → Todra Gorge

    Sunrise and sandboarding on the dunes, then breakfast and back by camel or 4×4. Through the market town of Rissani (busiest on Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday) and west along the foot of the Jebel Saghro to Alnif, known for its trilobite fossils, and the Tinghir palm grove. You sleep at the Todra Gorge, so you walk between its 300 m walls in the late afternoon, when the day visitors have gone.

    • About 220 km, about 4.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Todra Gorge
    • Breakfast & dinner
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Todra Gorge → Tinejdad → Goulmima → Errachidia

    An early walk in the gorge if you like, before the first cars arrive. Then east by the oasis road: in Tinejdad, Ksar El Khorbat, a fortified village from about 1860 that is still lived in, with a small museum of oasis life, and the old ksar town of Goulmima. We drop you at Errachidia airport in good time for your flight, or at your hotel.

    • About 160 km, about 3 hours on the road
    • Drop-off at Errachidia airport or hotel
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

Why sleep at the Todra Gorge instead of just stopping?

At midday the narrow part of the gorge fills with cars and day visitors. Sleeping there lets you walk it in the late afternoon and early morning, when it is quiet and the light on the cliffs is at its best.

How long is the walk in the Todra Gorge?

The classic walk through the narrow part is flat and short, about 30 minutes to an hour on the riverbed. If you like walking, ask for a longer hike up through the palm groves and the villages above the gorge.

Which flights go to Errachidia?

Royal Air Maroc flies from Casablanca and Rabat, Ryanair from Marrakech, and Transavia from Paris-Orly in season. Most flights land in the evening; we meet every flight, at any hour.

What if my flight lands late at night or is delayed?

No problem. Your driver-guide follows your flight and waits in the arrivals hall with your name on a sign, then drives you to your hotel in Merzouga. That is why the first night is in a hotel and the camp comes on day 2.

What is the desert camp like?

A private Berber tent with real beds in the Erg Chebbi dunes, with dinner and breakfast at the camp. All three camps in our table have a private bathroom in the tent; see the Accommodations section above.
