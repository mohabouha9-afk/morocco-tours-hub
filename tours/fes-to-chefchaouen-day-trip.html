Overview

The blue city of Chefchaouen sits in the Rif Mountains, about 3.5 hours from Fes. Its whitewashed alleys painted in every shade of blue are Morocco's most photographed medina. On this private day trip your driver-guide takes you there and back, with a guided walk through the highlights and free time to explore at your own pace.

No shopping stops unless you ask. Lunch is wherever you choose. The round trip is about 400 km and 10 hours door-to-door — a long day, but the only way to see Chefchaouen from Fes without staying overnight.

Programme

  1. 1
    08:00

    Pick-up in Fes

    Your driver-guide meets you at your hotel or riad (riad = nearest gate) at 08:00. We confirm the exact time on WhatsApp the day before.

  2. 2
    11:30

    Arrival in Chefchaouen

    The drive is about 200 km through the Rif foothills and the town of Ouazzane. You arrive around 11:30 and start the guided walk.

  3. 3
    11:45

    Guided walk through the medina

    About 2 hours with your guide: Plaza Uta el-Hammam, the Kasbah museum (optional entry), the Grand Mosque exterior, the souks, and the Ras El Maa waterfall where the river emerges from the mountain.

  4. 4
    14:00

    Free time for lunch and exploring

    Your guide suggests a few local places; you choose where to eat (around €10–15). Then wander the blue alleys, visit the Spanish Mosque viewpoint (15 min walk uphill), or shop for wool blankets and goat cheese.

  5. 5
    16:30

    Depart Chefchaouen

    Meet your driver at the agreed point and begin the return drive through the Rif.

  6. 6
    19:30

    Back in Fes

    Drop-off at your hotel, riad or the station. The day is long but you have seen the blue city without an overnight stay.

  • This is a suggestion: tell us your preferences

  • Summer: can be warm in the middle of the day; spring/autumn ideal

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the drive from Fes to Chefchaouen?

About 200 km, 3.5 hours each way on a good mountain road through the Rif foothills. The round trip is 7 hours of driving, so we leave at 08:00 and return around 19:30.

Is a day trip worth it with that much driving?

If you only have one day, yes: you get 4–5 hours in the blue city with a guide and free time. But it is a long day. With two days you can stay overnight and see the medina at sunrise and sunset, which are the best light for photos.

Can we stop at Volubilis on the way back?

Yes. Volubilis is about 1 hour off the direct route back to Fes. It adds ~2 hours and the Roman ruins are worth it. Add it in Make It Your Own when you book.

How much walking is there in the medina?

The guided walk is about 2 hours at a gentle pace, but Chefchaouen is on a hill with stairs and slopes. Wear comfortable shoes. The Spanish Mosque viewpoint is a 15-minute uphill walk from the main square.

Is there an entrance fee for the blue city?

No. The medina is free to enter. The Kasbah museum costs about 60 MAD if you want to go inside. The Grand Mosque exterior and the Ras El Maa waterfall are free.
