Tour Overview

Celebrate your honeymoon with an unforgettable 9-Day Morocco Honeymoon Tour from Marrakech, combining romantic riads, spectacular mountain scenery, traditional villages and the magic of the Sahara Desert.

This private honeymoon tour is designed for couples who want to discover Morocco at a relaxed pace without joining a group. From the colorful streets of Marrakech to the peaceful landscapes of the Atlas Mountains, the dramatic Dades Valley and the golden dunes of Merzouga, each day offers a different experience.

The highlight of your Morocco honeymoon is your time in the Sahara. Enjoy a private journey into the dunes by camel, watch the sunset together and spend a romantic night in a comfortable desert camp beneath the stars.

With private transportation, carefully selected accommodation and plenty of time for romantic moments, this Morocco honeymoon tour from Marrakech is a wonderful way to experience the country together.

"More than a trip... It's a story to tell!"

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Arrival in Marrakech: A Romantic Evening in the Red City

Welcome to Morocco and the beginning of your 9-day Morocco honeymoon tour from Marrakech. Your private driver will meet you at Marrakech airport or your hotel and take you to your riad.

After check-in and some time to relax, begin discovering the atmosphere of Marrakech together. Walk through the old medina, explore the colorful souks and enjoy the lively atmosphere around Jemaa el-Fna.

In the evening, enjoy a romantic Moroccan dinner and your first night in the Red City.

Marrakech combines traditional architecture, riads, gardens, souks and historic streets, making it a natural beginning for a romantic Morocco honeymoon.

~Arrival & city km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 2

Marrakech → High Atlas Mountains → Aït Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate

After breakfast, leave Marrakech and begin your private journey across the High Atlas Mountains.

Travel through mountain villages and stop at panoramic viewpoints to admire the changing scenery. Continue toward Aït Ben Haddou, one of Morocco's most famous historic ksour.

Explore its narrow streets and traditional earthen architecture together before continuing to Ouarzazate.

Enjoy a relaxed evening at your accommodation, surrounded by the landscapes of southern Morocco.

~≈200 km km Ouarzazate Breakfast
Day 3

Ouarzazate → Skoura → Rose Valley → Dades Valley

Today you continue through the beautiful landscapes of southern Morocco.

Visit the Skoura oasis, where palm trees and traditional kasbahs create a peaceful setting. Continue through the Valley of Roses toward the Dades Valley.

Along the way, your private driver can make stops at scenic viewpoints and traditional villages.

Arrive in Dades in the afternoon and enjoy some quiet time together. The valley's dramatic rock formations and winding roads provide a beautiful setting for a romantic evening.

~≈160 km km Dades Valley Breakfast & Dinner
Day 4

Dades → Todra Gorge → Erfoud → Merzouga

After breakfast, leave the Dades Valley and continue toward the Sahara.

Travel through the Todra region and stop to explore the impressive Todra Gorge. Continue through Erfoud and Rissani before arriving in Merzouga.

In the afternoon, begin your camel trek into the golden dunes. As the sun starts to set, enjoy the peaceful desert atmosphere together while the colors of the dunes change around you.

Arrive at your desert camp for a romantic dinner and an evening beneath the stars.

~≈260 km km Luxury Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & Dinner
Day 5

Merzouga: A Romantic Sahara Experience

Wake early and watch the sunrise together over the dunes.

After breakfast, enjoy a private 4x4 excursion around the Merzouga desert. Visit the Hassi Labied oasis, Khamlia village and a local nomadic family.

You can experience traditional Gnawa music in Khamlia and learn more about life in the desert. Depending on seasonal conditions, you can also visit Lake Dayet Srij, an interesting contrast to the surrounding dunes.

Return to your accommodation and enjoy a relaxed evening together.

~≈40 km km Merzouga Breakfast
Day 6

Merzouga → Rissani → Ziz Valley → Erfoud

Leave Merzouga after breakfast and continue through the landscapes of the Tafilalet region.

Visit Rissani, an important historic town in the region, before continuing toward Erfoud.

Travel through the Ziz Valley, stopping at panoramic viewpoints where the green palm oasis contrasts beautifully with the surrounding mountains.

This is a slower day designed to give you time to enjoy the scenery and each other's company after your Sahara adventure. Arrive at your accommodation in the afternoon and enjoy a relaxing evening.

~≈120 km km Erfoud / Ziz Valley Breakfast
Day 7

Erfoud → Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou Area

Continue your honeymoon journey toward Ouarzazate through the changing landscapes of southern Morocco.

Travel through mountain valleys, palm oases and traditional villages, with stops along the way for photographs and refreshments.

Return toward the Aït Ben Haddou area and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of southern Morocco.

The evening can be reserved for a romantic dinner and relaxation at your accommodation.

~≈300 km km Aït Ben Haddou / Ouarzazate Breakfast
Day 8

Ouarzazate → High Atlas Mountains → Marrakech

After breakfast, begin your return journey to Marrakech.

Cross the High Atlas Mountains and enjoy the spectacular mountain scenery along the way. Since this is a private honeymoon tour, you can stop at viewpoints whenever you wish.

Arrive in Marrakech in the afternoon and enjoy your final evening together.

Spend some time in the medina, enjoy a romantic dinner or relax at your riad.

~≈200 km km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 9

Marrakech: Departure

Enjoy a relaxed final morning in Marrakech.

Depending on your departure time, you can take a final walk through the medina, enjoy breakfast at your riad or do some last-minute shopping in the souks.

Your private driver will then transfer you to Marrakech airport or your preferred departure point.

Your 9-Day Morocco Honeymoon Tour from Marrakech ends with unforgettable memories of Marrakech, the Atlas Mountains and the Sahara Desert.

~Departure transfer km End of tour Breakfast

Why Choose This Tour?

Our Morocco honeymoon tour from Marrakech is completely private and designed around the two of you.

Instead of following a fixed group schedule, you can enjoy the journey at a comfortable pace, with time for romantic experiences, beautiful scenery and relaxing evenings.

The route combines Marrakech, the High Atlas, Aït Ben Haddou, Dades, Todra Gorge and Merzouga, giving you a varied honeymoon experience that includes culture, nature, adventure and the Sahara.

Most importantly, the itinerary gives you time together rather than trying to fit too many destinations into nine days.
