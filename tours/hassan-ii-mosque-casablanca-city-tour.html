Overview

Step inside the Hassan II Mosque, the great mosque on the Atlantic with a 210 m minaret and room for 25,000 worshippers, then see the city around it: the old medina, the white Art Deco downtown, the Habous quarter and the ocean road.

Visits inside run only at set times, so we plan your morning around one. Your guide explains the mosque before you go in, your car waits at each stop, and there are no shop stops. Ideal on arrival day, before a tour or on a long layover.

Highlights

  • Hassan II Mosque: carved cedar, marble and a roof that opens to the sky
  • The esplanade: the minaret above the waves
  • Old medina: quiet lanes and the Sqala sea bastion
  • Downtown: Art Deco façades around Place Mohammed V
  • Habous quarter: arcades, olive sellers and almond pastries
  • The Corniche: the ocean road to Aïn Diab
  • And much more along the way

Programme

  1. 1
    08:30

    Pick-up at your hotel

    Your driver and guide meet you at your hotel, the port or the airport. We confirm the time on WhatsApp the day before.

  2. 2
    09:00

    Inside the Hassan II Mosque

    A mosque guide leads the visit of the prayer hall and the ablution hall below, about 45–60 minutes; your guide gets your tickets and explains the story first.

  3. 3
    10:15

    The esplanade and the ocean

    Walk the huge square and the sea wall around the minaret, with time for photos, before the car takes you to the old town.

  4. 4
    10:45

    Old medina and the Sqala

    A short walk through the lanes of the old medina, past Bab Marrakech, to the cannons of the 18th-century Sqala bastion.

  5. 5
    11:30

    Art Deco downtown

    Place Mohammed V and its white 1920s–30s buildings, the Central Market and the Sacré-Cœur cathedral, mostly from the car with short stops.

  6. 6
    12:15

    Habous quarter, then lunch

    Arcaded lanes built in the 1930s, olive and spice sellers, almond pastries, the gates of the Royal Palace; then the Corniche for lunch or back to your hotel around 13:30.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who guides us inside the mosque?

Visitors can only go inside with a guide from the mosque itself, in English, French, Spanish or German. Your own guide buys the tickets, tells you the story before you go in and waits for you at the exit.

Can we visit on a Friday?

Yes, but Friday has fewer visit times (09:00, 10:00 and 15:00), and during Ramadan the times change. Tell us your date and we book the morning around the next visit.

Can we do it on a layover?

Yes, with about 7 hours or more between flights. The airport is about 35 km from the city (45–60 minutes); we pick you up at arrivals, plan the mosque and bring you back in time for check-in.

Is Casablanca worth a day?

Half a day is right for most travellers: the mosque is the star, and the medina, Art Deco downtown and the Habous fill the rest of the morning. If you have more time, Rabat is an hour away.

Will we stop at shops?

No. The Habous has olive sellers and pastry shops, and you can buy if you wish; nobody takes you to a shop you did not ask for.
