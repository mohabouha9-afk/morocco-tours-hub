Tour Overview

This 3-day Morocco desert tour from Agadir to Merzouga and Marrakech is a great option if you are staying on the Atlantic coast but want to experience the Sahara without returning to Agadir.

The journey takes you through some of southern Morocco's most beautiful landscapes, including the Anti-Atlas, Taliouine, Ouarzazate, Aït Ben Haddou, Dades Valley and Todra Gorge before reaching the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi in Merzouga.

The highlight comes in the desert, where you will ride a camel through the dunes around sunset and spend the night in a Sahara desert camp. The next morning, you will watch the sunrise before continuing your journey toward Marrakech.

This is a private tour, so you can enjoy the route with your own driver and stop along the way whenever you want for photographs, coffee or a short break.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Agadir → Taroudant → Taliouine → Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → Dades Valley

Your journey starts early in the morning with pickup from your hotel or riad in Agadir. Leaving the Atlantic coast behind, you will drive through the Souss Valley toward Taroudant, a charming walled town often known for its traditional souks and relaxed atmosphere.

From Taroudant, the road continues toward Taliouine, an area famous for saffron and surrounded by the landscapes of the Anti-Atlas. As you continue east, the scenery becomes increasingly dry and mountainous.

The journey then takes you toward Ouarzazate and the famous Aït Ben Haddou. You will have time to walk through this historic fortified village and explore its narrow paths and traditional earthen buildings.

After the visit, continue through the Valley of Roses toward the Dades Valley, where you will spend the night.

~470 km Dades Valley Dinner
Day 2

Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Erfoud → Rissani → Merzouga

After breakfast, leave the Dades Valley and continue toward the Sahara. The road passes through dramatic landscapes and small Berber villages before reaching the Todra Gorge.

You will have time to walk through the gorge and admire its impressive cliffs before continuing toward Tinghir and Erfoud. The scenery gradually changes as you move farther into the desert region.

You will then pass through Rissani, an important town in the Tafilalet region, before reaching Merzouga and the Erg Chebbi dunes.

In the afternoon, leave the vehicle and begin your camel ride into the desert. As you cross the golden dunes, the landscape becomes quieter and the colors begin to change with the setting sun.

After reaching the camp, enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner and spend the evening around the campfire listening to local music. Then relax under the stars in the peaceful Sahara.

~300 km Sahara desert camp, Merzouga Breakfast & Dinner
Day 3

Merzouga → Sahara Sunrise → Rissani → Ouarzazate → Marrakech

Wake up early to experience sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes. Watching the first light reach the sand is one of the most memorable moments of the trip. After breakfast, leave the camp by camel or 4x4 and meet your driver in Merzouga.

Today you will begin the long journey toward Marrakech. The route takes you through Rissani and the landscapes of southern Morocco before continuing toward Ouarzazate.

There will be opportunities to stop along the way for photographs, lunch and short breaks. As you approach Marrakech, the landscape changes again, with the road eventually leading toward the High Atlas Mountains and the busy streets of the Red City.

You will arrive in Marrakech in the evening and be dropped off at your hotel or riad.

~560 km End of tour: Marrakech Breakfast

FAQ

Where does this tour start and end?

It starts with pickup from your hotel or riad in Agadir and ends with drop-off in Marrakech, so it is ideal if you are staying on the Atlantic coast but want to see the Sahara without returning to Agadir.

Is this a private tour?

Yes. You travel with your own driver in a private air-conditioned vehicle and can stop along the way for photographs, coffee or a short break whenever you like.

Is the camel ride and desert camp included?

Yes. A sunset camel ride into the Erg Chebbi dunes and one night in a Sahara desert camp with dinner and music are included, along with the sunrise the next morning.

How long are the driving days?

This is a fast-paced route with some long drives, especially on Day 1 from Agadir and Day 3 to Marrakech. If you would prefer a more relaxed pace, we can extend it to 4 or 5 days.

What should I bring for the desert?

Bring a light jacket for cool desert evenings, comfortable shoes, sunglasses, sunscreen and a scarf for the dunes. Nights at the camp can be chilly even after warm days.
