Overview

From Tangier, Chefchaouen is only about 2 to 2.5 hours away, past Tetouan and up into the green Rif Mountains. That short drive is what makes this day work: you get around four hours in the blue medina, with a local guide for the highlights and free time for lunch and photos, and you are back in Tangier for dinner.

It suits travellers who arrive by ferry from Spain, by cruise ship or by train and want to see Chefchaouen without changing hotel. Your driver stays with you all day, and there are no shopping stops unless you ask.

Programme

  1. 1
    08:30

    Pick-up in Tangier

    Your driver-guide meets you at your hotel, at the nearest gate to your riad, or at the Tangier Ville port exit with a name sign. We confirm the time on WhatsApp the day before.

  2. 2
    10:45

    Through the Rif to Chefchaouen

    About 120 km past Tetouan and into the Rif Mountains; the last hour is a winding mountain road. A coffee stop on the way if you like.

  3. 3
    11:00

    Guided walk through the medina

    About 2 hours with a local guide: Plaza Uta el-Hammam, the Kasbah (museum entry optional), the souks and the Ras El Maa spring.

  4. 4
    13:00

    Lunch and free time

    Your guide suggests a few places; you choose where to eat (around €10–15). Then explore on your own or walk up to the Spanish Mosque viewpoint, about 20 minutes uphill.

  5. 5
    16:00

    Leave Chefchaouen

    Meet your driver at the agreed gate and drive back down through the Rif towards the coast.

  6. 6
    18:30

    Back in Tangier

    Drop-off at your hotel, riad or the port, or at a restaurant if you go out for dinner. Cruise guests: the return is planned around your all-aboard time.

Good to know

  • This is a suggestion: tell us your preferences
  • About 10 hours door-to-door, 4.5 to 5 hours of it driving
  • The medina is a hillside of lanes and stairs: wear comfortable shoes
  • Summer middays are hot with little shade: bring water and a hat
  • Winter in the Rif can be cold and wet: bring a jacket
  • No shopping stops unless you ask

Costs on the day

  • Kasbah museum: about 60 MAD per adult (optional)
  • All other sites are free: the blue medina, Uta el-Hammam square, Ras El Maa and the Spanish Mosque viewpoint
  • Lunch in Chefchaouen: around €10–15 per person, depending on what you eat
  • Public toilets: a few dirhams, so keep some coins
  • Tips: up to you
  • Cash: bring some dirhams; small payments are easier in cash

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Chefchaouen worth a day trip from Tangier?

Yes, Tangier is the easiest place to do it from. The drive is about 2 to 2.5 hours, so you get around 4 hours in town: enough for the main sights, photos and lunch without rushing. If you want the quiet early morning and the evening light, stay a night instead.

I’m arriving by cruise ship or ferry. Will I be back in time?

Tell us your all-aboard or ferry time when you book. We meet you at the Tangier Ville port exit with a name sign, shorten the free time if needed, and plan to have you back at the port with time to spare. From Tanger Med the drive is about the same length.

Is it OK for children or older travellers?

Yes, at an easy pace. The town is on a hillside with stairs, so the guide can keep to the flatter lower medina and stop often. Strollers don’t work in the lanes; bring a baby carrier for small children.

Can you pick us up in Tetouan or Asilah?

Yes. From Tetouan the drive is only about 1 hour 15 minutes each way. From Asilah, add about 45 minutes each way. Tell us where you are staying when you book.

  2. Pay a small deposit to confirm; the rest is paid on the day, in cash or by card.
