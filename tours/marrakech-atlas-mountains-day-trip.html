Overview

Imlil is the mountain village at the foot of Toubkal, North Africa’s highest peak, about 1,740 m up and 1.5 hours from Marrakech. Walnut groves, terraced fields and earth-red Berber villages climb the valley, with snow on the summits from winter into spring.

The walk is made for you, from an easy hour in the valley to a longer climb to Aremd. Your local professional guide walks with you, the day stays in the mountains with no detours, and lunch is on a terrace facing the peaks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do we need a guide in Imlil?

On the trail towards Toubkal a local guide is required, with checkpoints from Aremd upwards, and men at the Imlil car park offer their services to every visitor. Your local professional guide is already in the price, so there is nothing to arrange or pay on the spot.

How hard is the walk?

You choose. The easy option is about an hour on wide mule paths; the walk to Aremd takes 2–3 hours with some steady uphill, but no climbing. Bad knees or small children? We keep it short, or drive you to the viewpoints instead.

Is Imlil worth it if we are not big walkers?

Yes, if you enjoy views and a slow lunch: the village itself is small, but the valley, a short walk and a terrace facing the peaks make a lovely day. If you would rather not walk at all, the Ourika Valley, with lunch by the river, suits you better.

Is it OK to visit after the 2023 earthquake?

Yes. The road, the trails and the guesthouses are open, and most homes have been rebuilt, though you will still see some building work. Visiting helps: the valley lives from walkers and guests.

What is the weather like up there?

Imlil is about 8–10 °C cooler than Marrakech. From December to March days are around 8–14 °C with snow on the peaks; spring and autumn are 15–22 °C; summer stays near 24–30 °C while the city passes 35 °C.

How do I book, and what if it snows?

  1. Send your request with the form or on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay a small deposit to confirm; the rest is paid on the day, in cash or by card.

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before. If snow or heavy rain closes the paths, we shorten the walk, or you choose a new date or a refund.
