Overview

Five days across the north of Morocco, starting where Africa faces Europe. Tangier’s Kasbah and Cap Spartel on the first day, then up into the Rif Mountains to Chefchaouen, the blue town, with a night there for the sunset and the quiet early lanes.

South by the Roman ruins of Volubilis, holy Moulay Idriss and the imperial city of Meknes to Fes, with two nights and a full guided day in its old medina. We meet you off the plane, train or ferry, and leave you at Fes airport or the station at the end.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Tangier: arrival and the city

    Your driver-guide meets you at Tangier airport, the train station, the ferry port or your hotel. Then the Kasbah and its view over the Strait of Gibraltar, the medina, the Grand and Petit Socco, and out to Cap Spartel, where the Atlantic meets the Mediterranean, and the Caves of Hercules.

    • About 60 km in and around Tangier
    • Night: Tangier
    • Breakfast from day 2
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Tangier → the Rif Mountains → Chefchaouen

    Through the green hills of the Rif to Chefchaouen, the blue town. The afternoon in the blue lanes and the Outa el-Hammam square under the old kasbah, then the walk up to the Spanish Mosque for the sunset over the town and the mountains.

    • About 125 km, about 2.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Chefchaouen
    • Breakfast
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Moulay Idriss → Meknes → Fes

    An early walk in the quiet blue streets, then south to Volubilis, the Roman city with its columns and floor mosaics, the holy hill town of Moulay Idriss, and Meknes for the great Bab Mansour gate and the old royal quarter. Fes by late afternoon.

    • About 260 km, about 4.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Fes: a guided day in the medina

    With a local Fes guide in Fes el-Bali, one of the largest car-free medieval towns in the world: the Bou Inania Madrasa, the Al Quaraouiyine quarter, the Nejjarine fountain, the Chouara tanneries from a rooftop, then the Mellah and a viewpoint over the old city. No shop stops unless you ask.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  5. 5
    Day 5

    Fes: departure

    A free morning in Fes if your flight or train is later, then your driver-guide takes you to Fes-Saïss airport, the train station or anywhere in the city.

    • Riad to Fes airport, about 30 minutes
    • Ends in Fes: airport, station or hotel
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you meet us off the ferry from Spain?

Yes. Ferries from Tarifa arrive at the Tangier city port, a few minutes from the medina; ferries from Algeciras arrive at Tanger Med, about an hour east. Your driver-guide waits at the exit with your name on a sign; tell us your crossing when you book.

Is one night in Chefchaouen enough?

For most travellers, yes: you arrive early afternoon, see the sunset from the Spanish Mosque and walk the empty lanes the next morning. If you love walking, add a second night for the Akchour waterfalls in the Rif.

Will the Fes guide take us to shops?

No shop stops unless you ask. The tanneries are seen from the terrace of a leather shop, which is free; the staff will show you their goods, but you never have to buy. Say no thanks and walk on.

How do we leave Fes at the end?

Fes-Saïss airport is about 30 minutes from the medina, and trains run from Fes to Rabat, Casablanca and Tangier. We drop you wherever you need to be, at the time of your flight or train.

How long are the drives?

Short: about 2.5 hours from Tangier to Chefchaouen and about 4.5 hours of driving on day 3, split by Volubilis, Moulay Idriss and Meknes. Day 1 stays around Tangier and day 4 is on foot in Fes.
