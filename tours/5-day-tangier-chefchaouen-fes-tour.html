Tour Overview

Discover the fascinating north of Morocco on this 5-day private Tangier, Chefchaouen and Fes tour. From the cosmopolitan atmosphere of Tangier to the blue-painted streets of Chefchaouen and the historic treasures of Fes, this journey offers a perfect introduction to Morocco's culture, history and landscapes.

Explore Tangier's historic medina and scenic coastline, wander through the enchanting blue streets of Chefchaouen, and visit the ancient Roman ruins of Volubilis. Along the way, discover the imperial city of Meknes and the holy town of Moulay Idriss before arriving in Fes, Morocco's cultural and spiritual capital.

With private transportation, flexible sightseeing and carefully selected accommodation, this tour allows you to experience northern Morocco at a comfortable pace.

"More than a trip... It's a story to tell!"

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Tangier: Arrival & City Tour

Your 5-day Tangier, Chefchaouen and Fes tour begins with a private pick-up from Tangier Ibn Battouta Airport, Tangier train station, ferry port or your hotel.

Start exploring Tangier, a fascinating city located where the Atlantic Ocean meets the Mediterranean Sea. Discover the historic Kasbah and wander through the old medina, where narrow streets are filled with traditional shops, cafés and local life.

Visit the Kasbah Museum area and enjoy panoramic views across the Strait of Gibraltar. Continue to the Grand Socco and Petit Socco before exploring the city's modern districts.

You can also visit Cap Spartel, where the Atlantic and Mediterranean meet, and the nearby Hercules Caves.

In the late afternoon, return to your accommodation and enjoy a relaxing evening in Tangier.

~60 km Tangier Breakfast
Day 2

Tangier → Chefchaouen

After breakfast, leave Tangier and travel through the beautiful Rif Mountains towards Chefchaouen, one of Morocco's most charming destinations.

Known as the Blue Pearl of Morocco, Chefchaouen is famous for its distinctive blue-painted buildings, peaceful atmosphere and spectacular mountain setting.

After arriving, enjoy a guided or self-guided walk through the old medina. Explore the narrow blue streets, traditional houses, small artisan workshops and colorful markets. Visit Plaza Uta el-Hammam, the heart of the medina, and admire the historic Kasbah from outside or visit its gardens and museum.

For panoramic views of Chefchaouen, walk towards the Spanish Mosque, especially beautiful around sunset.

Spend the evening enjoying Moroccan cuisine at a local restaurant or relaxing at your traditional riad.

~120 km Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 3

Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Moulay Idriss → Meknes → Fes

After breakfast, continue your journey towards Fes, with several fascinating historical stops along the way.

Your first stop is Volubilis, Morocco's best-preserved Roman archaeological site and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Explore the ancient ruins and admire the remarkably preserved mosaics, columns and remains of Roman buildings.

Continue to the nearby holy town of Moulay Idriss, an important pilgrimage destination and one of Morocco's most atmospheric hilltop towns.

Afterwards, travel to Meknes, one of Morocco's four imperial cities. Discover highlights including Bab el-Mansour, one of the country's most impressive monumental gates, and the historic medina and old royal quarter.

Continue to Fes in the late afternoon. After checking into your riad, enjoy a relaxing evening and prepare for the following day's exploration of the city's fascinating medina.

~240 km Fes Breakfast
Day 4

Fes: Full-Day Guided City Tour

Today is dedicated entirely to exploring Fes, the cultural and spiritual heart of Morocco.

After breakfast, meet your local licensed guide and begin your walking tour of the historic Fes el-Bali medina, one of the world's largest car-free urban areas.

Walk through its maze of narrow streets and discover traditional souks, artisan workshops, historic mosques and beautiful riads. Visit important landmarks such as the Bou Inania Madrasa, Al Quaraouiyine area, Nejjarine Fountain and the famous Chouara Tanneries.

Your guide will introduce you to the city's fascinating history, architecture and traditions while helping you discover places that can easily be missed when exploring independently.

In the afternoon, visit the Mellah, the former Jewish quarter, and enjoy panoramic views over the ancient medina from one of the city's viewpoints. Return to your riad in the evening.

Fes Breakfast
Day 5

Fes: Departure

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast at your riad before your private driver transfers you to Fes-Saïss Airport, the train station or another departure point according to your travel plans.

If your departure is later in the day, you can enjoy some free time in Fes for shopping or exploring the city at your own pace.

This is the perfect opportunity to purchase Moroccan handicrafts, traditional ceramics, leather goods, spices and souvenirs from your journey.

Your 5-day Tangier, Chefchaouen and Fes tour comes to an end with a private transfer to your chosen departure point.

End of tour: Fes Breakfast
