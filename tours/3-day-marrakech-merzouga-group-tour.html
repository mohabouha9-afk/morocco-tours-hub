Tour Overview

Experience the highlights of southern Morocco on our 3-day group tour from Marrakech to Merzouga. This affordable Sahara Desert adventure takes you across the High Atlas Mountains, through dramatic valleys and ancient kasbahs, and finally to the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi.

Travel with other visitors in a shared, air-conditioned vehicle and enjoy some of Morocco's most famous landscapes along the way. Visit the spectacular Aït Ben Haddou, pass through Ouarzazate and the Valley of Roses, explore the Dades and Todra Gorges, and continue towards Merzouga.

The highlight of the trip is your arrival at the Sahara Desert, where you will ride camels across the Erg Chebbi dunes around sunset and spend the night at a desert camp. The following morning, watch the sunrise before beginning the journey back to Marrakech. This is one of the most popular ways to experience the Sahara when you have limited time in Morocco.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Marrakech → High Atlas → Aït Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate → Dades Valley

Your 3-day Marrakech to Merzouga group tour begins with an early morning pickup from your Marrakech hotel or riad. Travel towards the High Atlas Mountains and cross the spectacular Tizi n'Tichka Pass, enjoying panoramic views of Berber villages and mountain landscapes. Continue to the famous Aït Ben Haddou, a historic fortified ksar and one of southern Morocco's most impressive sights.

After exploring the kasbah, continue to Ouarzazate, known as the gateway to the desert. The journey continues through the Valley of Roses and the Valley of a Thousand Kasbahs before reaching the Dades Valley. In the evening, check into your hotel or traditional kasbah and enjoy dinner. This first day combines mountains, historic architecture and dramatic southern landscapes.

~360 km Dades Valley Dinner
Day 2

Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Erfoud → Rissani → Merzouga Sahara Desert

After breakfast, continue your journey towards the Sahara. Drive through the Dades Valley and towards Tinghir, where you will stop to explore the impressive Todra Gorge. Take a walk between the towering canyon walls before continuing through the palm-filled oasis and towards Erfoud, famous for fossils and traditional stone craftsmanship.

Continue to Rissani before reaching Merzouga and the spectacular Erg Chebbi dunes. In the late afternoon, leave the vehicle and begin your camel trek into the Sahara. Ride through the golden dunes as the sun begins to set, arriving at your desert camp in time for dinner. After dinner, enjoy traditional Berber music around the campfire and spend the night beneath the stars.

~300 km Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & Dinner
Day 3

Merzouga → Sahara Sunrise → Draa Valley → Ouarzazate → Marrakech

Wake early to enjoy the sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes. After breakfast at the camp, return to Merzouga by camel or 4×4 and begin the long journey back to Marrakech. The return route takes you through the pre-Sahara landscapes and the beautiful Draa Valley, with its extensive palm oases and traditional kasbahs.

Continue towards Ouarzazate before crossing the High Atlas Mountains once again. There will be stops along the way for photographs, refreshments and breaks. Because this is a long driving day, you should expect to arrive back in Marrakech in the evening. The route is demanding, but it allows travelers with only three days to experience the Sahara and return to Marrakech.

~560 km End of tour: Marrakech Breakfast

Why Choose This Tour?

Our 3-day Marrakech to Merzouga group tour is ideal for travelers who want to experience the Sahara without the higher cost of a private tour.

By sharing transportation with other travelers, you can reduce the cost while still visiting the major highlights of southern Morocco. It is also a great option for solo travelers who want to meet people from different countries during their Moroccan adventure.

The itinerary follows the classic Sahara route through the High Atlas, Aït Ben Haddou, Dades Valley, Todra Gorge and Merzouga.

If you prefer more flexibility, a private tour is also available, allowing you to choose your departure time, stops and accommodation.

FAQ

Is this a shared group tour?

Yes. You will travel with other travelers in a shared air-conditioned vehicle. Group size depends on the departure and vehicle available.

Where does the tour start and finish?

The tour starts and finishes in Marrakech.

Is camel trekking included?

Yes. A camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes is included, normally around sunset.

Where do we sleep?

The first night is in a hotel or traditional accommodation in the Dades Valley. The second night is at a Sahara Desert Camp in Merzouga.

Is the desert camp private?

For the group tour, the desert experience is shared with other travelers. Private tents can depend on the camp and accommodation category selected.

Is the 3-day tour suitable for everyone?

The tour is suitable for most travelers, but it is important to understand that it involves long driving days, especially the return to Marrakech on Day 3. A 4-day tour is recommended for travelers who prefer a more relaxed pace.

Can I book this tour if I am traveling alone?

Yes. Solo travelers are welcome and can join the shared group.

Can I upgrade to a private tour?

Yes. If you prefer your own vehicle and a more flexible itinerary, we can arrange a private Marrakech to Merzouga tour instead.
