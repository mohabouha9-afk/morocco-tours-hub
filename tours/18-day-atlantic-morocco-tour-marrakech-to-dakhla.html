Tour Overview

Experience the spectacular Atlantic side of Morocco on this 18-Day Atlantic Morocco Tour from Marrakech to Dakhla. This unique private journey combines the vibrant atmosphere of Marrakech with charming coastal towns, surfing destinations, dramatic beaches, fishing communities and the remote landscapes of Morocco's deep south.

Begin with two nights in Marrakech before traveling to the relaxed Atlantic town of Essaouira, where you will spend two nights discovering its medina, harbor and beaches. Continue south through Taghazout and Agadir before reaching the spectacular cliffs and beach of Legzira.

The journey continues with two nights in peaceful Mirleft and two nights in Tan-Tan before traveling to Laayoune. From Laayoune, drive directly to Dakhla in one day, following the remote Atlantic desert road.

The tour ends with five nights in Dakhla, giving you plenty of time to discover the famous lagoon, beaches, desert landscapes and optional water activities without rushing.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Arrival in Marrakech

Welcome to Morocco! Your Atlantic adventure begins in Marrakech, one of the country's most famous and vibrant cities. Upon arrival at Marrakech Menara Airport, your private driver will meet you and transfer you comfortably to your riad or hotel.

Depending on your arrival time, enjoy a relaxed introduction to the city. You can take a gentle walk through the historic medina, discover colorful streets and admire traditional Moroccan architecture.

In the evening, visit the famous Jemaa el-Fna square, the heart of Marrakech. As the sun goes down, the square becomes filled with food stalls, local performers, musicians and the sounds of everyday Moroccan life.

Enjoy your first Moroccan dinner and take some time to relax after your journey. Your first night is intentionally easy-going, giving you time to settle into Morocco before beginning your Atlantic road trip.

Marrakech Breakfast (on arrival)
Day 2

Marrakech Guided City Tour

After breakfast, discover the highlights of Marrakech with a local licensed guide. Begin in the historic medina and explore the city's fascinating cultural heritage.

Visit the impressive Koutoubia Mosque, the beautiful Bahia Palace and the Saadian Tombs. Continue through the colorful souks, where local artisans sell carpets, leather goods, ceramics, spices, lamps, textiles and traditional Moroccan handicrafts.

Your guide will introduce you to the history, architecture and traditions of the Red City while helping you discover some of the quieter corners of the medina. Depending on your interests, you can also visit the Majorelle Garden or other cultural attractions.

The afternoon is free for you to enjoy Marrakech at your own pace. Relax at your riad, continue shopping or enjoy Moroccan tea in a traditional café.

In the evening, return to Jemaa el-Fna or enjoy dinner at a Moroccan restaurant.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 3

Marrakech → Essaouira

After breakfast, leave Marrakech and travel west toward the Atlantic Ocean. The journey takes you through beautiful Moroccan countryside and the famous argan tree region.

Along the way, you may have the opportunity to see goats climbing argan trees, a distinctive sight of this part of Morocco.

Continue to Essaouira, one of Morocco's most charming Atlantic destinations. After checking into your riad, begin exploring the historic medina and its narrow streets.

Visit the fishing harbor, where traditional blue boats contrast beautifully with the Atlantic Ocean. Walk along the old ramparts and discover artisan workshops selling woodwork, jewelry, textiles and handmade crafts.

Later, enjoy a walk along the sandy beach before returning to the medina. Essaouira's relaxed atmosphere makes it the perfect first coastal stop after Marrakech.

~190 km Essaouira Breakfast
Day 4

Essaouira: Full Coastal Experience

Enjoy a second day in Essaouira without having to pack your bags. After breakfast, continue discovering this atmospheric coastal town at your own pace.

Explore the medina's colorful streets, visit the historic Skala and walk through the fishing port. Essaouira is well known for its seafood, so this is an excellent opportunity to enjoy a fresh fish lunch overlooking the harbor or ocean.

The beach also offers opportunities for surfing and other water activities, depending on the season and weather conditions.

You can spend the afternoon relaxing on the beach, visiting local galleries and artisan workshops or enjoying Moroccan tea in one of the town's cafés.

As the afternoon turns to evening, take a final walk along the Atlantic waterfront and enjoy the sunset. Essaouira offers a peaceful and authentic coastal experience before you continue south toward Morocco's surfing destinations.

Essaouira Breakfast
Day 5

Essaouira → Taghazout

After breakfast, continue south along the Atlantic coast toward Taghazout, a small fishing and surfing village famous for its relaxed atmosphere and beautiful coastline.

The journey takes you through coastal landscapes and the argan-growing region. Upon arrival, check into your accommodation and explore the village on foot.

Taghazout has become internationally known for surfing, with several beaches and surf breaks attracting visitors from around the world. Depending on sea conditions, you can arrange a surfing lesson or simply enjoy watching surfers along the coast.

Take a walk through the village and discover small cafés and restaurants serving fresh seafood and Moroccan dishes.

In the evening, find a peaceful viewpoint along the coast and watch the sun set over the Atlantic. Taghazout is a perfect place to slow down and experience Morocco's laid-back coastal lifestyle.

~170 km Taghazout Breakfast
Day 6

Taghazout → Agadir

After breakfast, leave Taghazout and continue south toward Agadir. The drive is relatively short, allowing plenty of time to explore the city.

Agadir is known for its broad sandy beach, modern waterfront and relaxed Atlantic atmosphere. Visit the Agadir Oufella area for panoramic views over the city, harbor and coastline.

Continue along the Corniche and enjoy a walk beside the beach. You can also explore local markets and discover Moroccan handicrafts and products.

Agadir provides a contrast to the traditional medinas of Marrakech and Essaouira. Its modern character, wide streets and long beachfront make it an ideal place for a relaxing stop.

Spend the afternoon at your hotel, explore the waterfront or enjoy some time on the beach. In the evening, enjoy fresh seafood or traditional Moroccan cuisine before continuing south along the Atlantic coast.

~20 km Agadir Breakfast
Day 7

Agadir → Sidi Ifni → Legzira

After breakfast, continue south toward the spectacular southern Atlantic coastline. Travel through Sidi Ifni, a charming coastal town with distinctive architecture and a relaxed atmosphere.

Continue toward Legzira, one of the most spectacular beaches in southern Morocco. The area is famous for its dramatic red cliffs and enormous natural rock formations overlooking the Atlantic.

Upon arrival, spend time exploring the beach and walking beneath the cliffs. The landscape is particularly beautiful around sunset, when the warm light brings out the red and orange colors of the rock formations.

Legzira is an excellent destination for photographers and nature lovers. Take time to enjoy the sound of the waves and the peaceful atmosphere of this wild section of the Moroccan coast.

After sunset, return to your accommodation and enjoy a relaxed evening.

~160 km Legzira Breakfast
Day 8

Legzira → Mirleft

After breakfast, continue along the coast toward Mirleft, a peaceful destination surrounded by cliffs, beaches and small fishing communities.

The drive is relatively short, allowing you to stop at coastal viewpoints and beaches along the way.

Upon arrival in Mirleft, check into your accommodation and begin exploring the town and surrounding coastline. Mirleft is known for its relaxed atmosphere and beautiful beaches, making it an ideal destination for travelers who want to escape the busier tourist areas.

Depending on your interests, enjoy a coastal walk, surfing, photography or simply relax beside the Atlantic.

In the afternoon, discover local cafés and small shops before watching the sunset from one of the coastal viewpoints. Enjoy a peaceful dinner and prepare to spend another day discovering this beautiful part of southern Morocco.

~40 km Mirleft Breakfast
Day 9

Mirleft: Beaches & Coastal Exploration

Enjoy a second day around Mirleft. After breakfast, explore some of the beautiful beaches and coastal landscapes surrounding the town.

The day can be customized according to your interests. You can enjoy a beach walk, try surfing, discover nearby fishing areas or simply relax beside the Atlantic Ocean.

Mirleft's coastline is particularly attractive because of the combination of dramatic cliffs, sandy beaches and traditional rural landscapes.

Take time to enjoy a relaxed lunch featuring fresh seafood or Moroccan cuisine. The afternoon is free for photography and exploration. Travelers looking for relaxation can simply enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of the coast.

Having two nights in Mirleft allows you to experience the region without rushing and provides a welcome break from the longer driving days that follow. Enjoy your final evening in this charming coastal town.

Mirleft Breakfast
Day 10

Mirleft → Tan-Tan

After breakfast, begin your journey farther south toward Tan-Tan. As you leave the Mirleft region, the landscape gradually becomes drier and more remote.

Follow the Atlantic route through wide-open landscapes, small settlements and coastal areas. This section of the journey gives you a first real impression of southern Morocco and the transition between the Atlantic coast and the Sahara.

Upon arrival in Tan-Tan, check into your hotel and enjoy some time to relax.

Tan-Tan has strong connections to the traditional culture of southern Morocco and has historically been an important meeting point between coastal communities and Saharan regions.

Depending on your arrival time, explore the town or surrounding landscapes before enjoying dinner. The evening is free to relax and prepare for another day exploring the southern Atlantic region.

~130 km Tan-Tan Breakfast
Day 11

Tan-Tan: Southern Atlantic Exploration

Today, enjoy a second night in Tan-Tan, allowing you to explore the surrounding region without immediately continuing south.

After breakfast, discover local markets and the atmosphere of this southern Moroccan town. You can also explore nearby landscapes and coastal viewpoints depending on your interests.

As you travel farther south, Saharan influences become increasingly visible in local culture, food, clothing and landscapes.

Enjoy a relaxed lunch before continuing your exploration. The afternoon can be dedicated to photography, visiting local areas or simply relaxing at your hotel. This additional night helps break up the long journey toward Dakhla and gives you more time to appreciate the changing landscapes of southern Morocco.

In the evening, enjoy dinner and rest before tomorrow's journey toward Laayoune.

Tan-Tan Breakfast
Day 12

Tan-Tan → Laayoune

After breakfast, continue south along the Atlantic coast toward Laayoune, one of the major cities of Morocco's southern region.

The landscape becomes increasingly arid, with enormous open spaces, desert terrain and long stretches of Atlantic coastline.

The road provides an authentic experience of southern Morocco, far from the busy cities and traditional tourist routes. Your private driver can make stops along the way for photographs, refreshments and short breaks.

Arrive in Laayoune and check into your hotel. Depending on your arrival time, explore the city and discover local markets, cafés and streets. Laayoune provides an interesting introduction to the culture and atmosphere of Morocco's far south.

Enjoy dinner and relax at your hotel, as tomorrow you will undertake one of the longest and most spectacular drives of the journey: Laayoune to Dakhla.

~320 km Laayoune Breakfast
Day 13

Laayoune → Dakhla → Atlantic Desert Road

Today, continue directly from Laayoune to Dakhla in one day. Leave early after breakfast and follow the spectacular Atlantic desert road south.

The journey takes you through vast and remote landscapes where the Sahara meets the Atlantic Ocean. Long stretches of desert, rocky terrain and distant ocean views create an extraordinary sense of space.

Your private driver will make comfortable stops along the route for photographs, refreshments and short breaks.

As you travel farther south, the landscape becomes increasingly wild and remote. The road itself becomes part of the adventure, offering a unique opportunity to experience the scale of Morocco's Atlantic Sahara.

Arrive in Dakhla later in the day and check into your hotel or resort. After the long drive, enjoy a relaxing evening and your first views of the famous Dakhla Lagoon.

~530 km Dakhla Breakfast
Day 14

Dakhla: Lagoon & Atlantic Experience

After breakfast, begin exploring Dakhla Lagoon, one of the most remarkable natural landscapes of Morocco's southern Atlantic coast.

The lagoon is surrounded by desert, creating a spectacular contrast between golden sand and turquoise water. Spend the day discovering the coastline and enjoying the peaceful environment.

Depending on your interests and local conditions, you can participate in water activities, enjoy a boat excursion, go kayaking or simply relax beside the lagoon.

Dakhla is internationally recognized for kitesurfing because of its wind and lagoon conditions. Beginners and experienced travelers can arrange activities through local providers. For photographers, the region provides exceptional opportunities, especially during sunrise and sunset.

Return to your accommodation in the afternoon and enjoy a relaxed evening. With several nights in Dakhla, you can enjoy the destination without rushing from one activity to another.

~40 km Dakhla Breakfast
Day 15

Dakhla: Desert & Coastal Adventure

Today, explore more of the landscapes surrounding Dakhla. After breakfast, travel through the desert and along the Atlantic coast to discover remote beaches and spectacular viewpoints.

The combination of desert and ocean creates some of the most unusual scenery in Morocco. Depending on your interests, enjoy a private 4×4 excursion, visit a remote beach or take part in optional water activities.

You can also spend time around the lagoon and enjoy its peaceful atmosphere.

Adventure travelers may choose kitesurfing, paddleboarding, kayaking or other activities, subject to weather and local availability.

Return to your accommodation later in the afternoon and enjoy another spectacular southern sunset. The evening is free to relax and enjoy dinner.

~60 km Dakhla Breakfast
Day 16

Dakhla: Free Day

Today is a completely flexible day in Dakhla. After the long Atlantic road journey, enjoy a slower day and choose how you want to experience this remarkable destination.

Relax beside the lagoon, enjoy the beach or participate in an optional activity. You may choose surfing, kitesurfing, kayaking, paddleboarding or a boat excursion depending on conditions.

Alternatively, explore Dakhla town and discover local cafés, restaurants and shops.

Photography enthusiasts can spend the day capturing the unusual combination of desert, lagoon and Atlantic Ocean. This free day gives you the flexibility to make the most of Dakhla according to your own interests.

In the evening, enjoy a relaxed dinner and another peaceful sunset over the lagoon.

Dakhla Breakfast
Day 17

Dakhla: Lagoon, Oyster Farm & Relaxation

Enjoy another full day in Dakhla. After breakfast, explore more of the famous lagoon and its surrounding landscapes.

Depending on local availability, visit an oyster farm and discover how oysters are cultivated in the waters of the lagoon. This is an interesting way to learn about the local marine environment and regional economy.

Continue exploring the coastline or enjoy an optional boat excursion.

For travelers who prefer relaxation, simply spend the day beside the lagoon, enjoy the peaceful desert surroundings and take photographs. The afternoon remains flexible, allowing you to choose another activity or enjoy your accommodation.

As evening approaches, watch the sunset over the water and enjoy your final full evening in Dakhla. This extended stay gives you time to appreciate the destination rather than treating it as simply a stop on the way south.

~40 km Dakhla Breakfast
Day 18

Dakhla: Departure

After breakfast, your 18-Day Atlantic Morocco Tour from Marrakech to Dakhla comes to an end.

Depending on your flight schedule, enjoy some final free time in Dakhla before your private transfer to Dakhla Airport.

Your journey has taken you from the historic streets of Marrakech to the Atlantic atmosphere of Essaouira, the surfing village of Taghazout, Agadir, Legzira, Mirleft and Tan-Tan.

You have traveled through the increasingly remote landscapes of southern Morocco and followed the Atlantic desert road from Laayoune to Dakhla.

Your five-night stay in Dakhla has allowed you to experience the famous lagoon, beaches, desert landscapes and optional water activities at a relaxed pace. We hope you leave Morocco with beautiful memories of its Atlantic coast, southern landscapes, local culture and unique desert-ocean scenery.

End of tour: Dakhla Breakfast

Why Choose This Tour?

Our 18-Day Atlantic Morocco Tour from Marrakech to Dakhla is designed for travelers who want to discover the less-explored side of Morocco.

Rather than following a traditional Marrakech–Fes–Sahara itinerary, this journey follows the Atlantic coast deep into southern Morocco, combining historic cities, beaches, surfing villages, dramatic cliffs, fishing communities and vast desert landscapes.

The itinerary includes longer stays in Essaouira, Mirleft, Tan-Tan and Dakhla, allowing you to slow down and experience each destination instead of constantly changing hotels.

The five nights in Dakhla are particularly valuable, giving you enough time to enjoy the lagoon, beaches, desert scenery and optional water activities.

As a completely private and customizable tour, the journey can be adapted to your interests and preferred pace. Whether you are interested in surfing, photography, nature, adventure or simply discovering Morocco's Atlantic coast, this itinerary offers an exceptional way to experience the country.
