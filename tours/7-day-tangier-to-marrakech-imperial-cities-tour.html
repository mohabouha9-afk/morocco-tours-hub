Tour Overview

Experience the highlights of northern and central Morocco on this 7-Day Morocco Tour from Tangier to Marrakech. This carefully designed private itinerary connects the Mediterranean port city of Tangier with the blue streets of Chefchaouen, the historic medina of Fes, the imperial city of Meknes, Morocco's capital Rabat, the modern metropolis of Casablanca and the vibrant Red City of Marrakech.

The route combines Morocco's history, architecture, culture and everyday life in one week. Chefchaouen is known for its blue-painted medina and mountain setting, while Fes is renowned for its historic medina. Meknes, Rabat and Marrakech showcase different chapters of Morocco's imperial heritage.

Traveling by private vehicle also gives you flexibility to stop for photographs, local food and viewpoints along the way.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Tangier: Arrival & City Tour

Your 7-day Morocco tour from Tangier to Marrakech begins with a private pickup from Tangier airport, ferry terminal or your hotel.

Start exploring Tangier, a fascinating city overlooking the meeting point of the Mediterranean Sea and Atlantic Ocean. Visit the historic Kasbah, walk through the old Medina and explore the Grand Socco, one of the city's lively meeting points.

Continue toward Cape Spartel, where the Atlantic Ocean and Mediterranean Sea meet, and visit the nearby Caves of Hercules if time allows. Tangier's location has given it a unique blend of Moroccan, Mediterranean and European influences.

In the afternoon, enjoy free time to explore the Medina, shop for local handicrafts or relax at a traditional Moroccan café. Your first evening is free to enjoy Tangier at your own pace before returning to your accommodation.

Tangier Breakfast
Day 2

Tangier → Chefchaouen → Fes

After breakfast, depart Tangier and travel toward the Rif Mountains and the famous blue city of Chefchaouen.

Arrive in Chefchaouen and explore its charming Medina, where blue-painted buildings, narrow lanes, small squares and local craft shops create one of Morocco's most recognizable urban landscapes. Chefchaouen is built on a mountainside and is also known for walking routes in the surrounding Rif Mountains.

Take time to explore the Kasbah, Plaza Uta el-Hammam and the colorful streets surrounding the old town. You will have time for lunch in the Medina and opportunities to take photographs of the famous blue architecture.

In the afternoon, continue toward Fes. The journey takes you through changing landscapes as you leave the Rif Mountains behind. Arrive in Fes in the evening and check into your riad or hotel.

~310 km Fes Breakfast
Day 3

Full-Day Fes Sightseeing Tour

Today is dedicated to exploring Fes, Morocco's historic spiritual and cultural center.

With a local guide, begin your sightseeing tour in the old Medina of Fes, one of Morocco's most important historic urban areas. Walk through its narrow streets and discover traditional souks, artisan workshops, historic buildings and colorful markets.

Visit important landmarks such as the Bou Inania Madrasa, the Nejjarine area and the famous tanneries. You can also explore the Jewish Quarter, known as the Mellah, and enjoy panoramic views over the old city.

Fes is particularly famous for traditional craftsmanship, including leatherwork, pottery, zellige tilework and metalwork.

After lunch, continue exploring the Medina and its surrounding historic areas. Your guide can adapt the visit according to your interests, whether you prefer history, architecture, shopping, food or photography. Return to your riad in the evening and enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner.

Fes Breakfast
Day 4

Fes → Meknes → Volubilis → Rabat

After breakfast, leave Fes and travel toward Meknes, one of Morocco's four imperial cities.

Explore the historic center and see important landmarks including Bab Mansour, the monumental gateway associated with Sultan Moulay Ismail, and the old imperial area. Continue to the Heri es-Souani granaries and the Agdal Basin before enjoying some free time.

From Meknes, continue to the nearby archaeological site of Volubilis. Explore the impressive Roman ruins, including ancient columns, mosaics and remains of the old city. The site provides an interesting contrast to Morocco's Islamic and imperial heritage.

Continue toward Rabat, Morocco's modern capital. In the late afternoon, visit the Hassan Tower, the Mausoleum of Mohammed V and the Kasbah of the Oudayas.

The combination of Meknes, Volubilis and Rabat makes this one of the most historically rich days of the tour.

~250 km Rabat Breakfast
Day 5

Rabat → Casablanca → Marrakech

After breakfast, continue south toward Casablanca, Morocco's largest city and economic center.

Begin your visit with the spectacular Hassan II Mosque, located beside the Atlantic Ocean. Its impressive architecture and seaside setting make it one of the city's most recognizable landmarks.

Continue through central Casablanca and see Place Mohammed V, the Habous Quarter and other notable areas of the city. You will have time for lunch before continuing toward Marrakech.

The road south takes you away from Morocco's Atlantic coast and toward the interior. As you approach Marrakech, the landscape gradually changes and the distinctive colors of the Red City begin to appear.

Arrive in Marrakech in the late afternoon or evening and check into your riad or hotel. The evening is free — you can explore the famous Jemaa el-Fna square, walk through the surrounding souks or enjoy dinner in the Medina.

~330 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 6

Full-Day Marrakech Sightseeing Tour

Today you will discover the highlights of Marrakech, one of Morocco's most famous cities.

Begin with a visit to the Koutoubia Mosque, the largest mosque in Marrakech and one of the city's most recognizable landmarks. Continue to the historic Saadian Tombs and the Bahia Palace, where you can admire traditional Moroccan architecture, decorated courtyards and intricate craftsmanship.

After lunch, explore the colorful souks of the Medina. Your guide can introduce you to local artisans producing leather goods, carpets, lamps, spices and other traditional products.

Continue toward Jemaa el-Fna, the heart of the old city, where the atmosphere changes throughout the day. If you prefer gardens and modern Marrakech, you can also visit the Majorelle Garden as an optional visit.

Spend your final evening enjoying Marrakech's restaurants, cafés and lively Medina atmosphere.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 7

Marrakech: Free Time & Departure

Enjoy your final morning in Marrakech at your own pace.

Depending on your departure time, you can have breakfast at your riad, do some last-minute shopping in the souks or take a relaxed walk through the Medina.

If you have a late flight, we can arrange additional sightseeing or a visit to one of Marrakech's gardens, museums or traditional neighborhoods.

At the agreed time, your private driver will transfer you to Marrakech Menara Airport for your departure. Your 7-day Morocco tour from Tangier to Marrakech ends with memories of Morocco's Mediterranean north, blue mountain villages, ancient imperial cities, Atlantic coast and vibrant southern capital.

Why Choose This Tour?

This itinerary is ideal for travelers who want to see the highlights of northern Morocco and the imperial cities in just one week.

Instead of returning to the starting point, the route moves naturally from Tangier south to Marrakech, allowing you to experience several different sides of Morocco without unnecessary backtracking.

You will see the blue mountain streets of Chefchaouen, the historic Medina of Fes, the imperial heritage of Meknes, Roman history at Volubilis, the capital city of Rabat, the modern Atlantic atmosphere of Casablanca and the vibrant culture of Marrakech.

The route connects Tangier, Chefchaouen, Fes, Meknes, Rabat, Casablanca and Marrakech — a strong introduction to the country's cultural and historical diversity.

Because this is a private tour, the itinerary can be customized according to your interests, travel pace and accommodation preferences.

FAQ

Is 7 days enough to travel from Tangier to Marrakech?

Yes. Seven days is enough to cover the main cities on this northern Morocco route, although the itinerary is relatively active. It is particularly suitable for first-time visitors who want to see several major destinations in one trip.

Is this a private Morocco tour?

Yes. The tour is private, with your own vehicle and driver. You do not have to join a shared group.

How many nights do we spend in Fes?

You spend two nights in Fes, giving you a full day to explore the historic Medina and surrounding areas.

Do we visit Chefchaouen?

Yes. Chefchaouen is included between Tangier and Fes, with time to explore the famous blue Medina.

Is Volubilis included?

Yes. The Roman ruins of Volubilis are visited on Day 4 while traveling from Fes toward Meknes and Rabat.

Do we visit Casablanca?

Yes. Casablanca is included on Day 5, with a visit to the Hassan II Mosque and other important areas of the city.

Is Marrakech included?

Yes. You spend two nights in Marrakech, including a full-day guided sightseeing tour.

Can we add more time in Marrakech?

Yes. We can extend the itinerary with additional nights in Marrakech or add excursions such as Essaouira, the Agafay Desert or the Atlas Mountains.

Can we start this tour from Tangier Airport?

Yes. We can arrange pickup from Tangier Ibn Battuta Airport, the ferry terminal or your hotel.

Can the tour end somewhere other than Marrakech?

Yes. The itinerary can be customized if you need to finish at Marrakech Airport, Casablanca Airport, Fes or another Moroccan destination.

Is this tour suitable for families?

Yes. The tour can be adapted for families, including slower sightseeing, family-friendly accommodation and additional breaks during longer driving days.
