Overview

One week through the four imperial cities of Morocco, from the Strait of Gibraltar to Marrakech. The blue town of Chefchaouen and the Roman ruins of Volubilis on the way to Meknes, then two nights in Fes with a local guide in the oldest medina in the country.

Rabat, the capital, by the ocean, the Hassan II Mosque in Casablanca, and two nights in Marrakech with a guided day in the Red City. Short, easy drives on good roads, your own car and driver-guide all the way, and no shop stops you didn’t ask for.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Tangier → Tetouan → Chefchaouen

    Your driver-guide meets you off the ferry at the port, at Tanger Med, at the airport or at your hotel. A walk in the white Andalusian medina of Tetouan, then up into the green Rif hills to Chefchaouen, the blue town, in time for sunset from the Spanish Mosque.

    • About 125 km, about 2.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Chefchaouen
    • Breakfast from day 2
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Meknes → Fes

    An early walk in the blue lanes, then south to Volubilis, the Roman town with its mosaics and triumphal arch, and Meknes, the imperial city of Moulay Ismail, with the great gate of Bab Mansour and his royal granaries. Your riad in the Fes medina by late afternoon.

    • About 260 km, about 5 hours on the road
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Fes: a guided day in the medina

    With a local Fes guide through Fes el-Bali, the oldest of the imperial cities: the Bou Inania Madrasa, the Nejjarine fountain, the coppersmiths of Place Seffarine, the brass gates of the royal palace and a rooftop above the Chouara tanneries. Shops only if you ask.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Fes → Rabat → Casablanca

    West by motorway to Rabat, the capital: the blue-and-white lanes of the Kasbah of the Oudayas above the ocean, the Hassan Tower and the royal Mausoleum of Mohammed V, with its guards at the gate. On down the coast to Casablanca for the evening on the Corniche.

    • About 300 km, about 3.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Casablanca
    • Breakfast
  5. 5
    Day 5

    Hassan II Mosque → Marrakech

    A guided visit inside the Hassan II Mosque, built over the Atlantic, with its 210 m minaret and carved cedar ceilings. Then south by motorway across the plains to Marrakech and your riad in the medina by mid-afternoon, with the evening free on Jemaa el-Fnaa.

    • About 240 km, about 3 hours on the road
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  6. 6
    Day 6

    Marrakech: a guided day in the Red City

    With a local Marrakech guide: the Koutoubia, the painted cedar ceilings of the Bahia Palace, the Saadian Tombs, the Ben Youssef Madrasa and the souks. In the afternoon, your driver can take you to the Majorelle Garden or you rest by the riad pool.

    • Day on foot, short drives in town
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  7. 7
    Day 7

    Marrakech, end of the tour

    A free morning for a last walk in the souks or a hammam, then your driver takes you to Marrakech airport, the train station or your next hotel, at the time that suits your flight.

    • Airport or hotel transfer
    • Ends at Marrakech airport or your hotel
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the four imperial cities of Morocco?

Fes, Meknes, Rabat and Marrakech, each a royal capital at some point in Moroccan history. This tour visits all four, plus Chefchaouen, Tetouan, the Roman ruins of Volubilis and Casablanca.

Can we visit the Hassan II Mosque on any day?

Guided visits run Saturday to Thursday, several times a day; the mosque is closed to visitors on Fridays. If day 5 falls on a Friday, we see it on the evening of day 4 or the next morning instead.

Is this tour good without the desert?

Yes, if you prefer cities, history and food to long drives. The longest day is about five hours on the road, and you sleep two nights in both Fes and Marrakech. Add the Sahara any time: it takes two more days from Fes.

Will you meet us at the ferry or the airport?

Yes, at any hour. Ferries from Tarifa arrive in the city port of Tangier; from Algeciras at Tanger Med, about 45 minutes away. Your driver-guide waits at the exit with your name and follows any delay.

Are the city guides included?

Yes. A local guide walks you through Fes on day 3 and Marrakech on day 6, and the mosque visit has its own official guide. Your driver-guide does the rest, so there is no one else to pay apart from entrance fees.

How do I book and pay?

  1. Send your request with the form or on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay a 20–30% deposit by bank transfer to confirm; the rest is paid on arrival in cash (EUR, USD or MAD) or by card.

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before the tour.
