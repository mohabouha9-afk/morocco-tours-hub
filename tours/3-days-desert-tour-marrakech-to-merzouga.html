Overview

Three days from Marrakech to the Erg Chebbi dunes at Merzouga and back, in your own car with your own driver-guide. You cross the High Atlas, walk through Aït Ben Haddou, sleep in the Dades Valley and walk in the Todra Gorge before a camel takes you into the dunes for sunset.

The road itself is part of the trip: High Atlas peaks, palm-filled valleys and kasbahs change around you every hour. Because the tour is private, you stop when you want, eat where you like and never stop at a shop you didn’t ask for.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Marrakech → Tizi n’Tichka → Aït Ben Haddou → Dades Valley

    Pick-up at 08:00 at your hotel, or at the nearest gate if your riad is in the medina. The road climbs over the High Atlas by the Tizi n’Tichka pass (2,260 m), with photo stops. At Aït Ben Haddou your driver-guide walks you up through the ksar; lunch nearby. Then Ouarzazate, the Valley of Roses and the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs to the Dades Valley for dinner.

    • About 320 km, 6 to 7 hours on the road
    • Night: Dades Valley
    • Dinner & breakfast
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Merzouga

    After breakfast, the Todra Gorge: walk on the flat riverbed between rock walls about 300 m high. On through the Tinghir palm grove, Erfoud and Rissani to Merzouga. Leave your big bags in the car, take a small bag for the night and ride a camel into the Erg Chebbi dunes for sunset. Dinner in the camp, music around the fire and a sky full of stars.

    • About 270 km, about 5 hours on the road
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Breakfast & dinner
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Merzouga → Draa Valley → Ouarzazate → Marrakech

    Wake early for sunrise over the dunes and try the sandboards before breakfast. Back to the car by camel or 4×4. This is the longest day: west through the Draa Valley palm groves to Ouarzazate, then over the Atlas again. We stop whenever you need a break, and you are back at your hotel in Marrakech around 19:00.

    • About 560 km, 9 to 10 hours on the road
    • Back in Marrakech around 19:00
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 3 days enough for the Sahara?

Yes, to see the dunes, ride a camel and spend one night under the stars, with sunset and sunrise. But Merzouga is about 560 km from Marrakech, so much of the trip is on the road. If you have one more day, add a night in Merzouga or break the drive back.

What is the desert camp like?

A private Berber tent with real beds in the Erg Chebbi dunes, with dinner and breakfast at the camp. All three camps in our table have a private bathroom in the tent; see the Accommodations section above.

When is the best time to go, and how cold are the nights?

October to April is the most pleasant. From December to February, desert nights can drop to about 3–5 °C, so bring a warm layer; the camp has blankets. June to August is very hot in the middle of the day.

Do we have to eat at tourist restaurants?

No. Lunch is not included, so you choose: your driver-guide can suggest simple local cafés or restaurants with a view, and you decide. There are no shopping stops unless you ask for one.

Can we skip the camel ride?

Yes. A 4×4 can take you to the camp instead, and in the morning you can also choose the camel or the 4×4 to come back. Just tell us when you book.

How do I book and pay?

  1. Send your request with the form or on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay a 20–30% deposit by bank transfer to confirm; the rest is paid on arrival in cash (EUR, USD or MAD) or by card.

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before the tour.
