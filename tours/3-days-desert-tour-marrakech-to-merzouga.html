Tour Overview

Experience the magic of southern Morocco on an unforgettable 3-day Marrakech to Merzouga desert tour. Travel from the vibrant streets of Marrakech across the spectacular High Atlas Mountains, explore the UNESCO-listed Aït Ben Haddou, discover the dramatic Dades Valley and Todra Gorge, and finally arrive at the golden Erg Chebbi dunes of Merzouga.

The highlight of the journey is your camel trek through the Sahara at sunset, followed by a magical evening around the campfire beneath the desert stars. The next morning, watch the sunrise over the dunes before beginning your scenic journey back to Marrakech.

"More than a trip... It's a story to tell!"

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Marrakech → High Atlas → Aït Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate → Dades Valley

Your 3-day Marrakech to Merzouga tour begins with an early morning departure from your hotel or riad in Marrakech. Travel south through the spectacular High Atlas Mountains, crossing the famous Tizi n'Tichka Pass, which reaches approximately 2,260 metres above sea level.

Enjoy several photo stops along the mountain road before continuing towards Aït Ben Haddou. Explore this remarkable fortified ksar, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, with its traditional earthen architecture and beautiful views over the surrounding landscape.

After lunch, continue to Ouarzazate, often known as the gateway to the Sahara, before driving through the Valley of Roses and along the famous Road of a Thousand Kasbahs. Arrive in the Dades Valley in the late afternoon, where you will enjoy dinner and spend the night in a traditional hotel or riad.

~360 km Dades Valley Dinner & breakfast
Day 2

Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Erfoud → Merzouga

After breakfast, continue your journey towards the Sahara Desert. Begin with a visit to the spectacular Todra Gorge, one of Morocco's most impressive natural landscapes. Take a relaxing walk between the towering limestone cliffs before continuing through the palm-filled oasis of Tinghir.

The journey then takes you across the changing landscapes of southeastern Morocco towards Erfoud, famous for its fossils and dates. Continue through Rissani and the desert landscapes until you reach Merzouga and the magnificent Erg Chebbi dunes.

In the late afternoon, exchange your vehicle for a camel and ride through the golden dunes as the sun begins to set. Arrive at your desert camp, where you will enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner, Berber music around the campfire and an unforgettable night beneath the stars.

~360 km Sahara Desert Camp, Merzouga Breakfast & dinner
Day 3

Merzouga → Sunrise → Draa Valley → Ouarzazate → Marrakech

Wake early to experience one of the most beautiful moments of your Morocco desert tour: sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes. After breakfast at the camp, return by camel or 4×4 to Merzouga and begin your journey back to Marrakech.

Travel through the spectacular landscapes of southeastern Morocco, with stops for photographs and refreshments along the way. Depending on the route and road conditions, you can travel through the Draa Valley and the area around Agdz and Ouarzazate before crossing the High Atlas Mountains again.

Although Day 3 involves a long drive, the changing scenery makes the journey part of the experience. Arrive in Marrakech in the evening, where your private driver will drop you off at your hotel or riad.

~560 km End of tour: Marrakech Breakfast

FAQ

How long is the camel trek?

The camel trek is around 1 to 1.5 hours across the dunes to reach the desert camp, timed for sunset.

Is the tour suitable for children?

Yes. Families are very welcome and we can adapt the pace and camel trek for young children.

What is the accommodation like in the desert camp?

Comfortable Berber tents with real beds, private or shared bathrooms depending on the camp, and dinner under the stars.

Can we customize this tour?

Absolutely — every tour is private. We can adjust the itinerary, stops and hotels to suit you.

What is the best time to visit Merzouga?

October to April offers the most pleasant temperatures, though the desert is beautiful year-round.
