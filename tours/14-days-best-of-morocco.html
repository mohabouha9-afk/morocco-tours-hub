Tour Overview

Discover the very best of Morocco on this 14-Day Grand Morocco Tour from Casablanca, an immersive private journey combining imperial cities, blue mountain towns, ancient Roman history, spectacular mountain landscapes and the legendary Sahara Desert.

Your adventure begins in Casablanca and continues to Morocco's capital, Rabat, before heading north to the beautiful blue city of Chefchaouen. Explore the ancient Roman ruins of Volubilis and the imperial city of Meknes before spending two nights discovering the historic medina of Fes.

The journey then continues through the Middle Atlas Mountains and the spectacular Ziz Valley toward Merzouga and the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi. Experience a camel trek, Sahara sunset and a night under the stars before continuing through Rissani, Todra Gorge, Dades Valley, the Valley of Roses and Ouarzazate. After visiting the famous Aït Ben Haddou, cross the High Atlas Mountains to Marrakech. Enjoy a guided tour of the Red City before returning to Casablanca for your departure.

"More than a trip... It's a story to tell!"

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Casablanca → Rabat

Welcome to Morocco! Your 14-day Morocco Grand Tour begins in Casablanca, Morocco's largest city and economic capital. After meeting your private driver at the airport, begin exploring the city's most important highlights.

Visit the magnificent Hassan II Mosque, beautifully located beside the Atlantic Ocean. Admire its impressive architecture and detailed Moroccan craftsmanship before continuing through the city.

Depending on your arrival time, explore the Corniche and some of Casablanca's modern neighborhoods before departing for Rabat.

Morocco's capital offers a wonderful introduction to the country's history. Visit the Hassan Tower, the Mausoleum of Mohammed V and the atmospheric Kasbah of the Udayas overlooking the Bou Regreg River and Atlantic coast.

Continue through the medina before checking into your hotel or riad. Enjoy a relaxing evening and your first Moroccan dinner.

~100 km Rabat Breakfast
Day 2

Rabat → Chefchaouen

After breakfast, leave Rabat and travel north through beautiful Moroccan countryside toward the Rif Mountains and Chefchaouen, the famous Blue Pearl of Morocco.

Upon arrival, check into your traditional riad and begin exploring the charming old medina. Chefchaouen is known for its blue-painted buildings, narrow streets, colorful doors and peaceful mountain atmosphere.

Walk through the medina and discover local artisan shops selling handmade textiles, pottery, leather goods and other traditional products. Continue to Plaza Uta el-Hammam, the heart of the old town, where you can enjoy Moroccan tea while watching local life.

Later, walk toward the Spanish Mosque viewpoint for panoramic views over Chefchaouen and the surrounding Rif Mountains. Sunset is particularly beautiful from this area.

Return to your riad and enjoy a relaxed evening in one of Morocco's most atmospheric destinations.

~200 km Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 3

Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Moulay Idriss → Meknes → Fes

Today, continue your journey toward Morocco's imperial cities. After breakfast, leave Chefchaouen and travel through the countryside toward Volubilis, one of the country's most important archaeological sites.

Explore the ancient Roman ruins and admire preserved mosaics, columns, temples and old streets. Continue to the nearby holy town of Moulay Idriss, dramatically built across two hills and surrounded by beautiful landscapes.

Continue to Meknes, one of Morocco's four imperial cities. Discover the impressive Bab Mansour, the historic medina and areas connected to the city's royal heritage.

After sightseeing, continue to Fes, the spiritual and cultural capital of Morocco. Check into your riad and enjoy a relaxing evening.

You can take a short walk around the area near your accommodation or simply rest before tomorrow's full-day exploration of Fes.

~240 km Fes Breakfast
Day 4

Fes: Guided City Tour

Today is dedicated to exploring Fes, one of Morocco's oldest and most fascinating cities. After breakfast, meet your local licensed guide and begin a walking tour through Fes el-Bali, the historic medina and UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Enter a maze of narrow streets filled with traditional markets, artisan workshops, historic buildings and colorful souks. Visit important landmarks including the Bou Inania Madrasa, Nejjarine Fountain and the famous traditional tanneries.

Explore the areas around Al Quaraouiyine and learn about the city's long history as a center of education, religion and craftsmanship. Your guide will explain the traditions and architecture behind the places you visit while helping you navigate the medina comfortably.

In the afternoon, visit the newer part of Fes and enjoy panoramic views over the old city.

The evening is free for you to relax at your riad or enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner.

Fes Breakfast
Day 5

Fes → Ifrane → Azrou → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

After breakfast, leave Fes and begin your journey toward the Sahara Desert. Travel through the Middle Atlas Mountains and stop in Ifrane, a beautiful mountain town famous for its European-style architecture and green surroundings.

Continue toward Azrou and its cedar forests. Depending on conditions, you may see Barbary macaques among the trees. Continue through spectacular mountain scenery toward Midelt, situated between the Middle and High Atlas ranges.

After Midelt, the landscape gradually changes as you approach the Ziz Valley. Stop at a panoramic viewpoint to admire the enormous palm-filled oasis surrounded by rocky cliffs.

Continue toward Merzouga and the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi. Upon arrival, begin your camel trek into the desert. Ride across the dunes while watching the sun set over the Sahara.

Arrive at your desert camp, enjoy dinner and traditional music, and spend the night beneath the stars.

~470 km Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & dinner
Day 6

Merzouga: Full Sahara Desert Experience

Wake early to watch the sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes. After breakfast at the camp, return toward Merzouga by camel or 4×4.

Today is dedicated to discovering the desert region and experiencing local Saharan culture. Visit a traditional village and learn more about the communities living around the dunes. Depending on local conditions, you may also visit nomadic families and discover their traditional way of life.

Continue exploring the desert area by private 4×4, with opportunities for photography and optional activities such as quad biking or sandboarding.

The afternoon is free to relax and enjoy the desert at a slower pace. Take a walk through the dunes or simply enjoy the peaceful atmosphere.

In the evening, experience another spectacular Sahara sunset before enjoying dinner and traditional music.

~40 km Merzouga Breakfast & dinner
Day 7

Merzouga → Rissani → Erfoud → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley

After breakfast, leave Merzouga and continue through the landscapes of southeastern Morocco. Begin with a visit to Rissani, a historic trading town and important gateway to the Sahara.

If the local market is operating, explore its lively souks and discover traditional products from the region. Continue to Erfoud, famous for fossils and fossil workshops.

The road then leads toward the Todra Valley and the dramatic Todra Gorge. Stop here to walk between enormous limestone cliffs rising on both sides of the narrow valley.

Continue through palm groves and Berber villages toward the Dades Valley. The landscape changes constantly, with red rock formations, green agricultural terraces and winding mountain roads.

Arrive at your hotel or riad in the Dades Valley in the evening. Enjoy dinner and relax in preparation for tomorrow's journey through the Valley of Roses and Ouarzazate.

~300 km Dades Valley Breakfast
Day 8

Dades Valley → Valley of Roses → Skoura → Ouarzazate

After breakfast, continue west through the spectacular landscapes of southern Morocco.

Drive through the Valley of Roses, a fertile region famous for rose cultivation and traditional rosewater production. Continue toward Skoura, a beautiful palm oasis surrounded by historic kasbahs. Take time to appreciate the contrast between the green palm groves and the surrounding dry landscapes.

Continue to Ouarzazate, often called the gateway to the Sahara and southern Morocco. Visit the impressive Taourirt Kasbah and learn more about the history of this important southern city. Ouarzazate is also famous for its connection to international cinema and has been used as a filming location for numerous productions.

Spend the afternoon exploring the city or relaxing at your accommodation.

Enjoy a peaceful evening and prepare for tomorrow's visit to Aït Ben Haddou and the spectacular High Atlas Mountains.

~120 km Ouarzazate Breakfast
Day 9

Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → High Atlas Mountains → Marrakech

After breakfast, continue to Aït Ben Haddou, one of Morocco's most famous fortified villages and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Walk through the ancient ksar, exploring its narrow lanes, traditional earthen buildings and historic kasbahs. Climb toward the upper section for panoramic views across the surrounding countryside. Aït Ben Haddou is also famous for its cinematic history and has appeared in numerous international film and television productions.

Continue toward the High Atlas Mountains. The road climbs through spectacular mountain landscapes, passing Berber villages and dramatic valleys. Cross the Tizi n'Tichka mountain pass before descending toward the Marrakech plain.

Arrive in Marrakech in the afternoon or early evening and check into your riad or hotel.

After several days exploring the desert and mountains, enjoy a relaxing evening in the Red City.

~200 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 10

Marrakech: Guided City Tour

Today, discover the cultural treasures of Marrakech with a local licensed guide. Begin in the historic medina and visit some of the city's most important landmarks.

Explore the Koutoubia Mosque, the Saadian Tombs and the beautiful Bahia Palace, famous for its intricate decoration, colorful tilework and peaceful courtyards.

Continue through the lively souks, where artisans sell carpets, leather products, ceramics, spices, lamps and traditional Moroccan handicrafts. Your guide will explain the history and traditions of the Red City while helping you navigate the medina.

Later, visit the famous Jemaa el-Fna square, the heart of Marrakech. Depending on your interests, you can also visit the Majorelle Garden or another cultural attraction.

The afternoon is free for shopping, relaxation or enjoying a traditional Moroccan hammam.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 11

Marrakech → Essaouira → Marrakech

Today, enjoy a private day trip from Marrakech to Essaouira, Morocco's charming Atlantic coastal town. Travel west through the countryside and the argan tree region before reaching the ocean.

Upon arrival, explore the historic medina, protected by impressive walls and filled with artisan workshops, cafés and small shops.

Visit the fishing harbor and see the traditional blue boats. Walk along the old Skala ramparts and enjoy beautiful views over the Atlantic. Essaouira is famous for its relaxed atmosphere and fresh seafood, making it an excellent place to enjoy a leisurely lunch.

After lunch, explore more of the medina or walk along the beach. This day offers a wonderful contrast to the desert and mountain landscapes of the previous days, giving you the opportunity to experience Morocco's Atlantic coast before returning to the Red City.

In the late afternoon, return to Marrakech and enjoy a free evening.

~380 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 12

Marrakech → Casablanca

After breakfast, leave Marrakech and begin the journey north toward Casablanca.

Depending on your interests and departure schedule, you can enjoy some free time in Marrakech before leaving. This may be an opportunity to complete your shopping, revisit part of the medina or simply relax at your hotel.

Your private driver will then take you through the Moroccan countryside toward Casablanca. Upon arrival, check into your hotel and enjoy a relaxed afternoon. If time permits, you can explore Casablanca's Corniche, visit a local market or enjoy a final walk through the city.

The evening provides an opportunity to relax after your long journey through Morocco and enjoy a final Moroccan dinner.

Your adventure has taken you from Atlantic cities and imperial capitals to the Rif Mountains, Middle Atlas, Sahara Desert and High Atlas.

~240 km Casablanca Breakfast
Day 13

Casablanca: Free Day

Enjoy a relaxed final full day in Casablanca. After breakfast, the day can be customized according to your interests.

You can revisit the Hassan II Mosque area, explore the Corniche or discover some of the city's modern architecture and local neighborhoods.

For travelers interested in shopping, Casablanca offers a wide variety of traditional Moroccan products as well as modern shopping areas. You can also enjoy a relaxed lunch featuring Moroccan or international cuisine. Alternatively, simply use the day to rest after your extensive journey.

In the afternoon, take a final walk along the Atlantic coast and enjoy the ocean atmosphere. Your private driver remains available for transfers and assistance according to your schedule.

In the evening, enjoy your final Moroccan dinner and take time to reflect on the many landscapes, cities and cultural experiences you have discovered during your 14-day Morocco Grand Tour.

Casablanca Breakfast
Day 14

Casablanca: Departure

After breakfast, your 14-Day Morocco Grand Tour from Casablanca comes to an end.

Depending on your flight schedule, enjoy some final free time before your private transfer to Casablanca Mohammed V International Airport or your preferred departure point. If your flight is later in the day, you may have time for a final Moroccan coffee, breakfast or short walk.

During this journey, you have experienced some of Morocco's greatest highlights: Casablanca and Rabat, the blue streets of Chefchaouen, ancient Volubilis, imperial Meknes and Fes, the Middle Atlas Mountains, the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi, Todra Gorge, Dades Valley, Ouarzazate, Aït Ben Haddou, the High Atlas Mountains and Marrakech.

We hope you leave Morocco with wonderful memories, beautiful photographs and a deeper appreciation for its history, landscapes and culture.

End of tour: Casablanca Breakfast
