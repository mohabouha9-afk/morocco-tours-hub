Overview

From Ouarzazate, the Sahara is already close. In two days you ride the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs through the palms of Skoura, the Valley of Roses and the Dades Valley, walk into the Todra Gorge and reach the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi for a camel ride at sunset and a night in a camp under the stars.

At dawn you climb a dune for the sunrise, then come home a different way: Rissani, the fossils of Alnif and the old kasbahs of N’Kob on the quiet southern road. No road twice, your own car and driver-guide, stops when you want them and no shop stops you didn’t ask for.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Ouarzazate → Skoura → Valley of Roses → Dades → Todra Gorge → Merzouga

    Pick-up at your hotel or riad in Ouarzazate, or at the airport, around 07:30. East on the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs: the palm grove of Skoura and Kasbah Amridil, the rose gardens of Kalaat M’Gouna and the red villages of the Dades Valley. Lunch where you like, then a walk between the 300-metre walls of the Todra Gorge, and on past Erfoud to the dunes of Erg Chebbi. Your big bags stay in the car: you ride a camel into the sand for sunset, then dinner, drums by the fire and a night in the camp.

    • About 390 km, about 8 to 9 hours on the road
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Dinner & breakfast
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Sunrise on Erg Chebbi → Rissani → Alnif → N’Kob → Draa Valley → Ouarzazate

    Up early to climb a dune for sunrise, sandboarding if you like, breakfast in the camp and back by camel or 4×4 to your car. A different road home: Rissani, the old caravan town, then west past Alnif, the fossil village, across the dark hills of the Jebel Saghro to Tazzarine and the kasbahs of N’Kob, and along the date palms of the Draa Valley to Ouarzazate, at your hotel or the airport in the early evening.

    • About 360 km, about 8 hours on the road
    • Drop-off at your Ouarzazate hotel or the airport
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the drive from Ouarzazate to Merzouga?

About 390 km and 7 hours of driving, so 8 to 9 hours with the stops at Skoura, the Dades Valley and the Todra Gorge. The way back by the southern road is about 360 km. It is a lot of road, but every hour of it is new country.

Why come back a different way?

Most tours drive the same road twice. We come home by the quiet southern road: Rissani, the fossil village of Alnif, the Jebel Saghro hills, Tazzarine, the kasbahs of N’Kob and the palms of the Draa Valley. Different landscapes, very little traffic.

Is it a private tour or a shared minibus?

Private: only your group, in your own car with your own driver-guide. Everyone gets a window seat, you stop for photos when you want and nobody rushes you.

Will we reach the dunes for sunset?

Yes. We leave Ouarzazate around 07:30, a little earlier in winter when the sun sets early, so you are on your camel before the light turns gold. We agree the exact pick-up time on WhatsApp when you book.

Can you pick us up at Ouarzazate airport?

Yes, we meet you at the airport or at your hotel or riad in town, and drop you back at either one at the end. Arriving the evening before? Spend the night in Ouarzazate and we leave the next morning.

How do I book and pay?

  1. Send your request with the form or on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay a 20–30% deposit by bank transfer to confirm; the rest is paid on arrival in cash (EUR, USD or MAD) or by card.

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before the tour.
