Overview

Step off the ferry from Spain and your driver-guide is waiting. Ten days from the Strait of Gibraltar to Marrakech: the white seaside town of Asilah, two slow days in blue Chefchaouen, the Roman ruins of Volubilis and Meknes, and a guided day in the medina of Fes.

Then over the Middle Atlas to the dunes of Erg Chebbi for two nights, a camp after a sunset camel ride and a hotel after a day in the desert, back west with a night at the Todra Gorge and another beside the ksar of Aït Ben Haddou, and over the High Atlas to Marrakech. Your own car and driver-guide all the way, and no shop stops you didn’t ask for.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Tangier → Cap Spartel → Asilah → Chefchaouen

    Your driver-guide meets you off the ferry at the port, or at Tangier airport or your hotel. Out to Cap Spartel, where the Atlantic meets the Mediterranean, then down the coast to Asilah, a whitewashed fishing town with murals on its walls, for lunch by the sea. Inland through the green Rif hills to Chefchaouen for the evening.

    • About 215 km, about 3.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Chefchaouen
    • Breakfast from day 2
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Chefchaouen: a free day in the blue town

    A slow day in the lanes of blue houses, the Outa el-Hammam square, the Kasbah garden and the Ras el-Maa spring, with sunset from the Spanish Mosque on the hill. For walkers, your driver-guide can take you to the Akchour waterfalls in the Talassemtane park instead.

    • Free day; Akchour on request
    • Night: Chefchaouen
    • Breakfast
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Meknes → Fes

    South through the Rif to Volubilis, the Roman town on the plain, with its mosaics and triumphal arch, then Meknes, the city of Moulay Ismail, for the great gate of Bab Mansour and Place el-Hedim. On to Fes in the late afternoon.

    • About 260 km, about 5 hours on the road
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Fes: a guided day in the medina

    With a local Fes guide: the Bou Inania Madrasa, the Nejjarine fountain, the coppersmiths of Place Seffarine and a rooftop above the Chouara tanneries, ending at the brass gates of the Royal Palace. Shops only if you ask.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  5. 5
    Day 5

    Fes → Ifrane → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

    South over the Middle Atlas around 07:30: Ifrane, the cedar forest of Azrou with its Barbary macaques, and lunch in Midelt. Down the palm gorges of the Ziz Valley to the dunes of Erg Chebbi, where you ride a camel into the sand for sunset. Dinner, drums by the fire and a night in the camp.

    • About 470 km, about 8 hours on the road
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Breakfast & dinner
  6. 6
    Day 6

    A full day around Merzouga by 4×4

    Sunrise and sandboarding, breakfast, and back by camel or 4×4. A day in the desert at your pace: tea with a nomad family, the Gnawa musicians of Khamlia, the black stony plains and the Rissani market. A hot shower and a calm night in a hotel by the dunes.

    • 4×4 day around Merzouga
    • Night: Merzouga hotel
    • Breakfast & dinner
  7. 7
    Day 7

    Merzouga → Alnif → Tinghir → Todra Gorge

    West along the Jebel Saghro, past Alnif and its trilobite fossils, to the long palm grove of Tinghir. You sleep at the Todra Gorge and walk between its 300 m walls in the late afternoon, when the day visitors have gone.

    • About 220 km, about 4.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Todra Gorge
    • Breakfast & dinner
  8. 8
    Day 8

    Todra → Dades Gorge → Valley of Roses → Aït Ben Haddou

    Up the Dades Gorge to the hairpin bends of Tissadrine, then the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs through the Valley of Roses and Skoura. Past Ouarzazate to Aït Ben Haddou, where you sleep beside the ksar and see it turn red at sunset.

    • About 275 km, about 5.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Aït Ben Haddou
    • Breakfast & dinner
  9. 9
    Day 9

    Aït Ben Haddou → Tizi n’Tichka → Marrakech

    An early walk through the quiet lanes of the ksar, then over the High Atlas by the Tizi n’Tichka pass, 2,260 m, and down to Marrakech by lunchtime. The evening is yours on Jemaa el-Fnaa.

    • About 175 km, about 3.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  10. 10
    Day 10

    Marrakech: a guided morning and the end of the tour

    With a local guide: the Koutoubia, the Bahia Palace, the souks and the Ben Youssef Madrasa. The tour ends at your riad, or we take you to Marrakech airport at the time you need.

    • Morning on foot in the medina
    • Ends at your riad or Marrakech airport
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

Will you meet us when the ferry from Spain arrives?

Yes. Ferries from Tarifa arrive at the port in the city of Tangier, and from Algeciras at Tanger Med, about 45 minutes away. Send us your crossing and time; your driver-guide waits at the exit with your name, and we follow any delay.

Can we bring our own car on the ferry instead?

This tour is in our car with your driver-guide, so you leave your car in Spain. Many travellers park in Tarifa or Algeciras and cross on foot, which is quicker at the border.

Why two nights in Chefchaouen?

Arriving by ferry often means a slow first morning. With a full free day in Chefchaouen you catch up, see the blue town early and late, and walk up to the Spanish Mosque for sunset or hike to the Akchour waterfalls.

Which is the longest drive?

Day 5, Fes to Merzouga, about 470 km with stops in Ifrane, the cedar forest and Midelt, timed for the sunset camel ride. The other road days are three to five and a half hours.

Can we end in Casablanca instead of Marrakech?

Yes. Marrakech to Casablanca is about 2.5 hours by motorway, so we can drop you at Casablanca airport on day 10 or add a night there.

How do I book and pay?

  1. Send your request with the form or on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay a 20–30% deposit by bank transfer to confirm; the rest is paid on arrival in cash (EUR, USD or MAD) or by card.

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before the tour.
