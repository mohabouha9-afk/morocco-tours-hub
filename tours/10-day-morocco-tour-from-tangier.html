Tour Overview

Discover the very best of Morocco on this unforgettable 10-day private Morocco tour from Tangier to Marrakech, combining the country's northern treasures, imperial cities, breathtaking mountains and magical Sahara Desert. Your journey begins in Tangier, where the Mediterranean and Atlantic worlds meet, before continuing to the famous blue-painted streets of Chefchaouen.

Explore the ancient Roman ruins of Volubilis and the imperial city of Meknes before discovering the fascinating medieval medina of Fes with a local guide. From Fes, cross the Middle Atlas Mountains and continue through the Ziz Valley toward Merzouga and the golden Erg Chebbi dunes.

Enjoy an unforgettable Sahara experience with camel trekking, desert sunset, sunrise and traditional music. Continue through Rissani, Todra Gorge and Dades Valley before visiting Ouarzazate and Aït Ben Haddou. Finally, cross the High Atlas Mountains and discover vibrant Marrakech.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Tangier: Arrival & City Tour

Welcome to Morocco! Your private driver will meet you at Tangier airport, ferry port or your accommodation and begin your Moroccan adventure. Depending on your arrival time, start exploring this fascinating northern city, which has been influenced by Moroccan, Andalusian, Mediterranean and international cultures for centuries. Discover the historic Kasbah and wander through the narrow streets of the old medina, where traditional houses, cafés and small artisan shops create a wonderful atmosphere. Continue to the Grand Socco and Petit Socco before exploring the area around the Kasbah. You can also visit Cap Spartel, where the Atlantic Ocean meets the Mediterranean Sea, and the nearby Hercules Caves. Enjoy spectacular coastal views and time for photographs before returning to Tangier. In the evening, check into your comfortable hotel or traditional riad and relax after your journey.

~60 km Tangier Breakfast
Day 2

Tangier → Chefchaouen

After breakfast, leave Tangier and travel south toward the beautiful Rif Mountains and the famous Blue City of Chefchaouen. The journey takes you through peaceful countryside and scenic mountain landscapes before arriving at one of Morocco's most charming destinations. Chefchaouen is famous for its blue-painted buildings, winding lanes, colorful doors and relaxed atmosphere. Spend the day exploring the historic medina on foot and discover Plaza Uta el-Hammam, the Kasbah and traditional artisan shops. You will have plenty of opportunities to take beautiful photographs while walking through the atmospheric blue streets. For panoramic views over the city and Rif Mountains, you can walk toward the Spanish Mosque. Enjoy some free time to relax in a traditional café or discover local Moroccan handicrafts. In the evening, experience the peaceful atmosphere of Chefchaouen after the day visitors have left.

~120 km Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 3

Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Moulay Idriss → Meknes → Fes

After breakfast, depart Chefchaouen and continue toward Fes, with fascinating historical stops along the way. Your first visit is Volubilis, one of Morocco's most important archaeological sites and the country's best-preserved Roman ruins. Walk among ancient columns, temples and beautiful mosaics while learning about the history of this remarkable settlement. Continue to the picturesque town of Moulay Idriss, built across green hills and considered one of Morocco's important pilgrimage destinations. Afterwards, continue to Meknes, one of the country's four imperial cities. Explore the impressive Bab Mansour gate, the old medina and the historic monuments associated with Sultan Moulay Ismail. After sightseeing, continue to Fes and check into your traditional riad. Enjoy some free time to relax or take an evening walk around the surroundings of the ancient medina.

~240 km Fes Breakfast
Day 4

Fes: Full-Day Guided City Tour

Today is dedicated to discovering Fes, one of Morocco's most fascinating cultural and historical cities. After breakfast, meet your local licensed guide and begin exploring Fes el-Bali, the ancient walled medina and UNESCO World Heritage Site. Walk through its maze of narrow streets and discover traditional souks, historic mosques, madrasas and artisan workshops. Visit highlights such as the Bou Inania Madrasa, Al-Attarine Madrasa, Nejjarine Fountain and the famous Chouara Tanneries. Your guide will explain the history, traditions and architecture that make Fes unique. Explore workshops where local craftsmen continue producing leather goods, pottery, metalwork, carpets and other traditional products using techniques passed down through generations. Enjoy lunch at a traditional Moroccan restaurant before continuing your sightseeing. In the afternoon, visit the area around the Royal Palace and enjoy panoramic views over the old city.

Fes Breakfast
Day 5

Fes → Ifrane → Azrou → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

After breakfast, leave Fes and begin an unforgettable journey toward the Sahara Desert. Cross the beautiful Middle Atlas Mountains and stop in Ifrane, a charming mountain town known for its distinctive architecture and cool climate. Continue through the cedar forests around Azrou, where you may have the opportunity to see Barbary macaques. Continue toward Midelt, surrounded by dramatic mountain landscapes and known for its agricultural production. From Midelt, travel through the changing scenery toward the spectacular Ziz Valley. Stop at a panoramic viewpoint to admire the lush palm-filled oasis contrasting with the surrounding rocky mountains. Continue toward Merzouga as the landscape gradually transforms into the vast Sahara. Upon arrival, begin your camel trek across the golden Erg Chebbi dunes. Ride through the desert while watching the sunset before reaching your desert camp. Enjoy a traditional dinner, local music and a magical evening beneath the stars.

~470 km Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & dinner
Day 6

Merzouga: Sahara Desert Experience

Wake early to witness the spectacular sunrise over the golden Erg Chebbi dunes. After breakfast, return to Merzouga and enjoy a full day exploring the surrounding Sahara region without having to travel to another destination. Your private 4×4 will take you around the desert landscapes, where you can discover traditional nomadic communities and learn about their way of life. Continue to Khamlia village, famous for its Gnawa music and African cultural heritage. Depending on local conditions, you can also visit Lake Dayet Srij, a seasonal lake that attracts birds and creates a beautiful contrast with the surrounding desert. Adventure lovers can enjoy optional activities such as quad biking, while those seeking relaxation can spend time around the dunes. In the afternoon, return toward Erg Chebbi and enjoy free time for photography, sandboarding or simply taking in the silence of the Sahara. Watch another beautiful sunset before dinner and traditional music around the campfire.

~40 km Merzouga Breakfast & dinner
Day 7

Merzouga → Rissani → Erfoud → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley

After breakfast, leave the Sahara Desert and continue through the dramatic landscapes of southeastern Morocco. Your first stop is Rissani, an historic desert town and former trading center linking the Sahara with northern Morocco. If the local market is open, explore the lively souk, where you can discover dates, spices, traditional crafts and local products. Continue to Erfoud, famous for its fossils and fossilized marble products. From Erfoud, travel toward Tinghir and the spectacular Todra Gorge. Take a walk between the enormous limestone cliffs and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of this natural wonder. Continue through the green palm oasis toward the Dades Valley, one of southern Morocco's most beautiful regions. Along the route, admire traditional villages, ancient kasbahs, dramatic rock formations and mountain landscapes. Arrive at your accommodation and enjoy a relaxing evening surrounded by the peaceful scenery of the valley.

~300 km Dades Valley Breakfast
Day 8

Dades Valley → Valley of Roses → Skoura → Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → Marrakech

After breakfast, continue your journey through the beautiful landscapes of southern Morocco. Travel through the Valley of Roses, famous for its rose cultivation, traditional villages and scenic mountain scenery. Continue to Skoura, a green oasis filled with palm trees and impressive mud-brick kasbahs. Proceed to Ouarzazate, often known as the Gateway to the Sahara and famous for its film studios and spectacular desert surroundings. From Ouarzazate, visit Aït Ben Haddou, one of Morocco's most famous fortified villages and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Walk through its narrow streets and admire its traditional earthen architecture while enjoying panoramic views from the upper part of the village. Continue across the High Atlas Mountains via the spectacular Tizi n'Tichka Pass. Enjoy several opportunities to stop for photographs before descending toward Marrakech. Arrive in the Red City in the evening and check into your traditional riad.

~360 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 9

Marrakech: Full-Day Guided City Tour

Today, discover the fascinating city of Marrakech with a local licensed guide. Begin your exploration in the historic medina and visit some of its most important monuments, including the Bahia Palace, Saadian Tombs and Koutoubia Mosque. Continue through the colorful souks, where artisans create traditional Moroccan carpets, leather products, lamps, spices and other handicrafts. Visit the famous Jemaa el-Fnaa square, the lively heart of Marrakech, and experience the unique atmosphere of this legendary city. Your guide will introduce you to Marrakech's history, architecture and traditions while giving you time to take photographs and explore. Enjoy lunch at a traditional Moroccan restaurant before continuing your sightseeing. Depending on your interests, you may also visit the Ben Youssef Madrasa or explore the modern Gueliz district. In the afternoon, enjoy free time for shopping, relaxation or independent exploration.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 10

Marrakech: Departure

Enjoy your final morning in Morocco at your own pace. Depending on your departure time, you may have time to enjoy breakfast at your riad, take a final walk through the medina, shop for souvenirs in the colorful souks or relax with a traditional Moroccan tea or coffee. If your flight is later in the day, we can arrange additional free time or sightseeing according to your schedule. At the appropriate time, your private driver will pick you up and transfer you to Marrakech airport for your departure. Your 10-day private Morocco tour from Tangier to Marrakech comes to an end after an unforgettable journey through northern Morocco, the Rif Mountains, imperial cities, the Sahara Desert and the spectacular landscapes of southern Morocco. You will leave with wonderful memories of Chefchaouen's blue streets, Fes's ancient medina, the golden dunes of Merzouga, Aït Ben Haddou and the vibrant atmosphere of Marrakech.

End of tour: Marrakech Breakfast
