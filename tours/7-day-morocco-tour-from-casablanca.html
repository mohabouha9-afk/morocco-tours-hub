Tour Overview

Discover the incredible diversity of Morocco on this 7-Day Morocco Tour from Casablanca, combining the country's historic imperial cities, spectacular mountains, ancient kasbahs, and unforgettable Sahara Desert landscapes. Beginning in Casablanca, this private journey takes you through Rabat and the fascinating spiritual city of Fes before heading south toward the golden dunes of Merzouga.

Travel through the Middle Atlas Mountains, explore the beautiful Ziz Valley, and experience the magic of the Sahara with a camel trek across the Erg Chebbi dunes. Continue through Rissani, Todra Gorge, Dades Valley, Ouarzazate, and the famous Aït Ben Haddou Kasbah before crossing the High Atlas Mountains to Marrakech.

Designed as a private and flexible experience, this itinerary allows you to discover Morocco comfortably while enjoying scenic stops, cultural experiences, and plenty of opportunities for photographs.

"More than a trip... It's a story to tell!"

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Casablanca → Rabat → Fes

Your 7-Day Morocco Tour from Casablanca begins with a private pickup from Casablanca Airport, your hotel, or another convenient location. Start by exploring Casablanca, Morocco's largest city and an important economic center. Visit the magnificent Hassan II Mosque, situated dramatically beside the Atlantic Ocean, and enjoy some time along the Corniche.

Continue to Rabat, the elegant capital of Morocco. Explore the historic Kasbah of the Udayas, the impressive Hassan Tower, and the Mausoleum of Mohammed V. Your driver will introduce you to the city while allowing time for photographs and sightseeing.

After lunch, continue toward Fes through the Moroccan countryside. Watch the landscape gradually change as you travel north toward one of Morocco's most historic cities. Arrive in Fes in the late afternoon and check into your traditional riad or comfortable hotel.

The evening is free to relax and enjoy your first evening in the cultural capital of Morocco.

~300 km Fes Breakfast
Day 2

Fes → Ifrane → Azrou → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

After breakfast, begin your journey from Fes toward the Sahara Desert. Travel through the Middle Atlas Mountains and stop in Ifrane, a beautiful mountain town famous for its European-inspired architecture and peaceful surroundings.

Continue through the cedar forests near Azrou, where you may have the opportunity to see Barbary macaques. The journey then continues toward Midelt, known for its apples and spectacular mountain scenery.

Cross the dramatic landscapes of the Middle and High Atlas before reaching the Ziz Valley. Stop at a panoramic viewpoint to admire the green palm groves stretching through the desert landscape.

Continue toward Erfoud and Rissani before reaching Merzouga in the afternoon. Here, your camel trek begins across the golden Erg Chebbi dunes. Ride through the peaceful Sahara while watching the sunset transform the dunes into beautiful shades of gold and orange.

Arrive at your desert camp, enjoy a traditional dinner, and spend the evening under the stars.

~470 km Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & dinner
Day 3

Merzouga: Sahara Desert Experience

Wake early to enjoy one of the most beautiful moments of your Morocco desert tour—sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes. Watch the first sunlight illuminate the endless golden sands before returning to camp for breakfast.

Today is dedicated to discovering the Merzouga region and experiencing its unique desert culture. Depending on your interests, visit a nearby village and learn about local traditions, music, and everyday life around the Sahara. You can also explore the desert landscape by private 4×4.

For adventure lovers, there is time for sandboarding on the dunes. Alternatively, you can relax at your accommodation and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of the desert.

In the afternoon, return toward the dunes for another spectacular sunset. As darkness falls, enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner accompanied by local music around the campfire.

Spend the evening looking up at the clear desert sky, far away from city lights. This peaceful Sahara experience is one of the unforgettable highlights of your week in Morocco.

~40 km Merzouga Breakfast & dinner
Day 4

Merzouga → Rissani → Erfoud → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley

After breakfast, leave Merzouga and continue through the fascinating landscapes of southeastern Morocco. Begin with a visit to Rissani, an historic trading town and former caravan center. If your visit coincides with market day, explore its lively traditional souk, where locals trade dates, spices, livestock, and handcrafted products.

Continue to Erfoud, famous for its fossils and beautifully polished stone products. Travel onward through the changing desert landscapes toward Tinghir.

One of the day's main highlights is the spectacular Todra Gorge. Walk through the narrow canyon and admire the enormous limestone cliffs rising dramatically on both sides. The gorge is especially impressive when sunlight illuminates the rock walls.

After exploring Todra, continue toward the Dades Valley, passing palm oases, Berber villages, and dramatic rock formations. Arrive at your hotel in the late afternoon and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of this beautiful valley.

~300 km Dades Valley Breakfast
Day 5

Dades Valley → Valley of Roses → Skoura → Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → Marrakech

After breakfast, continue your journey through southern Morocco. Drive along the spectacular Dades Valley and through the Valley of Roses, known for its rose cultivation and traditional cosmetic products.

Continue to Skoura, a beautiful oasis surrounded by palm trees and historic kasbahs. Then travel to Ouarzazate, often called the "Hollywood of Morocco" because of its long history of international film productions.

From Ouarzazate, continue to Aït Ben Haddou, one of Morocco's most famous attractions and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Explore the ancient fortified village, walk through its narrow lanes, and admire its traditional earthen architecture.

After lunch, begin the spectacular drive across the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka Pass. Enjoy panoramic mountain views as you descend toward Marrakech.

Arrive in Marrakech in the evening and check into your hotel or riad. The remainder of the evening is free to relax.

~360 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 6

Marrakech: Guided City Tour

Today you will discover Marrakech, one of Morocco's most colorful and atmospheric imperial cities. After breakfast, meet your local guide and begin exploring the historic medina.

Visit the impressive Bahia Palace, the Saadian Tombs, and the exterior of the famous Koutoubia Mosque. Continue through the lively souks, where artisans create and sell carpets, leather goods, ceramics, lanterns, spices, and other traditional Moroccan products.

Your guide will introduce you to the history and culture of Marrakech while helping you navigate the fascinating maze of the old medina. Take time to enjoy a traditional Moroccan lunch or stop for a refreshing mint tea.

In the afternoon, explore Jemaa el-Fnaa, the famous central square that becomes particularly lively as the day progresses. You can also visit additional parts of the medina or enjoy some free time for shopping.

Return to your riad or hotel in the evening and enjoy your final night in Marrakech.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 7

Marrakech: Departure

Enjoy your final breakfast in Morocco and take some free time in Marrakech depending on your departure schedule. You may wish to enjoy a final walk through the medina, visit a local café, or purchase traditional Moroccan souvenirs.

At the appropriate time, your private driver will meet you at your hotel or riad and transfer you to Marrakech Menara Airport or your preferred departure point.

Your 7-Day Morocco Tour from Casablanca comes to an end after an unforgettable journey through Morocco's imperial cities, mountain landscapes, ancient kasbahs, valleys, and the Sahara Desert.

From the Atlantic coast of Casablanca to the golden dunes of Merzouga and the colorful streets of Marrakech, this private itinerary gives you an authentic taste of Morocco in just seven days.
