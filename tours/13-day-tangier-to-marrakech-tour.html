Overview

Thirteen days from the Strait of Gibraltar to Marrakech by the road few tours take. Two nights in the blue town of Chefchaouen, the Roman ruins of Volubilis and Meknes, two nights in Fes with a guided day in its medina, and two by the great dunes of Erg Chebbi.

Then the deep south: off-road to the quiet oasis of Ouzina, across the old Paris–Dakar rally tracks to Zagora, up the palm groves of the Draa Valley to Aït Ben Haddou, and three nights in Marrakech to finish slowly. Your own car and driver-guide all the way.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Tangier → Cap Spartel → Tetouan → Chefchaouen

    Your driver-guide meets you off the ferry at the port, at Tanger Med, at the airport or at your hotel. Cap Spartel, where the Mediterranean meets the Atlantic, the white medina of Tetouan, then up into the green Rif hills to Chefchaouen, the blue town.

    • About 150 km, about 3 hours on the road
    • Night: Chefchaouen
    • Breakfast from day 2
  2. 2
    Day 2

    A free day in Chefchaouen

    No driving: the quiet blue lanes before the day visitors arrive, the kasbah garden on Outa el Hammam square, sunset from the Spanish Mosque. Ask your driver for a half-day to the Akchour waterfalls if you like to walk.

    • No driving unless you ask
    • Night: Chefchaouen
    • Breakfast
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Meknes → Fes

    South to Volubilis, the Roman town with its mosaics and triumphal arch, and Meknes, with the great gate of Bab Mansour. Your riad in the Fes medina by late afternoon.

    • About 260 km, about 5 hours on the road
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Fes: a guided day in the medina

    With a local Fes guide: the Bou Inania Madrasa, the Nejjarine fountain, the coppersmiths of Place Seffarine and a rooftop above the Chouara tanneries. Shops only if you ask.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast
  5. 5
    Day 5

    Fes → Ifrane → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

    South over the Middle Atlas around 07:30: Ifrane, the cedar forest with its Barbary macaques, lunch in Midelt and the palm gorges of the Ziz Valley to Erg Chebbi. A camel ride into the dunes for sunset, dinner and drums by the fire in the camp.

    • About 470 km, about 8 hours on the road
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Breakfast & dinner
  6. 6
    Day 6

    A full day around Merzouga by 4×4

    Sunrise and sandboarding, then a day with no long road: tea with a nomad family, the Gnawa musicians of Khamlia, the black stony plains and the lake of Dayet Srij after a wet winter. A hot shower and a calm night in a hotel by the dunes.

    • 4×4 day around Merzouga
    • Night: Merzouga hotel
    • Breakfast & dinner
  7. 7
    Day 7

    Merzouga → Taouz → Ouzina, off-road

    South along the dunes to Taouz and its rock carvings, then off the tarmac onto desert tracks to Ouzina, a small oasis by its own quiet dunes, close to the Algerian border. Few cars, wide horizons and an evening in a family guesthouse.

    • About 60 km, half of it off-road
    • Night: Ouzina guesthouse
    • Breakfast & dinner
  8. 8
    Day 8

    Ouzina → Sidi Ali → the Paris–Dakar tracks → Zagora

    The great desert crossing: by 4×4 past the dunes of Ramlia, the dry lake of Maider and the oasis of Sidi Ali, then west on the old Paris–Dakar rally tracks across the stony hamada to Zagora, in the palm groves of the Draa.

    • About 170 km, most of it off-road
    • Night: Zagora
    • Breakfast & dinner
  9. 9
    Day 9

    Zagora → Draa Valley → Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou

    North up the Draa Valley, a 100 km ribbon of palm groves and earthen kasbahs, over the Tizi n’Tinifift pass to Ouarzazate, then on to Aït Ben Haddou for a sunset walk up through the ksar and a night beside it.

    • About 190 km, about 4 hours on the road
    • Night: Aït Ben Haddou
    • Breakfast & dinner
  10. 10
    Day 10

    Aït Ben Haddou → Tizi n’Tichka → Marrakech

    An early look at the ksar in the morning light, then over the High Atlas by the Tizi n’Tichka pass, 2,260 m, and down to your Marrakech riad by early afternoon.

    • About 190 km, about 4 hours on the road
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  11. 11
    Day 11

    Marrakech: a guided day in the Red City

    With a local Marrakech guide: the Koutoubia, the painted ceilings of the Bahia Palace, the Ben Youssef Madrasa and the souks. Jemaa el-Fnaa comes alive after dark with music and food stalls.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  12. 12
    Day 12

    Marrakech: a day of your own

    A slow day after the desert: the Majorelle Garden, a hammam, a cooking class, or a day trip to the Atlas villages or Essaouira with your driver. Tell us what you like and we plan it with you.

    • Free day, driver on request
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  13. 13
    Day 13

    Marrakech, end of the tour

    A last breakfast on the roof terrace, then your driver takes you to Marrakech airport, the train station or your next hotel, at the time that suits your flight.

    • Airport or hotel transfer
    • Ends at Marrakech airport or your hotel
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

How rough are the off-road days?

Days 7 and 8 are on stony desert tracks, with sandy patches, so the 4×4 rocks and bumps for a few hours. Our drivers know these tracks well and stop often. There is no climbing or walking needed, and children usually love it.

Why a night in Ouzina?

Merzouga to Zagora by the desert tracks is too long for one comfortable day. A night in Ouzina, a small oasis with its own quiet dunes, splits the crossing and gives you an evening far from any crowd.

Is it safe near the Algerian border?

Yes. The tracks pass through Moroccan army and police checkpoints, which are routine. Keep your passport handy; your driver does the talking and has made this crossing many times.

When is the best time for this tour?

Spring and autumn, roughly March to May and September to November. Summer is very hot on the desert tracks, and winter nights are cold in the desert and over the Atlas, though the days are often sunny.

Will you meet us at the ferry or the airport?

Yes, at any hour. Ferries from Tarifa arrive in the city port of Tangier; from Algeciras at Tanger Med, about 45 minutes away. Your driver-guide waits at the exit with your name and follows any delay.
