Tour Overview

Discover Morocco from north to south on this unforgettable 13-Day Morocco Tour from Tangier to Marrakech. This private journey combines the blue streets of Chefchaouen, the historic Medina of Fes, the dramatic landscapes of the Middle Atlas, the golden dunes of Merzouga, the remote desert village of Ouzina, the Draa Valley and Zagora, Ouarzazate, Aït Ben Haddou and the High Atlas Mountains.

The route is designed for travelers who want more than a quick visit to the famous cities. After exploring northern Morocco, you will travel deep into the south and experience two contrasting desert regions: Erg Chebbi near Merzouga and the quieter landscapes around Ouzina and Zagora.

You will enjoy camel trekking, a Sahara Desert camp, desert 4×4 excursions, visits to nomadic communities, oasis landscapes, ancient kasbahs and scenic mountain roads before finishing in Marrakech.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Tangier: Arrival & City Tour

Your 13-day Morocco tour from Tangier to Marrakech begins with a private pickup from Tangier Ibn Battuta Airport, the ferry terminal or your hotel.

Begin exploring Tangier, the fascinating city overlooking the meeting point of the Mediterranean Sea and Atlantic Ocean. Visit the historic Kasbah and walk through the narrow streets of the old Medina.

Continue to the Grand Socco, one of Tangier's lively central squares, before heading toward Cape Spartel. From here, enjoy impressive views over the Atlantic Ocean and Mediterranean Sea. You can also visit the nearby Caves of Hercules if your arrival time allows.

In the afternoon, return to central Tangier and enjoy free time to explore the Medina, browse local shops or relax at a traditional café. Your first evening is free to enjoy dinner and discover Tangier's unique atmosphere.

Tangier Breakfast
Day 2

Tangier → Chefchaouen

After breakfast, leave Tangier and travel south through the Rif Mountains toward Chefchaouen, Morocco's famous Blue City.

The journey takes you into the mountains, where the scenery becomes greener and more rugged. On arrival, check into your riad and begin exploring the charming old Medina.

Walk through the blue-painted streets and discover small squares, local craft shops, traditional houses and colorful doorways. Visit Plaza Uta el-Hammam and the historic Kasbah before enjoying lunch at a local restaurant.

The afternoon can be spent at a relaxed pace. You can climb toward the Spanish Mosque for panoramic views over Chefchaouen or simply wander through the quiet streets of the Medina.

Chefchaouen is one of the most photogenic destinations in Morocco, but its appeal goes beyond its blue architecture. The surrounding Rif Mountains and relaxed atmosphere make it a wonderful contrast to busy Moroccan cities.

~115 km Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 3

Chefchaouen → Fes

Enjoy breakfast before leaving Chefchaouen and continuing toward Fes, one of Morocco's most important cultural and historic cities.

The road takes you through the Rif and northern Moroccan countryside before reaching the Fes region.

Depending on your departure time, you can stop for lunch along the way. Arrive in Fes in the afternoon and check into your traditional riad inside or near the historic Medina.

You can spend the rest of the day relaxing or take an introductory walk through the surrounding area. Your guide can also recommend a traditional Moroccan restaurant for dinner.

Fes is famous for its centuries-old Medina, traditional crafts, souks and historic architecture. Enjoy your first evening in Fes and experience the atmosphere of one of Morocco's oldest imperial cities.

~200 km Fes Breakfast
Day 4

Full-Day Fes Sightseeing Tour

Today is dedicated to discovering Fes with a local licensed guide.

Enter the historic Medina and explore its maze of narrow streets, traditional souks and artisan workshops. Visit the Bou Inania Madrasa, the Nejjarine area and the famous leather tanneries.

Continue through the old city to discover traditional Moroccan craftsmanship, including zellige tilework, pottery, leather and metalwork.

After lunch, visit the Mellah, the historic Jewish Quarter, and continue to viewpoints overlooking the ancient city. Your guide can adapt the tour according to your interests, whether you are particularly interested in architecture, history, culture, food or shopping.

Fes offers a completely different experience from Chefchaouen, with a much larger and more historic Medina. Return to your riad in the evening and enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner.

Fes Breakfast
Day 5

Fes → Ifrane → Cedar Forest → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

Today begins your journey toward the Sahara Desert. Leave Fes after breakfast and travel through the Middle Atlas Mountains. Stop in Ifrane, often known for its distinctive European-style architecture and alpine surroundings.

Continue through the Cedar Forest near Azrou, where you may see Barbary macaques in the area. The road then crosses the Middle Atlas toward Midelt, known for its agricultural landscapes and mountain surroundings.

After lunch, continue toward the dramatic Ziz Valley. Stop at a panoramic viewpoint overlooking the extensive palm-filled oasis.

As you continue south, the landscape gradually changes from mountains to desert. By late afternoon, you will reach Merzouga and the dunes of Erg Chebbi. Depending on your arrival time, you can begin your camel trek across the dunes and watch the sunset from the Sahara.

Arrive at your desert camp for dinner, traditional music and a night beneath the stars.

~470 km Private Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & Dinner
Day 6

Merzouga: Full-Day Sahara Desert Exploration

Wake early for a spectacular Sahara sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes. After breakfast at the camp, return to Merzouga by camel or 4×4.

Today is dedicated to discovering the desert region surrounding Erg Chebbi.

Visit the Hassi Labied oasis and, depending on seasonal conditions, Lake Dayet Srij. Continue to Khamlia village, where you can experience traditional Gnawa music and learn about the cultural heritage of the region.

You will also have the opportunity to meet a local nomadic family. Share Moroccan tea and learn about their traditional way of life in the desert. After lunch, explore desert tracks and rocky landscapes by private 4×4. Optional activities such as quad biking and sandboarding can also be arranged.

Return to Merzouga in the afternoon and enjoy some free time to relax around your hotel or explore the dunes on foot.

~40 km Merzouga Breakfast & Dinner
Day 7

Merzouga → Ouzina → Ramlia → Desert Tracks

Leave Merzouga after breakfast and travel deeper into the remote desert landscapes toward Ouzina.

This is one of the most adventurous days of the itinerary. Instead of following the main paved road, you will travel through desert tracks and isolated landscapes between Erg Chebbi and the Ouzina region.

Stop along the way for photographs and enjoy the silence of the desert away from the busier tourist routes. Depending on the conditions, the route can include the area around Ramlia and other small desert settlements.

Ouzina is known for its impressive red sand dunes and remote atmosphere. The area provides a more intimate experience of southeastern Morocco and is ideal for travelers looking to discover a quieter side of the Sahara.

In the afternoon, arrive at your accommodation near Ouzina and enjoy the peaceful desert surroundings. Spend the evening with a traditional Moroccan dinner and enjoy the clear desert sky.

~120 km Ouzina Breakfast & Dinner
Day 8

Ouzina → Alnif → Tazzarine → Zagora

After breakfast, continue your journey west toward Zagora.

Leave the remote Ouzina desert behind and travel through the dramatic landscapes of southern Morocco. The road passes through desert plateaus, mountain landscapes and small Berber villages.

Continue toward Alnif, an area known for fossils and geological landscapes. Depending on your interests, you can make a short stop before continuing toward Tazzarine.

The scenery gradually changes as you approach the Draa Valley. Palm groves begin to appear and the landscape becomes greener.

Continue to Zagora, an important town at the entrance to the Draa Valley and historically connected with trans-Saharan caravan routes. After checking into your accommodation, enjoy free time to relax or explore the town. In the evening, enjoy dinner and the peaceful atmosphere of the southern oasis region.

~250 km Zagora Breakfast & Dinner
Day 9

Zagora → Draa Valley → Agdz → Ouarzazate

After breakfast, begin your journey through the spectacular Draa Valley, one of Morocco's most impressive oasis landscapes.

The valley is lined with thousands of palm trees, traditional villages and ancient kasbahs. Along the route, stop at panoramic viewpoints and small villages to experience the scenery and local way of life.

Continue toward Agdz, an oasis town surrounded by palm groves and historic kasbahs. You can visit Tamnougalt Kasbah, one of the area's most interesting historical sites, depending on your schedule.

After lunch, continue north toward Ouarzazate. The landscape gradually changes from oasis to mountains and rocky plateaus.

Arrive in Ouarzazate in the afternoon. Known as the gateway to the desert and a center of Morocco's film industry, the city makes a convenient base for exploring the surrounding kasbahs and valleys.

~170 km Ouarzazate Breakfast & Dinner
Day 10

Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → Ounila Valley → Marrakech

After breakfast, visit Aït Ben Haddou, one of Morocco's most famous fortified villages.

Explore the historic ksar with your guide and walk through its narrow lanes between traditional earthen buildings. The site is particularly famous for its architecture and connections with international film productions.

Continue toward Marrakech through the landscapes of the High Atlas. If road and weather conditions allow, the route can include scenic mountain viewpoints and Berber villages.

Cross the Tizi n'Tichka Pass, one of the major mountain crossings between southern Morocco and Marrakech. Stop for photographs along the way before descending toward the Marrakech plain.

Arrive in Marrakech in the late afternoon or evening and check into your riad or hotel. After several days exploring the desert and southern valleys, Marrakech provides a lively change of atmosphere.

~200 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 11

Full-Day Marrakech Sightseeing Tour

Today you will explore the highlights of Marrakech with a local guide.

Begin with the Koutoubia Mosque and continue to the historic Saadian Tombs and Bahia Palace. These sites offer an introduction to Marrakech's architecture, royal history and traditional craftsmanship.

After lunch, enter the colorful souks of the Medina. Walk through the narrow streets filled with local artisans selling carpets, leather goods, spices, lamps, pottery and other Moroccan products.

Continue to Jemaa el-Fna, Marrakech's famous central square. During the day it is a busy market area, while in the evening it becomes one of the city's liveliest gathering places.

If you prefer gardens and modern Marrakech, you can also visit the Majorelle Garden as an optional addition. Spend the evening exploring Marrakech at your own pace.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 12

Free Day in Marrakech

Enjoy a relaxed day in Marrakech with no long-distance driving.

After breakfast, you are free to explore the city independently or choose from several optional activities.

You can return to the souks for shopping, visit additional museums and palaces, enjoy a traditional hammam or simply relax at your riad.

For travelers interested in Moroccan food, this is also an excellent opportunity to take a cooking class or enjoy a guided food experience. Alternatively, we can arrange a private day trip to the Atlas Mountains, Ourika Valley or another nearby destination.

Keeping this day free gives you time to enjoy Marrakech without feeling that every hour of your vacation is scheduled. In the evening, enjoy your final Moroccan dinner and take one last walk through the Medina and Jemaa el-Fna.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 13

Marrakech: Departure

Enjoy your final breakfast in Morocco.

Depending on your flight time, you can have some free time for last-minute shopping, a relaxed coffee or a final walk through the Medina.

At the agreed time, your private driver will transfer you to Marrakech Menara Airport or another agreed departure point.

Your 13-day Morocco tour from Tangier to Marrakech ends after an unforgettable journey through northern Morocco, imperial cities, the Sahara Desert, remote desert villages, oasis valleys, kasbahs and the High Atlas Mountains.

End of tour: Marrakech Breakfast

Why Choose This Tour?

This 13-day Tangier to Marrakech Morocco tour is designed for travelers who want to experience both the famous highlights and the less-visited landscapes of southern Morocco.

The route does not simply take you from one major city to another. It combines Morocco's northern Mediterranean atmosphere with Chefchaouen and Fes, then gradually moves south through the Atlas Mountains into the Sahara.

The inclusion of Merzouga, Ouzina and Zagora gives you three very different desert experiences. Merzouga offers the iconic Erg Chebbi dunes and camel trekking, while Ouzina provides a quieter and more remote desert atmosphere. Zagora introduces you to the vast Draa Valley and its palm-filled oasis landscapes.

The final section through Ouarzazate, Aït Ben Haddou and the High Atlas creates a spectacular transition from the desert back to Marrakech.

Because this is a private and fully customizable tour, the itinerary can be adapted to your preferred travel pace, accommodation category and interests.

FAQ

Is 13 days enough for this Morocco tour?

Yes. Thirteen days gives you enough time to combine northern Morocco, Fes, the Sahara and the major attractions of southern Morocco without making every day a long driving day.

Does the tour include Chefchaouen?

Yes. Chefchaouen is included between Tangier and Fes, with an overnight stay allowing you to explore the Blue City at a relaxed pace.

How many nights do we spend in Fes?

You spend two nights in Fes, including a full day for sightseeing.

Does this tour include the Sahara Desert?

Yes. Merzouga and Erg Chebbi are central parts of the itinerary, including camel trekking, a private desert camp and a full-day 4×4 desert excursion.

Why include Ouzina?

Ouzina provides a quieter and more remote desert experience than the main Merzouga route. It is particularly suitable for travelers who want to discover less-visited landscapes and desert communities.

Is Zagora included?

Yes. You spend a night in Zagora and travel through the beautiful Draa Valley before continuing to Agdz and Ouarzazate.

Do we visit Aït Ben Haddou?

Yes. Aït Ben Haddou is included on the way from Ouarzazate to Marrakech.

Is Marrakech sightseeing included?

Yes. Day 11 includes a guided Marrakech sightseeing tour, followed by a free day on Day 12.

Is this a private tour?

Yes. Your group will have a private vehicle and driver throughout the tour.

Can we change the accommodation level?

Yes. We can arrange Standard, Mid-Range or Luxury accommodation according to your budget and preferences.

Can we add Essaouira?

Yes. If you have additional days available, the itinerary can be extended to include Essaouira.

Can the tour end at Marrakech Airport?

Yes. We can arrange your final transfer directly to Marrakech Menara Airport according to your flight schedule.
