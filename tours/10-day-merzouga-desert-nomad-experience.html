Tour Overview

Discover the best of southern and central Morocco on this 10-Day Morocco Tour from Fes, combining historic cities, mountain landscapes, ancient kasbahs and the spectacular Sahara Desert.

Starting in Fes, this private journey takes you through the Middle Atlas Mountains before reaching Merzouga and the Erg Chebbi dunes. The Sahara experience goes beyond a simple camel ride: you will spend time with a local nomadic family, discover traditional desert communities and explore the surrounding desert by private 4×4.

Continue through the Ziz Valley, Todra Gorge, Dades Valley, Valley of Roses and Ouarzazate before visiting the famous Aït Ben Haddou. The final part of the journey takes you across the High Atlas Mountains to Marrakech, with a private day trip to Essaouira before departure.

"More than a trip... It's a story to tell!"

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Fes: Arrival

Your 10-Day Morocco Tour from Fes begins with your arrival in Fes. Your private driver will meet you at Fes-Saïss Airport, train station or another agreed location and transfer you to your hotel or traditional riad in the city.

Depending on your arrival time, you can enjoy a relaxed introduction to Fes or take some time to settle into your accommodation. The city is one of Morocco's most important cultural and spiritual centers, famous for its historic medina, traditional crafts and centuries-old architecture.

In the evening, enjoy your first Moroccan dinner and prepare for the journey ahead.

If you arrive early enough, your driver can also suggest a short walk or viewpoint overlooking the old city.

Fes Breakfast
Day 2

Fes: Guided City Tour

After breakfast, enjoy a full guided tour of Fes with a local licensed guide. Explore the historic medina, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and discover its maze of narrow streets filled with markets, workshops and traditional architecture.

Visit important sites such as the Bou Inania Madrasa, Nejjarine area and traditional artisan quarters. Depending on access and opening times, you can also see the famous tanneries and learn about the traditional leather-making process.

Your guide will introduce you to the history, architecture and traditions that make Fes one of Morocco's most fascinating cities.

Take a break for lunch at a local restaurant before continuing your exploration.

The afternoon can be dedicated to shopping, discovering additional parts of the medina or relaxing at your riad. Your private driver remains available for transfers outside the medina.

Fes Breakfast
Day 3

Fes → Ifrane → Azrou → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

After breakfast, leave Fes and begin your journey toward the Sahara Desert. Travel through the Middle Atlas Mountains, with your first stop in Ifrane, a distinctive mountain town known for its cool climate and European-style architecture.

Continue through the cedar forests near Azrou, where Barbary macaques may sometimes be seen. The road then leads toward Midelt, surrounded by impressive mountain scenery.

After lunch, continue toward the spectacular Ziz Valley. Stop at a panoramic viewpoint overlooking the palm-filled oasis and rocky landscapes.

As you approach Merzouga, the landscape changes dramatically and the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi begin to appear.

In the late afternoon, enjoy a camel trek across the dunes. Watch the desert sunset before reaching your private Sahara camp. Enjoy dinner, traditional Berber music and a peaceful evening beneath the stars.

~470 km Private Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & dinner
Day 4

Merzouga: Full Sahara & Nomad Experience

Wake early to watch the sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes before breakfast. Today is dedicated to experiencing the Sahara and learning more about the people who call this desert home.

Travel by private 4×4 to visit a local nomadic family living in the desert region. Spend time learning about their traditional lifestyle, daily routines and relationship with the surrounding environment. This provides a more personal perspective on desert life than simply visiting the dunes.

Continue to Khamlia village, known for its Gnawa music and cultural traditions. You can also explore areas where fossils and minerals are found and discover different landscapes surrounding Erg Chebbi.

After lunch, enjoy some free time. Try sandboarding, relax at your accommodation or simply enjoy the views.

In the afternoon, return toward the dunes for another beautiful desert sunset before spending a relaxed evening in Merzouga.

~40 km Merzouga Breakfast
Day 5

Merzouga → Rissani → Erfoud → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley

After breakfast, leave Merzouga and continue toward the Dades Valley. Begin with a stop in Rissani, a historic trading center on the edge of the Sahara. If the traditional souk is open, explore its lively atmosphere and discover local products.

Continue to Erfoud, famous for its fossil and mineral workshops. This is an interesting stop for travelers interested in geology and Morocco's ancient landscapes.

Continue through the changing scenery toward the Todra Gorge. Take time to walk through the dramatic canyon, surrounded by towering limestone cliffs.

After the gorge, continue through the oasis landscapes toward the Dades Valley. Along the way, you will see palm groves, traditional villages and impressive rock formations.

Arrive at your hotel or riad in the afternoon and enjoy a relaxing evening in the valley.

~300 km Dades Valley Breakfast
Day 6

Dades Valley → Valley of Roses → Skoura → Ouarzazate

After breakfast, continue through the beautiful landscapes of the Dades Valley. Travel along the famous Road of a Thousand Kasbahs toward the Valley of Roses, known for its rose cultivation and traditional cosmetic products.

Continue to the oasis of Skoura, where palm groves surround historic kasbahs and agricultural fields. This peaceful landscape offers excellent opportunities for photographs and short walks.

Continue to Ouarzazate, often called the gateway to the Sahara. The city has a long connection with the film industry and has served as a location for numerous international productions.

Depending on your interests and opening times, you can visit a film-related attraction or explore the town's kasbah area.

Enjoy a relaxed evening at your accommodation and prepare for the following day's journey toward Aït Ben Haddou and Marrakech.

~120 km Ouarzazate Breakfast
Day 7

Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → High Atlas Mountains → Marrakech

After breakfast, visit Aït Ben Haddou, one of Morocco's most famous fortified villages and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Walk through its narrow paths and discover the traditional earthen architecture built into the hillside.

After exploring the kasbah, continue toward the High Atlas Mountains. Depending on the route and conditions, there may be opportunities to stop for photographs and enjoy views of traditional mountain villages.

Cross the Tizi n'Tichka Pass, one of Morocco's most scenic mountain routes connecting the southeast with Marrakech.

As you descend from the mountains, the landscape gradually changes from rugged peaks to the plains surrounding Marrakech.

Arrive in Marrakech in the late afternoon or evening and check into your riad or hotel. The evening is free to relax or take a first walk around the nearby area.

~200 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 8

Marrakech: Guided City Tour

After breakfast, meet your local licensed guide for a private tour of Marrakech. Discover the historic medina and some of the city's most important landmarks.

Explore the lively souks, traditional artisan workshops and historic streets before visiting famous attractions such as the Bahia Palace, Koutoubia Mosque area and other important parts of the old city, depending on opening times.

Continue toward Jemaa el-Fnaa, the famous central square, where you can experience the atmosphere of Marrakech and discover local food, crafts and traditional entertainment.

Enjoy lunch at a local restaurant before continuing your exploration.

The afternoon can be adapted to your interests. You may prefer additional sightseeing, shopping in the souks or relaxing at your accommodation. In the evening, enjoy free time in Marrakech and discover the city's restaurants and lively atmosphere.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 9

Marrakech → Essaouira → Marrakech

Today, enjoy a private day trip from Marrakech to Essaouira, a beautiful Atlantic coastal city known for its historic medina, fishing harbor and relaxed atmosphere.

Leave Marrakech after breakfast and travel west through the countryside. Depending on the season, you may see the famous argan trees and learn more about the importance of argan products in local communities.

On arrival in Essaouira, explore the historic medina, walk along the old ramparts and visit the lively fishing port. Enjoy fresh seafood or another local lunch before having free time to explore the charming streets and artisan shops.

The city's coastal atmosphere provides a pleasant contrast to the inland landscapes experienced earlier in the tour.

In the afternoon, begin the return journey to Marrakech. Arrive in the evening and enjoy your final night in Morocco.

~380 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 10

Marrakech: Departure

After breakfast, enjoy some free time depending on your departure schedule. You may use the morning for a final walk around Marrakech, shopping for souvenirs or simply relaxing at your accommodation.

Your private driver will then transfer you to Marrakech Menara Airport or another agreed departure point.

This marks the end of your 10-Day Morocco Tour from Fes, after a journey covering historic Fes, the Middle Atlas Mountains, the Sahara Desert, authentic nomadic experiences, Todra Gorge, Dades Valley, Aït Ben Haddou, the High Atlas Mountains and Marrakech.

Whether you are continuing your travels or flying home, you will leave Morocco with a deeper understanding of its landscapes, traditions and local communities.

End of tour: Marrakech Breakfast
