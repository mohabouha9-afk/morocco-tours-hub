Overview

Aït Ben Haddou is the earthen ksar of Gladiator and Game of Thrones: a UNESCO-listed village of clay kasbahs rising above the Ounila river, on the old caravan road between the Sahara and Marrakech.

It is the longest day trip from Marrakech, about 3.5 hours each way over the Tizi n’Tichka pass, so we make the hours count: an early start, your own guide in the ksar, and time to climb to the granary instead of a one-hour photo stop.

Programme

  1. 1
    07:30

    Pick-up in Marrakech

    Your driver meets you at your hotel, or at the nearest gate if your riad is in the medina. We confirm the exact time and place on WhatsApp the day before.

  2. 2
    09:00

    Over the Tizi n’Tichka pass

    The road climbs through Berber villages to 2,260 m, with stops at the viewpoints and for coffee. Many bends: ask for the front seat if you get car-sick.

  3. 3
    11:00

    The ksar of Aït Ben Haddou

    Your guide leads you across the river and up the clay alleys, past the film locations, to the old granary and its view over the valley. About two hours, at your pace.

  4. 4
    13:15

    Lunch facing the kasbah

    Lunch on a terrace across the river: tagine, couscous or salads, around €10–12 a person (not included). Your guide checks the price first.

  5. 5
    14:30

    Ouarzazate: studios or kasbah

    Twenty minutes on to Ouarzazate for a guided visit of the Atlas Film Studios or the Taourirt Kasbah, the old Glaoui palace: your choice, about an hour.

  6. 6
    16:00

    Back over the mountains

    About 3.5 hours back with a coffee stop, at your hotel or riad around 19:30, or straight to a restaurant if you go out for dinner.

Good to know

  • This is a suggestion: tell us your preferences
  • A long day: about 7 hours on the road in total
  • The pass can be cold or snowy from December to February; the ksar is hot in summer
  • Steep, uneven alleys: wear walking shoes; not possible with wheelchairs or strollers
  • Road works on the Tichka road can add some time
  • Bring a layer and sun protection, whatever the season

Costs on the day

  • Lunch facing the kasbah: around €10–12 per person (about 100–120 MAD)
  • Atlas Film Studios: about 80 MAD per person, guided visit
  • Taourirt Kasbah: a small entry fee
  • Houses inside the ksar: a few dirhams, only if you go in
  • Tips: up to you
  • Cash: bring some dirhams for entries, cafés and toilet stops

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it worth it as a day trip?

It is a long day: about 7 hours on the road for 4 hours at the sites. If you love history or films and cannot fit in a longer trip, yes. If you have the time, sleep in Aït Ben Haddou for sunset, or make it day one of a Sahara tour.

Do we have to pay to enter the ksar?

No, there is no ticket. Men by the river sometimes ask for an “entrance fee”: it is not real. A family showing you inside their home may ask a few dirhams, which is fair. Your guide is already in the price, so nobody else needs paying.

Is the Tizi n’Tichka road safe?

Yes: it is a paved main road, wider than it was, with many bends and some road works that can add time. Our drivers know it well and stop whenever you need. If snow closes the pass in winter, we change the plan or the date.

Atlas Film Studios or Taourirt Kasbah?

The studios show sets from famous films on a guided tour (about 80 MAD) and are fun for film fans and children. The Taourirt Kasbah is a real 19th-century palace of the Glaoui family. There is time for one; tell us which you prefer.

Which films were shot here?

Aït Ben Haddou has appeared in Lawrence of Arabia, Gladiator, The Mummy, Kingdom of Heaven and Game of Thrones, among many others. Your guide points out the scenes, and some villagers will tell you about the films they were extras in.

How do I book, and what if it snows?

  1. Send your request with the form or on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay a small deposit to confirm; the rest is paid on the day, in cash or by card.

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before. If snow closes the pass, you choose a new date or a refund.
