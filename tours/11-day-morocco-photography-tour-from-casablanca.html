Tour Overview

Discover Morocco through your camera on this 11-Day Morocco Photography Tour from Casablanca, combining historic cities, colorful medinas, mountain landscapes, traditional villages, dramatic valleys and the Sahara Desert.

This private Morocco photography tour is designed for travelers who want to experience the country at a comfortable pace while having time to stop for beautiful landscapes, architecture and local scenes. From the Atlantic coast of Casablanca and Rabat to the blue streets of Chefchaouen and the ancient medina of Fes, every part of the journey offers something different to photograph.

The southern part of the tour brings you into the Atlas Mountains and Sahara, with two nights around Merzouga, a camel trek, desert sunrise and sunset, a visit to a nomadic family and time to explore the desert by 4x4.

"More than a trip... It's a story to tell!"

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Arrival in Casablanca → Hassan II Mosque → Rabat

Your 11-day Morocco photography tour from Casablanca begins with a private pickup from Casablanca airport or your hotel.

Start by exploring Casablanca and the Atlantic coast. Depending on your arrival time, enjoy a photography stop around the Hassan II Mosque and the surrounding coastline, followed by a drive through some of the city's most interesting areas.

Continue to Rabat, Morocco's capital. In the afternoon, visit the Kasbah of the Udayas, where whitewashed walls, traditional doors and views toward the Atlantic create beautiful photography opportunities.

Enjoy a relaxed evening in Rabat.

~≈90 km km Rabat Breakfast
Day 2

Rabat → Chefchaouen

After breakfast, travel north through the Moroccan countryside toward the Rif Mountains and Chefchaouen.

Arrive in the afternoon and begin exploring the famous blue medina. Take your time photographing the narrow streets, blue walls, traditional doors, stairs, small shops and mountain scenery.

The private format gives you flexibility to stop whenever you find an interesting composition. In the late afternoon, the changing light creates a different atmosphere throughout the blue streets.

Enjoy a relaxed evening in Chefchaouen and continue exploring the medina after the busiest hours.

~≈250 km km Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 3

Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Meknes → Fes

Begin the morning with a final photography walk through Chefchaouen before leaving the Rif Mountains.

Continue toward Volubilis and explore the ancient Roman ruins surrounded by open countryside. The archaeological remains, columns and mosaics provide opportunities for historical and landscape photography.

Continue to Meknes for a short visit and photography stop around the old city and Bab Mansour before traveling onward to Fes.

Arrive in Fes in the afternoon and enjoy your first walk through the historic medina.

~≈300 km km Fes Breakfast
Day 4

Fes: Full-Day Photography Experience

Today is dedicated to Fes photography.

Explore the old medina with a local guide and discover traditional architecture, colorful souks, artisan workshops, historic gates, riads and narrow streets.

You will have time to photograph Moroccan crafts and everyday life, including traditional woodworking, metalwork, textiles and other local details.

The afternoon remains flexible so you can spend more time in the areas that interest you most or return to locations when the light becomes better.

~City tour km Fes Breakfast
Day 5

Fes → Ifrane → Cedar Forest → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

Today begins your journey toward the Sahara. Leave Fes and cross the Middle Atlas Mountains, with stops around Ifrane and the cedar forests before continuing toward Midelt.

The landscape gradually changes from green mountain scenery to more open and arid surroundings. Continue through the Ziz Valley and stop at a panoramic viewpoint to photograph the contrast between the palm oasis and surrounding mountains.

Continue toward Erfoud and Rissani before reaching Merzouga.

In the afternoon, begin your camel trek into the dunes and enjoy your first Sahara sunset.

~≈470 km km Luxury Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & Dinner
Day 6

Merzouga: Desert Photography & Local Life

Wake early for Sahara sunrise photography. From the dunes near the camp, watch the first light move across the desert before returning for breakfast.

Later, explore the Merzouga area by private 4x4. Visit the Hassi Labied oasis, Khamlia village and a local nomadic family. You will have time to photograph the different landscapes and discover another side of life in the Sahara.

Depending on seasonal conditions, continue to Lake Dayet Srij before returning toward Merzouga.

The afternoon is reserved for another desert photography session and sunset among the dunes.

~≈40 km km Merzouga Breakfast
Day 7

Merzouga → Rissani → Erfoud → Todra Gorge → Dades

Leave the Sahara after breakfast and travel through Rissani and Erfoud toward the Todra region.

Along the route, stop for photographs of desert villages, palm groves and changing landscapes.

Continue to Todra Gorge, where towering rock walls create a dramatic setting for landscape photography. Take a walk through the gorge before continuing toward the Dades Valley.

Arrive in the afternoon and explore the surrounding valley. The winding roads, unusual rock formations and traditional villages provide excellent opportunities for photography.

~≈300 km km Dades Valley Breakfast & Dinner
Day 8

Dades → Rose Valley → Skoura → Ouarzazate

After breakfast, continue through the Valley of Roses toward Skoura.

This section of the journey offers a completely different photographic experience, with palm groves, mud-brick villages and traditional kasbahs surrounded by the Atlas landscape.

Stop in Skoura to explore the oasis and photograph the traditional architecture before continuing to Ouarzazate.

Known as a gateway to southern Morocco, Ouarzazate is surrounded by kasbahs and dramatic landscapes. Spend the afternoon exploring the town or photographing the surrounding scenery.

~≈160 km km Ouarzazate Breakfast
Day 9

Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → High Atlas → Marrakech

Today you continue toward Marrakech, with a major photography stop at Aït Ben Haddou.

Explore the narrow streets and traditional earthen architecture of the historic ksar. Take time to photograph it from different viewpoints and capture the surrounding landscape.

After the visit, continue through the High Atlas Mountains. The changing scenery offers opportunities for panoramic photography and photographs of mountain villages along the route.

Cross the Tizi n'Tichka area before descending toward Marrakech. Arrive in Marrakech in the late afternoon.

~≈200 km km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 10

Marrakech: Full-Day Photography Experience

Today is dedicated to Marrakech photography.

Explore the old medina, colorful souks, traditional riads, historic doors and architectural details. Walk through the narrow streets and discover the different colors and textures of the Red City.

Visit the area around Jemaa el-Fna and the Koutoubia before continuing through the souks.

The afternoon can be used for additional photography walks or to return to locations when the light becomes softer. Marrakech provides a completely different photographic atmosphere from the blue streets of Chefchaouen and the open landscapes of the Sahara.

~City tour km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 11

Marrakech: Departure

Enjoy a relaxed final morning in Marrakech depending on your departure time.

Take a final walk through the medina, photograph the traditional architecture or spend some time exploring the local souks.

Your private driver will then transfer you to Marrakech airport or another agreed departure point.

Your 11-Day Morocco Photography Tour from Casablanca ends after a journey through Morocco's Atlantic coast, Rif Mountains, imperial cities, Atlas landscapes, Sahara Desert, valleys and southern kasbahs.

~Departure transfer km End of tour Breakfast

Why Choose This Tour?

This private Morocco photography tour combines some of the country's most varied landscapes and cultural experiences without making the itinerary feel rushed.

You will have opportunities for street photography in Fes and Chefchaouen, architectural photography in the imperial cities and southern kasbahs, landscape photography in the Atlas Mountains and valleys, and desert photography in Merzouga.

The two nights around Merzouga give you more time to experience the Sahara instead of arriving for only one evening and leaving the next morning.

Because the tour is private, photography stops can be adapted to your interests and pace.

FAQ

Is this a private Morocco photography tour?

Yes. This is a private tour with your own vehicle and driver. There are no shared groups.

Do I need professional photography equipment?

No. The tour is suitable for professional photographers, hobby photographers and travelers who simply enjoy photography.

Can we make extra photography stops?

Yes. The private itinerary allows you to stop at viewpoints, villages and landscapes whenever appropriate.

How many nights do we spend in the Sahara?

The itinerary includes two nights around Merzouga, including one night at a Sahara desert camp.

Can the tour be customized?

Yes. The route, accommodation category, activities and pace can be adjusted for your travel dates and photography interests.
