Overview

Watch the sun come up over the Atlas from a hot air balloon. For about an hour you drift quietly over villages, palm groves and the stony hills outside Marrakech, with the snowy peaks on the horizon.

We pick you up privately before dawn, fly with experienced licensed pilots and finish with a Berber breakfast, so you are back at your hotel by mid-morning with the whole day still ahead.

Programme

  1. 1
    05:30

    Pick-up before dawn

    Your driver collects you about 1.5 hours before sunrise: around 05:00 in summer, 06:30 in winter. We confirm the exact time on WhatsApp the day before.

  2. 2
    06:15

    Tea while the balloon fills

    At the take-off site you warm up with mint tea and pastries while the crew inflates the balloon and the pilot gives the safety briefing.

  3. 3
    07:00

    Sunrise flight

    About an hour in the air as the sun comes up over the Atlas, drifting over villages, palm groves and stony hills at up to around 1,000 m.

  4. 4
    08:00

    Landing

    The pilot shows you the landing position, then sets down gently wherever the wind has taken you. The ground crew follows and drives you back.

  5. 5
    08:30

    Berber breakfast

    Breakfast in a tent: pancakes, bread, eggs, olives, honey and mint tea, with your flight certificate.

  6. 6
    09:30

    Back to Marrakech

    At your hotel or riad by about 10:00–10:30 (times shift with the sunrise), with the rest of the day free.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a balloon flight in Marrakech safe?

With an established, licensed operator, yes: thousands fly here every year. Problems are rare and almost always linked to taking off in strong wind, which is why the pilot’s no-fly decision is final. We only work with experienced, licensed teams, never the cheapest.

What happens if the weather is bad?

If the pilots cancel for wind or weather, you choose another morning or a full refund. Book the flight early in your stay, so there is a spare morning if it moves.

How many people share the basket?

Shared baskets usually carry about 16 to 20 people, divided into compartments, so everyone has a place at the edge. Prefer just the two of you or your family? A private basket can be arranged.

What time is the pick-up?

About 1.5 hours before sunrise: around 05:00 in summer and 06:30 in winter. You are back at your hotel by about 10:00–10:30. Do not plan it on the morning you fly home.

Will I feel sick or afraid of heights?

Most people are surprised how calm it is: the balloon moves with the wind, so there is no breeze, no bumps and no feeling of falling. The basket sides are chest-high with handles to hold.

How do I book and pay?

  1. Send your request with the form or on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay a small deposit to confirm; the rest is paid on the day, in cash or by card.

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before the flight.
