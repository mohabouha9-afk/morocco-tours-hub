Overview

Watch the sun come up over the Atlas from a hot air balloon. The flight drifts quietly over villages, palm groves and the stony desert outside Marrakech, with the mountains on the horizon.

After landing you share mint tea and a Berber breakfast before your driver takes you back to your hotel, usually by mid-morning, with the rest of the day free.

Highlights

  • Sunrise flight of about 1 hour
  • Views of the High Atlas from the air
  • Licensed, experienced pilots
  • Berber breakfast after landing
  • Flight certificate
  • Back at your hotel by mid-morning

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if the weather is bad?

Safety comes first. If the pilots cancel, we move your flight to another morning or refund it.

How high does the balloon fly?

It changes with the wind, usually from a few hundred metres up to around 1,000 metres.

Is the flight private?

Your transfer is private. Shared baskets are standard; a private basket for two can be booked on request.
