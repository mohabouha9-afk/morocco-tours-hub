Tour Overview

Experience the best of Morocco on this unforgettable 10-day private tour from Casablanca to Marrakech, combining imperial cities, the magical Blue City of Chefchaouen, ancient Roman ruins, the cultural treasures of Fes and the spectacular Sahara Desert.

Your journey begins in Casablanca before continuing to Morocco's capital, Rabat, and the enchanting blue streets of Chefchaouen. Discover Volubilis and Meknes before spending time exploring the historic medina of Fes. From there, cross the Middle Atlas Mountains and continue toward Merzouga and the golden Erg Chebbi dunes.

Enjoy two nights in the Sahara region, giving you more time to experience the desert without rushing. Continue through Rissani, Erfoud, Todra Gorge and Dades Valley before discovering Ouarzazate and the UNESCO-listed Aït Ben Haddou. Finally, cross the High Atlas Mountains and finish your Moroccan adventure in Marrakech.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Casablanca → Rabat

Your Moroccan adventure begins with pickup at Casablanca airport or your accommodation. Start by discovering Casablanca, Morocco's largest city and important economic center. Visit the spectacular Hassan II Mosque, dramatically located beside the Atlantic Ocean, and enjoy time along the Corniche. Depending on your arrival time, you may also explore the Habous Quarter and Place Mohammed V. In the afternoon, continue to Rabat, Morocco's elegant capital. Discover the historic Kasbah of the Udayas, the Hassan Tower and the impressive Mausoleum of Mohammed V. Rabat offers a wonderful combination of royal history, Andalusian architecture and coastal charm. After sightseeing, check into your traditional riad or comfortable hotel and enjoy a relaxing evening.

~100 km Rabat Breakfast
Day 2

Rabat → Chefchaouen

After breakfast, leave Rabat and travel north toward the Rif Mountains and the famous blue city of Chefchaouen. The journey takes you through beautiful Moroccan countryside before reaching the mountains. Upon arrival, begin exploring Chefchaouen's atmospheric medina, famous for its blue-painted walls, narrow lanes, colorful doors and traditional artisan shops. Walk through Plaza Uta el-Hammam, visit the historic Kasbah and enjoy the relaxed atmosphere of the old town. For panoramic views, you can walk toward the Spanish Mosque overlooking the city and surrounding Rif Mountains. Chefchaouen is one of Morocco's most photogenic destinations and offers a peaceful contrast to the larger imperial cities. Spend the evening enjoying local cuisine or relaxing at your riad.

~200 km Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 3

Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Moulay Idriss → Meknes → Fes

After breakfast, depart Chefchaouen and travel toward the historic heart of Morocco. Your first major stop is Volubilis, the country's most important Roman archaeological site. Explore ancient ruins, impressive columns, preserved mosaics and the remains of this once-important Roman settlement. Continue to Moulay Idriss, a picturesque hilltop town and important pilgrimage destination. Afterward, continue to Meknes, one of Morocco's four imperial cities. Discover the magnificent Bab Mansour gate, the old medina and historic imperial monuments associated with Sultan Moulay Ismail. In the afternoon, continue toward Fes. After checking into your riad, enjoy some free time to relax or explore the surroundings of the ancient city. The evening is yours to experience the atmosphere of Fes at your own pace.

~240 km Fes Breakfast
Day 4

Fes: Full-Day Guided City Tour

Today is dedicated entirely to exploring Fes with a local licensed guide. Discover Fes el-Bali, one of the world's most fascinating medieval medinas, with its maze of narrow streets, traditional workshops and historic architecture. Visit important landmarks such as the Bou Inania Madrasa, Al-Attarine Madrasa, the famous Chouara Tanneries and the historic Nejjarine area. Explore the colorful souks where craftsmen continue traditional Moroccan techniques, including leatherwork, metalwork, pottery and textiles. Your guide will explain the history, architecture and traditions that make Fes one of Morocco's cultural capitals. You will also have time to enjoy lunch in a traditional restaurant and browse the artisan shops. In the afternoon, continue discovering the city or return to your riad to relax. Enjoy a free evening in the medina.

Fes Breakfast
Day 5

Fes → Ifrane → Azrou → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

After breakfast, begin your journey toward the Sahara Desert. Leave Fes and cross the beautiful Middle Atlas Mountains. Stop in Ifrane, known for its European-style architecture and mountain atmosphere, before continuing through the cedar forests around Azrou. You may have the opportunity to see Barbary macaques in their natural environment. Continue to Midelt, surrounded by dramatic mountain scenery and famous for its agricultural produce. The road then descends toward the spectacular Ziz Valley, where palm groves and green oases contrast beautifully with the surrounding rocky landscape. Continue toward Merzouga, where the golden Erg Chebbi dunes gradually appear on the horizon. Arrive in the desert and enjoy a camel trek across the dunes as the sun begins to set. Reach your Sahara desert camp and enjoy a traditional dinner, music and the peaceful atmosphere beneath the stars.

~470 km Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & dinner
Day 6

Merzouga: Sahara Desert Experience

Wake early to watch the sunrise over the spectacular Erg Chebbi dunes. After breakfast, enjoy a full day exploring the desert region without having to pack and leave. Travel by private 4×4 around the dunes and discover the different landscapes surrounding Merzouga. Visit a traditional nomadic family and learn about their way of life, then continue to Khamlia village, known for its Gnawa music and African cultural heritage. Depending on your interests, you can also visit Lake Dayet Srij or enjoy time in the nearby villages. For adventure lovers, optional quad biking or other desert activities can be arranged. Return toward the dunes in the afternoon and enjoy free time to relax, take photographs or try sandboarding. As evening arrives, admire another beautiful Sahara sunset before returning to your camp for dinner and traditional music around the campfire.

~40 km Merzouga Breakfast & dinner
Day 7

Merzouga → Rissani → Erfoud → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley

After breakfast, leave the Sahara and continue through the spectacular landscapes of southeastern Morocco. Begin with Rissani, a historic desert town and former trading center. If the local market is open, explore its lively souk, famous for dates, spices, traditional crafts and local products. Continue to Erfoud, known for its fossils and beautiful stone craftsmanship. From Erfoud, travel toward the dramatic Todra Gorge. Walk between the enormous limestone cliffs and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of this spectacular natural landscape. Continue through the palm-filled oasis of Tinghir and toward the Dades Valley. Along the way, admire traditional villages, kasbahs and rugged mountain scenery. The Dades Valley is famous for its unusual rock formations and winding roads. After arriving at your accommodation, relax and enjoy the surrounding landscapes.

~300 km Dades Valley Breakfast
Day 8

Dades Valley → Valley of Roses → Skoura → Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → Marrakech

After breakfast, continue through the beautiful Valley of Roses, where traditional villages and cultivated fields create a picturesque landscape. Travel onward to Skoura, a palm oasis famous for its historic mud-brick kasbahs. Continue to Ouarzazate, often called the Gateway to the Sahara and known for its film studios and dramatic desert scenery. Your next stop is Aït Ben Haddou, one of Morocco's most famous fortified villages and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Explore its narrow streets, traditional earthen architecture and panoramic viewpoints. After the visit, cross the High Atlas Mountains via the spectacular Tizi n'Tichka Pass. Stop along the mountain road to admire the scenery and take photographs. Continue down toward Marrakech, arriving in the evening. Check into your riad and enjoy your first evening in the Red City.

~360 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 9

Marrakech: Full-Day Guided City Tour

Today, discover Marrakech with a local licensed guide. Begin in the historic medina and explore some of the city's most important landmarks, including the Bahia Palace, Saadian Tombs and Koutoubia Mosque. Continue through the colorful souks, where local artisans create carpets, leather goods, lamps, spices and traditional handicrafts. Visit the famous Jemaa el-Fnaa square, the lively heart of Marrakech, and experience its unique atmosphere. Your guide will introduce you to the history and traditions behind the Red City while allowing time for photographs and shopping. Depending on your interests, you may also visit the Ben Youssef Madrasa or explore the modern Gueliz district. Enjoy lunch at a traditional Moroccan restaurant before continuing your exploration. In the afternoon, return to your riad or enjoy free time to discover Marrakech independently.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 10

Marrakech: Departure

Enjoy your final morning in Morocco at your own pace. Depending on your flight schedule, you may have time for breakfast at your riad, last-minute shopping in the souks, a relaxing Moroccan coffee or additional sightseeing. If your departure is later in the day, your private driver can arrange extra free time according to your flight schedule. At the appropriate time, you will be transferred to Marrakech airport for your departure. Your 10-day private Morocco tour from Casablanca comes to an end after an incredible journey through Morocco's Atlantic cities, Rif Mountains, imperial heritage, Sahara Desert, dramatic valleys and vibrant Marrakech. You will leave with unforgettable memories of Chefchaouen's blue streets, Fes's ancient medina, the golden dunes of Merzouga and Morocco's rich culture and hospitality.

End of tour: Marrakech Breakfast
