Tour Overview

Experience the very best of Morocco on this 17-Day Morocco Tour from Tangier, a comprehensive private journey from the Mediterranean north to the Sahara Desert and the Atlantic coast.

The itinerary combines Morocco's historic imperial cities, mountain landscapes, desert regions, traditional villages and Atlantic coastal towns. You will discover Tangier, Chefchaouen, Fes, Meknes, Rabat, Casablanca, Marrakech, Merzouga, Ouzina, Zagora, Ouarzazate and Essaouira.

The longer duration allows you to travel at a more comfortable pace, with free time in several destinations instead of constantly moving from one hotel to another. This tour is ideal if you want to experience Morocco's culture, history, mountains, Sahara and Atlantic coast in one journey.

"More than a trip... It's a story to tell!"

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Arrival in Tangier: City Tour

Your 17-day Morocco tour from Tangier begins with a private pickup from Tangier Ibn Battuta Airport, ferry terminal or your hotel.

Begin exploring Tangier, the fascinating city located between the Mediterranean Sea and Atlantic Ocean. Visit the historic Kasbah and walk through the old Medina, discovering traditional houses, small shops and lively squares.

Continue to the Grand Socco and Mendoubia Gardens before driving toward Cape Spartel, where you can enjoy panoramic views over the Atlantic and Mediterranean. If time permits, visit the nearby Caves of Hercules.

In the afternoon, return to Tangier and enjoy free time to explore the Medina, shop for local handicrafts or relax at a traditional café.

Tangier has a unique international character because of its location at the entrance to the Mediterranean and its long history of cultural exchange.

~City tour km Tangier Breakfast
Day 2

Tangier → Tetouan → Chefchaouen

After breakfast, leave Tangier and travel toward the Rif Mountains. Make a stop in Tetouan, known for its whitewashed architecture and Andalusian influence.

Explore the historic Medina and enjoy some free time before continuing toward Chefchaouen. The road climbs into the Rif Mountains, and the scenery becomes increasingly dramatic.

Arrive in Chefchaouen, Morocco's famous Blue City, in the afternoon. Check into your traditional riad before exploring the charming old Medina.

Walk through the blue-painted streets, visit Plaza Uta el-Hammam and explore the Kasbah. You will have plenty of opportunities to photograph the colorful doors, narrow lanes and mountain views.

In the evening, enjoy dinner in one of the traditional restaurants around the Medina.

~≈120 km km Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 3

Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Meknes → Fes

After breakfast, leave Chefchaouen and travel toward the historic heart of Morocco.

Your first major stop is Volubilis, where you can explore the impressive Roman ruins, including ancient columns, mosaics and remains of the old city.

Continue to Meknes, one of Morocco's four imperial cities. Visit Bab Mansour, the old imperial quarter, the Moulay Ismail Mausoleum area and the impressive granaries and water reservoir.

After lunch, continue toward Fes. Arrive in Fes in the late afternoon and check into your riad. The evening is free to relax after the day's sightseeing or take a short walk around the area.

Fes will be your base for the next two nights, giving you enough time to discover its historic Medina without rushing.

~≈250 km km Fes Breakfast
Day 4

Full-Day Fes Sightseeing Tour

Today is dedicated entirely to Fes, one of Morocco's most important cultural and historic cities.

With a local licensed guide, explore the famous old Medina, with its narrow streets, traditional souks, artisan workshops and historic buildings.

Visit the Bou Inania Madrasa, Nejjarine area and the famous tanneries. Continue through the Medina to discover traditional Moroccan crafts, including leather, pottery, metalwork and zellige tilework.

After lunch, visit the Mellah, the historic Jewish Quarter, and continue to panoramic viewpoints overlooking the ancient city. Your guide can adapt the tour to your interests, whether you prefer architecture, history, shopping, food or photography.

Return to your riad in the evening and enjoy a relaxed Moroccan dinner.

~City tour km Fes Breakfast
Day 5

Fes → Ifrane → Cedar Forest → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

Today you begin your journey toward the Sahara Desert. Leave Fes after breakfast and travel through the Middle Atlas Mountains. Stop in Ifrane and continue toward the Cedar Forest near Azrou.

The road then crosses the Middle Atlas toward Midelt, where you can stop for lunch.

Continue south through increasingly dramatic landscapes toward the Ziz Valley. Stop at a panoramic viewpoint overlooking the palm-filled oasis before continuing toward Erfoud and Rissani.

By late afternoon, arrive in Merzouga, where the landscape changes dramatically as the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi appear.

Depending on your arrival time, begin your camel trek into the dunes and watch the sunset from the Sahara.

Arrive at your private desert camp for dinner, traditional music and an unforgettable night beneath the stars.

~≈470 km km Private Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & Dinner
Day 6

Merzouga: Full-Day Sahara Desert Exploration

Wake early to watch the Sahara sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes.

After breakfast, return to Merzouga and begin a full-day 4×4 exploration of the desert.

Visit the Hassi Labied oasis and, depending on seasonal conditions, Lake Dayet Srij. Continue to Khamlia village, where you can experience traditional Gnawa music.

You will also have the opportunity to visit a nomadic family and share Moroccan tea while learning about traditional desert life.

After lunch, explore the desert tracks and rocky landscapes surrounding Erg Chebbi. Optional activities such as quad biking and sandboarding can be arranged.

Return to Merzouga in the afternoon and enjoy free time to relax at your accommodation.

~≈40 km km Merzouga Breakfast & Dinner
Day 7

Merzouga → Ouzina → Ramlia → Desert Landscapes

Today you leave the main Merzouga route and travel deeper into southeastern Morocco toward Ouzina.

The journey takes you through remote desert landscapes and off-road tracks. Depending on road conditions, you can travel through areas around Ramlia and other small desert settlements.

This is a quieter side of the Sahara, away from the larger tourist routes. Ouzina is known for its red dunes, remote location and traditional desert atmosphere.

Stop along the way for photographs and enjoy the silence of the surrounding landscape.

Arrive in Ouzina in the afternoon and check into your desert accommodation.

The evening is free to relax, enjoy Moroccan tea and watch the sunset over the dunes.

~≈120 km km Ouzina Breakfast & Dinner
Day 8

Ouzina → Alnif → Tazzarine → Zagora

After breakfast, continue west through the landscapes of southern Morocco toward Zagora.

Travel through desert plateaus and mountain scenery toward Alnif, a region known for fossils and geological landscapes.

Continue toward Tazzarine, surrounded by oasis landscapes and palm groves. As you approach the Draa Valley, the scenery becomes greener.

Arrive in Zagora, an important town in the Draa Valley and a historic starting point for journeys across the Sahara.

After checking into your accommodation, enjoy free time to explore the town or relax at your hotel.

In the evening, enjoy dinner and the peaceful atmosphere of southern Morocco.

~≈250 km km Zagora Breakfast & Dinner
Day 9

Zagora → Draa Valley → Agdz → Ouarzazate

Today explore the spectacular Draa Valley, one of Morocco's great oasis regions.

Travel through extensive palm groves, traditional villages and ancient kasbahs. Stop at panoramic viewpoints and enjoy the contrast between the green oasis and surrounding desert mountains.

Continue toward Agdz and visit the area around Tamnougalt Kasbah, depending on your interests and schedule.

After lunch, continue north toward Ouarzazate through changing mountain landscapes.

Arrive in Ouarzazate in the afternoon. You can explore the town or visit one of its famous film-related attractions if desired.

Ouarzazate is an important gateway between the High Atlas and Morocco's southern desert regions.

~≈170 km km Ouarzazate Breakfast & Dinner
Day 10

Ouarzazate → Skoura → Dades Valley → Todra Gorge

Leave Ouarzazate after breakfast and travel east through the Valley of Roses toward Skoura, a beautiful palm oasis known for its kasbahs.

Continue toward the Dades Valley and enjoy the spectacular landscapes of the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs.

Stop at panoramic viewpoints and traditional villages before continuing toward Tinghir.

In the afternoon, visit the impressive Todra Gorge, where towering limestone cliffs rise dramatically above the river valley.

Take a walk through the gorge and enjoy the peaceful surroundings before checking into your accommodation.

This day offers a striking contrast to the Sahara, combining oasis landscapes, mountains, traditional architecture and dramatic rock formations.

~≈200 km km Tinghir / Todra Gorge Breakfast & Dinner
Day 11

Todra Gorge → Dades Valley → Aït Ben Haddou → Marrakech

After breakfast, continue toward the Dades Valley and Ouarzazate before traveling to Aït Ben Haddou.

Explore the famous fortified ksar and walk through its narrow lanes between traditional earthen buildings.

After visiting Aït Ben Haddou, continue toward the High Atlas Mountains.

Cross the spectacular Tizi n'Tichka Pass, stopping at viewpoints along the way to enjoy the mountain scenery and Berber villages.

The road then descends toward Marrakech. Arrive in Marrakech in the late afternoon or evening and check into your riad or hotel.

After several days in the desert and mountains, Marrakech offers a lively change of atmosphere.

~≈360 km km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 12

Full-Day Marrakech Sightseeing Tour

Today discover the highlights of Marrakech with a local guide.

Visit the Koutoubia Mosque, Saadian Tombs and Bahia Palace before entering the colorful souks of the Medina.

Explore traditional artisan workshops and discover carpets, leather goods, spices, lamps and Moroccan handicrafts.

After lunch, visit Jemaa el-Fna and enjoy the lively atmosphere of Marrakech's historic center.

If desired, you can also visit the Majorelle Garden or another museum. Your guide can adjust the sightseeing according to your interests.

Spend the evening enjoying Marrakech's restaurants, cafés and traditional atmosphere.

~City tour km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 13

Marrakech → Essaouira

After breakfast, leave Marrakech and travel west toward the Atlantic coast.

Arrive in Essaouira, a charming fortified coastal city known for its white-and-blue architecture, fishing port, historic Medina and relaxed atmosphere.

Explore the UNESCO-listed Medina, walk along the historic ramparts and visit the harbor where local fishermen bring in their daily catch.

Enjoy a seafood lunch near the port or choose a traditional Moroccan restaurant inside the Medina.

The afternoon is free to explore the small shops, galleries and artisan workshops or walk along the beach.

Essaouira provides a completely different atmosphere from Marrakech and the Sahara, making it an excellent addition to a longer Morocco itinerary.

~≈190 km km Essaouira Breakfast
Day 14

Full Day in Essaouira

Enjoy a relaxed day in Essaouira without long-distance driving.

After breakfast, explore more of the Medina and its traditional craft shops. Essaouira is particularly known for wood carving, local art and its lively fishing culture.

Walk along the Skala and enjoy panoramic Atlantic Ocean views.

You can also spend time at the beach, visit local cafés or enjoy freshly prepared seafood.

For travelers interested in Moroccan culture, we can arrange an optional cooking class or local experience.

The free day is designed to give you a break after the long desert and mountain sections of the tour. Enjoy your final evening beside the Atlantic before returning to Marrakech the following day.

~At leisure km Essaouira Breakfast
Day 15

Essaouira → Marrakech

After breakfast, leave Essaouira and return to Marrakech.

The journey takes you through the Moroccan countryside and the famous argan tree region. Depending on your interests, you can stop at a women's cooperative to learn about traditional argan oil production.

Continue toward Marrakech and arrive around midday or in the afternoon. The remainder of the day is free.

You can use the afternoon for shopping in the souks, visiting a museum, enjoying a traditional hammam or simply relaxing at your riad.

This flexible day gives you additional time in Marrakech before your departure.

~≈190 km km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 16

Free Day in Marrakech

Enjoy a completely free day in Marrakech.

There is no long-distance travel today, allowing you to experience the city at your own pace.

You can revisit the Medina, shop for souvenirs, visit additional palaces or museums, or enjoy a traditional Moroccan cooking experience.

If you prefer nature, we can arrange an optional excursion into the Atlas Mountains or Ourika Valley.

In the evening, return to Jemaa el-Fna for one final experience of Marrakech after dark. Enjoy your last Moroccan dinner and relax at your riad.

~At leisure km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 17

Marrakech: Departure

Enjoy your final breakfast in Morocco.

Depending on your flight time, you can have free time for last-minute shopping or a final walk through the Medina.

At the agreed time, your private driver will transfer you to Marrakech Menara Airport or another agreed departure point.

Your 17-day Morocco tour from Tangier ends after an extensive journey through Morocco's Mediterranean north, Rif Mountains, imperial cities, Sahara Desert, desert villages, oasis valleys, High Atlas Mountains and Atlantic coast.

~Departure transfer km End of tour Breakfast

Why Choose This Tour?

A 17-day Morocco tour gives you something shorter itineraries cannot: time.

Rather than rushing between Morocco's famous destinations, this itinerary allows you to spend multiple nights in key locations and includes free days in Marrakech and Essaouira.

You will experience the country from several completely different perspectives: the Mediterranean atmosphere of Tangier, the Rif Mountains around Chefchaouen, the historic culture of Fes, the vast Sahara around Merzouga and Ouzina, the palm-filled Draa Valley, the kasbahs of southern Morocco, the High Atlas Mountains and the Atlantic coast.

The route follows the logic of Morocco's classic “Grand Tour,” connecting destinations including Tangier, Chefchaouen, Fes, Merzouga, Ouarzazate, Marrakech and Essaouira.

This is therefore an excellent choice for travelers who want a complete Morocco experience rather than a short city or desert trip.

FAQ

Is 17 days enough to see Morocco?

Yes. Seventeen days is an excellent length for a comprehensive Morocco itinerary. It allows you to combine the north, imperial cities, Sahara, southern Morocco, Marrakech and the Atlantic coast without making the entire trip feel rushed.

Is this a private Morocco tour?

Yes. The itinerary is designed as a private tour with your own vehicle and driver.

Does the tour include Chefchaouen and Fes?

Yes. Both are important stops, with an overnight in Chefchaouen and two nights in Fes.

Does this Morocco tour include the Sahara Desert?

Yes. You will spend time in Merzouga and Erg Chebbi, including a camel trek, desert camp and 4×4 desert exploration.

Why are Ouzina and Merzouga both included?

They offer different desert experiences. Merzouga is famous for the large Erg Chebbi dunes and classic camel-camp experience, while Ouzina provides a quieter and more remote desert atmosphere.

Does the tour include Marrakech?

Yes. You have several nights in Marrakech, including a guided sightseeing day and free time.

Is Essaouira included?

Yes. The itinerary includes two nights in Essaouira, followed by a return to Marrakech.

Can we add Casablanca and Rabat?

Yes. If you want a more complete northern Morocco route, we can modify the itinerary to include Rabat and Casablanca. This would require adjusting some of the free days or extending the tour.

Can we include Ouzoud Waterfalls?

Yes. Ouzoud can be added if you prefer a nature-focused itinerary, although the route would need to be adjusted.

Can we change the number of nights in each city?

Absolutely. Since this is a private and customizable tour, the number of nights can be adjusted according to your interests.

What accommodation options are available?

We can arrange Standard, Mid-Range or Luxury accommodation, including comfortable hotels, traditional riads and a private Sahara Desert Camp.

Can the tour start at Tangier ferry port?

Yes. Pickup can be arranged from Tangier ferry terminal, Tangier Airport or your hotel.

Can the tour end at Marrakech Airport?

Yes. We can arrange the final transfer directly to Marrakech Menara Airport according to your flight time.
