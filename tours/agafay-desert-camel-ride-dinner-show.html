Overview

Ride a camel through the stony hills of Agafay while the sun goes down behind them, then sit down to dinner at a desert camp, with music and a fire show under the stars.

We drive you straight there in your own car, with no shop on the way, so you are on the camels for the whole sunset, and we bring you back to your hotel by about 22:30.

Programme

  1. 1
    Afternoon

    Pick-up at your hotel

    Your driver collects you about 2.5 hours before sunset: around 15:30 in winter, 17:30 in summer. Every time below follows the sunset; we confirm yours on WhatsApp the day before.

  2. 2
    Before sunset

    Into the Agafay hills

    About 40 minutes south-west of the city, with no shop on the way. Mint tea at the camp while the camel team gets the camels ready.

  3. 3
    Sunset

    Camel ride at sunset

    About an hour at an easy walking pace over the hills, timed so the sun sets while you ride. The camel team leads and your guide walks with you.

  4. 4
    Dusk

    Time at the camp

    Take photos as the light fades, sit by the fire or relax in the lounge tent. Put on your warm layer: the desert cools quickly after dark.

  5. 5
    Evening

    Dinner and fire show

    Salads, a tagine, couscous, fruit and mint tea at your own table, then Berber music, drums and a fire show around the campfire.

  6. 6
    About 22:00

    Back to Marrakech

    A 40-minute drive back to your hotel or riad, there by about 22:30, or we drop you in town if your evening goes on.

Good to know

  • This is a suggestion: tell us your preferences
  • Evenings are cool even in summer, and close to freezing in winter: bring a warm layer
  • Closed shoes for the camel ride and the sandy paths
  • Agafay is a stone desert of hills, not sand dunes
  • Pregnant, or a bad back? Skip the camels and join us at the camp

Costs on the day

  • Soft drinks: about 20–30 MAD at the camp
  • Alcohol: only at some camps, about €6–8 for a beer
  • Quad ride or pool: on request, paid at the camp
  • Tips: up to you
  • Cash: bring some dirhams, camps rarely take cards

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the camel ride long enough?

Many shared tours give you 15 to 20 minutes. Ours lasts about an hour and is timed for the sunset; tell us if you would like it shorter or longer.

Will the camp be crowded?

Some camps welcome several buses an evening. You arrive in your own car and have your own table, and we can choose a smaller, quieter camp: just ask when you book.

Are the camels treated well?

We work with camel teams we know: the camels rest between rides and are led at a walking pace. If you would rather not ride, walk beside the caravan or wait for us at the camp.

Can you cater for vegetarians or allergies?

Yes. A vegetarian tagine is easy, and vegan, gluten-free or children’s meals can be arranged: tell us when you book.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. The camels walk slowly, and young children ride with a parent or are led by the camel team. For little ones we can serve dinner earlier and have you back by about 21:00.

How do I book and pay?

  1. Send your request with the form or on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay a small deposit to confirm; the rest is paid on the day, in cash or by card.
