Overview

The Ouzoud waterfalls drop about 110 metres in three tiers into a red-rock gorge in the Middle Atlas: the highest falls in Morocco. Olive groves, old water mills and wild Barbary macaques line the path from the top down to the pools.

It is a long day, 2.5 to 3 hours each way, so we keep the rest simple: no shop stops, a guide who walks at your pace, and time to sit by the water instead of rushing back to a bus.

Programme

  1. 1
    08:00

    Pick-up in Marrakech

    Your driver meets you at your hotel, or at the nearest gate if your riad is in the medina. We confirm the exact time and place on WhatsApp the day before.

  2. 2
    09:30

    Coffee stop on the way

    About halfway, a break for coffee, toilets and a stretch. The whole drive is about 150 km, 2.5 to 3 hours, across the plain and into the Middle Atlas hills.

  3. 3
    11:00

    The top of the falls

    Your guide starts at the viewpoints above the gorge for the full view, then walks you down past old water mills and olive trees, where the macaques often sit.

  4. 4
    11:45

    Boat ride and the pools

    At the bottom, a small boat takes you into the spray (about 25 MAD, not included). In summer there is time for a cold dip in the calmer pools.

  5. 5
    13:00

    Lunch facing the falls

    You choose the terrace: tagine, grilled meat or salads, around €10 a person (not included). Your guide checks the price before you order.

  6. 6
    14:30

    The climb up and the drive back

    The way up is the hard part: steps most of the way, taken at your pace with café breaks. Then the drive back, in Marrakech around 18:00.

Good to know

  • About 600–700 steps lead back up: wear shoes with grip
  • Not possible with wheelchairs or strollers; tell us about knees, heart or small children
  • The macaques are wild: never feed them
  • The falls flow all year, strongest from March to May
  • Summer middays are hot (often above 35 °C) and the busiest time
  • The road has bends: ask for the front seat if you get car-sick

Costs on the day

  • Lunch facing the falls: around €10 per person (about 100–120 MAD), depending on what you eat
  • Boat ride: about 25 MAD per person
  • Mint tea or a soft drink: about 15–30 MAD; ask the price before you order
  • Guide and parking: already paid, nothing to hand over
  • Tips: up to you
  • Cash: bring some dirhams; the cafés and boats rarely take cards

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ouzoud worth the long drive?

If you love waterfalls and have four days or more in Marrakech, yes: they are the highest in Morocco. With only one or two days, the 5–6 hours on the road is a lot; choose the Ourika Valley, or stay a night at Ouzoud and enjoy the falls without the rush.

How hard is the walk back up?

Going down takes about 30–45 minutes and is easy. Coming back is about 600–700 steps, so take it slowly with café breaks; older travellers manage it at their own pace. Your guide can also take the gentler trail on the other side of the gorge.

Are the monkeys dangerous?

The Barbary macaques are wild but used to people. Do not feed them, even when someone sells you popcorn: food makes them grab and scratch. Keep snacks and anything shiny in your bag, and stay away from mothers with babies.

Will anyone ask us for money at the falls?

Not for guiding or parking. Men at the car park offer to guide visitors and often lead them to shops, but your licensed guide is already in the price and walks with you. A polite “no, thank you” is enough.

Can we swim?

In summer, yes, in the calmer pools at the base, never right under the main fall. There are no changing rooms, so wear swimwear underneath. After rain the water turns brown and fast: stay out then.

How do I book, and what if it rains?

  1. Send your request with the form or on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay a small deposit to confirm; the rest is paid on the day, in cash or by card.

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before. If heavy rain makes the paths unsafe, you choose a new date or a refund.
