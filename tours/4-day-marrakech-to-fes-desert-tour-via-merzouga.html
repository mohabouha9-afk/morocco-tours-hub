Tour Overview

Experience the highlights of southern Morocco and the Sahara Desert on this 4-Day Marrakech to Fes Desert Tour via Merzouga. This private journey connects Marrakech and Fes while taking you through the spectacular High Atlas Mountains, ancient kasbahs, dramatic valleys and the golden Erg Chebbi dunes.

Starting in Marrakech, cross the High Atlas Mountains and visit the UNESCO-listed Aït Ben Haddou before continuing through Ouarzazate and the Valley of Roses. You will spend your first night in the beautiful Dades Valley. The following day takes you through the impressive Todra Gorge, Erfoud and Rissani before reaching Merzouga. Enjoy a camel trek across the Sahara at sunset and spend the night in a private desert camp.

With an additional day compared with the classic 3-day route, you can enjoy a more relaxed Sahara experience and explore the Merzouga area by 4×4. The final day takes you through the Ziz Valley and Middle Atlas Mountains before arriving in Fes.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Marrakech → High Atlas Mountains → Aït Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate → Dades Valley

Your private Marrakech to Fes Desert Tour begins with pickup from your hotel or riad in Marrakech. Travel south across the spectacular High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka Pass. Along the way, stop at scenic viewpoints to admire mountain valleys, Berber villages and dramatic landscapes. Continue toward Aït Ben Haddou, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Morocco's most famous fortified villages. Walk through its narrow streets and discover traditional earthen architecture. After lunch, continue to Ouarzazate, known as the gateway to the Sahara and famous for its film studios and kasbahs. Continue through the Valley of Roses and along the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs toward the Dades Valley. Enjoy the changing scenery before arriving at your hotel or riad.

~330 km Dades Valley Breakfast
Day 2

Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Erfoud → Rissani → Merzouga

After breakfast, continue toward the Sahara Desert. Drive through the Dades Valley and enjoy the spectacular rock formations before reaching Todra Gorge. Take a walk between the towering limestone cliffs and enjoy time for photographs. Continue through the pre-Saharan landscapes toward Erfoud, a town famous for fossils, dates and traditional stone craftsmanship. Continue to Rissani, an historic trading center and an important gateway to the Sahara. If the local market is open, you can explore its lively souk and experience a more traditional side of southeastern Morocco. In the afternoon, arrive in Merzouga at the edge of Erg Chebbi. Enjoy some time to relax before beginning your camel trek into the golden dunes. Ride across the Sahara while watching the sunset before reaching your private desert camp.

~360 km Private Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & dinner
Day 3

Merzouga: Full Sahara Desert Experience

Wake early to watch the sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes before breakfast. Today is dedicated to exploring the Sahara region at a relaxed pace. Your private 4×4 will take you around the desert, beginning with a visit to a local nomadic family. Learn about their traditional lifestyle and how they have adapted to life in the desert. Continue to Khamlia village, known for its Gnawa music, and enjoy a traditional musical performance. You can then visit areas famous for fossils and discover the geological history of the region. Depending on the season, you may also visit the seasonal lake near Merzouga. There will be time for sandboarding, photography and enjoying the dunes without rushing. Return to your accommodation in the afternoon and enjoy a peaceful evening in the Sahara region.

~40 km Merzouga Breakfast
Day 4

Merzouga → Ziz Valley → Midelt → Azrou → Ifrane → Fes

After breakfast, begin the final stage of your journey from the Sahara to Fes. Travel north through the spectacular Ziz Valley, stopping at a panoramic viewpoint overlooking its palm-filled oasis and rocky landscapes. Continue toward Midelt, located between the High and Middle Atlas Mountains. After a break, continue through the Middle Atlas forests toward Azrou. Stop in the cedar forests, where you may have the opportunity to see Barbary macaques. Continue to Ifrane, a charming mountain town known for its distinctive architecture and alpine atmosphere. From Ifrane, descend toward the fertile Saïss Plain and continue to Fes. Arrive in the evening and receive a private drop-off at your hotel or traditional riad. Your 4-Day Marrakech to Fes Desert Tour via Merzouga ends with the arrival in Fes.

~560 km End of tour: Fes Breakfast
