Tour Overview

Discover the history, culture, landscapes, and desert magic of Morocco on this unforgettable 8-Day Morocco Imperial Cities & Sahara Tour. Designed as a private journey, this itinerary combines the country's most fascinating imperial cities with the spectacular landscapes of the Moroccan Sahara.

Begin in Casablanca and explore Morocco's economic capital before discovering Rabat, the elegant political capital. Continue to Meknes and the ancient Roman ruins of Volubilis before spending time exploring the historic medina of Fes.

From Fes, travel through the Middle Atlas Mountains, cedar forests, Midelt, and the spectacular Ziz Valley before reaching Merzouga and the golden Erg Chebbi dunes. Experience a camel trek, Sahara sunset, traditional desert camp, and sunrise over the dunes. Continue through Rissani, Todra Gorge, Dades Valley, Ouarzazate, and the famous Aït Ben Haddou Kasbah before crossing the High Atlas Mountains to vibrant Marrakech.

"More than a trip... It's a story to tell!"

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Casablanca → Rabat

Your 8-Day Morocco Imperial Cities & Sahara Tour begins with a private pickup from Casablanca Airport, your hotel, or another convenient location. Start by exploring Casablanca, Morocco's largest city and an important economic center.

Visit the magnificent Hassan II Mosque, dramatically positioned beside the Atlantic Ocean. Continue along the Corniche and discover some of the city's modern and historic neighborhoods before traveling toward Rabat.

Arrive in Morocco's capital and explore its most important landmarks, including the impressive Hassan Tower and the Mausoleum of Mohammed V. Continue to the Kasbah of the Udayas, where whitewashed buildings and narrow streets overlook the Bou Regreg River and Atlantic coastline.

Depending on your arrival time, you can also visit the old medina and enjoy a traditional Moroccan lunch.

In the afternoon, check into your hotel or riad and enjoy some free time to relax or explore Rabat independently.

~100 km Rabat Breakfast
Day 2

Rabat → Volubilis → Moulay Idriss → Meknes → Fes

After breakfast, leave Rabat and travel toward the historic heart of Morocco. Your first stop is Volubilis, an impressive ancient Roman archaeological site surrounded by beautiful countryside.

Explore the ruins and admire the remains of Roman villas, columns, ancient streets, and beautifully preserved mosaics. The site offers a fascinating glimpse into Morocco's Roman history.

Continue to Moulay Idriss, one of Morocco's most important pilgrimage towns. Enjoy time to explore its charming streets and panoramic viewpoints before continuing to Meknes.

Discover Meknes, one of Morocco's four imperial cities. Visit the impressive Bab Mansour, explore the historic medina, and see other important landmarks depending on opening times.

After lunch, continue toward Fes. Arrive in the late afternoon and check into your traditional riad or hotel. The evening is free to relax and enjoy your first taste of Fassi cuisine or take a short walk around the historic surroundings.

~230 km Fes Breakfast
Day 3

Fes: Guided City Tour

Today is dedicated to discovering Fes, one of Morocco's most important imperial cities and a major center of history, culture, religion, and craftsmanship.

After breakfast, meet your local guide and begin exploring the ancient medina, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Walk through its maze of narrow streets and discover traditional souks filled with colorful textiles, spices, ceramics, leather goods, and handmade crafts.

Visit important historical landmarks such as the Bou Inania Madrasa, the Nejjarine area, and the famous traditional tanneries. You will also learn about Al Quaraouiyine, one of the historic institutions associated with Fes's long intellectual tradition.

Enjoy a traditional Moroccan lunch in the medina before continuing your exploration. In the afternoon, visit the Mellah, the former Jewish quarter, and enjoy panoramic views over the old city from one of the surrounding viewpoints.

Return to your riad in the evening and enjoy some free time to relax.

Fes Breakfast
Day 4

Fes → Ifrane → Azrou → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

After breakfast, leave Fes and begin your journey toward the Sahara Desert. Travel through the beautiful Middle Atlas Mountains and stop in Ifrane, a charming mountain town famous for its alpine-style architecture and green surroundings.

Continue through the cedar forests near Azrou, where you may have the opportunity to see Barbary macaques. The landscape gradually changes as you travel toward Midelt, known for its apples and spectacular mountain scenery.

Continue across the Atlas landscapes toward the Ziz Valley. Stop at a panoramic viewpoint to admire the dramatic contrast between the green palm groves and the surrounding rocky landscape.

Travel onward through Erfoud and Rissani before reaching Merzouga and the spectacular Erg Chebbi dunes. In the late afternoon, begin your camel trek across the golden sands. Watch the desert change color as the sun sets over the dunes before arriving at your Sahara camp.

Enjoy a traditional dinner, local music, and an unforgettable evening beneath the stars.

~470 km Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & dinner
Day 5

Merzouga: Sahara Desert Experience

Wake early to witness the breathtaking Sahara sunrise. Watch the first rays of sunlight spread across the Erg Chebbi dunes before returning to camp for breakfast.

Today is dedicated to discovering the desert and experiencing local Saharan culture. Travel around the Merzouga region by private 4×4 and discover the landscapes surrounding the dunes.

Depending on your interests, visit a local community known for Gnawa music, explore traditional desert villages, or visit the seasonal lake near Merzouga when conditions permit.

You will also have time for optional activities such as sandboarding on the dunes. Alternatively, simply relax and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of the Sahara. In the afternoon, return toward the dunes and find a beautiful location to enjoy another spectacular desert sunset.

As evening arrives, enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner accompanied by local music around the campfire. Far from city lights, the Sahara offers an incredible opportunity to admire the stars and experience the peaceful silence of the desert.

~40 km Merzouga Breakfast & dinner
Day 6

Merzouga → Rissani → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley

After breakfast, leave Merzouga and continue through southeastern Morocco. Begin with a visit to Rissani, a historic trading town that played an important role in the ancient caravan routes.

If the traditional souk is open, explore its lively atmosphere and discover dates, spices, handicrafts, and local products. Continue through Erfoud, famous for its fossils and beautifully polished stone products.

Travel onward toward Tinghir and visit the spectacular Todra Gorge. Take a walk through the canyon surrounded by enormous limestone cliffs. The dramatic scenery makes this one of the highlights of southern Morocco.

Continue through palm-filled valleys and traditional Berber villages toward the Dades Valley. Along the way, enjoy opportunities for photographs and scenic stops. Arrive at your hotel or riad in the afternoon and relax after your journey.

The Dades Valley is particularly beautiful in the late afternoon, when the surrounding cliffs and rock formations take on warm colors.

~300 km Dades Valley Breakfast
Day 7

Dades Valley → Valley of Roses → Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → Marrakech

After breakfast, continue your journey through the spectacular landscapes of southern Morocco. Travel through the Valley of Roses, known for its fragrant rose fields and traditional cosmetic products.

Continue to Skoura, a beautiful oasis filled with palm trees and historic kasbahs, before reaching Ouarzazate. Often called the "Hollywood of Morocco," the city has been associated with numerous international film productions.

Continue to Aït Ben Haddou, one of Morocco's most famous fortified villages and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Explore its ancient earthen buildings, narrow pathways, and panoramic viewpoints.

After lunch, begin the spectacular journey across the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka Pass. Enjoy breathtaking views of mountain valleys, Berber villages, and rugged landscapes along the route.

Descend toward Marrakech and arrive in the evening. Check into your hotel or riad and enjoy a relaxing final evening in Morocco.

~360 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 8

Marrakech: Guided Tour & Departure

After breakfast, discover the highlights of Marrakech with your local guide. Explore the historic medina and visit some of the city's most important landmarks, including the Bahia Palace, Saadian Tombs, and the famous Koutoubia Mosque.

Continue through the colorful souks, where local artisans sell carpets, leather goods, ceramics, spices, lanterns, and other traditional Moroccan handicrafts.

Visit Jemaa el-Fnaa, Marrakech's famous central square, and enjoy time for photographs, shopping, or a traditional mint tea.

Depending on your departure time, you may have some free time to explore more of Marrakech or relax at your accommodation.

At the appropriate time, your private driver will transfer you to Marrakech Menara Airport or your preferred departure location. Your 8-Day Morocco Imperial Cities & Sahara Tour ends with unforgettable memories of Morocco's historic cities, ancient Roman heritage, Atlas Mountains, Sahara Desert, traditional villages, and vibrant cultural experiences.

End of tour: Marrakech Breakfast
