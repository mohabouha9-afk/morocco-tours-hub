Overview

The four imperial cities of Morocco and the Sahara in eight days. From the Hassan II Mosque on the Atlantic to Rabat, the capital, then Meknes and the Roman ruins of Volubilis on the way to Fes, for a guided day in its thousand-year-old medina.

Then over the Middle Atlas to the dunes of Erg Chebbi for a sunset camel ride and a night in the camp, back west by the Todra Gorge and the kasbahs of the south to a night beside the ksar of Aït Ben Haddou, and over the High Atlas to Marrakech. Your own car and driver-guide all the way, and no shop stops you didn’t ask for.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Casablanca → Hassan II Mosque → Rabat

    Pick-up at your hotel or Casablanca airport in the morning. A guided visit inside the Hassan II Mosque, built over the Atlantic, with the tallest minaret in Africa, then the Corniche. An hour up the coast to Rabat, the capital: the blue and white lanes of the Kasbah des Oudayas, the Hassan Tower and the royal Mausoleum of Mohammed V.

    • About 100 km, about 1.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Rabat
    • Dinner & breakfast
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Rabat → Meknes → Volubilis → Fes

    Inland to Meknes, the city of Sultan Moulay Ismail: the great gate of Bab Mansour, Place el-Hedim and the old granaries, with lunch in the medina. In the afternoon, a walk through Volubilis, the Roman town on the plain, with its mosaics, basilica and triumphal arch, then on to Fes for the evening.

    • About 270 km, about 4 hours on the road
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast & dinner
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Fes: a guided day in the medina

    With a local Fes guide, a whole day in the oldest medina of Morocco: the Bou Inania Madrasa, the Nejjarine fountain, the coppersmiths of Place Seffarine, the Al-Attarine Madrasa and a rooftop above the Chouara tanneries, and the brass gates of the Royal Palace. Shops only if you ask.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast & dinner
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Fes → Ifrane → Midelt → Ziz Valley → Merzouga

    South over the Middle Atlas around 07:30: the alpine town of Ifrane and the cedar forest of Azrou, where Barbary macaques sit by the road, then lunch in Midelt. Down the palm gorges of the Ziz Valley to the dunes of Erg Chebbi, where your big bags stay in the car and you ride a camel into the sand for sunset. Dinner, drums and a night in the camp.

    • About 470 km, about 8 hours on the road
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Breakfast & dinner
  5. 5
    Day 5

    Merzouga → Rissani → Todra Gorge → Dades Valley

    Sunrise and sandboarding on the dunes, breakfast, and back by camel or 4×4. Through Rissani, the old caravan town, and along the Jebel Saghro to the palm grove of Tinghir and a walk between the 300 m walls of the Todra Gorge. On to the Dades Valley for the evening.

    • About 270 km, about 5 hours on the road
    • Night: Dades Valley
    • Breakfast & dinner
  6. 6
    Day 6

    Dades → Valley of Roses → Skoura → Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou

    Down the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs through the Valley of Roses and the Skoura palm grove to Ouarzazate and the Taourirt Kasbah. An easy afternoon at Aït Ben Haddou: walk up through the ksar to its old granary and watch it turn red at sunset from your terrace.

    • About 150 km, about 3 hours on the road
    • Night: Aït Ben Haddou
    • Breakfast & dinner
  7. 7
    Day 7

    Aït Ben Haddou → Tizi n’Tichka → Marrakech

    Over the High Atlas by the Tizi n’Tichka pass, 2,260 m, with stops at the viewpoints and the Berber villages, and down to Marrakech by lunchtime. The afternoon is yours, and Jemaa el-Fnaa comes alive at sunset.

    • About 175 km, about 3.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Marrakech riad
    • Breakfast
  8. 8
    Day 8

    Marrakech: a guided morning and the end of the tour

    With a local guide: the Koutoubia, the painted ceilings of the Bahia Palace, the souks and the Ben Youssef Madrasa. The tour ends at your riad, or we take you to Marrakech airport at the time you need.

    • Morning on foot in the medina
    • Ends at your riad or Marrakech airport
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we visit the Hassan II Mosque on any day?

Guided visits run Saturday to Thursday, not on Fridays. If your tour starts on a Friday, we see the mosque from the outside and the Corniche, or plan the visit for your last morning if you fly home from Casablanca.

What are the four imperial cities?

Rabat, Meknes, Fes and Marrakech: the four cities that served as capitals of Moroccan dynasties. This tour visits all four, with a full guided day in Fes and a guided morning in Marrakech.

How long is the drive from Fes to the desert?

About 470 km, eight hours with stops in Ifrane, the cedar forest and Midelt. It is the one long day, timed so you reach the dunes for the sunset camel ride; the days after are three to five hours.

Can you pick us up at Casablanca airport?

Yes. We meet you in arrivals with a name sign and follow your flight. If you land in the afternoon, we can start with a night in Casablanca and keep the mosque for the next morning.

Are the city guides included?

Yes. A local professional guide walks you through the Fes medina and the Marrakech medina. In Casablanca the mosque has its own guides on the visit, and in Rabat, Meknes and Volubilis your driver-guide shows you around.

How do I book and pay?

  1. Send your request with the form or on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay a 20–30% deposit by bank transfer to confirm; the rest is paid on arrival in cash (EUR, USD or MAD) or by card.

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before the tour.
