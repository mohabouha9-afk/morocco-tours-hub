Tour Overview

The 4-Day Marrakech, Essaouira & Agadir Tour is a 4-day private Morocco tour that travels Marrakech → Essaouira → Agadir. You'll spend your time exploring the souks, palaces and gardens of Marrakech, enjoying the Atlantic port of Essaouira and relaxing on the beaches of Agadir.

Sea breezes, ramparts, fresh seafood and the vibrant energy of Marrakech. Like every Morocco tour we offer, it is 100% private — just for your group — and can be tailored to your dates, pace, hotels and budget.

"More than a trip... It's a story to tell!"

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1–2

Marrakech

Days 1–2 begin in Marrakech. In Marrakech you'll dive into the maze of souks, the buzzing Jemaa el-Fnaa square, the Bahia Palace, the Koutoubia minaret and tranquil gardens — the perfect introduction to the Red City's colour, scent and energy. You travel throughout in a private, air-conditioned vehicle with your own driver-guide, stopping for photos and mint tea along the way. You travel privately with your own driver-guide, so the day flexes around you — stop for photos, mint tea or a long lunch whenever you like. Along the route the scenery shifts constantly, from palm oases and Berber villages to mountain passes and wide desert horizons. There's time to meet local people, browse the markets and taste authentic Moroccan cooking, from fragrant tagines to freshly baked bread. Your accommodation is hand-picked for character and comfort, whether a traditional riad, a family-run hotel or a desert camp beneath the stars.

~200 km Marrakech Dinner

Essaouira

Day 3 takes you on from Essaouira. In the Atlantic port of Essaouira you'll stroll the wind-swept ramparts, watch blue boats in the fishing harbour, browse art galleries and enjoy freshly grilled seafood in the laid-back medina. ~190 km Essaouira Breakfast & dinner
Day 4

Agadir

Day 4 takes you on from Agadir. In the modern beach resort of Agadir you'll find a wide sandy bay, a lively seafront promenade and a hilltop kasbah looking out over the Atlantic Ocean. The tour ends with free time to relax and take in your final destination before your onward journey or airport transfer.
