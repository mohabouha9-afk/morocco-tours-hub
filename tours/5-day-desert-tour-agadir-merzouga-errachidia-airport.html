Overview

From the beaches of Agadir across the whole of the south to the Sahara: the red walls of Taroudant, the saffron villages of the Anti-Atlas, a night beside the ksar of Aït Ben Haddou, the kasbahs of Skoura and the Dades and Todra gorges, at an easy pace.

Then two nights by the dunes of Erg Chebbi: a sunset camel ride and a night in the camp, and a whole day around Merzouga with nomads and Gnawa music. On the last morning you fly out from Errachidia, two hours away, instead of driving ten hours back. Your own car and driver-guide all the way, and no shop stops you didn’t ask for.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Agadir → Taroudant → Taliouine → Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou

    Pick-up at your hotel in Agadir around 07:30. Inland through the argan groves of the Souss to Taroudant and its red earth ramparts, then up into the Anti-Atlas by Taliouine, where the saffron fields flower purple in late October and November. Past Ouarzazate to Aït Ben Haddou, where you sleep beside the ksar and watch it glow at sunset.

    • About 420 km, about 7.5 hours on the road
    • Night: Aït Ben Haddou
    • Dinner & breakfast
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Aït Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate → Skoura → Valley of Roses → Dades Gorge

    An early walk up through the empty lanes of the ksar to the old granary, before the day groups arrive. Past the Taourirt Kasbah in Ouarzazate to the Skoura palm grove and Kasbah Amridil, then along the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs through the Valley of Roses. An easy afternoon up the Dades Gorge, past the Monkey Fingers rocks to the hairpin bends of Tissadrine.

    • About 180 km, about 4 hours on the road
    • Night: Dades Valley
    • Breakfast & dinner
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Dades → Todra Gorge → Erfoud → Merzouga

    To the palm grove of Tinghir and a walk between the 300 m walls of the Todra Gorge in the morning, before the midday groups. East along the oasis road to Erfoud and past Rissani to the dunes of Erg Chebbi. Your big bags stay in the car and you ride a camel into the sand for sunset, then dinner, drums by the fire and a night in the camp.

    • About 320 km, about 6.5 hours on the road
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: desert camp
    • Breakfast & dinner
  4. 4
    Day 4

    A full day around Merzouga by 4×4

    Sunrise and sandboarding on the dunes, breakfast, and back by camel or 4×4. Then a whole day in the desert at your pace: mint tea with a nomad family in their tent, the Gnawa musicians of Khamlia, the black stony plains, the lake of Dayet Srij after a wet winter and the old caravan market of Rissani. A quiet evening in a hotel at the foot of the dunes.

    • 4×4 day around Merzouga
    • Night: Merzouga hotel (or Errachidia for an early flight)
    • Breakfast & dinner
  5. 5
    Day 5

    Merzouga → Erfoud → Ziz Valley → Errachidia airport

    We leave at the time your flight needs. North through Erfoud and its fossil workshops and along the palm groves of the Ziz Valley to Errachidia, where your driver-guide helps you with your bags at the airport. No long drive back to the coast: the tour ends two hours from the dunes.

    • About 120 km, about 2 hours on the road
    • Drop-off at Errachidia airport
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Merzouga from Agadir?

About 10 hours by road, so we split it over three days with nights at Aït Ben Haddou and in the Dades Valley. Day 1 is the longest, about 7.5 hours with stops; the next two are about 4 and 6.5 hours.

Why end at Errachidia airport and not back in Agadir?

Errachidia is two hours from the dunes, Agadir about ten. Flying out saves you a whole day in the car. Flights go to Casablanca and Rabat, with connections home, and to Marrakech and some European cities.

What if our flight leaves early in the morning?

The Royal Air Maroc flight to Casablanca leaves Errachidia early, around 06:15. Then we drive to Errachidia after the desert day and you sleep in a hotel about 10 minutes from the airport. Send us your flight time when you book.

Why one night in the camp and one in a hotel?

The camp gives you the sunset, the fire and the stars; the hotel gives you a hot shower and a calm last evening before your flight. If you prefer, both nights can be in the camp.

Can we end in Fes or Marrakech instead?

Yes, with the same car and driver-guide. From Merzouga, Fes is about 8 hours north through the Middle Atlas; Marrakech is about 9 hours back over the High Atlas. We can also go on to Chefchaouen, Tangier, Rabat or Casablanca.

How do I book and pay?

  2. Pay a 20–30% deposit by bank transfer to confirm; the rest is paid on arrival in cash (EUR, USD or MAD) or by card.

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before the tour.
