Tour Overview

This 5-day private desert tour from Agadir to Merzouga takes you across southern Morocco, from the Atlantic coast to the dunes of Erg Chebbi, before finishing at Errachidia Airport. It combines historic villages, mountain scenery, green oases and time in the Sahara, with overnight stops that allow you to explore more than just the places along the main road.

Your first two days take you through Taroudant, the saffron-growing region of Taliouine, Ouarzazate and the fortified village of Aït Ben Haddou. You will then travel through Skoura, the Valley of Roses and the Dades Valley before visiting Todra Gorge on the way to Merzouga.

In Merzouga, you will spend one night in a Sahara desert camp and another in a hotel or riad near the dunes. A sunset camel ride, a private 4×4 excursion and a visit to Khamlia give you time to enjoy the desert rather than arriving one evening and leaving the next morning.

The tour is arranged exclusively for you, with a private vehicle, an English-speaking driver and stops along the route. On Day 5, your driver will take you from Merzouga to Errachidia Moulay Ali Cherif Airport (ERH) according to your flight schedule.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Agadir → Taroudant → Taliouine → Taznakht → Ouarzazate

Your private driver will collect you from your hotel, riad or Agadir Airport in the morning. Leaving the coast behind, you will travel through the Souss Valley toward Taroudant, a traditional town surrounded by historic ramparts. Take a short walk near the old walls and, if time allows, visit part of the local market before continuing inland.

The journey takes you toward Taliouine, a region known for saffron cultivation. Here, the scenery begins to change as you travel through rocky hills, mountain villages and open countryside. Stop for lunch along the way, then continue through Taznakht toward Ouarzazate.

This is one of the longer driving days of the tour, so your driver will arrange breaks for refreshments and photographs. On arrival in Ouarzazate, check into your hotel or riad and enjoy a quiet evening. If you arrive early enough, you can take a short walk around the town before dinner.

~360 km Ouarzazate Dinner
Day 2

Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → Skoura → Valley of Roses → Dades Valley

After breakfast, drive to Aït Ben Haddou, a fortified village built from traditional earthen materials. Cross into the old ksar and walk through its narrow lanes, passing houses and historic buildings on the way to the upper viewpoint. From there, you can look across the village and the surrounding valley before returning to your vehicle.

Continue through Ouarzazate toward Skoura, where palm groves and old kasbahs stand alongside cultivated fields. The route then leads through the Valley of Roses, with opportunities to stop in small villages and enjoy the changing mountain scenery.

By afternoon, you will reach the Dades Valley. Depending on the time available, your driver can take you to a viewpoint overlooking the winding road and distinctive rock formations. Afterwards, settle into your hotel or riad, enjoy dinner and spend the evening surrounded by the valley's peaceful landscapes.

~160 km Dades Valley Breakfast & Dinner
Day 3

Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Erfoud → Rissani → Merzouga

Leave the Dades Valley after breakfast and travel toward Tinghir, passing villages and palm groves on the way to Todra Gorge. Stop for a walk through the canyon, where steep rock walls rise on either side of the riverbed. You will have time to enjoy the scenery and take photographs before continuing toward the Sahara.

After lunch along the route, travel through Erfoud and Rissani. As you approach Merzouga, the landscape opens into dry plains, with the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi appearing in the distance.

On arrival, meet your camel guide and begin your ride into the dunes. Stop to watch the sunset before continuing to your desert camp. Enjoy a Moroccan dinner and an evening around the campfire, then spend the night in your private tent beneath the Sahara sky.

~300 km Sahara desert camp, Merzouga Breakfast & Dinner
Day 4

Merzouga → Khamlia → Nomadic Communities → Erg Chebbi

Wake up early to watch the sunrise over the dunes. After breakfast at the camp, return to Merzouga by camel or 4×4 and meet your driver for a private desert excursion. Today, you can explore the area around Erg Chebbi without another long road transfer.

Travel along the desert tracks toward Khamlia, a village known for its Gnawa musical traditions. Spend some time listening to local musicians before continuing through the rocky landscapes and small settlements surrounding the dunes. If a local family is available to welcome visitors, you may also have the opportunity to learn about nomadic life in the region.

Stop for lunch, then return to your accommodation in Merzouga. The afternoon is free to relax, walk near the dunes or arrange an optional quad-bike or buggy excursion. Enjoy your final evening in the Sahara and prepare for the airport transfer the following day.

~60 km Hotel or riad in Merzouga Breakfast
Day 5

Merzouga → Rissani → Erfoud → Errachidia Airport

After breakfast, meet your private driver for the final stage of your tour. Leave the Erg Chebbi dunes behind and travel north through Rissani and Erfoud toward Errachidia.

The drive usually takes around two hours, but your departure time will be planned according to your flight and the airport's recommended check-in time. If your schedule allows, you can stop briefly for refreshments or photographs. For an early flight, your driver will take you directly to the airport without additional sightseeing stops.

You will be dropped off at Errachidia Moulay Ali Cherif Airport (ERH), where your private desert tour comes to an end. If your flight departs very early, we can adjust the itinerary so that you spend the fourth night in Errachidia rather than Merzouga, reducing the journey required on departure day.

~140 km End of tour: Errachidia Airport Breakfast

FAQ

How far is Merzouga from Agadir?

Agadir and Merzouga are separated by a long overland journey across southern Morocco. This itinerary divides the trip into three travel days, with overnight stops in Ouarzazate and the Dades Valley before arriving in the Sahara.

Where does the 5-day desert tour start and end?

The tour starts in Agadir and ends at Errachidia Airport. Pickup can be arranged from your hotel, riad or Agadir Airport, depending on your arrival plans.

Is this a private tour or a shared group tour?

This is a private tour. You will travel with your own driver and vehicle, without joining an unrelated group. The itinerary can be adjusted to suit your preferred accommodation, interests and flight schedule.

How many nights will we spend in the Sahara Desert?

You will spend two nights in the Merzouga area: one in a desert camp and one in a hotel or riad near the dunes. This gives you time for both a sunset camel ride and a full-day desert excursion.

Can we visit the Sahara without riding a camel?

Yes. A camel ride is not required to stay at the desert camp. A 4×4 vehicle can take you to the camp, allowing you to enjoy the dunes, sunset and overnight stay without riding a camel.

What should we pack for the desert?

Bring comfortable walking shoes, sunglasses, sunscreen, a hat and a small overnight bag for the camp. A warm layer is useful for evenings and early mornings, especially during the cooler months. Keep your camera or phone charged for the sunset and sunrise.

Can we take a flight from Errachidia Airport after the tour?

Yes. Your driver will transfer you to Errachidia Airport on Day 5. Share your flight number and departure time before booking so the final day can be arranged accordingly. If your flight is very early, staying in Errachidia on the fourth night may be more convenient.

Can the tour be customized?

Yes. You can request a different accommodation category, additional desert activities or changes to the sightseeing stops. We can also adjust the final night's location to suit your departure flight.
