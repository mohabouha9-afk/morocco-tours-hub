Tour Overview

Discover the best of Morocco on this 14-Day Morocco Tour from Marrakech, a carefully designed private journey combining imperial cities, historic kasbahs, dramatic mountains, authentic Berber villages, Atlantic coastlines and the spectacular Sahara Desert.

Starting and ending in Marrakech, this itinerary takes you through the High Atlas Mountains to Aït Ben Haddou and Ouarzazate before continuing into the Dades Valley and the magnificent Todra Gorge. You will experience the Sahara at Erg Chebbi with a camel trek, sunset over the dunes and a night in a private desert camp.

The journey continues through Fes, Chefchaouen, Rabat and Casablanca, with time to explore Morocco's cultural highlights without rushing. You will also visit Essaouira, enjoy traditional Moroccan cuisine and discover landscapes that change dramatically from mountains and desert to the Atlantic coast. This is a private and fully customizable Morocco tour, allowing you to travel at your own pace and adapt the itinerary to your interests.

Itinerary

A detailed day-by-day journey through Morocco's most breathtaking landscapes.

Day 1

Marrakech: Arrival

Welcome to Morocco! Upon arrival at Marrakech airport, your private driver will meet you and transfer you to your traditional riad or hotel in the heart of the city. Depending on your arrival time, you can relax or begin discovering Marrakech independently. In the evening, head toward Jemaa el-Fna, the famous main square, where the city becomes especially lively after sunset. Explore the surrounding souks filled with spices, handicrafts, leather goods and Moroccan textiles. Your first evening is a wonderful introduction to the atmosphere of Marrakech, with street food, musicians and local vendors creating a colorful scene.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 2

Marrakech: Guided City Tour

Today is dedicated to exploring Marrakech with a private local guide. Discover the historic Medina and its fascinating architecture, beginning with important landmarks such as the Bahia Palace, Saadian Tombs and Koutoubia Mosque. Walk through the narrow souks and learn about traditional Moroccan crafts and daily life. You can also visit the famous Majorelle Garden and explore the surrounding modern district of Gueliz. Your guide will help you discover hidden corners of the old city while explaining the history and traditions behind the monuments. In the afternoon, you will have free time to enjoy Marrakech at your own pace, perhaps relaxing at your riad, visiting local shops or enjoying Moroccan tea from a rooftop café.

Marrakech Breakfast
Day 3

Marrakech → Aït Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate → Dades Valley

After breakfast, leave Marrakech behind and drive across the spectacular High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka Pass. Enjoy panoramic views of mountain villages and changing landscapes before reaching the UNESCO-listed Aït Ben Haddou, one of Morocco's most famous fortified villages. Explore its narrow alleys and traditional earthen architecture with your driver or local guide. Continue toward Ouarzazate, known as the gateway to the desert and an important filming location for international movies and series. From Ouarzazate, travel through the scenic Valley of Roses toward the Dades Valley. The road becomes increasingly dramatic as rocky cliffs and colorful landscapes surround you. Arrive at your hotel in the afternoon and relax after a fascinating day of travel.

~330 km Dades Valley Breakfast
Day 4

Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Erfoud → Merzouga

After breakfast, continue toward the Sahara Desert. Drive through the spectacular Dades Valley and enjoy the changing scenery as you approach the Todra Gorge, famous for its towering limestone cliffs. Take a walk through the gorge and enjoy the peaceful surroundings. Continue toward Erfoud, an important center for fossils and dates, before crossing the desert landscapes toward Merzouga. As you approach Erg Chebbi, the golden Sahara dunes appear on the horizon. In the afternoon, exchange your vehicle for camels and begin your camel trek into the dunes. Ride across the golden sands while watching the changing colors of the desert at sunset. Arrive at your private Sahara camp, where you will enjoy dinner, traditional music and a peaceful evening beneath the stars.

~300 km Sahara Desert Camp Breakfast & dinner
Day 5

Merzouga: Full Sahara Desert Experience

Wake early to experience the beautiful Sahara sunrise before breakfast. After returning from the dunes, your private driver will take you on a 4×4 excursion around the Merzouga area. Visit a local nomadic family and learn about their traditional way of life in the desert. Continue to an area known for fossils and explore the surrounding landscapes. You may also visit the village of Khamlia, famous for its Gnawa music, and enjoy a traditional musical performance. Depending on your interests, there will be time for sandboarding, photography and relaxing beside the dunes. You can also explore the seasonal lake near Merzouga when water levels permit. In the afternoon, return to your accommodation and enjoy a relaxed evening in the desert region.

~40 km Merzouga Breakfast & dinner
Day 6

Merzouga → Rissani → Ziz Valley → Midelt

After breakfast, leave Merzouga and begin your journey north through the fascinating landscapes of southeastern Morocco. Stop in Rissani, an important historic trading town and the birthplace of the Alaouite dynasty. If the market is open, explore the lively souk where locals shop for food, spices, clothing and traditional products. Continue toward Erfoud and then travel through the dramatic Ziz Valley, where palm groves contrast beautifully with the surrounding rocky mountains. Enjoy panoramic viewpoints along the route before continuing toward Midelt, known as the apple capital of Morocco. The scenery gradually changes from desert to high mountain landscapes as you approach the Middle Atlas. Arrive in Midelt in the afternoon and enjoy a relaxing evening at your hotel.

~250 km Midelt Breakfast
Day 7

Midelt → Azrou → Ifrane → Fes

After breakfast, continue through the Middle Atlas Mountains toward Fes. Along the way, stop in the cedar forests near Azrou, where you may see Barbary macaques living among the trees. Continue to Ifrane, often called the "Switzerland of Morocco" because of its alpine-style architecture, clean streets and mountain setting. Take some time to explore the town before continuing toward Fes. The landscape gradually changes as you descend from the mountains toward the fertile plains surrounding the imperial city. Arrive in Fes in the afternoon and check into your traditional riad. The evening is free to relax or take a first walk around the city. Fes is one of Morocco's most fascinating destinations, famous for its ancient Medina, traditional crafts and rich cultural heritage.

~250 km Fes Breakfast
Day 8

Fes: Guided City Tour

Today you will explore Fes with a private local guide. Begin in the ancient Medina of Fes el-Bali, one of the largest pedestrian-only historic areas in the world. Walk through its narrow streets and discover colorful souks, traditional workshops and centuries-old architecture. Visit important landmarks including the Bou Inania Madrasa, Nejjarine Fountain and the historic tanneries. You will also learn about traditional Moroccan craftsmanship, including leatherwork, ceramics and metalwork. Continue to the Jewish Quarter, known as the Mellah, and visit viewpoints overlooking the old city. Your guide will explain the history of Fes and its importance as a religious, cultural and intellectual center. After the tour, enjoy free time to explore independently, shop in the souks or relax at your riad.

Fes Breakfast
Day 9

Fes → Chefchaouen

After breakfast, depart Fes and travel toward the Rif Mountains to the famous blue city of Chefchaouen. The journey takes you through green hills and beautiful countryside before reaching this picturesque mountain town. After checking into your riad, begin exploring the old Medina on foot. Chefchaouen is famous for its blue-painted walls, charming streets, small squares and traditional handicraft shops. Take your time photographing the colorful doors and peaceful alleyways or enjoy Moroccan tea from a café overlooking the town. You can also walk toward the Spanish Mosque for a panoramic view of Chefchaouen, especially beautiful around sunset. The evening is free to enjoy dinner in one of the local restaurants and experience the relaxed atmosphere of the Rif Mountains.

~200 km Chefchaouen Breakfast
Day 10

Chefchaouen → Volubilis → Meknes → Rabat

Leave Chefchaouen after breakfast and continue toward the historic Roman ruins of Volubilis. Explore the archaeological site and admire its impressive mosaics, columns and remains of ancient Roman buildings. Continue to Moulay Idriss, one of Morocco's most important pilgrimage towns, before heading to Meknes. Discover the former imperial city, including Bab Mansour, the impressive historic gateway, and the old Medina. After exploring Meknes, continue toward Rabat, Morocco's modern capital and one of its historic imperial cities. Depending on your arrival time, you may visit landmarks such as the Hassan Tower and the Kasbah of the Udayas. Check into your hotel or riad and enjoy a relaxing evening in Rabat.

~300 km Rabat Breakfast
Day 11

Rabat → Casablanca → El Jadida

After breakfast, continue along Morocco's Atlantic coast toward Casablanca. Visit the impressive Hassan II Mosque, one of Morocco's most recognizable landmarks, beautifully positioned beside the Atlantic Ocean. Continue south along the coast toward El Jadida, a historic Portuguese-influenced city. Explore the old fortified quarter and enjoy the relaxed atmosphere of the Atlantic coast. El Jadida is a pleasant contrast to the busy imperial cities, offering ocean views, historic architecture and local cafés. Take some time to walk along the waterfront and enjoy fresh seafood if you wish. Your private driver will then take you to your accommodation for the evening. The rest of the day is free to relax and enjoy the coastal atmosphere.

~190 km El Jadida Breakfast
Day 12

El Jadida → Essaouira

After breakfast, continue along the Atlantic coast toward Essaouira. The journey offers beautiful coastal scenery and a chance to see the landscapes that make this part of Morocco special. Upon arrival in Essaouira, check into your traditional riad before exploring the historic Medina. Walk through the narrow streets, browse local craft shops and visit the old harbor and Skala ramparts. Essaouira is known for its fresh seafood, relaxed atmosphere, colorful fishing boats and strong artistic character. Enjoy a seafood lunch near the port or discover one of the restaurants inside the Medina. In the afternoon, take a walk along the beach or relax at a rooftop café overlooking the Atlantic Ocean. The evening is free to enjoy Essaouira at your own pace.

~280 km Essaouira Breakfast
Day 13

Essaouira → Marrakech

Enjoy a relaxed morning in Essaouira before returning to Marrakech. Depending on your interests, you can spend some additional time walking through the Medina, shopping for handmade products or enjoying the beach. Depart later in the morning and travel inland toward Marrakech. Along the way, you may see the famous argan trees and, with some luck, goats climbing into their branches. Once you arrive in Marrakech, check into your accommodation and enjoy free time for the rest of the day. You can return to Jemaa el-Fna, explore the souks for last-minute shopping or simply relax at your riad. This final evening gives you another opportunity to enjoy the unique atmosphere of Marrakech before the end of your Morocco journey.

~190 km Marrakech Breakfast
Day 14

Marrakech: Departure

Your 14-Day Morocco Tour from Marrakech comes to an end today. Depending on your flight schedule, enjoy a final breakfast at your hotel or riad and some free time in Marrakech. Your private driver will meet you at the agreed time and transfer you comfortably to Marrakech Menara Airport for your departure flight. If your flight is later in the day, we can also arrange additional sightseeing, shopping or a relaxed morning before your airport transfer. We recommend allowing enough time for the airport journey and check-in. We hope you leave Morocco with wonderful memories of the Sahara dunes, historic medinas, mountain landscapes, Atlantic coast and the warm hospitality you experienced throughout your private journey.

End of tour: Marrakech Breakfast
