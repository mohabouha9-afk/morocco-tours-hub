Overview

Four days across the north of Morocco, from the old medina of Fes to the Strait of Gibraltar. A guided day in Fes el-Bali, then the imperial gate of Meknes, the Roman ruins of Volubilis and holy Moulay Idriss on the way up into the Rif Mountains.

Two nights in Chefchaouen, the blue town, with a day at the river pools and waterfalls of Akchour, then Tangier, its Kasbah and Cap Spartel, where two seas meet. The tour ends at the airport, the ferry or the train, wherever your journey goes next.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Fes: a guided day in the medina

    Your driver-guide and a local Fes guide meet you at your riad in the morning for Fes el-Bali: the Bou Inania Madrasa, the Nejjarine fountain, the Al Quaraouiyine quarter, the Chouara tanneries from a rooftop, and after lunch the Mellah and a viewpoint over the old city. No shop stops unless you ask.

    • Day on foot in the medina
    • Night: Fes riad
    • Breakfast from day 2
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Fes → Meknes → Volubilis → Moulay Idriss → Chefchaouen

    West to Meknes for the great Bab Mansour gate, then the Roman city of Volubilis with its columns and floor mosaics, and a walk in Moulay Idriss, the holy town on its hills. North through the Rif to Chefchaouen by late afternoon, for a first stroll in the blue lanes.

    • About 265 km, about 5 hours on the road
    • Night: Chefchaouen
    • Breakfast
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Akchour and the Rif Mountains

    Half an hour into the mountains to Akchour, where a river path leads through a green gorge to turquoise pools and waterfalls. Walk as far as you like, or rest by the water. Back in Chefchaouen in the afternoon for the Outa el-Hammam square and the sunset from the Spanish Mosque.

    • About 60 km there and back
    • Night: Chefchaouen
    • Breakfast
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Chefchaouen → Tangier

    Through the Rif to Tangier: the Kasbah and the medina, the Grand and Petit Socco, then out to Cap Spartel, where the Atlantic meets the Mediterranean, and the Caves of Hercules. The tour ends at Tangier airport, the ferry port, the train station or your hotel.

    • About 125 km to Tangier, plus Cap Spartel
    • Ends in Tangier: airport, port, station or hotel
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you drop us at the ferry to Spain?

Yes. Ferries to Tarifa leave from the Tangier city port, a few minutes from the medina; ferries to Algeciras leave from Tanger Med, about an hour east. Tell us your ferry and we time the last day so you board without rushing.

How hard is the walk at Akchour?

You choose. From the car park, a riverside path reaches the first pools in under an hour; the big waterfall and the natural rock arch called God’s Bridge take longer, with some scrambling. Your driver-guide knows the trails and you can turn back anytime.

Why two nights in Chefchaouen?

One night is enough for the blue lanes; the second gives you a full day in the Rif at Akchour and two sunsets from the Spanish Mosque, without a long drive. It is the slow heart of this tour.

Will the Fes guide take us to shops?

No shop stops unless you ask. The tanneries are seen from the terrace of a leather shop, which is free; the staff will show you their goods, but you never have to buy. Say no thanks and walk on.

How long are the drives?

Day 2 is the longest, about 5 hours of driving split by Meknes, Volubilis and Moulay Idriss. Akchour is half an hour from Chefchaouen, and Chefchaouen to Tangier about 2.5 hours. Day 1 is on foot.
