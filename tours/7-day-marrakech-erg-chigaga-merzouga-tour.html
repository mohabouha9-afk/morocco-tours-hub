Overview

Seven days from Marrakech to both of Morocco’s great sand seas, in your own 4×4 with your own driver-guide. You cross the High Atlas, walk through Aït Ben Haddou and the Todra Gorge, and ride a camel into the tall dunes of Erg Chebbi at Merzouga for your first night in the desert.

Then the road ends. Desert tracks take you to the ochre dunes of Ouzina and along the old Paris–Dakar rally route to Zagora, then off-road again to Erg Chigaga, the emptiest dunes in Morocco, for a second night under the stars. Because the tour is private, you stop when you want and never stop at a shop you didn’t ask for.

Itinerary

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Marrakech → Tizi n’Tichka → Aït Ben Haddou → Dades Valley

    Pick-up at 07:00 at your hotel, or at the nearest gate if your riad is in the medina. Over the High Atlas by the Tizi n’Tichka pass (2,260 m), then Aït Ben Haddou, where your driver-guide walks you up through the ksar. After lunch, the Skoura palm oasis and the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs to the red cliffs of the Dades Valley.

    • About 320 km, 6 to 7 hours on the road
    • Night: Dades Valley
    • Dinner & breakfast
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Erfoud → Merzouga

    A walk on the flat riverbed of the Todra Gorge, between rock walls about 300 m high, then the fossil workshops of Erfoud and the old caravan town of Rissani. At Merzouga, leave your big bags in the 4×4 and ride a camel into the Erg Chebbi dunes for sunset. Dinner in the camp and music around the fire.

    • About 270 km, about 5 hours on the road
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: Merzouga camp
    • Breakfast & dinner
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Merzouga → Khamlia → Taouz → Ouzina

    Sunrise on the dunes and the sandboards before breakfast. Tea with a nomad family, Gnawa music in Khamlia, then south past the rock carvings of Taouz and off the road to Ouzina, a quiet village below ochre-red dunes. A short day, with the late afternoon free on the dunes and a night far from any crowd.

    • About 60 km, half of it desert track
    • Night: Ouzina
    • Breakfast & dinner
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Ouzina → Ramlia → Sidi Ali → Paris–Dakar track → Zagora

    The big off-road day. Through the palm oasis of Ramlia to Sidi Ali, then west on the old Paris–Dakar rally tracks, past the clay pan of Lake Maider, across stony plains and dry riverbeds. Lunch at a simple stop on the way. In the late afternoon the palm groves of the Draa Valley appear and you reach Zagora.

    • About 170 km, mostly desert track, 6 to 7 hours
    • Night: Zagora
    • Breakfast & dinner
  5. 5
    Day 5

    Zagora → Tamegroute → M’Hamid → Erg Chigaga

    Tamegroute’s green-glazed pottery and its library of old Koranic manuscripts, then lunch in M’Hamid, where the road ends. Your 4×4 crosses about 50 km of desert to Erg Chigaga, the largest and emptiest sand sea in Morocco. A camel ride at sunset and your second night in the dunes.

    • About 100 km by road, then 50 km of desert track
    • Camel ride about 1 hour
    • Night: Erg Chigaga camp
    • Breakfast & dinner
  6. 6
    Day 6

    Erg Chigaga → Iriqui dry lake → Foum Zguid → Ouarzazate

    Sunrise over Erg Chigaga, then west by 4×4 across the Iriqui dry lake, flat to the horizon, to Foum Zguid, where the road begins again. Lunch, then through Taznakht, the carpet-weaving town, to Ouarzazate for your last dinner of the tour.

    • About 100 km of desert track, then 155 km by road
    • Night: Ouarzazate
    • Breakfast & dinner
  7. 7
    Day 7

    Ouarzazate → Tizi n’Tichka → Marrakech

    A relaxed breakfast, then back over the High Atlas, with photo stops at the Tichka viewpoints and the Berber villages below. You are at your hotel in Marrakech by early afternoon, in time for the medina or an evening flight.

    • About 200 km, about 4 hours on the road
    • In Marrakech by early afternoon
    • Breakfast

Frequently Asked Questions

Why visit both Merzouga and Erg Chigaga?

They are Morocco’s two great sand seas and feel very different. Erg Chebbi at Merzouga has the tallest dunes, up to about 150 m, near a road. Erg Chigaga is lower, about 50–60 m, but bigger and almost empty, reached only by 4×4. This tour links them through the desert instead of driving back the same way.

How much of the tour is off-road?

Parts of four days: about 30 km of track to Ouzina, about 170 km from Ouzina to Zagora on the old Paris–Dakar tracks, then about 50 km into Erg Chigaga and 100 km out across the Iriqui lake. It is bumpy in places but safe in a 4×4 with a driver who knows the tracks.

What are the two desert camps like?

Private Berber tents with real beds, with dinner and breakfast in the camp. The midrange and luxury camps in our table have a private bathroom in your tent; the standard Erg Chigaga camp shares bathrooms. Water and power come by 4×4 at Erg Chigaga, so camps there are simpler.

When is the best time for this tour?

Spring (March to May) and autumn (late September to November) are best: mild days for the off-road crossings and clear desert skies. Summer heat in the deep south makes the long track days hard, and winter nights in the camps drop close to freezing.

What happens on the last day?

Only a short drive over the High Atlas from Ouarzazate. You are back at your hotel in Marrakech by early afternoon, so an evening flight works, or you have time for the medina.
