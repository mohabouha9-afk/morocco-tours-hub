Agafay, the Desert Next to Marrakech

The Agafay desert starts about 40 km south-west of Marrakech, around 40 minutes by car. It is a stone desert, not a sea of sand: pale rolling hills and gravel plains, with the High Atlas on the horizon, snow-capped in winter. Desert camps are spread across the hills, so you get the silence, the camels and the sunset without the long road to the Sahara.

Here are all our things to do in Agafay: a day trip with lunch and sunset, a camel ride with dinner and a fire show, a night in a desert camp, quad or buggy rides on the desert tracks, and a sunrise balloon flight. Each one is private, with pick-up at your hotel, and the price is agreed before you go.

Agafay FAQ

Is Agafay a real desert?

Yes, but a stone desert: pale rolling hills and gravel plains with the High Atlas behind, and no sand dunes. For tall golden dunes you need Merzouga, about two days from Marrakech by road.

How far is Agafay from Marrakech?

About 40 km south-west of the city, around 40 minutes by car. Every trip includes the pick-up at your hotel, or at the nearest gate if your riad is in the medina.

When is the best time to visit Agafay?

October to May. Summer middays are hot, often above 35 °C, so in summer we go in the late afternoon or at sunrise. Evenings are cool from October to April: bring a jacket.

Agafay or Merzouga?

Agafay if you only have an evening or a day: camels, dinner and the sunset without a long drive. Merzouga if you can give the desert 2 to 3 days and want real sand dunes. See our desert tours from Marrakech.

Can we stay the night in Agafay?

Yes. Our Agafay desert camp overnight adds a night in your own tent to the camel ride and dinner show: you wake up to the sunrise over the hills and we drive you back to Marrakech in the morning.

How do I book and pay?

Send your request with the form on each activity page or on WhatsApp. Pay a small deposit to confirm; the rest is paid on the day, in cash or by card. Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before.
