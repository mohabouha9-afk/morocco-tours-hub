Chefchaouen, Before the Crowds

Chefchaouen is calmer and cooler than the big imperial cities, and small enough to walk end to end. The only catch is its fame: from late morning the main lanes fill with day visitors and photo queues. So we time your visit: a guided walk first, then free time away from the busy corners, or a night in town for the empty lanes at sunrise and the sunset from the Spanish Mosque.

Here is how we do Chefchaouen: day trips from Tangier and Fes, tours that sleep in the blue city, what to see there, how long to stay and how to get there. Each trip is private, with pick-up at your hotel, riad or port, and the price is agreed before you go.

Things to Do in Chefchaouen

  • The Blue Medina

    Lanes, stairways and doorways in every shade of blue. Come early for soft light and empty streets, and ask before photographing people.

  • Uta el-Hammam & the Kasbah

    The main square, with cafés under the red walls of the 15th-century Kasbah. Its small museum and garden cost about 60 MAD; the tower looks over the roofs.

  • The Spanish Mosque

    A 15 to 20-minute walk uphill to the best view of the town, with the whole blue city below the Rif peaks at sunset.

  • Ras El Maa

    The spring at the top of the medina, where the river comes out of the mountain and locals meet by the water.

  • Local Crafts

    Wool blankets and garments, woven rugs, leather and the local goat cheese. With us there are no shop stops: you buy only if you want to.

  • Akchour Waterfalls

    Half an hour into the Rif: a river path through a green gorge to turquoise pools, best from May to October. A full day on our 4-day Fes to Tangier tour.

Getting to Chefchaouen

  • No Airport or Train

    Chefchaouen is reached by road only. The nearest airports are Tangier and Fes, and we drive you from either.

  • From Tangier

    About 120 km, 2 to 2.5 hours past Tetouan. We meet ferry and cruise guests at the port exit on our Tangier day trip.

  • From Fes

    About 200 km, 3.5 hours through the Rif foothills and Ouazzane. Our Fes day trip leaves at 08:00 and is back by 19:30.

  • To Your Riad

    Cars cannot enter the medina lanes, so your driver stops at the nearest gate. The town is on a slope with stairs: pack light and wear good shoes.

Chefchaouen FAQ

Is a day trip enough, or should we stay a night?

A day trip gives you about four hours in town: enough for the main lanes, the square, the Kasbah and lunch. If you can, stay one night: the day visitors leave in the afternoon, you see the sunset from the Spanish Mosque and walk the empty blue lanes the next morning. Two nights leave time for the Akchour waterfalls.

Is it better to come from Tangier or from Fes?

From Tangier the drive is only about 2 to 2.5 hours, so the Tangier day trip is the easier day. From Fes it is about 3.5 hours each way and an 11-hour day; if you are based in Fes, a night in Chefchaouen on the 3-day tour is more relaxed.

Is Chefchaouen safe?

Yes, it is one of the calmest towns in Morocco. Expect shop owners to invite you in, and young men to offer you hashish from time to time: it is illegal in Morocco, so a polite “no, thank you” and walking on is enough. With your guide, nobody insists.

When is the best time to visit Chefchaouen?

Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November), with mild days for the steep lanes and the green hills around the town. Summer is cooler than the plains but busy, especially in August. Winter is cold and often wet, sometimes with snow on the peaks, but very quiet.

Can we see the Akchour waterfalls?

Yes. Akchour is about half an hour into the Rif from Chefchaouen, with a river path to turquoise pools and waterfalls; it is best from May to October. Our 4-day Fes to Tangier tour gives it a full day, and either day trip can swap part of the medina time for it.
