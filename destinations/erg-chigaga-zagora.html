Zagora and Erg Chigaga, the Southern Sahara

South of Marrakech, over the High Atlas and past Aït Ben Haddou, the road follows the Draa Valley through palm groves and kasbahs to Zagora, about 6 to 7 hours away. Around Zagora the desert is stony with low dunes: the shortest real desert night from Marrakech, in 2 days. The road ends 1.5 hours further south at M’Hamid. From there, only a 4×4 reaches Erg Chigaga, about 50 km of desert track away: the biggest and emptiest dune sea in Morocco.

Here are all our tours to the south: 2 days to Zagora, 3 or 4 days to Erg Chigaga, a camp night if you are already in M’Hamid, and longer tours that add Merzouga. Each one is private, and the price is agreed before you go.

On the Way South

  • Aït Ben Haddou

    The famous mud-brick ksar over the High Atlas, on every tour on the way out or back.

  • The Draa Valley

    Palm groves, kasbahs and villages from Agdz to Zagora, the greenest road in the south.

  • Tamegroute

    Its green pottery and an old Koranic library, a short stop south of Zagora.

  • M’Hamid

    The last village at the end of the road, where the 4×4 track to the dunes begins.

  • The Erg Chigaga Dunes

    A sea of sand with few camps: a sunset camel ride, dinner by the fire and a sky full of stars.

  • The Iriqui Dry Lake

    The way back by 4×4 across a flat lakebed on the old Paris–Dakar track to Foum Zguid.

Erg Chigaga & Zagora FAQ

Erg Chigaga or Zagora: which should we choose?

Zagora if you have 2 days: low dunes and stony desert, about 6 to 7 hours from Marrakech on paved road. Erg Chigaga if you have 3 days or more: a real sea of dunes, 50 km past the end of the road, reached only by 4×4.

Erg Chigaga or Merzouga?

Merzouga’s Erg Chebbi is taller, about 150 m, and easy to reach on paved roads, but busier. Erg Chigaga’s dunes are lower, about 50 to 60 m, yet the sand sea is bigger and almost empty: no village, no road, very few camps.

How do we get to Erg Chigaga?

Drive to M’Hamid, the last village on the road south, about 7 to 8 hours from Marrakech or 1.5 hours from Zagora. From there it is about 50 km of desert track, 1.5 to 2.5 hours by 4×4. Our tours from Marrakech include the 4×4 all the way.

Can we drive our own car or come by bus?

Yes, as far as M’Hamid: buses run there and to Zagora, and the road is paved. A normal car cannot reach the dunes, so you park in M’Hamid and we take you in by 4×4, as on our Erg Chigaga camp night.

When is the best time to go?

October to April. From June to August days pass 40 °C and the 4×4 ride is tiring; from December to February nights drop close to freezing, so bring warm layers. Book early for Christmas, New Year and Easter.
