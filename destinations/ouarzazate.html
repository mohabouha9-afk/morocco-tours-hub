More Than a Photo Stop

Most travellers see Ouarzazate on day one of a desert tour: a long drive over the Tizi n’Tichka pass and an hour at Aït Ben Haddou between coaches. It deserves more. With your own guide you cross the river, climb to the old granary above the ksar and choose between the film studios and the Taourirt Kasbah, and with a night here you see the clay walls at sunset and sunrise, when the day visitors have gone.

Here is how we do Ouarzazate: the day trip from Marrakech, desert tours that start or sleep here, the kasbahs and valleys around it, how long to stay and how to get there. Each trip is private, with pick-up at your hotel, riad or Ouarzazate airport, and the price is agreed before you go.

Things to Do around Ouarzazate

  • The Ksar of Aït Ben Haddou

    The UNESCO-listed clay village of Gladiator and Game of Thrones, above the Ounila river. Cross the river, walk the alleys and climb to the old granary for the view. A few houses inside ask a few dirhams to go in.

  • The Taourirt Kasbah

    In Ouarzazate itself: the 19th-century palace of the Glaoui family, a maze of towers, rooms and painted ceilings. A small entry fee.

  • The Atlas Film Studios

    Morocco’s film capital: walk the sets of the international films shot in the desert around town, on a guided visit (about 80 MAD).

  • Skoura & Kasbah Amridil

    A palm oasis just east of Ouarzazate on the kasbah road, with the restored Kasbah Amridil, one of the finest in the south (small fee to go in).

  • The Valley of Roses & the Dades

    The rose gardens of Kalaat M’Gouna, then the red villages, the Monkey Fingers rocks and the hairpin road up the Dades Gorge.

  • Telouet on the Old Road

    The Glaoui palace on the old caravan road over the Atlas, with painted cedar ceilings, carved plaster and tiled halls. An easy add-on to the day trip or on the way back to Marrakech.

Getting to Ouarzazate

  • From Marrakech

    About 190 km to Aït Ben Haddou over the Tizi n’Tichka pass, 3.5 hours with stops, then half an hour on to Ouarzazate.

  • Over the Pass

    A good paved road up to 2,260 m with many bends: ask for the front seat if you get car-sick. Cold or snowy from December to February.

  • By Air

    Ouarzazate has a small airport. We pick you up there for our 2-day desert tour or any private route.

  • On to the Desert

    About 140 km and 2.5 hours to the Dades Gorge, and about 360 km to the Merzouga dunes: a full day with stops.

Ouarzazate FAQ

Is the day trip from Marrakech worth it?

Yes, if the Sahara is not in your plans: you cross the High Atlas, walk the ksar of Aït Ben Haddou with a guide and see Ouarzazate in one long day, about 7 hours on the road in total. If you are going to the desert anyway, skip the day trip: Aït Ben Haddou is day one of our 3-day Merzouga tour.

Should we stay a night in Aït Ben Haddou or in Ouarzazate?

Aït Ben Haddou for the ksar at its best: the sunset on the clay walls and a quiet sunrise before the day visitors arrive. Ouarzazate, about half an hour away, has more hotels and is closer to the studios and the Taourirt Kasbah. Both work as a first night on the way to the desert.

Is the Tizi n’Tichka road safe? I get car-sick.

Yes, it is a good paved road, but it climbs to 2,260 m with many bends, and road works can add some time. Ask for the front seat, and we stop at the viewpoints and whenever you need. From December to February the pass can be cold or snowy; your driver checks the road the day before.

When is the best time to visit Ouarzazate?

Spring and autumn, with warm days and cool nights. Summer is very hot in the ksar and the valleys, so we start early. Winter days are sunny and pleasant, but nights are cold and the pass may have snow.

Can we start a desert tour in Ouarzazate?

Yes. Our 2-day Ouarzazate to Merzouga tour picks you up at your hotel or the airport around 07:30, goes out by the Dades Valley and the Todra Gorge to a camp in the dunes, and comes back by the southern oases. Staying in Skoura or Aït Ben Haddou? We pick you up there.

How do I book and pay?

Send your request with the form on each tour page or on WhatsApp. Pay a 20–30% deposit by bank transfer to confirm; the rest is paid on arrival in cash (EUR, USD or MAD) or by card. Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before.
