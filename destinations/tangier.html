Why Tangier?

Spain on the horizon, white lanes up to the Kasbah, the Atlantic meeting the Mediterranean. International until 1956, Tangier inspired Matisse and Paul Bowles, and still serves mint tea with a sea view at Café Hafa. It draws day visitors from Spain, cruise guests, art lovers and travellers starting north. One day will charm you; two add blue Chefchaouen.

How Are You Arriving?

Most travellers reach Tangier by ferry from Spain, on a cruise, by air or train, or overland from Fes. There are two ports: Tanger Ville in town and Tanger Med about 50 km out, so check yours before you travel.

Tangier Month by Month

The best time to visit Tangier is April to June, or September and October, for walking and day trips. Summer is for swimming. Winter is mild but often rainy, and the city can be windy, so pack a layer.

MonthsWhat it’s likeWhat to do
April to JuneBest timeMild to warm, little rain by June.Good for walking and day trips.
July to AugustBeach weatherWarm and dry. Best for swimming.Sightsee early or late in the day.

Good to Know

  • Tangier is generally safe; a firm “no, thank you” stops most touts.
  • Ask the taxi fare before you get in, or let our driver collect you.
  • Morocco is on GMT+0: Spain is 1 hour ahead in winter, 2 in summer.
  • Catching a ferry or ship? Tell us the time and we plan around it.
  • Windy and hilly: bring a light layer and comfortable shoes.
  • No dress code; covering shoulders and knees draws less notice.

Tangier FAQ

Is Tangier worth visiting?

Yes, for the Kasbah, the sea views and its artistic past. It is a lively port city rather than a quiet old town; if you want calm blue lanes, add Chefchaouen. Chefchaouen day trip.

Is Tangier safe for solo women and at night?

Yes, with normal city care. Some women get comments from men: walk on without answering. At night, stay on lit, busy streets or take a taxi back. With our private guide you are not on your own.

Can I visit Tangier in one day from Spain?

Yes. The ferry from Tarifa takes about an hour to the town port. Bring your passport and leave a margin before your return sailing. Day tour from Tarifa.

Can I walk into town from the cruise port?

From Tanger Ville, yes: about 10 to 15 minutes to the Grand Socco, uphill at the end. Some ships use Tanger Med, far out of town, so check with your cruise line. We can meet you at either.

Can I use euros in Tangier?

Many tourist shops and restaurants accept them, often at a poorer rate. Carry some dirhams for taxis, tips and small purchases; ATMs are easy to find in town.

Where should I stay in Tangier?

The medina or Kasbah for old lanes and sea views, though cars may not reach your door. The seafront and new town for modern hotels and easy pick-ups.

How do I get from Fes to Tangier?

By train, or by private transfer in about 4 to 5 hours. Going through Chefchaouen takes longer but lets you see the blue town on the way. 4-day tour from Fes.
