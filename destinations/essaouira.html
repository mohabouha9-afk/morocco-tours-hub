Essaouira, Without the Rush

Essaouira is the easy-going opposite of Marrakech: a small medina you can walk end to end, few touts, grilled fish at the port and a long beach. The catch is the road: 2.5 to 3 hours each way, and shared tours add a shop stop and leave you about four hours in town. In a private car you leave when you want, skip the shops and get about five hours by the sea, or stay the night and see the town when the day visitors have gone.

Here is how we do Essaouira: the day trip from Marrakech, tours that sleep by the sea, what to see there, how long to stay and how to get there. Each trip is private, with pick-up at your hotel or riad, and the price is agreed before you go.

Things to Do in Essaouira

  • The Medina

    A UNESCO-listed walled town of white and blue houses, small enough to walk end to end and far calmer than the Marrakech souks. About 2–3 km on foot, mostly flat.

  • The Skala Ramparts

    The sea walls with their row of bronze cannons facing the Atlantic: the best place in town for the sunset.

  • The Fishing Port

    Blue boats, the morning catch on the quay and the harbour grills. Choose your fish and agree the price by weight before they cook it. The Skala du Port tower has a small entry fee.

  • The Beach

    A long, windy bay for walks, kitesurfing, and camel or horse rides on the sand. Better for a stroll than for sunbathing.

  • The Mellah & Bayt Dakira

    The old Jewish quarter and its restored house of memory, a reminder of the Muslim and Jewish families who built the port together.

  • Crafts & Gnawa Music

    The thuya-wood workshops under the ramparts, art galleries and the Gnawa music the town is known for, with its famous festival each year. No shop stops with us: you buy only if you want to.

How Many Days in Essaouira?

  • A Day Trip

    About five hours in town from Marrakech: the medina and ramparts with a guide, lunch at the port and a walk by the sea.

  • One Night

    The best fit for most: the sunset on the Skala and the port at its best hour, early morning as the boats come in.

  • Two Nights

    A free day by the sea: the Mellah, the woodworkers, a long beach walk and a rooftop sunset, with no packing.

  • On the Way South

    Essaouira is the first stop of the coast road to Imsouane, Taghazout and Agadir. See the tours below.

Getting to Essaouira

  • From Marrakech

    About 180–190 km west on a flat road, 2.5 to 3 hours with a coffee stop. The easiest start, and good news if you get car-sick.

  • From Agadir

    About 170 km on the coast road through argan hills by Imsouane, about 3 hours. Our 6-day tour starts at Agadir airport.

  • By Bus

    About 3 hours from the Marrakech bus station on fixed times, plus taxis at both ends. Fine for a long stay, tight for a day trip.

  • To Your Riad

    The medina is car-free, so your driver stops at the nearest gate and a porter with a cart takes your bags to the door.

Essaouira FAQ

Is a day trip enough, or should we stay a night?

A day trip gives you about five hours in town: the medina, the ramparts, lunch at the port and a walk by the sea. If you can, stay one night: the day visitors leave in the late afternoon, you watch the sun set over the ocean from the Skala and see the blue boats come in the next morning. Our 3-day Marrakech & Essaouira tour does exactly that.

Why not just take the bus from Marrakech?

The bus takes about 3 hours from the station on fixed times, so with taxis at both ends a day trip leaves little time by the sea. With a private car you leave from your door, stop when you like and come back when you are ready; the road is flat all the way, good news if you get car-sick.

Is it windy? Can we swim?

The Atlantic wind blows most afternoons, strongest from June to August, so bring a light jacket even in summer. The long beach is better for walks, kitesurfing and camel or horse rides than for sunbathing; on calm days a swim is possible.

When is the best time to visit Essaouira?

Spring and autumn, with mild sunny days and less wind. Summer stays fresh while Marrakech is hot, which is why many come for a break from the heat. Weekends are busier with Moroccan visitors; Tuesday to Thursday is calmer.

Can we continue down the coast to Agadir?

Yes. From Essaouira the coast road runs south through argan hills to Imsouane and the surf village of Taghazout. Our 4-day tour ends in Agadir, and the 7-day coastal tour gives you two nights in Essaouira before the coast and comes back to Marrakech.
