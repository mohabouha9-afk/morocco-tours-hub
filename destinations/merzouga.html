Merzouga and the Erg Chebbi Dunes

Merzouga is a small village on the edge of Erg Chebbi, the highest sand dunes in Morocco, about 560 km from Marrakech and 470 km from Fes. Most visitors come for one night in a desert camp, but there is much more to do: sunset and sunrise camel rides, quad and buggy tours on the dunes, a day by 4×4 to nomad families and the Gnawa village of Khamlia, and a medfouna cooking class with a local family.

Book one activity or combine several, always private and with the price agreed before you arrive. Below are all our things to do in Merzouga, ideas for combining them, and the desert tours that bring you here from Marrakech, Fes or Errachidia.

Merzouga FAQ

When is the best time to visit Merzouga?

March to May and September to November, with warm days and cool nights. Winter days are sunny but nights drop close to freezing; from June to August the dunes pass 40 °C, so activities move to early morning and evening.

How do we get to Merzouga?

By car it is about 560 km from Marrakech and 470 km from Fes; CTM and Supratours buses arrive every day, and Errachidia airport is about 2 hours away. We can pick you up, or bring you on one of our desert tours from Marrakech or from Fes.

Where can we leave a rental car?

At our meeting point in Merzouga, free of charge, while you ride to the camp or go out on an activity. Bring a small bag for the night and leave the big luggage in the car.

Can we combine several activities?

Yes, and it is the best way to see Merzouga: a quad or 4×4 by day, camels at sunset and a night in the camp. Tell us your dates and we plan the timings.

What should we wear in the desert?

Light layers, a scarf against sun and blowing sand, sunglasses and closed shoes; a warm jacket for evenings from November to March. Cover shoulders and knees in the villages.

How do I book and pay?

Send your request with the form on each activity page or on WhatsApp. Pay a small deposit to confirm; the rest is paid on the day, in cash or by card. Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before.
