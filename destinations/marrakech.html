Marrakech, Without the Hassle

Marrakech is where most Morocco trips begin, and the city can be a lot on day one: lanes with no signposts, airport taxis that skip the meter, “guides” who ask for money after showing you the way. A local at your side turns all of that into the good part: the souks, the palaces, the food stalls at dusk.

Here is everything we do in Marrakech: tours in the city, day trips to the Atlas, the coast and the waterfalls, and the desert. Each one is private, with pick-up at your riad or hotel, and the price is agreed before you go.

How Many Days in Marrakech?

  • One Day

    The medina with a guide in the morning, then Jemaa el-Fnaa at dusk, or our food tour for dinner.

  • Two Days

    Add Majorelle and the palaces at 08:00, before the crowds, and a hammam or cooking class in the afternoon.

  • Three Days

    Add one day trip: the Atlas villages, the Ourika Valley or Essaouira on the coast.

  • Four Days or More

    Add the desert: an evening in Agafay, or 2 to 3 days to the dunes of Zagora or Merzouga.

Marrakech FAQ

How many days do we need in Marrakech?

Two to three days for the city itself: the medina, the gardens and palaces, an evening in Jemaa el-Fnaa. Add one day for each day trip, and two to three more if you want the Sahara.

Do we need a guide in the medina?

On your first day, yes. The lanes are a maze, signposts are few and people offer to “help” for a fee. With our medina tour nobody bothers you, there are no shop stops, and your guide shows you how to find your way back alone.

How do we get from the airport to our riad?

Menara airport is 5 km from the medina, 15 to 25 minutes by car. With our airport transfer your driver waits in arrivals with your name, then walks you from the nearest gate to the riad door, so no taxi haggling.

When is the best time to visit Marrakech?

Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November): warm days and comfortable evenings. Summer is often above 40 °C, so we sightsee early and late. Winter days are mild and sunny, with cool evenings.

Can we see the Sahara from Marrakech?

Yes. Agafay, 40 minutes away, gives you camels and a sunset in an evening, without dunes. For sand dunes, Zagora takes 2 days and Merzouga 3. Compare them in our desert tours from Marrakech.

How do I book and pay?

Send your request with the form on each tour page or on WhatsApp. Pay a 20–30% deposit by bank transfer to confirm; the rest is paid on arrival in cash (EUR, USD or MAD) or by card. Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before.
