Our Story

Local experts, unforgettable journeys

Morocco Tours Hub is a local, owner-run travel company based in Marrakech. We were born from a simple belief: that the best way to experience Morocco is privately, at your own pace, with people who call this country home.

From the golden dunes of the Sahara to the blue lanes of Chefchaouen, our team designs journeys that go beyond the ordinary — combining iconic highlights with the authentic encounters that make a trip truly yours.

Our Team

Meet the People Behind Your Moroccan Journey

Behind every unforgettable journey is a team of people who know Morocco not only as a destination, but as home.

Our team is made up of passionate local travel professionals, experienced drivers, knowledgeable guides, and trusted partners who share one goal: to make your time in Morocco comfortable, authentic, and truly memorable.

From the moment you arrive, we are here to take care of the details while you enjoy the experience. Our drivers know the roads, mountains, valleys, and desert routes, while our local guides bring Morocco's history, culture, traditions, and stories to life.

We believe that traveling with a local team gives you a different way to discover Morocco. Beyond the famous sights, we love introducing our guests to small villages, local families, traditional food, desert landscapes, and everyday Moroccan life.

Local Knowledge. Personal Service. Genuine Hospitality.

We are proud to be a Moroccan team, and we treat every traveler as a guest rather than simply a customer. Whether you are traveling as a couple, family, group of friends, or solo traveler, we take the time to understand what you are looking for and adapt your journey around you.

Our tours are private and flexible, allowing you to travel at your own pace, stop for photographs, discover places beyond the usual routes, and enjoy Morocco your way.

Why Travel With Our Team?

  • Local Moroccan experience – We know the destinations, roads, culture, and traditions firsthand.
  • Experienced drivers – Our drivers are familiar with Morocco's cities, mountains, valleys, and desert routes.
  • Professional local guides – Discover the history and culture of Morocco with knowledgeable local guides.
  • Personal attention – We are available before and during your trip to help make everything run smoothly.
  • Private journeys – Your tour is designed around your travel plans rather than a fixed group schedule.
  • Authentic experiences – We love sharing the Morocco beyond the main tourist attractions.
  • Flexible travel – Your itinerary can be adapted to your interests, pace, and preferences.

More Than a Tour – A Local Connection

For us, tourism is about people. It is about sharing stories over a glass of Moroccan mint tea, watching the sunset over the Sahara, meeting local communities, discovering hidden corners of the country, and creating memories that stay with you long after you return home.

We are here to welcome you to Morocco, show you the country we love, and help you experience it in your own way.
