Our Story

Morocco Tours Hub is a local, owner-run travel company based in Marrakech. We were born from a simple belief: that the best way to experience Morocco is privately, at your own pace, with people who call this country home.

From the golden dunes of the Sahara to the blue lanes of Chefchaouen, our team designs journeys that go beyond the ordinary — combining iconic highlights with the authentic encounters that make a trip truly yours.

Our Mission

To show you Morocco the way we know it — privately, honestly and at your pace. One clear price agreed before you travel, and a local team looking after every detail until you fly home.

Our Vision

To be the local team travellers recommend to their friends first — known for kept promises, fair prices and journeys that feel personal from the first message to the last goodbye.

What Makes Us Different

  • Local Knowledge

    Our driver-guides were born here. They know the roads, the shortcuts of the medina and the story behind every kasbah. Your guide is Moroccan, never subcontracted abroad.

  • Personalized Trips

    No fixed group schedule. We build the route around your dates, your pace and what you love — and adjust it on the road. Change a stop or a day: just ask your guide.

  • Transparent Pricing

    We work directly with drivers, riads and camps — no middlemen. Your quote lists what is included and what is not. What we agree on is what you pay.

  • Trusted Stays

    Riads, kasbahs and desert camps we know and work with, chosen for comfort, cleanliness and real Moroccan hospitality. We tell you where you sleep before you book.

  • With You 24/7

    From your first message to your flight home, our English-speaking team is one WhatsApp away. A real person answers, before and during your trip.

  • Authentic Encounters

    Mint tea with nomad families, lunch in a Berber village, the café locals use — the Morocco beyond the postcards. Meet locals, share real moments.
