## Mouhou Tours 4-day Fes round trip (top result)
- D1 Fes → Ifrane → Azrou cedars (monkeys) → Midelt lunch → Tizi n'Talghomt → Ziz → Merzouga, "7 h 30 / 550 km", camp.
- D2 4×4 around Merzouga: Khamlia Gnawa, black desert, old mining village, nomad family tea; afternoon camel ride, 2nd camp night.
- D3 sunrise → Rissani market → Todra Gorge, night Todra (200 km, 3 h).
- D4 Todra → **Aït Hani → Agoudal** (High Atlas back road) → Rich → Fes, "9 h / 500 km"; main road via Errachidia if minivan or bad weather.
- 08:00 start, back 17:00–19:00; nearest road for car-free riads; camel 40 min–1 h 30; 4×4 alternative; deposit, balance cash; full refund ≥3 days.
- Their "Good to know": "Fes is approximately 470 km from Merzouga… 7 to 8 hours".
