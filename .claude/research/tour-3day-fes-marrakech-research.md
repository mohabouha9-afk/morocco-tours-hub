## The proven route (identical on every top listing)
1. Fes → Ifrane → Azrou cedar forest (Barbary macaques) → Midelt lunch → Ziz Valley → Erfoud → Merzouga. Sunset camel ride, camp. marrakech-desert-trips gives "about 8 hours"; our OSRM road `fm4-d1` is ~470 km.
2. Sunrise → Rissani → Tinghir → Todra Gorge walk → Dades Valley night (~270 km, ~4–5 h).
3. Valley of Roses → Skoura → Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → Tizi n'Tichka → Marrakech in the late afternoon (~320 km, ~6 h). GYG gives drop-off "approximately 6:00 PM"; marrakech-desert-trips gives "~17:00".
- Pick-up: marrakech-desert-trips "08:00 all year round"; GYG "early". **We use around 07:30**, the same as our 4-day Fes → Marrakech page (owner to confirm).
- Total about 1,030 km. The direct motorway Fes → Marrakech is about 530 km, 5–6 h. The train takes about 7 h via Rabat and Casablanca (marrakech-desert-trips FAQ).
