## What the market sells
- **Palmeraie** = the standard: flat sandy tracks, 15–20 min from the medina, 1 h taster or 2 h ride; beginners welcome (marrakech.me, overmoroccotours).
- **Agafay** = stony hills with Atlas views, ~40–45 min away, 1 h ride + tea, ~2.5–3 h in total with transfer (azizagafaydesert.com).
- **Atlas** foothills = half or full day, for riders who can trot and canter.
- Steps everywhere: hotel pick-up → meet the horses, helmet fitted, short briefing (how to sit, reins, signals) → ride, pace set by the least experienced rider → mint tea → drop-off.
- Beginners: handler leads the horse for the first ~15 minutes (marrakech.me); "slow ride with the occasional trot is what you want as a beginner" (GYG review via search).
- Best time: morning or the hour before sunset; summer midday 42 °C in the Palmeraie. Ramadan: mornings.

## Worries (reviews, guides)
- **Horse welfare** varies by stable (overmoroccotours); reviews split between "healthy, well looked after" and "slow, sleepy horses".
- **Guide without English**, "not worth the money", litter in the countryside (Viator reviews via search).
- **Weight limits** differ: 90 kg on one listing, 100–110 kg typical (marrakech.me), heavier riders must say so in advance.
- **Children**: lead-rein pony from 4–5, horse led on foot from ~6, alone from ~8–10 (marrakech.me); Aziz: alone from 8.
- **Helmets** not always given on short rides (overmoroccotours).
- **What to wear**: long trousers (not shorts or slippery leggings), closed shoes with a small heel, sun protection, scarf for dust.

## Decisions (flag "owner to confirm")
- Palmeraie ride, a full hour in the saddle, sunset (pick-up ~2 h before sunset) or 09:00; about 3 h door to door.
- Helmet in every size, briefing, beginners led on a lead rope first, walk and trot; canter only for confident riders.
- Our local guide (English speaking) with you + the stable's horse guide; mint tea at the stables.
- Children from about 6 (horse led on foot), alone from about 10; riders over about 100 kg tell us in advance.
- Make It Your Own: 2-hour ride, Agafay ride instead (Atlas views, longer drive), camel ride the same afternoon.
- Better fits: Palmeraie camel ride (calmer, no riding skill), Agafay camel & dinner (desert scenery + evening), Atlas & Imlil day trip (on foot in the mountains).
