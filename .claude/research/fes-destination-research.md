## What visitors worry about (WebSearch: marocmama tannery scam, journalofnomads, simply-morocco "Scams in Fes", worldnomads)
- Touts say "you're going the wrong way" / offer to show the tannery, then steer to leather shops on commission; some terraces ask money for a view that is officially free.
- Fake guides; a licensed guide makes most of it disappear.
- Many shops and attractions close on Friday (marocmama) → not used on the page (not on our own pages; owner to confirm if wanted).
- Getting lost: ~9,000 lanes, phone maps lose their way (our medina tour FAQ).

## Facts used (from our own pages)
- Medina tour: 3–4 h, ~2 km mostly downhill from Bab Boujeloud, no shop stops, tannery from a leather-shop terrace, Qaraouiyine open to Muslims only (Al-Attarine next door shows the same art).
- Airport: Fes Saiss (FEZ), 20–25 min; flight tracking; nearest gate (Bab Boujeloud, Bab Guissa, Bab Ftouh) + porter to the riad door; pick-up 3 h before departure.
- Days: 2 for the medina (guide on day 1), a 3rd for Volubilis & Meknes (blog best-things/best-time).
- Best time: spring + autumn; summer hot, medina holds heat (early/late); winter cool, sometimes rainy, quiet (blog best-time-to-visit-fes).
- Sights (blog best-things): Bab Boujeloud, Bou Inania (14th c., open to non-Muslims), Al-Qaraouiyine (859), Chouara, Nejjarine museum & fountain, Seffarine coppersmiths, Mellah + Ibn Danan synagogue, Marinid Tombs view at sunset, Jnan Sbil gardens.
- Desert (desert-tours-from-fes): Merzouga ~470 km, ~8 h via Ifrane, cedar forest, Midelt lunch, Ziz Valley; 2 days = 8 h each day, 3 days adds a full day; Fes→Marrakech train ~7 h via Rabat and Casablanca.
- Day trips: Chefchaouen (~3.5 h each way, 08:00–19:30), Volubilis/Moulay Idriss/Meknes (~9.5 h, 1.5 h to Volubilis), Middle Atlas Ifrane & Azrou (~8 h, 1 h to Ifrane).
