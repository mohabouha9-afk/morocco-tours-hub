## Booking-site demand (web search, 2026-11-23)
- **Three Valleys** (Atlas + Ourika/Asni + Lalla Takerkoust + short camel ride + Berber lunch): listings with 6,862, 5,113 and 2,490 reviews, so it is among the top-selling Marrakech day trips. Camel ride is 20–25 min and often optional. Weekly markets: Tuesday Tahannaout, Friday Tamslouht, Saturday Asni, Sunday Takerkoust. Overlaps our Atlas and Ourika pages (the roadmap called it optional).
- **Palmeraie camel ride**: 962 and 1,442 reviews (HelloTickets); 2,490 Civitatis is another listing. Common complaints: the ride is shorter than advertised (40 min sold as 90), and the palm grove has dry or dead palms and litter. Sunset rides get the best reviews.
- **Paragliding** (Aguergour / Lalla Takerkoust, Agafay views): Airbnb 599 reviews at 4.93, Trip.com 164. Wind can delay a flight by days. Needs a licensed partner (owner to confirm).
