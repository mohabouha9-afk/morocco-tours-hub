## Facts used
- Hassan II Mosque (from `casablanca-hassan2-research.md`, official site): guided visits several times every morning plus the afternoon, fewer on Fridays and in Ramadan; 140 MAD per foreign adult; shoulders/knees covered; shoes carried in a bag; about 45–60 min inside.
- Roads: our saved OSRM roads `ti4` (Casablanca → Rabat → Fes, ~300 km), `fm4-d1` (Fes → Merzouga ~470 km), `fm4-d2` (Merzouga → Dades ~270 km) and `fm4-d3` (Dades → Marrakech ~320 km). Total about 1,350 km.
- Meals follow the 6-day Casablanca page: no dinner in Fes; dinners at the camp and in Dades.

## What makes it unique
- The shortest desert tour from Casablanca (before this page the shortest was 6 days). It turns the trip to Marrakech into the journey, with the Hassan II Mosque inside, and drives no road twice.
