# "Our Morocco Tours" page (tours/index.html) — competitor research, 2026-11-25

Method: built-in browser read of Intrepid, TourRadar, Agencia Marrocos Tours (owner's reference), Morocco Fabulous Travel; web search for day-trip choice and booking worries. Firecrawl was out of credits (not used). G Adventures page returned 404. Not covered: Exodus, Responsible Travel, GetYourGuide/Viator category pages (only seen via search snippets).

## What our page has today
Hero (73 journeys) > trust strip > browse bar (By city / By theme tabs + search) > 7 city sections > 9 theme sections (Sahara, Grand, Northern, Coast, Day Trips from Marrakech, Student, Family, Wellness, Photo, Honeymoon) > closing banner "Couldn't Find the Right Tour?". No FAQ, no "how to choose", no reviews strip, no best-sellers row. Day trips: 6 Marrakech trips are shown inside the Marrakech city section AND again in the theme section "Day Trips from Marrakech" (duplicate). Fes day trips (Chefchaouen, Volubilis/Meknes, Ifrane/Azrou), Tangier (Chefchaouen, Tarifa) and Marrakech Three Valleys exist as pages but are NOT on this page.

## What competitors do
- Intrepid: featured-trips row on top ("Last minute deals"), rating + review count on every card, labels (Comfort / Premium / Original), physical-rating, "Compare" button, "Tailor-made" box, "Morocco at a glance" (currency, language, plug type), "Get to know" guides, 13-question Morocco FAQ (visa, insurance, tipping, packing, water, safety, solo women/LGBTQ, accessibility), review block with star breakdown.
- TourRadar: filters by duration, budget, start city, group type (private/small group), theme; "Best time to visit" by month; "Travel Morocco your way" links; guides; "Contact our Morocco travel experts".
- Agencia Marrocos Tours (owner's reference): plain city-grouped list (Marrakech, Fes, Casablanca, Tanger, Ouarzazate, Errachidia, Rabat, Agadir) + "Haven't found the right itinerary?" CTA. Our page already beats it on structure.
- Morocco Fabulous Travel: "BEST SELLER" labels, itineraries by length (7/9/10/12 days), private vs shared split, student trips, separate "Marrakech one-day trips" and "Activities" links.

## Gaps on our page (ranked)
1. No "Most popular / best sellers" row at the top (Intrepid, Fabulous Travel).
2. No "which tour is right for me" help (by days available, by situation).
3. No FAQ on the page (Intrepid has 13).
4. No "browse by length" (3 days, 4-5, 6-8, 9+) — our tabs are only city and theme.
5. No rating/reviews on cards or a reviews strip (we have real 4.9 / 5,000+ / 98% in reviews.html).
6. No "Best time to go" pointer (TourRadar, Intrepid).
7. Day trips only for Marrakech and duplicated; none for Fes/Tangier shown.
8. No "how booking works" strip (deposit, balance on arrival, what's included) — top worry in forums.

## Traveller worries found (sources: Rick Steves forum, MarHire, podcast on Morocco scams, Nerdbot, Intrepid FAQ)
- Private vs group: which is better, cost per person once 3-4 travellers.
- Can I trust a local company / is the deposit safe / what does the deposit cover / cancellation terms.
- What is included (entrance fees, meals, camp type), total price in writing, hidden extras, forced shopping stops, group size in the vehicle.
- How many days do I need; desert is ~9 h drive each way from Marrakech, long drives eat short trips.
- Where to start (Marrakech / Casablanca / Fes / Tangier from Spain) and one-way vs loop.
- Safety, solo women, kids, winter snow in the Atlas, best season, visa, insurance.
- Short on time / one day: closest and easiest = Agafay (45 min); mountains = Ourika (1 h); waterfall = Ouzoud (~3 h); coast = Essaouira (2.5-3 h each way).
