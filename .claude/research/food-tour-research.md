## 2. Worries, with real quotes
1. **Stall scams in the square** — TripAdvisor review title "Avoid food stall 75 - total rip-off", "Be aware to ask for the price beforehand!!!"; Instagram tip: "NEVER! eat at stalls in the middle of Jemaa El Fna Square"; Reddit thread "Food Vendor Scam - Jemaa El-Fnaa Square".
2. **Food safety** — [marrakechprivate.com](https://www.marrakechprivate.com/blog/street-food-jemaa-el-fna-guide): "The stalls are inspected by the municipality and the food safety is generally solid."; "Go between 8 PM and 10 PM when the turnover is highest and everything is fresh off the grill."; "never eat at a stand where someone is grabbing your arm".
3. **Paying too much** — same source: "the difference between a 120 MAD tourist meal and a 70 MAD local feast".
4. **Big groups** — "15 or so" on shared tours → ours is private.

## 4. Decisions for our page
- 17:30 → ~21:30: Koutoubia/riad → Mellah spice market (olives, dates, preserved lemon, ras el hanout) → Riad Zitoun el Kedim (msemen with honey and amlou, harira) → mechoui alley (mechoui, tanjia from the hammam furnace) → Jemaa el-Fnaa at night (snail broth, orange juice; guide picks stalls and agrees prices) → pastries + mint tea on a terrace.
