## Our angle
- **No long drive day**: the desert is 2 h from the airport, and the 560 km Merzouga → Marrakech road is split over 3 days (≈220 / ≈275 / ≈175 km). Competitors cram Dades → Marrakech into one 314 km day.
- **Quiet hours** at the two busiest sights: sleep at the Todra Gorge (walk it before the day buses) and at Aït Ben Haddou (ksar at sunset/early morning, like our 5-day Fes → Marrakech tour).
- Hotel first, camp night 2 (evening flights). One driver-guide all 5 days.

## Facts
- Kasbah Amridil (Skoura, the kasbah on the old 50-dirham note): entry **30 MAD** (slowmorocco.com) vs **40 MAD** (Tripadvisor review) → write "about 30–40 MAD". 30–45 min visit.
- Dades Gorge road: not dangerous (Tripadvisor forum); Monkey Fingers rocks, Tissadrine hairpin bends ("staircase"); roadside, free.
- Todra Gorge free; Aït Ben Haddou no ticket; Taourirt small fee.
