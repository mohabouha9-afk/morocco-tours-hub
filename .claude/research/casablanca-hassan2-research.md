## Official facts (Fondation Mosquée Hassan II, fmh2.ma/en/node/564)
- Non-Muslims enter only on a guided visit run by the mosque; its own guides lead the visit inside.
- Times 16 Sep – 14 Mar: Sat–Thu 09:00, 10:00, 11:00, 12:00 and 15:00; Fri 09:00, 10:00 and 15:00.
- Times 15 Mar – 15 Sep: same mornings, afternoon 15:00 and 16:00 (Fri too).
- Ramadan: Sat–Thu 09:00, 10:00, 11:00, 14:00; Fri 09:00, 14:15.
- Prices: foreign adults 140 MAD; residents + foreign students 70; children over 6 + Moroccan students 30; under 6 free; private VIP visit (≤10 people) 2,200 MAD.
- Dress: shoulders and torso covered, below the knees; "Shorts and sleeveless clothes are absolutely forbidden". Shoes off, carried in a bag given at the entrance. No video cameras.
- Other sources (departly, 2026): visit 45–60 min inside, 30–45 min more on the esplanade; prayer hall holds 25,000, retractable roof; no headscarf needed for women on the tourist visit; tours can fill in peak season, arrive 20–30 min early. (Its own slot list differs from the official one: we use the official site.)

## Competitors (what they include)
- Headout half-day 4 h 30: mosque (inside morning/afternoon; exterior only for late departures), old medina, Habous, Mohammed V + United Nations squares, Central Market, Rick's Café (outside), Notre-Dame de Lourdes, Arab League Park. A/C car, hotel pick-up in the centre, water. Not wheelchair accessible.
- Blogs: mosque ticket is an extra cost on most tours ("bring some extra cash for the ticket").

## Worries found
- Weak guides at the mosque (the mosque assigns its own; you can't choose) → our guide sets the scene outside and on the esplanade.
- Confusion over entry fees; forced stops at rug and cosmetic shops → no shop stops, fee stated clearly.
- Layovers: GoWithGuide — under ~5 h stay at the airport; 6–8 h = mosque only; 9–11 h = mosque + one more stop.
- Friday/Ramadan slot changes; dress code refusals.

## Decisions for our page
- Private half day, about 5 hours: pick-up 08:30, mosque 09:00 slot, esplanade, old medina + Sqala, Art Deco downtown (Place Mohammed V), Habous quarter, Corniche/Ain Diab. Afternoon version with the 15:00 slot.
- Mosque ticket paid on site (owner rule: entrance fees on site), our guide queues for it.
