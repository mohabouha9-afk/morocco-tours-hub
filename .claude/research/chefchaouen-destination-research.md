## Facts used (from our own pages)
- Tangier: ~120 km, 2–2.5 h past Tetouan, port pick-up, ~4 h in town (Tangier day trip). Tetouan ~1 h 15, Asilah +45 min.
- Fes: ~200 km, 3.5 h via Ouazzane, 08:00–19:30, 4–5 h in town (Fes day trip).
- Kasbah museum ~60 MAD; Spanish Mosque 15–20 min uphill; Ras El Maa; medina/square free.
- Akchour: half an hour, ~60 km return, river pools (4-day Fes→Tangier); May–October best (day trips).
- Kasbah 15th c.; crafts: wool, blankets, leather, goat cheese (blog). No airport or train (blog).
- Tours sleeping there: 3-day Fes loop (1 night), 4-day Fes→Tangier (2, Akchour), 5-day Tangier→Fes (1), 7-day northern (2, Akchour, Asilah, Larache); also 8-day from Tangier, 7-day from Casablanca and ~12 others.
