## Errachidia airport (ERH) departures — the key fact for day 5
- FlightStats, ERH departures 16 Nov 2026: **only one flight that day, AT1447 to Casablanca at 06:15** (arr. 07:30; Iberia/Qatar/Gol codeshares).
- flightsfrom.com: 5 routes — Casablanca (RAM, ~6/week, 53% of departures), Rabat (RAM), Marrakech (Ryanair, 0–1/day), Paris-Orly (Transavia, ending 27/03), Barcelona (Vueling, from 30/12 to 27/03). New: London Gatwick (Neos).
- → Most departures are the early Casablanca flight: from Merzouga (2 h) that means leaving around 03:00. So **night 4 in Errachidia** (≈10 min from the airport) for an early flight; Merzouga hotel when the flight is later.

## Decisions (owner rule 2026-11-16: decide route/nights/stops from research)
- **Night 1 at Aït Ben Haddou** instead of Ouarzazate: no 60 km back-track on day 2, the ksar at sunset and a quiet early walk before the day groups (same as our 3-day Agadir and 5-day Errachidia tours).
- **Night 2 in the Dades Gorge** after an easy day (Skoura, Kasbah Amridil, Valley of Roses, Tissadrine bends).
- **Todra Gorge mid-morning on day 3** (before the midday groups), then the oasis road to Erfoud and Merzouga in time for the sunset camel ride, camp night 3.
- **Day 4 = full day around Merzouga by 4×4** (nomad family, Khamlia Gnawa, black desert, Dayet Srij after rain, Rissani market), night 4 Merzouga hotel — or Errachidia for the early flight.
- Pick-up **07:30** in Agadir (same long day 1 as the 3-day Agadir tour). Owner to confirm.
- Key fact 4 = **"Desert: 2 nights by the dunes"** (the unique point: a camp night + a full desert day, and no 10-hour drive back).
