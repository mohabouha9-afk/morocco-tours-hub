## Todra Gorge (Tripadvisor attraction 4.4 / 1,088)
- Praise: limestone walls up to ~300 m, climbers on the cliffs; "The trail itself isn’t long or demanding".
- Worries: crowds and noise in the narrow part ("noise from the cars, buses, and people"), "Lots of sellers there with trinkets", "You don’t need a lot of time".
- Tip from a guided-walk review: "go early in the morning!" → **sleeping at the gorge = walk it in the quiet evening and early morning**, before the day buses.
- Forum: stay inside the narrow gorge (Yasmina, Les Roches) or 7 km further at Auberge Le Festival (cave rooms); snow at 2,000 m unlikely before Christmas.
- Hotels near the gorge (Tripadvisor): Guesthouse Panorama Todra 4.8/201 · La Petite Gorge 4.7/123 · Auberge Le Festival 4.5/22 (same picks as the 4-day Fes tour).

## Road sights
- **Alnif** (day 3, between Rissani and Tinghir): trilobite fossil village.
- **Ksar El Khorbat, Tinejdad** (day 4): fortified village built ~1860, still lived in, with the **Oasis Museum**; a Tripadvisor review: "The museum is 20 Moroccan dirham" (several floors, texts in French, Arabic, English). ~50 km from Tinghir (Wikipedia, hoteltomboctou.com).
- **Goulmima**: old ksar town on the Gheris oasis, on the road to Errachidia.
