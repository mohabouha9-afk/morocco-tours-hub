## 2. Worries, ranked, with real quotes

### 1. Ride too short and slow
- GetYourGuide AI summary (26k product): "Some travelers say the quad ride felt brief and slow, and ask for more time."
- TripAdvisor (quad-only product): "we had 2 full hours of quad biking split up half way through with a" tea break — and the reviewer calls it "Really worth every penny".
→ **Ours: 2 hours on the quad, tea break halfway; pace set for you.**

### 2. Forced to share a quad
- GetYourGuide review (47k product): "they require two people to ride on the ATV, and I couldn't enjoy it as much as I would have liked".
- GetYourGuide (12k product) summary: "clearer information on quad sharing and pace would help".
→ **One quad per driver; sharing only if you want (e.g. a child riding with a parent).**

### 3. Dust
- [Morocco Escape guide](https://www.morocco-escape.com/guides/quad-biking-agafay/): "Very dusty on dry days. The Agafay plateau is compacted earth and the quads kick up significant dust."
- Spotify review episode: "If you're sensitive to dust, expect a fair amount of it kicked up by passing quads."
→ Helmet, goggles and a scarf (cheich) provided; old clothes; contact lenses → glasses.

### 4. Safety, losing control
- [Agafay camp blog](https://agafaydesertluxurycamp.com/quad-biking-agafay-desert-marrakech/): "At one point, I lost control and veered into a ditch, but thanks to our guide's immediate help, both I and the quad came out unharmed."
- GetYourGuide review: "guides on quads ensured safety for all".
→ Briefing + practice laps, instructor in front, slower pace for first-timers; closed shoes ("sandals are dangerous" — Morocco Escape).

### 5. Licence, ages, insurance
- Morocco Escape: "Most Agafay operators do not require a driving license"; "riders to be 16+ for their own quad"; under 16 = passengers.
- [marrakechquadbiking.com FAQ](https://marrakechquadbiking.com/help-center-faq.html): "Driving Quad/Buggy: 16 years old · Passenger on Quad: 8 years old · Passenger in Buggy: 5 years old".
- [marrakechlove.com](https://marrakechlove.com/en/marrakech-quad-biking/): "check that your travel insurance covers quad biking: some policies exclude this activity."
→ FAQ licence + children; Good to know = check your travel insurance.

### 6. Heat and time of day
- Morocco Escape: "avoid midday in July-August heat"; best light late afternoon (3 pm winter, 4 pm summer).
→ Morning or late-afternoon slot; FAQ "morning or sunset?".

## 3. What people love
- "fun quad biking", helpful guides (GetYourGuide summaries); Atlas views from the plateau, Berber villages, Lalla Takerkoust lake visible from some routes (Morocco Escape).

## 4. Decisions for our page
- 3–4 hours door to door; 2 h riding; morning (≈09:00) or late-afternoon (≈15:00) pick-up; 40 min drive, no stops.
- Included: private vehicle + driver, local professional guide, 2-hour ride with instructor (one quad per driver), helmet/goggles/scarf, fuel + briefing, mint tea.
- Not included: lunch/dinner, camel ride, photos/video, tips.
- Think twice → camel ride & dinner show (relax, no driving) / 3-day Merzouga (sand dunes).
- FAQ: licence/experience · own quad? · is it safe · children · morning or sunset · booking.
- **Owner to confirm:** 2 h ride, one quad per driver, ages 16+/8+, damage rule, insurance wording, tea stop place, times.
