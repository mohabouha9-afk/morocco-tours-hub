## Fes medina: what travellers worry about
- **Tannery terraces**: the Chouara views are from leather shops' rooftops, "free" but with sales pressure (morocco-escape scams guide; TripAdvisor forum "Chouara Tannery: should I hire a guide?").
- **Fake / unofficial guides and commission shops**: "friendly guide" who steers you to carpet/leather/argan shops for 30–50% commission; "beware of the tour guide scam" thread (Fes forum).
- **Getting lost**: Fes el-Bali is "one of the most complex navigational environments in the world"; a guide for the first day removes the problem.
- Positive reviews of private Fes guides praise context and history, "made the shopping experience feel comfortable instead of stressful or pushy".
→ Our answer: a local professional guide, **shops only if you ask**, tannery view with no buying expected, guide stays with you.
