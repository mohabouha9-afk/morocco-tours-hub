## 1. The market (what everybody sells)
- GetYourGuide category: 106 cooking classes in Morocco. Marrakech leaders: Cooking Class with Pickup **4.6 / 2,628** (3 h, chef, starter + tagine, certificate); Traditional Cooking Class & Food Market **4.8 / 2,815** (2 h 30); Dishes with a Local Chef **5.0 / 2,015**; Local Home class **4.8 / 929**; Local Family **4.9 / 870**; La Maison Arabe workshop **4.9 / 782**; Atlas Berber family **4.8 / 440**; Flavors of Marrakech market + class **5.0 / 474** (TripAdvisor 5.0 / 247); vegetarian/vegan class **4.8 / 241**.
- Prices: 300–800 MAD (€28–73) per person, 3–4 hours ([marrakechprivate.com](https://www.marrakechprivate.com/guides/marrakech-cooking-class-guide)); La Maison Arabe 100–120 €, Amal 40–60 € ([morocco-escape.com](https://www.morocco-escape.com/guides/cooking-classes-marrakech/)).
- Almost all = tagine + cooked salads (+ sometimes bread, mint tea), many with a market visit. That is the "generic" baseline.

## 2. What goes wrong (complaints, real quotes)
1. **Watching, not cooking** — morocco-escape: "“Demonstration class” means you watch." "“Hands-on” means you make the dough, debone the chicken, and pound the spices yourself." Private GYG class: "Very hands-on but was not possible for all participants to get fully involved in all activities."
2. **Long waits / overruns** — GYG summary for the 2,628-review bestseller: "Pickup waits and overruns make the outing feel longer than expected".
3. **Market visit that teaches nothing** — private class review: "there was no communication between the actual instructor and the participants as to what she was looking for in the selected ingredients - type, quality, price, etc."
4. **Shorter than advertised** — "Was advertised as a 5 hour class but total experience was only 3 to 3.5 hours."
5. **Atmosphere at the meal** — same review: the owner arrived at lunch and "the relaxed atmosphere changed".

## 3. What people love
- Market visit "worth as much as the cooking itself" (marrakechprivate.com); learning to read a souk; take-home recipes; eating what you cooked; Friday-couscous / family-home feel; food left over given away (Flavors of Marrakech review).

## 4. Our angle (not generic)
| Generic class | Ours |
|---|---|
| Group demo, one pot | Private, **your own station**, nothing cooked for you |
| Market = walk-through | **Mellah market with your cook**: real vs fake saffron, olives, preserved lemons, herbs, bargaining shown |
| Bread from the shop | **Knead khobz and carry it to the ferran** (neighbourhood wood oven), collect it warm |
| Fixed menu | **Choose 1 tagine + 2 salads** before the day; Friday couscous |
| Recipe sheet | Recipes on WhatsApp **+ your own ras el hanout** mixed and bagged |
| Tea served | **Learn the high pour** |
| Pick-up waits | Own driver, time confirmed the day before |
| — | Options: **tanjia** sealed in the morning and slow-cooked in the hammam furnace; finish with the hammam page |
