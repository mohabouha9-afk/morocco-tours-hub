## Traveller worry answered
One-hour photo stop at Aït Ben Haddou on day one of shared desert tours → own guide, granary climb, or a night for sunset/sunrise. Tichka bends / car sickness / winter snow → front seat, stops, driver checks the road.

## Facts used (from our own pages)
- Day trip: 07:30–19:30 (~12 h), 3.5 h each way, ~190 km, pass 2,260 m, ~2 h in the ksar, studios or Taourirt ~1 h, ~7 h road total, Film Studios ~80 MAD, Taourirt small fee, houses in the ksar a few dirhams, snow Dec–Feb, road works.
- Aït Ben Haddou ~30 km from Ouarzazate (blog; day trip says "twenty minutes on") → "about half an hour".
- 2-day tour: pick-up hotel/airport ~07:30, Skoura/Kasbah Amridil (small fee), Kalaat M'Gouna, Dades, Todra, back by Rissani/Alnif/N'Kob; pick-up in Skoura or Aït Ben Haddou.
- OSRM (2-day research): Ouarzazate → Dades Gorge ~140 km / 2.5 h; → Merzouga ~360 km.
- Telouet: Glaoui palace, painted cedar ceilings, carved plaster, tiled halls (4-day Merzouga FAQ).
- Tours sleeping on the kasbah road: 3-day Marrakech→Merzouga (Dades), 4-day Merzouga (Dades + Ouarzazate), 4-day Erg Chigaga (Ouarzazate), 7-day Marrakech→Fes (ABH + Dades), 5-day Fes→Marrakech (Dades + ABH), and many longer tours.
