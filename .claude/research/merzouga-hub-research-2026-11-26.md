## What travellers worry about
1. The drive (9h+, "two days in the car for one in the desert") -> 2 nights, one-way route, Errachidia flight.
2. How many nights (1 feels rushed; 2 most recommended).
3. Camp reality: not as pictured, shared toilets, cold, crowded camps close together, quad noise.
4. Where camps are: interior camps removed 2019; camps now along dune edges. Some travellers feel "tourist trap".
5. Cold Dec-Jan nights / 40C+ summer; wind in April.
6. Hidden costs, on-the-spot prices differ from online, touts, shop/carpet stops, tip pressure.
7. Camp vs hotel (many suggest one night each).
8. Practical: cash (ATMs reported in Rissani/Erfoud, one report of an ATM in Merzouga: verify), cards, Wi-Fi, safety, kids, luggage, camel comfort/welfare and 4x4 alternative.
