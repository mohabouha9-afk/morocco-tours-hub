## Route (OSRM, 2026-11-24)
- Tangier → Tetouan → Chefchaouen: **124 km, ~2 h 20 min** (62 km / 67 min + 63 km / 75 min). Direct (inland): 112 km, ~2 h 05.
- Operators say "about 2 h", "2 h 15 / 113 km", "2–3 h". Page says **about 2–2.5 h each way, ~120 km**.
- Rome2rio: ~107 km, 1 h 49 by car; CTM bus 5 departures a day (slower, budget option).
- Tanger Med port to Chefchaouen: 114 km, ~2 h 10 (daytrip.com transfer). Cruise ships mostly dock at **Tangier Ville** port (city centre).

## Traveller worries → answer on the page
- **Is ~5 h of driving worth it?** Blogger: "4 hours in the city was plenty" for highlights, photos and lunch. → Yes: ~4 h in town; from Tangier it is the shortest way to reach the blue city (Fes is 3.5 h each way).
- **Lots of walking / slopes / heat, little shade** (Klook review). → comfortable shoes, summer midday heat, water.
- **Disorganised pick-up** (Klook review). → private, WhatsApp confirmation the day before, driver at the hotel / port exit.
- **Cruise or ferry day: will I be back in time?** → Tangier Ville port pick-up, plan around the all-aboard time (owner to confirm the margin).
- **Tetouan worth adding?** Reviewer: "Tetouan was quick but nice for a stroll". → Make It Your Own: ~1 h walk in Tetouan's UNESCO medina on the way back.
- **Strollers** don't work in the medina; use a baby carrier. Small dirham notes for toilets.
- Lunch and fees not included on most listings. Kasbah museum ~60 MAD (same figure as the Fes page).
