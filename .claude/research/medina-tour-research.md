## 2. Worries, ranked, with real quotes

### 1. The tour is really a shopping tour
- TripAdvisor review: "this tour is really aimed at getting tourists into shops they have a deal with" — and: "We had indicated in advance that we did not want this, but still ended up in shops".
- [moratra.com](https://moratra.com/marrakech-medina-guided-tour/): unofficial guides "often steer you toward shops that pay them a commission".
→ **Ours: no shop stops unless you ask; if you want to buy, the guide helps you find fair prices and you never have to buy.**

### 2. Fake guides and the "this way is closed" trick
- [Reddit r/traveladvice](https://www.reddit.com/r/traveladvice/comments/1ubpn14/i_spent_3_days_writing_the_honest_list_of_what_to/): "the "this way is closed" guy at the medina gate. nothing is closed."
- moratra.com: official guides are licensed by the Ministry of Tourism and "carry a photo ID card".
→ Local professional guide included; with him nobody else approaches; FAQ on hassle.

### 3. Too fast / no time to browse
- GetYourGuide "could be better": "Route timing can leave less time to browse than some travelers expected".
- TripAdvisor: "our guide went quicker than we could keep up".
→ Private = your pace; free time in the souks; you choose which sights.

### 4. Heat and crowds
- Bahia Palace official site: "by 11:00 AM the palace fills with tour groups"; best at 9:00.
- TripAdvisor: walking "in 45 degree heat"; GetYourGuide: "Bring water and sun protection".
→ Morning start 09:00 (Bahia first) or late afternoon; in summer an early start.

### 5. Is a guide worth it at all?
- [Reddit r/travel](https://www.reddit.com/r/travel/comments/q9twyb/advice_travelling_to_marrakech/): a guided walk on the first night "helped us find our way around with the peace of mind that we had a guide with us".
- moratra.com verdict: guided first walk, then "explore the souks solo once you know the lay of the land".
→ FAQ "Is a guide worth it?" — our answer: do it on day 1, the guide shows you how to find your way back alone.

## 3. Facts used
- Bahia Palace: 100 MAD adult, 50 MAD child 7–13, open 9:00–17:00 ([bahiapalace.com](https://bahiapalace.com/)).
- Ben Youssef Madrasa: 50 MAD, open 9:00–19:00 ([official site](https://www.medersabenyoussef.ma/en/)).
- Le Jardin Secret: 80 MAD (official blog 2026) — optional.
- Koutoubia mosque: non-Muslims see it from the outside and gardens (well known; not stated in a source above).
- Moroccan modesty: shoulders and knees covered, closed shoes (moratra.com).

## 4. Decisions for our page
- 3 to 4 hours, morning (09:00) or afternoon (15:00); pick-up at your riad/hotel (riad = guide meets you at the door or nearest gate); hotels outside the medina = driver drops you at the Koutoubia.
- Route: Koutoubia → Bahia Palace → Mellah/spice square → Jemaa el-Fnaa → souks (Semmarine, Rahba Kedima, artisan quarters) → Ben Youssef Madrasa; mint tea on a rooftop café.
- Not included: entries (Bahia 100 MAD, Ben Youssef 50 MAD), drinks/food, tips.
- Make It Your Own: evening tour + Jemaa el-Fnaa food stalls, Jardin Secret, add Majorelle (link), photography focus, family pace, a cooking class or hammam after.
- Think twice → Majorelle & palaces tour (gardens, less walking) / food tour (eating focus).
- FAQ: will we be taken to shops · is it worth having a guide · how much walking/heat · what to wear · tanneries · booking.
