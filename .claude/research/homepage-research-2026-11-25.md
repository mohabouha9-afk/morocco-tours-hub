# Homepage research: what the best Morocco tour homepage should be (2026-11-25)

How it was done: Firecrawl search for "private Morocco tours", "Morocco tours tailor made local tour operator" and "Morocco desert tours from Marrakech". 11 homepages mapped section by section. Tripadvisor forum threads read in full; Reddit threads read from search excerpts only, because Reddit pages can't be scraped. Usability and SEO rules taken from NN/g and Google Search Central. Search volumes were **not** checked: OpenSEO had no credits, and Firecrawl ran out at the end. Intent is judged from what ranks.

## 1. Who ranks, and what that says about intent
- "private Morocco tours": local operators (authentic-morocco, sun-trails, experienceittours, puremoroccotours, moroccoprivatetours.net, local-morocco-tours, gateway2morocco) mixed with marketplaces (TourRadar, Tripadvisor, GetYourGuide, Viator) and Western luxury agencies (Kensington, Jacada, Artisans of Leisure, Intrepid). Searchers are **comparing companies**, so the homepage has to win a comparison, not just inspire.
- "desert tours from Marrakech": desert specialists (marrakech-desert-trips, moroccanjourneys, desertluxurycamp) plus GetYourGuide, Viator and Tripadvisor. This is a product search, so desert tours must be visible early.
- Reddit and Tripadvisor threads rank too ("Has anyone done a private Morocco tour?", "Quality & reliable 5-star private tour companies"). People check forums before they trust a company.

The visitor groups that keep showing up: (1) short desert trip from Marrakech or Fes, (2) a full 7–14 day Morocco itinerary from Casablanca or Marrakech, (3) Marrakech day trips and activities, (4) special trips: luxury, honeymoon, family, (5) early planners asking "is it safe?", "best time?", "how many days?".

Patterns:
- **Keyword H1:** 6 of 11 put the offer in the H1 ("Private Morocco tours…", "Marrakech Desert Tours…"). Brand-only H1s (ours, MFT, Jacada) come from well-known brands or older sites.
- **Reviews in the top 3 sections:** 6 of 11. The best ones **name the platform and the count, and link out to it** (gateway2morocco, marrakech-desert-trips, local-morocco-tours).
- **"How it works" in 3–4 steps:** 3 of 11, and they're the three strongest custom-tour sites (gateway2morocco, local-morocco-tours, experienceittours). **We don't have this.**
- **A real named person (founder or team):** gateway2morocco, experienceittours, sun-trails.
- **Licence or registration numbers:** gateway2morocco, marrakech-desert-trips, sun-trails (ONMT), moroccanjourneys (UK company number).
- **Help choosing:** a desert comparison table (marrakech-desert-trips), best time to visit (experienceittours, gateway2morocco).
- **What's included / payment explained:** gateway2morocco, sun-trails, agenciamarrocostours (in the FAQ).
- **Search filter in the hero:** local-morocco-tours and puremoroccotours. Both have large catalogues; neither top custom operator (gateway2morocco, experienceittours) uses one.

## 4. Rules from research (not opinion)
- **NN/g homepage principles:** say who you are and what you do at a glance (a tagline that explains the business, not a welcome); show real examples of the offer; give clear CTAs with specific labels (not "Learn more" or "Explore"); keep the design simple and standard; no autoplay video or auto-forwarding carousels; no popups (cookie consent excepted); nothing important hidden behind animation. Source: nngroup.com/articles/homepage-design-principles/
- **FAQ schema:** since 8 Aug 2023, Google shows FAQ rich results only for well-known government and health sites. The markup does no harm, but it brings no visible result. The FAQ is still useful for visitors and for AI answers. Source: developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/08/howto-faq-changes
- **Reviews:** a TravelBoom 2027 study (via Argophilia and Marcomm News) found ~71% won't book without reading reviews, and 67% use AI for trip planning but trust it less (56%).
- **Reply speed:** vendor data (not travel-specific, not peer-reviewed) says replying within minutes or within an hour converts several times better than next day. That supports WhatsApp as the main CTA.
- **Moroccan licence:** travel agencies are licensed by the Ministry of Tourism under Law 31-96 (decree 2-97-547). A reform (Law 11-16) exists; whether it is in force wasn't confirmed. **Owner: our licence number?**

## 5. Our homepage against this
Already strong (keep): WhatsApp + Explore Tours CTAs, the lifted trust strip, reviews second, desert tours early, day trips and activities, destinations, fleet, FAQ (7) with schema, blog, closing banner, NAP in the footer.

Gaps, by impact:
1. **No "How it works" steps.** Every top custom operator has one, and it answers the deposit, scam and "what happens next" worries at once. Suggested 4 steps from site facts: Message us → Itinerary + fixed price in writing → Small deposit secures the dates → Balance on arrival. **Owner to confirm the reply time.**
2. **Reviews don't name a platform.** "4.9 ★ / 5,000+ / 98%" with no source reads as self-made to forum readers. Add the platform, the count and a link. Also the hero's Tripadvisor link points to a listing named "Tour to Morocco" (Casablanca), not "Morocco Tours Hub". **Owner: is that our listing?**
3. **H1 is the brand only.** "Morocco Tours Hub" tells a new visitor nothing; 6 of 11 competitors use the keyword. Keep the brand visible, but make the H1 the offer.
4. **The answers to the forum's top worries are missing:** no shopping stops, own drivers (not subcontracted), itinerary with hotels named. These fit best in "What Sets Us Apart", which today half repeats the trust strip and "Our Agency" (private, deposit and English appear 2–3 times).
5. **No named people.** "Our Agency" has stats but no founder or team name. The hero avatars are team photos with `alt="Traveler"`.
6. **No licence number** (gateway2morocco, sun-trails and marrakech-desert-trips show theirs).
7. Optional: a small "Which desert trip suits you?" helper (Merzouga vs Zagora vs Chigaga, 2/3/4 days) linking to the existing blog posts.

Don't add (research says no, or it breaks owner rules): a hero search widget (only for large booking catalogues), a newsletter popup (NN/g), autoplay video or carousels, prices (owner rule; gateway2morocco shows them, the rule stands), invented badges.
