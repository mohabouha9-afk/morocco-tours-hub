## What searchers and forums ask (WebSearch: kimkim, Rough Guides, Dave's Travel Pages, Lonely Planet, first-timer blogs)
- How many days: 2–3 days for a first visit; 4+ with day trips or the desert.
- Airport taxis overcharge / refuse the meter → pre-booked transfer; riad pick-up.
- Fake guides and "helpful" people asking money in the medina; getting lost in the lanes.
- Summer heat (40 °C+): sightsee early or late.
- Private city tours are sold as half day (3–4 h) or full day.

## Facts used (all from our own pages)
- Airport: Menara, 5 km to the medina, 15–25 min; driver in arrivals with a name sign; cars stop at the nearest medina gate, driver walks you to the riad (marrakech-airport-transfer).
- Leave ~3 h before an international flight (airport transfer FAQ).
- Casablanca ⇄ Marrakech: ~3 h, 240 km of motorway; train from CMN airport = change at Casa Voyageurs (casablanca-to-marrakech-transfer research).
- Medina tour: ~4 h, ~5 km flat, no shop stops, tanneries on request; summer start early or 15:00.
- Majorelle: tickets online only in timed slots, we buy them; 08:00 is calm.
- Best time: spring (Mar–May) and autumn (Sep–Nov); summer often over 40 °C; winter mild days, cool evenings (blog best-time-to-visit-marrakech).
- Deserts from Marrakech: Agafay 40 min (no dunes); Zagora 6–7 h (360 km), 2 days; Erg Chigaga ~8 h, last 50 km by 4×4, 3 days; Merzouga 9–10 h (560 km), 3 days (2 possible) (erg-chigaga-zagora hub table).
- Payment: 20–30% deposit by bank transfer, balance on arrival in cash (EUR/USD/MAD) or card; free cancellation up to 24 h (owner).
