1. No way to filter by number of days (only trip type + city).
2. No "most popular" picks; 31 cards start with Casablanca.
3. Camps: no section and no link to either luxury camp page or the camp page (demand 210 + 110).
4. Merzouga activities: only a text box; no cards.
5. No "who is travelling" paths: family 6-day, 3-day shared group, student tours, yoga retreat not linked (group tour is only mentioned in an FAQ answer).
6. No "when to go" (month strip).
7. No "what's included / not included" and no booking steps (20–30% deposit, balance on arrival, free cancellation 24 h are already on the site).
8. FAQ has 6; missing: can we skip the camel (4×4 option exists), bathrooms and hot water, how cold, luggage and packing, power and Wi-Fi, deposit, kids, day trip to the Sahara possible?, what changes the price.
9. Comparison table lacks crowd level and "best for".
10. Reviews block is the homepage one; only one quote names a desert tour.
11. No real traveller photos on the page.

## Do not copy
Prices on cards (owner rule), countdown sales, "best price guarantee", shared-tour pushing, long SEO text walls, unproven badges.
