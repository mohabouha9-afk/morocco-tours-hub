# Three Valleys day trip from Marrakech — research (2026-11-23)

Firecrawl out of credits; research by web search + direct page reads (Viator and HelloTickets block automated reads).

## Demand
- "three valleys marrakech" ~70/month (small keyword) but very big booking demand: listings with 6,862, 5,113 and 2,490 reviews (gap file `marrakech-day-trip-gaps-2026-11-23.md`).
- Civitatis "Three Valleys & Atlas Mountains Tour + Lunch": 9.5/10 from 2,745 reviews (16,573 travellers), 10 h, shared minibus, ~US$20. Viator private version 4.5 from 1,575 reviews.

## What "Three Valleys" means (no fixed definition)
- Operators mix: **Ourika**, **Asni** (Imlil side, Moulay Brahim gorge), **Tahanaout**, sometimes Sidi Fares / Oukaimeden; plus the **Kik plateau** (a drive, not a valley) and **Lake Lalla Takerkoust** (7 km reservoir on the N'Fis river).
- Civitatis order: Lalla Takerkoust (optional camel ride + tea in a tent) → Azro village near Tahanaout (Berber house, breakfast) → weekly market → valleys and villages → waterfall (optional walk/swim) → lunch with a family → back.
- travelingaroundmorocco.com (private): Tafza village Berber house + tea (Ourika) → Oukaimeden photo stop → Asni souk (Saturday) → lunch (family home or countryside inn with pool) → Kik plateau (paragliders on the descent) → Lake Takerkoust tea → back.
- Pick-up usually 08:30–09:00, back 17:00–18:00.

## Facts
- Weekly markets: Mon Tnine Ourika (our Ourika page), Tue Tahanaout, Fri Tamslouht, Sat Asni, Sun Lalla Takerkoust.
- Asni ~47 km from Marrakech, ~1,150 m. Kik plateau sits above Asni / Moulay Brahim; altitude quoted 1,350–1,800 m (sources disagree → page says only "high plateau", no number). Views of Toubkal massif and down to the lake. Some sources say the plateau track needs a 4×4.
- Our loop (OSRM, saved routes `threevalleys` + `threevalleys-back`): Marrakech → Ourika (Tafza area) 31 km/40 min → Tahanaout 23 km → Asni 16 km (out leg 70 km, ~1.5 h) · Asni → Moulay Brahim → Kik plateau → Lalla Takerkoust → Marrakech 91 km (OSRM 3.3 h, slow on the plateau track; realistic ~2.5 h with stops). Total ~160 km, ~4 h on the road.
- Camel ride at the lake: 20–25 min typical, often optional (Civitatis "depending on option"; one Viator complaint: ~10 min ride). No reliable dirham price found → page: price agreed before you start.

## Worries (reviews, blogs)
- Long, packed day; lots of time in the car.
- Camel ride shorter than sold (10 min).
- Waterfall walk hard and underwhelming (one Viator review); Ourika stop often rushed.
- Commercial stops (argan cooperatives) — mostly liked, but "limit commercial stops" advice.
- Ourika busy on weekends.

## Our angle (decided by reasoning, owner to confirm)
- Different from our **Ourika page** (waterfall walk at Setti Fatma) and **Atlas & Imlil page** (a real walk at Imlil): this is the **variety day with almost no walking** — Berber house, souk on market days, plateau views, the lake. No waterfall (honest: go to Ourika for that).
- One loop, no road driven twice; private 4×4 so the plateau track is fine; time per stop set by you.
- Camel ride optional and its length agreed before you get on (answers the "10-minute ride" complaint).
- Lunch not included, ~€10 (as our other Atlas pages), with a Berber family or a countryside restaurant in the Asni valley.
- After heavy rain we skip the plateau track and return on the paved road.
