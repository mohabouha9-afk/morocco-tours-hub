## Intent
Compare, then book. The searcher already has Marrakech dates and decides: how many days, which desert, come back or end in Fes, who to trust. Results are marketplaces, local operators, "Zagora or Merzouga" guides.

## Traveller worries (ranked by how often they appeared)
1. The drive is very long; the last day is almost all road; little desert time.
2. Shopping and commission stops, forced lunch restaurants, upselling (scarves, extras).
3. Not what was promised: poor camp, broken air-con, extra transport to Fes, unclear inclusions.
4. Zagora is not high dunes (disappointment).
5. Camp reality: bathroom, hot water, cold nights, summer heat, food and stomach.
6. Camel is uncomfortable; can it be skipped.
7. Car sickness on mountain roads.
8. Solo women: safety.
9. Family with small children.
10. Luggage, charging, signal, cash for lunches.
