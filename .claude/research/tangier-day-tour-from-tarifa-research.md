## Ferry facts
- **FRS left Tarifa–Tangier Ville on 5 May 2025; Baleària runs it** (4 round trips a day from each port, per Bladi). Ferryhopper also lists Africa Morocco Link. Crossing ~1 h.
- Lands at **Tangier Ville** (city port, walking distance to the medina). Algeciras ferries land at **Tanger Med**, ~45 km east.
- **Moroccan passport stamp is done ON BOARD**: join the police queue as soon as you board, or you can wait 45–60 min on the ship after docking (andalucia.com, journalofnomads, HUBB). Spanish exit control at Tarifa is quick.
- Sailings stop in storms / strong east wind (Storm Nils Feb 2026: only the 09:00 from Tarifa sailed; Ingrid Jan, Regina Mar 2026). Apr 2026: technical fault kept 300 passengers on board 3 h.
- Time: Morocco is UTC+1 all year except Ramadan (UTC+0). Spain is UTC+1 winter, UTC+2 summer → Morocco 1 h behind Spain in summer (and in Ramadan), same time in winter. Ferry timetables are in local time.
- Passport required; most EU/UK/US/Canada/Australia passports need no visa.

## Route (OSRM)
- Tangier Ville port → Cap Spartel 15 km / ~25 min → Caves of Hercules 4 km / ~10 min → back to the port 17 km / ~20 min. Loop 36 km.

## Traveller worries → answers
- Passport queue on the ferry → tip on page (join the queue at once).
- Missing the return ferry / tight time → we plan back at the port 1 h before the sailing; the driver tracks your ferry.
- Rushed group tours, souk "free time" / shop pressure → private, your pace, no shop stops.
- Touts at the port → driver-guide waiting at the exit with a name sign.
- Ferry cancelled by wind → free date change or full refund of the deposit (**owner to confirm**).
- Ferry ticket not included: guest books Baleària (**owner to confirm** whether the agency books tickets).
- Time difference → note in Good to know.
