## The desert crossing (key finding)
- Fes Expedition itinerary: **Merzouga – Khamlia – Taouz – Ouzina = 120 km track + 30 km road**; **Ouzina – Ramlia – Tafraoute (Lake Maider) – Zagora – M'Hamid = 170 km track + 150 km road** (each a full day). https://fesexpedition.com/6-days-desert-tour-errachidia-to-merzouga/
- Morocco Off Road Tours sells **Merzouga → Zagora as a 3-day / 2-night expedition** ("Remote Sahara pistes, Ouzina and Ramlia oases, desert camp or guesthouse"). https://moroccooffroadtours.com/tour-search/
- Overlanders on Facebook ask whether the Zagora–Merzouga piste can be done in one day at all; Google Maps finds no route past Ramlia.
- A rally recce: "the technically challenging Ouzina dunes".
→ **M'Hamid → Merzouga in one day ≈ 400+ km with ~290 km of desert track = 10–12 h.** The old page's day 3 (~350 km) is not realistic as a comfortable private-tour day.

## Proposed fix (keeps 6 days and the page title "Zagora, Sidi Ali, Ouzina & Merzouga")
- D1 Marrakech → Tichka → Ouarzazate → Draa Valley → Zagora (~360 km)
- D2 Zagora → Tamegroute → off-road via Sidi Ali, Ramlia → **Ouzina** (~170 km track), night in the Ouzina dunes (camp/guesthouse)
- D3 Ouzina → Taouz (rock carvings area) → Khamlia → Merzouga (~150 km), afternoon free, night in a Merzouga hotel
- D4 Full day Merzouga (nomads, Gnawa in Khamlia, fossils) + sunset camel ride + camp
- D5 Rissani → Erfoud → Todra → Dades (~270 km)
- D6 Dades → Valley of Roses → Ouarzazate → **Aït Ben Haddou (only here)** → Tichka → Marrakech (~320 km)
- Drops M'Hamid (not in the title; M'Hamid + Erg Chigaga = the 4-day Chigaga tour).

## Unique selling point
The only tour on our site that crosses the desert **off-road from the Draa Valley to Erg Chebbi** (Sidi Ali, Ramlia, Ouzina) — two deserts, no road back the same way. Key fact 4 candidate: "Desert crossing: Off-road, Zagora to Merzouga".

## Worries to answer
- Remote: no phone signal for hours, few toilets/cafés → packed lunch / water in the car.
- Track rough, rain can close it (Ouzina floods are news when they happen) → driver changes route, back-up road via Tazzarine/Alnif.
- Near the Algerian border (Taouz/Ouzina): tourists travel normally with a local driver; police checkpoints.
- Two camp nights? (Ouzina + Merzouga) → offer hotel/guesthouse option.

## Think twice (real tours on our site)
- Shorter, paved: 3-day Marrakech → Merzouga.
- Only one desert, remote: 4-day Marrakech → Erg Chigaga.
- Longer, both big ergs: 7-day Erg Chigaga & Merzouga.

- **Mirage Morocco Travel** 8-day 4×4: "Merzouga · Khamlia · Taouz · Ouzina · Ramlia · Mharch" (hotel in Mharch), next day "Mharch · Paris-Dakar track · Zagora". https://www.miragemoroccotravel.com/tour/8-day-morocco-itinerary/
- **Porte de Sahara** (Ouzina riad) 2-day excursion: Merzouga – Khamlia – Taouz – Dakar rally track – Ouzina; back via nomads, baryte mines, Bourika oasis, Oubahou abandoned village ("Lost City"), Ramlia. Sights: rock carvings near Taouz/Jdaid. https://portedesahara.com/excursions-from-merzouga-desert/
- **Ouzina Rimal**: ochre dunes, fossil beds (orthoceras, crinoids), nomads with goats and camels, salt lakes, star observatory; auberge + bivouac tents. https://www.ouzinarimal.com/
→ Route confirmed: D2 Zagora → Paris-Dakar track → Sidi Ali / Lake Maider → Ramlia → Ouzina (night) · D3 Ouzina → Taouz → Khamlia → Merzouga.
