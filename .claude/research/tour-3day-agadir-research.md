## Road distances (OSRM; OSRM hours run high on these roads)
- Agadir → Taroudant → Taliouine → Ouarzazate **~390 km** (~7 h) (+ Aït Ben Haddou detour ~10 km).
- Ouarzazate → Dades → Todra → Merzouga **~390 km** (~7 h).
- Merzouga → Todra/Dades road → Ouarzazate → Tichka → Marrakech **~590 km** (9–10 h real).

## Takeaway
Merzouga in 3 days from Agadir always means two 7–10 h driving days. Honest options: (A) keep Merzouga with nights Ouarzazate + camp, say the drive honestly in the itinerary/FAQ and point to the 4-day; (B) Erg Chigaga instead (closer, wilder) — product change, owner's call.

## Decision (owner 2026-11-16: "Keep Merzouga"; then "search, look what others do… you are an expert")
- **End in Marrakech, not back in Agadir.** OSRM Merzouga → Draa → Taliouine → Agadir = **726 km** in one day: not realistic (the GYG / lovelymoroccotours "back to Agadir on day 3" plans understate it). Merzouga → Marrakech ≈ 560 km, 9–10 h, and Agadir is 2.5–3 h from Marrakech by motorway. Coming back to Agadir = the 4-day tour or an extra night.
- **Night 1 at Aït Ben Haddou** (Agadir → Taroudant → Taliouine → Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou ≈ 420 km): ksar at sunset; day 2 Aït Ben Haddou → Skoura → Dades → Todra → Erfoud → Merzouga ≈ 425 km, in time for the sunset camel ride. GYG pushes on to Tinghir on day 1 (~590 km) — too long.
- Pick-up **07:30** in Agadir (competitors 08:00; our day 1 is longer). Owner to confirm.
- Stops on day 1: Taroudant ramparts (short walk), Taliouine saffron country (flowers late Oct–Nov).
