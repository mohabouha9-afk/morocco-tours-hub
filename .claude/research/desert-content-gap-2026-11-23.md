# Desert content gap analysis (2026-11-23)

Competitors whose sitemaps were compared page by page: marrakech-desert-trips.com, mouhoutours.com, desert-merzouga-tours.com, privatedeserttours.com, dunesdeserts.com (Agafay). Also checked: GetYourGuide, Viator and Tripadvisor results for Merzouga, Erg Chigaga and Agafay.
OpenSEO had no credits, so volumes are the ones already on file in content-roadmap-2026-11.md.

## Verdict
Activities, day trips and multi-day routes are complete. Every competitor route and activity has a matching page, or is a feature on one of our pages.
- Sandboarding is on the camp pages and the Merzouga hub (Mouhou has a standalone page; it's a free camp extra, so the search demand is low).
- The sunrise camel ride is on the sunset page.
- The Agafay quad + camel + dinner combo is covered by the camel-dinner page.
- Lalla Takerkoust is covered by the Agafay buggy, quad and day-trip pages.

## Real gaps
1. **Erg Chigaga / Zagora destination hub.** We have Merzouga and Agafay hubs but none for the south. On file: "erg chigaga" 480 and "zagora desert" 210. GetYourGuide and Tripadvisor rank location pages for these searches.
2. **2-day Erg Chigaga tour from Zagora / M'Hamid.** It's listed on GetYourGuide, Viator and Tripadvisor. It suits self-drivers and travellers already in the south. It's a new product, so it needs the owner's OK.

## Considered and rejected (low demand)
- Ouarzazate → Merzouga tour (Mouhou, Private Desert Tours): already rated low priority in the roadmap.
- 2-day Errachidia tour: the 3/4/5-day tours already cover it.
- A standalone luxury desert tour page: the luxury camp pages and upgrades already cover it.
