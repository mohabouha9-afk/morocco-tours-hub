## Facts used (from our own pages)
- Day trip: 08:00–19:00 (~11 h), 2.5–3 h each way, flat road, ~190 km, ~5 h in town (shared tours ~4), local guide, no shop stops, medina car-free 2–3 km flat, Tue–Thu calmer, wind strongest June–August, Skala du Port small fee, fish priced by weight.
- 3-day: 1 night, sunset Skala + morning port, ~180 km / ~3 h. 4-day: 1 night → Imsouane → Taghazout → Agadir. 6-day Agadir → Marrakech: 1 night. 7-day coastal: 2 nights (free day: Mellah, Bayt Dakira, woodworkers, beach).
- Also sleep in Essaouira: 12-day Marrakech→Casablanca, 14-day from Marrakech, 18-day Atlantic (and the other grand tours).
- Agadir → Essaouira by the coast ~170 km, ~3 h (4-day page, reverse direction). Bus ~3 h (day-trip FAQ). Porter from the gate (3-day Good to know).
- Blog: Gnawa music and festival, thuya woodwork, kitesurf.
