## Facts (several sources)
- Marrakech → M'Hamid ≈ 450 km on the N9, 7–8 h; then **~50–60 km of piste, 1.5–2.5 h by 4×4** to the dunes (clevertourists.com; TA forum "2 hours by 4x4 from M'Hamid… 3 hours out to pavement at Foum Zguid").
- Track not signposted, changes after storms → driver who knows it.
- **Dune height: Chigaga crests ~50–60 m** (some guides wrongly say 300 m; a TA forum regular with GPS logs: "quite low"). Erg Chebbi up to ~150 m. → Never claim "biggest dunes". Sell **remoteness, silence, size of the sand sea**.
- Far fewer camps, spread out; no tour buses. Merzouga: "becoming a circus, camps everywhere… parties with DJs" (Reddit r/Morocco).
- Camps: basic tents with shared toilets → luxury with private bathrooms/solar; "expect simpler facilities at a given price level" because everything arrives by 4×4.
- Camel at Chigaga = shorter ride from camp at sunset/sunrise (most arrive by 4×4).
- Season: Oct–Apr comfortable; Dec–Feb nights near freezing; Jun–Aug > 40 °C; spring sand winds; snow can close Tichka.
- Tamegroute: 20–30 min, green pottery + old Koranic library (small fee/donation at the library).
- Taznakht = Berber carpets (a shop stop risk → only if asked).

## Traveller worries (forums)
1. **The bumpy track** — "some of it over very bumpy rocky trails", "some travelers don't want to make that drive" (TA). One calls a stretch near Foum Zguid "the most diabolical stone track I know". → sell it as the adventure, be honest in the itinerary meta + FAQ, sit in front if car-sick.
2. **Long last desert day** (camp → Iriqui → Foum Zguid → Ouarzazate) — "The last day will be quite long" (TA). Our version splits it with a night in Ouarzazate, so day 4 is short.
3. **Shared bathrooms at the standard camp** — choose the camp level knowingly.
4. **Dunes lower than Merzouga** — honest FAQ "Chigaga or Merzouga?".
5. **Cold nights in winter**, heat in summer.
6. Cost: Chigaga generally costs more than Merzouga (4×4 leg, fuel). No price on our page anyway.
