## 1. Demand and the market
- GetYourGuide [Traditional Hammam Massage 3h & Hotel Transfer](https://www.getyourguide.com/en-gb/marrakesh-l208/quad-palmeraie-marrakech-t482995/): **4.9 from 1,753 reviews**, 3 hours: hotel pick-up → steam + black soap + kessa (45 min) → 45-min massage → mint tea, chocolates, pastries → transfer back. Only lunch not included.
- Many similar products: women-only hammams, argan black soap rituals, spa hammams with transfers.
- Price signals: [excursions-maroc.com](https://excursions-maroc.com/en/travel-guide/morocco-hammam-guide/) private hammam in a riad or spa "150-500 MAD"; [morocco-unveiled.com](https://morocco-unveiled.com/blog-hammam): kessa 15 MAD + black soap 20 MAD at a local shop, "Tourist hammams charge 60–100 MAD for the same items".

## 2. Worries, ranked, with real quotes

### 1. Nudity and etiquette
- [Reddit r/Morocco](https://www.reddit.com/r/Morocco/comments/1ahpn0i/nudity_in_hammam_spa/): "Nudity is quite normal, especially for women. We, men, rarely go naked".
- [Farnatchi Spa guide](https://farnatchispa.com/a-guide-to-your-first-hammam-experience-in-marrakech/): "Women may have the option to go fully undressed or to keep on underwear/bikini bottoms. Men must wear bottoms."
→ FAQ "Do I have to be naked?"

### 2. Couples / mixed
- Farnatchi: "Men and women CANNOT share the same space – there are separate sections for both sexes."
- TripAdvisor forum [couples hammam](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g293734-i9196-k6573818-Reasonably_priced_couples_hammam-Marrakech_Marrakech_Safi.html): couples spas exist but are "aimed at tourists".
→ Couples = private room at a spa hammam; FAQ.

### 3. "Not a real hammam" / spa vs public
- TripAdvisor review title (Les Bains de Marrakech): "Nothing to do with real Moroccan hammam".
→ FAQ "Spa hammam or public hammam?"; public hammam on request in Make It Your Own.

### 4. Transfer timing
- GetYourGuide summary: "Some travelers mention pickup delays and unclear timing, so more reliable transfer coordination would help."
→ Our own driver, time confirmed on WhatsApp the day before.

### 5. Scrub strength, hygiene
- TripAdvisor: "The scrub was very intense and deep."
- Hammam Mouassine package lists a "New exfoliation glove (Kessa)".
→ FAQ "Does the scrub hurt?" + "Is it clean?"

## 3. Ritual facts used
Steam to open pores → savon beldi (olive black soap) → rinse → kessa scrub → rhassoul (ghassoul) clay → rinse; 40–60 min scrub at spas (Farnatchi); house rules: separate sexes, men keep bottoms on.
