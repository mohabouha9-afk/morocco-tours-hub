## What the market sells
- **One take-off site for everyone: Aguergour**, Atlas foothills south of Marrakech, on the Kik plateau edge above Lake Lalla Takerkoust. Take-off altitude quoted 1,200 / 1,300 / 1,500 m (sources disagree → page gives no number).
- "Agafay paragliding" listings use the same site; one reviewer says the "Agafay" part happened by the lake (marrakechlikelocals). Our page says Aguergour honestly; Agafay only as "views towards".
- Drive: 35–55 km, 45 min–1 h (operators); OSRM Marrakech → Lalla Takerkoust → Douar Aguergour = 57 km (route saved as `paragliding`). Page: "About 1 h each way".
- Flight: **10–20 minutes** standard (paraglidingmarrakech, skyaguergour, top10marrakech); 30–45 min only on strong thermal days (morocco-escape). Manawa sells ~25 min.
- Total outing: 4–5 h door to door (morocco-escape), 4–6 h (marrakechlikelocals), "a morning / half day".
- Take-off: tandem pilot, a run of ~8–10 steps, landing on foot on the flat plain below (morocco-escape).
- Times: thermals from ~10:00, best 11:00–14:00; winter launches ~10:30–11:00; July–August many schools fly mornings only (morocco-escape). Sunset flights are sold (skyaguergour, Tripadvisor "Agafay Desert Sunset") when the wind allows.
- Best season: October–April (morocco-escape); spring and autumn calmest, winter flyable but windier (top10marrakech).
- Included at operators: flight, helmet, harness, insurance claims (paraglidingmarrakech: "first and only insured" — self-claim), transfer (not with budget sellers), mint tea. **Photos/video: often a paid extra after landing** (paraglidingmarrakech ~20 photos + 2 videos for sale; morocco-escape GoPro add-on); included on some tours.
- Limits: 20–110 kg most operators (paraglidingmarrakech, Amanar), 100 kg one operator, 140 kg Manawa → we say "up to about 110 kg, tell us your weight". Age from 4–5 (paraglidingmarrakech / listing "5 and above who can run a few metres").

## Worries (reviews, guides)
1. **Wind: wait, delay, cancellation.** Viator: weather turned at the site, flights cancelled. Tripadvisor: start postponed to 13:30; "around an hour having tea before flying". Chergui (hot east wind) can ground flights for days in spring/summer (top10marrakech). ~10–15% of flights rescheduled (morocco-escape, estimate). → book early in the stay, pilot's call is final, new date or full refund.
2. **Safety / pilot credentials.** Listings say "certified pilot" without naming the body; morocco-escape advises asking for FRMF certification and the licence number; check travel insurance covers paragliding (top10marrakech). → page: experienced licensed tandem pilots from a partner school we know, insured; check your own travel insurance. Owner to confirm the partner and the licence wording.
3. **Motion sickness.** Pilot told marrakechlikelocals about 2 in 10 passengers feel unwell; her flight ended after ~10 min. → light breakfast, tablets if prone, tell the pilot: calm flight, no spins, land anytime.
4. **Short flight for a long morning** (10–20 min vs 4–5 h). → say it upfront; the drive by the lake and tea fill the rest; longer flight possible on thermal days, not promised.
5. **Paid video upsell.** → say clearly before (owner to confirm: included or paid at the site).
6. **Bait-and-switch** (camel ride/Berber tent advertised and not delivered, marrakechlikelocals). → we only list what happens.

## Decisions (flag "owner to confirm")
- Morning pick-up ~09:00 (later in winter by the wind), flight late morning; door to door about 4 h; sunset flight only when the pilot says the wind allows.
- Partner paragliding school at Aguergour, licensed tandem pilots, insurance, helmet + harness; our local guide/driver stays with you.
- Flight 10–20 minutes, longer on good days; mint tea at the take-off while you wait for the wind.
- Weight 20 to about 110 kg; children from about 6 (sources: 4–5; we chose the safer side); not in pregnancy, after recent surgery, or with heart or serious back problems (standard tandem exclusions, not stated by the sources).
- Photos & video: the pilot's GoPro, paid at the site (most common market practice) — owner to confirm price/included.
- Weather: if the pilot cancels, new date or full refund.
- Make It Your Own: combine with the Three Valleys day (Kik + lake same area), lunch by the lake, camel ride at Lalla Takerkoust, Agafay sunset & dinner after, balloon at sunrise another day, child seats.
- Better fits: hot-air balloon (calmer, no running, any wind fear), Agafay quad (adrenaline on the ground), Three Valleys (see the plateau and lake from the road).
