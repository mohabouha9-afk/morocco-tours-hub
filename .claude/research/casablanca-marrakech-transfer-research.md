## Facts
- Distance / time: Daytrip "244 km", "2 h 40 min" (city). OSRM: Casablanca centre → Marrakech 243 km / 2 h 54; Mohammed V airport (CMN) → Marrakech 227 km / 2 h 39 (routes saved as `casa-mk`, `cmn-mk`). Marriott listing: "three-hour (maximum) highway drive". Transfeero: 227 km from CMN.
- Road: A7 toll motorway the whole way, flat, no mountain bends.
- Traffic: weekday peaks in Casablanca 7–9 am and 4–7 pm (12go).
- Departures: "hotel pickup ... 3 hours before your flight" (Headout/GoWithGuide, too tight for CMN); our own airport research: be at the airport ~3 h before an international flight → leave Marrakech about 6 h before departure (my call, owner to confirm).

## Alternatives (what the traveller compares)
- Train: no direct line from the airport most of the day; airport train → Casa Voyageurs (~30 min, ONCF/ONDA site: trains ~06:50–22:50), then the Marrakech train; welcomepickups: "about 220 minutes", connection waits "up to 150 minutes". Bags on and off two trains, then a taxi from Marrakech station to the riad.
- Taxi: white grand taxis at CMN, fare bargained (nomadlawyer: "400 to 700 dirhams", needs bargaining skills); "avoid the touts" (welcomepickups).
- Private driver: door to door, 2.5–3 h; best with luggage, families, late arrivals (lorenzigo, nomadlawyer).
