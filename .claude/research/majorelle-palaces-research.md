## 1. Facts (official / recent)
- [Jardin Majorelle official site](https://www.jardinmajorelle.com/en/): tickets ONLY on tickets.jardinmajorelle.com (QR code), valid for the garden, the Pierre Bergé Museum of Berber Arts and the Musée YSL. Garden open every day 8:00–17:30 (last entry 17:00); Berber Museum 8:30–17:00. [Musée YSL](https://www.museeyslmarrakech.com/en/): 10:00–18:00.
- [opulentmediterra.com, 2026](https://opulentmediterra.com/jardin-majorelle-tickets-sold-out-marrakech/): "Tickets are sold online only, with a QR code — there’s no desk at the gate to fall back on"; timed slots that "sell out same-day"; garden "170 MAD"; "Budget about 1.5 hours for the garden, longer with the YSL Museum."
- Bahia Palace 100 MAD (children 7–13 50 MAD), 9:00–17:00, fills with groups by 11:00 ([bahiapalace.com](https://bahiapalace.com/entrance-fee)).
- El Badi Palace 100 MAD (TripAdvisor review 2026; "As of March 2026 it was under renovation" — partly).
- Saadian Tombs 9:00–16:45, "entered through a single narrow passage from the Kasbah Mosque, so the queue can build quickly" ([saadiantombs.com](https://saadiantombs.com/opening-hours)); 100 MAD.
- Dar El Bacha (Museum of Confluences) 60 MAD, Tue–Sun 9:30–18:00, closed Mondays ([darbacha.com](https://www.darbacha.com/)).

## 2. Worries, with real quotes
1. **Tickets / queues** — TripAdvisor: "had to queue for at least 20 mins, despite having a time slot". → We book the 08:00 slot.
2. **Crowded, small, overpriced** — TripAdvisor: "a thoroughly mediocre, small garden. It's expensive"; TikTok: "It's nice but small. It's really crowded". → Honest FAQ "Is it worth it?": small, ~1 h, calm at 08:00; the Berber Museum makes it.
3. **Saadian Tombs queue** — TripAdvisor forum: "sizeable queues both at the Saadian tombs". → Guide times the entrance.
4. **Overlap with our medina tour** (both had the Bahia) → swap Bahia for Dar El Bacha when combined.
