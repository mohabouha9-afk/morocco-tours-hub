## Distances (OSRM)
- Ouarzazate → Skoura → Dades/Boumalne → Tinghir (Todra) → Erfoud → Merzouga: **about 360 km, ~6.5 h driving** (+ stops ≈ 8 h).
- Merzouga → Rissani → Alnif → Tazzarine → N'Kob → Ouarzazate (southern road, N12): **about 360 km, ~7 h** driving.
- Ouarzazate → Dades Gorge: ~140 km, ~2.5 h. · Ouarzazate → Aït Ben Haddou → Marrakech: ~210 km, ~4.5 h.
- Merzouga → Marrakech: ~555 km, ~10 h (so ending in Marrakech in 2 days is not realistic).
Forum: Tripadvisor "Realistic travel time": Ouarzazate → Merzouga "around 4 hours if weather is good" is wrong-optimistic; others quote 6–8 h with stops.
