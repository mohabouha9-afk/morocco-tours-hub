# Toubkal 2-day trek from Marrakech — research (2026-11-19)

Owner: "look at what others do" (no own answers on refuge, mule, winter, 3-day) → page follows the standard competitor product.

## Demand
- Roadmap note: a similar 2-day trek has 846 reviews on one booking site. Our Atlas & Imlil day-trip page already points to "ask us about a Toubkal trek". OpenSEO had no credits.

## What every operator runs (Howlanders private 2-day, TourRadar 79556 "Trek in Morocco", Lokalee/Indyguide listings)
- Day 1: private car Marrakech → Imlil ~1.5 h; walk Imlil → Aremd → Mizane valley → Sidi Chamharouch shrine (lunch) → Toubkal refuge 3,207 m. Howlanders: 5–6 h, 12 km, +1,467 m.
- Day 2: early breakfast, summit ~3 h via the south cirque to 4,167 m, down to the refuge 2–3 h (lunch), down to Imlil, car to Marrakech. Howlanders: 10–12 h walking, 19.5 km; other listings 9–10 h.
- Included (both): transfers, local mountain guide (Howlanders max 1 guide / 12), mule for the main luggage, cook, meals (D1 lunch + dinner, D2 breakfast + lunch), refuge night.
- Not included: drinks/bottled water, sleeping bag (rent or bring), winter gear. Imlil rental prices (TourRadar operator): crampons 5 €/day, ice axe 3 €/day, boots 5 €/day, sleeping bag 2.5 €/day, poles 3 €/day.
- Refuge: shared rooms 12–27 bunks, shared bathrooms, limited hot water and electricity (Howlanders); a reviewer: modern, heated lounges, showers only cool, good food.
- Season: Howlanders — year-round possible, mid-April to mid-November strongly recommended; winter = snow and ice, extra gear and mountain experience. TourRadar: crampons, ice axe, gaiters November–April.
- Difficulty: "high difficulty / high intensity", good fitness, no technical skills. Howlanders cancellation 7 days (ours stays 24 h, owner rule).

## Worries found
- Altitude: the 2-day gains ~2,400 m in under a day from Imlil (an operator runs it over 4 days for this reason); 40% less oxygen at the top (explore.co.uk). → honest Good to know + 3-day option.
- Very long day 2 (10–12 h). Cold summit wind even in summer; winter nights about -5 °C.
- Refuge comfort (dorms, cool showers). No water provided on a cheap trek; young guide with poor English (TourRadar review).
- Guide rule: sources disagree (licensed guide mandatory with checkpoints vs strongly advised) → we say a guide is needed and included.
