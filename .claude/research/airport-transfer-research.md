## 2. What travellers worry about
1. **Riads cars can't reach** — [nmorocco.com](https://nmorocco.com/blog/marrakech-airport-to-medina): "the drive ends at a gate and you walk the rest, over uneven ground, in the dark, on day one, in a maze where every alley looks like the last one." Facebook question: "how do we get to the place with luggage, since taxis can't access the narrow streets?" → **Our main promise: walked to your riad door, porter for the bags.**
2. **Taxi touts / fares** — [staysafemorocco.com](https://staysafemorocco.com/marrakech-airport-to-medina/): petit taxi "about 70–150 MAD" by day, "150–240 MAD after 22:00", "Touts inside the terminal often quote 300–400 MAD". Reddit: "Let's talk airport taxis!!!" → FAQ "Why not just take a taxi?" (honest fares, our price agreed before landing).
3. **Late / early flights** — flight tracking ([moroccotriplines.com](https://moroccotriplines.com/marrakech-airport-to-medina/): "your driver tracks the flight and adjusts the pickup time").
4. **Departure queues** — Reddit: "numerous stories of people missing flight from Marrakech due to slow check in"; TripAdvisor forum: "1st queue to get in through the x-ray machines took around 20 minutes, 2nd queue at the check in was around the usual 20-30 minutes"; [blondieinmorocco](https://www.blondieinmorocco.com/marrakech-airport-morocco/): "be at Marrakech airport 3 hours before the flight". Busiest 6–9 am and 4–7 pm ([flightqueue.com](https://flightqueue.com/airport/RAK)).
5. **Cash** — nmorocco.com: use the bank ATMs (BMCE, Attijariwafa) in arrivals, not the exchange desks.
- Drive: "15 to 25 minutes depending on traffic" (nmorocco); real route RAK → Koutoubia gate 6.6 km (OSRM).
