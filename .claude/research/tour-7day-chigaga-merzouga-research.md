## New route (7 days, 6 nights)
D1 Marrakech → Tichka → Aït Ben Haddou → Skoura → Dades (~320 km) · D2 Todra → Erfoud → Rissani → Merzouga, sunset camel + camp (~270) · D3 sunrise, nomad family, Khamlia → Taouz → Ouzina (~60, half track) · D4 Ouzina → Ramlia → Sidi Ali → Lake Maider → Paris-Dakar track → Zagora (~170 track) · D5 Tamegroute → M'Hamid → 4×4 → Erg Chigaga, sunset camel + camp · D6 Iriqui → Foum Zguid → Taznakht → Ouarzazate · D7 Tichka → Marrakech by early afternoon (OSRM 197 km).
