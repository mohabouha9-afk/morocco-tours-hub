From Marrakech to the Sahara

Marrakech is where most Sahara trips begin. The road climbs over the High Atlas, passes the clay ksar of Aït Ben Haddou and the palm valleys of the south, and ends where the Erg Chebbi dunes rise out of the stony plain at Merzouga. It is a long way, about 560 km, so the length of your tour decides how much of it you enjoy and how much you just drive.

Choose a round trip if you fly home from Marrakech, or a one-way tour if you are heading on to Fes. Every tour below is private, with hotel or riad pick-up and the same driver-guide all the way.

Desert Tours from Marrakech FAQ

How far is the Sahara from Marrakech?

Merzouga is about 560 km away, 9 to 10 hours of driving without stops. Zagora is closer, about 7 hours, with lower dunes. Agafay is 40 minutes away but is a stony desert, not sand dunes. On our tours the road is split with stops at Aït Ben Haddou and the gorges.

Is a 2-day desert tour from Marrakech worth it?

If two days is all you have, yes: you get the sunset camel ride, a night in the dunes and the sunrise. But each day is 9 to 10 hours on the road. With three days the drive is split by a night in the Dades Valley, and the 2-day Zagora tour has less driving with lower dunes.

Can you pick us up from our riad in the medina?

Yes. Cars cannot enter the medina lanes, so your driver-guide meets you at the nearest gate to your riad at 08:00. Most riads will walk you there with your bags. Hotels outside the medina are picked up at the door.

I get car sick. Is the Tizi n’Tichka road a problem?

It is a good paved mountain road, but it has many bends. Take a tablet before you leave, sit in the front seat, and tell your driver-guide when you need a break: we stop whenever you want. In winter, snow can close the pass for a few hours; we wait or change the plan.

Can we end in Fes instead of coming back to Marrakech?

Yes. Our 3, 4 and 5-day tours cross the desert and end in Fes, so you never drive the same road back. The 7-day tour adds a night in Midelt and a guided day in Fes.

What happens to our luggage on the desert night?

Take a small bag with what you need for the night. Your big luggage stays in the vehicle with your driver-guide and is waiting for you in the morning.
