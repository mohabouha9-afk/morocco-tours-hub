Terms & ConditionsClear rules, agreed before you travel

Last updated: 23 November 2026

These terms cover every private tour, day trip, activity and transfer booked with Morocco Tours Hub ("we", "us"), a local agency based in Marrakech. They explain how booking, paying, changing and cancelling work, in plain words. By confirming a booking with us, you accept them.

Our terms in short

  • Your price is fixed in writing before you pay: what we agree on is what you pay.
  • A 20–30% deposit by bank transfer confirms your tour; the rest is paid on arrival.
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before your tour starts.
  • Every tour is private, and dates or stops can be changed when you ask.
  • If weather or safety stops an activity, you get another option or your money back for it.
  • If we ever have to cancel, you get every payment back in full.

How booking works

  • You send a request with our form, on WhatsApp or by email. This is free and commits you to nothing.
  • We send your itinerary and private price in writing, with exactly what is included. You can change it as many times as you like before booking.
  • You pay the deposit and we confirm your booking in writing. Your tour is booked only once you have this confirmation.

Your written confirmation (by email or WhatsApp) is the reference for your trip: route, dates, hotels, inclusions and price. Itineraries on our website are examples to start from.

Your price and what it includes

Prices are per trip, not per seat, and depend on your group size, dates, route and hotel level. Once we have confirmed your price in writing, it does not change. There are no fuel surcharges, hidden fees or extra charges on the day.

Each confirmation lists what is included. For most multi-day tours that means the private vehicle and fuel, your English-speaking driver-guide, hotel pick-up and drop-off, your nights, breakfasts and dinners, and the camel ride in the desert.

Usually not included, unless your confirmation says so: lunches and drinks, entrance fees to monuments, tips, flights and travel insurance. Each tour page shows these costs under "Costs on the way". Tips are always up to you.

Paying the deposit and the balance

  • Multi-day tours: a 20–30% deposit by bank transfer confirms your booking. The balance is paid on arrival, at the start of your tour.
  • Day trips, activities and transfers: a small deposit confirms your date, and the rest is paid on the day.
  • Paying on arrival: in cash (euros, US dollars or Moroccan dirhams) or by card.

Only pay to the bank account written in your booking confirmation, and keep your transfer receipt. If anyone asks you to pay somewhere else in our name, contact us first on +212 621 460 083. Our Privacy Policy explains how to spot fake messages.

Cancelling your tour

  • 24 hours or more before your tour starts: cancellation is free and your deposit is refunded in full.
  • Less than 24 hours before, or not showing up: the deposit is not refunded, because hotels, camps and your driver-guide are already committed. You never owe the balance.
  • Leaving a tour early: days you don't use are not refunded, but we refund any hotel or activity costs we manage to get back.

To cancel, send us a message by WhatsApp or email; the time of your message counts. Refunds are sent by bank transfer to the account you paid from.

Rather than cancel, you can often move your dates instead: see the next section.

Changing your dates or itinerary

Because every tour is private, we can usually move your dates, add or remove a night, change the route or your hotels, even after booking. Just ask as early as you can. If a change costs more or less (an extra night, a better room), we tell you the new price in writing first, and nothing changes until you agree.

During the tour, you decide the pace: longer photo stops, a different lunch place or a shorter day are part of travelling privately. Big changes on the road, such as a new overnight stop, depend on hotel availability.

Weather, road closures and safety

Morocco's mountain and desert roads can close for snow on the High Atlas passes, floods or sandstorms, and balloon flights and camel rides depend on wind. When this happens, your driver-guide's job is to keep you safe and keep the trip going:

  • We take another road, change the order of the days or move an activity to another time.
  • If an activity can't happen at all (for example a balloon flight cancelled by the pilots for wind), you choose another day or a full refund for that activity.
  • If we have to cancel your whole tour for a reason within our control, you get every payment back in full, or a new date if you prefer.

We can't be held responsible for events nobody controls, such as extreme weather, strikes, border or airport closures, health emergencies or government decisions. In those cases we always look for the best alternative with you, and refund what we can recover from hotels and partners.

Hotels, riads and desert camps

You stay in the hotels and camps named in your confirmation. If one becomes unavailable (rare, but it happens with overbooking or repairs), we tell you before your trip and book one of the same standard or better, at no extra cost to you.

Check-in and check-out times are set by each hotel; tell us if you arrive early or leave late and we'll ask for you. In the desert camp, your main luggage stays safely in the vehicle and you take a small bag for the night.

Pick-up, timing and flights

  • We pick you up at your hotel. If your riad is inside a medina where cars can't enter, we meet you at the nearest gate or car park, and someone can walk you there.
  • Your pick-up time is confirmed the day before. If you are running late, message your driver-guide; the day may then be shorter.
  • For airport pick-ups we follow your flight, so an early or late landing costs nothing extra. Tell us if your flight is changed or cancelled.

Your part

  • Documents: make sure every traveller has a valid passport and any visa needed for Morocco.
  • Insurance: we strongly recommend travel insurance covering medical costs, repatriation and cancellation. It is not included in our tours.
  • Tell us before booking about children's ages, health or mobility points, allergies and diets, so we plan the right car seats, rooms, walks and meals.
  • Activities: camel rides, quad biking, balloon flights and hikes carry some physical risk. Follow your guide's and the operator's safety instructions, and skip anything you're not comfortable with.
  • Respect: local customs, people and places, and the rule that everyone in the vehicle wears a seat belt.
  • Your belongings: keep passports, money and valuables with you or in your hotel's safe.

Our responsibility

We choose our hotels, camps, drivers, guides and activity partners carefully, and our vehicles are insured and maintained for Moroccan roads. We are responsible for delivering the services in your confirmation. We are not responsible for loss, injury, delay or damage caused by events beyond our reasonable control, by a traveller's own actions, or by services you book directly with others. Nothing in these terms limits any right you have under the law that cannot be limited.

If something goes wrong

Tell your driver-guide or message us on WhatsApp straight away, at any hour. Most problems (a room, a meal, the pace) can be fixed the same day while you are still travelling. If something is still not right after your trip, write to us within 30 days and we will reply personally.

Moroccan law

These terms are governed by the law of the Kingdom of Morocco, and the Moroccan courts are competent for any dispute, unless the law of your own country gives you rights that cannot be removed. We will always try to find a fair solution with you first.
