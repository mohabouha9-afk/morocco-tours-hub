Best Things to Do in Merzouga

22 February 2026 · Morocco Tours Hub

Merzouga is the gateway to the Erg Chebbi dunes — Morocco's most spectacular Sahara landscape and the classic desert experience for most visitors. This small village on the edge of the sand is where camel treks, desert camps and dune sunrises begin.

Here are the best things to do in Merzouga.

Top Things to Do in Merzouga

Camel Trek into the Erg Chebbi Dunes

The quintessential Sahara experience — ride a camel over the golden dunes at sunset to a desert camp for dinner, music and a night under the stars.

Watch Sunrise & Sunset Over the Dunes

The changing colours of the Erg Chebbi dunes at dawn and dusk are unforgettable. You don't need an activity — just climb a dune and watch.

Explore the Desert by 4×4

A desert 4×4 excursion reaches areas hard to access on foot — panoramic viewpoints, fossil sites and remote corners of the dunes.

Visit a Nomad Family

Meet a nomadic family living in the desert and learn about their traditional way of life — a highlight for many travellers.

Experience Gnawa Music in Khamlia

The nearby village of Khamlia is known for its Gnawa music, with roots in sub-Saharan Africa. Enjoy a live performance of drums and chanting.

Try Sandboarding

Slide down the dunes on a board — a fun activity for all ages, with boards available at many camps.

See the Seasonal Lake (Dayet Srji)

After rains, a seasonal lake near Merzouga attracts migratory birds, including flamingos — a surprising sight in the desert.

How Many Days Do You Need in Merzouga?

One night in a desert camp is the classic experience. Two nights let you enjoy a full day exploring the dunes, nomad camps and Khamlia without rushing. Merzouga is usually reached on a multi-day tour from Marrakech or Fes.

Best Time to Visit Merzouga

Spring and autumn are ideal, with warm days and comfortable nights. Summer is very hot; winter days are pleasant but desert nights are cold, so bring warm layers.

Final Thoughts

Merzouga delivers the Sahara of your imagination — golden dunes, camel treks and star-filled skies. Spend at least one night in a desert camp; two lets you experience the desert beyond the classic camel ride.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Merzouga known for?

The Erg Chebbi dunes — some of Morocco's tallest — plus camel trekking, desert camps and spectacular Sahara sunrises and sunsets.

How do you get to Merzouga?

By road on a multi-day tour, typically from Marrakech (via Aït Ben Haddou and the Dades Valley) or Fes (via the Middle Atlas and Ziz Valley). The village itself is reached on paved roads.

How many nights should you spend in Merzouga?

One night in a camp is the classic experience; two nights allow a full desert day exploring by 4×4, visiting nomads and Khamlia.

Is Merzouga or Zagora better?

For the iconic golden dunes, Merzouga (Erg Chebbi) is the better choice. Zagora is closer to Marrakech and suits very short trips.
