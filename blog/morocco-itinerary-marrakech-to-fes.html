Morocco Itinerary from Marrakech to Fes

7 January 2026 · Morocco Tours Hub

Travelling one-way from Marrakech to Fes is one of the best ways to see Morocco, because it links the country's two greatest cities with the High Atlas, the kasbah country and a night in the Sahara — all without backtracking. Here's how the route works and how many days you need.

Why Travel from Marrakech to Fes?

Instead of returning to Marrakech after the desert, continuing to Fes means you experience two very different imperial cities and see far more scenery along the way. It's the classic Moroccan road trip.

The Route & Main Stops

The journey crosses the High Atlas via the Tizi n'Tichka pass to Aït Ben Haddou and Ouarzazate, then follows the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs through the Dades Valley and Todra Gorge to Merzouga and the Erg Chebbi dunes. From the desert it heads north through the Ziz Valley and the Middle Atlas (Midelt, Azrou, Ifrane) to Fes.

How Many Days from Marrakech to Fes?

3 days: the fastest version — desert-focused, with one night in a Sahara camp.
4–5 days: more comfortable, with time in the Dades Valley and Todra Gorge.
7 days: adds full days exploring Marrakech and Fes at each end.

Suggested 5-Day Marrakech to Fes Itinerary

Day 1: Marrakech → Aït Ben Haddou → Dades. Day 2: Dades → Todra → Merzouga (camel trek, camp). Day 3: Sahara sunrise → Ziz Valley → Fes. Day 4: Fes guided tour. Day 5: Departure. See our full Morocco in 5 Days itinerary for details.

Best Time & How to Travel

Spring and autumn are ideal. Because the desert isn't served by public transport and the drives are long, this route is best done as a private tour with a driver, run one-way from Marrakech to Fes.

Final Thoughts

The Marrakech-to-Fes route packs mountains, kasbahs and the Sahara into a single journey between two iconic cities. Give it at least three days — five to seven is better — and travel one-way to see the most.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get from Marrakech to Fes?

Direct it's a long day's drive, but the whole point of this route is to break the journey over 3 to 7 days via the Sahara, so you enjoy the scenery and an overnight in the desert.

Can you do Marrakech to Fes in 3 days?

Yes — a 3-day desert tour is the fastest way, with one night in a Merzouga camp before continuing to Fes. Five days is more comfortable.

Is it better to go Marrakech to Fes or Fes to Marrakech?

Both work equally well. Choose the direction that matches your international flights so you can travel one-way without backtracking.

What are the main stops between Marrakech and Fes?

Aït Ben Haddou, Ouarzazate, the Dades Valley, Todra Gorge, Merzouga (Sahara), the Ziz Valley and the Middle Atlas towns of Midelt, Azrou and Ifrane.
