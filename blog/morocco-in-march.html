Morocco in March: Is It a Good Time to Visit?

13 July 2026 · Morocco Tours Hub

March marks the start of spring in Morocco — one of the best times to visit. Days warm up, landscapes turn green, and desert nights become far more comfortable than in winter. Here's what to expect from Morocco in March.

Weather in Morocco in March

Warm, pleasant days across the cities and desert, milder nights than winter (though still cool in the Sahara and mountains), snow easing on the High Atlas, and fresher, occasionally showery weather in the north.

Is March a Good Time to Visit Morocco?

Absolutely — it's one of the best all-round months, with comfortable weather for the desert, cities and lower mountains, green scenery and the start of the spring season before the busiest weeks.

Best Things to Do in Morocco in March

Take a Sahara desert tour in comfortable conditions, explore Marrakech and Fes without the summer heat, enjoy green valleys and blossoming landscapes, and begin lower-altitude hiking in the Atlas.

What to Pack for March

Light clothing for warm days with a warm layer for cooler evenings, especially in the desert and mountains, plus sun protection and a scarf.

Final Thoughts

March is a wonderful time to visit Morocco — spring warmth, green landscapes and comfortable desert nights, before the peak-season crowds arrive. A great choice for a first trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is March a good time to visit Morocco?

Yes — it's one of the best months, with warm spring days, green scenery and comfortable conditions for the desert and cities.

Is the weather good in Morocco in March?

Generally yes: warm, pleasant days with milder nights than winter, though the desert and mountains are still cool after dark.

Is March good for a desert tour?

Very — spring brings warm days and more comfortable nights than winter, making it a prime time for the Sahara.
