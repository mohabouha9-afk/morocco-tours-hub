Privacy PolicyYour details, used only for your trip

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Morocco Tours Hub ("we", "us") is a local tour agency based in Marrakech. This page explains, in plain words, what we know about you, why, who else sees it and how you can have it changed or deleted.

Our privacy in short

  • We only ask for what we need to plan and run your private tour.
  • We never sell your details and never send newsletters or adverts.
  • Hotels, camps and your driver-guide get only the details they need for your booking.
  • Our website sets no cookies and uses no tracking or advertising tools.
  • We never ask for your card number or a passport scan by email or WhatsApp.
  • One message to us is enough to see, correct or delete your details.

What information we collect

Only what you choose to give us when you ask about or book a tour:

  • Contact details: your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number and country.
  • Trip details: dates, number of travellers, children's ages, preferred hotels or room types, pick-up address and flight times for airport transfers.
  • Needs you tell us about: diets, allergies, mobility or health points that matter for the trip. We use these only to look after you and share them only with the people who need them (for example the desert camp's kitchen), and you can ask us to delete them after your tour.
  • Our messages: the emails, WhatsApp messages and calls we exchange to plan your trip.
  • Payment records: the amount, date and payer name of your deposit and balance. We never see or keep your full card number.

We don't ask for more than this, and we never collect information about you from other sources.

What happens when you send our enquiry form

Our contact and tour forms don't store anything on our website. When you press send, your answers open in WhatsApp as a ready-written message, and nothing reaches us until you send it there yourself. You can edit or delete any line first.

Messages and emails then reach us through WhatsApp (run by Meta) and Gmail (run by Google), so their own privacy policies also apply to how those messages are carried and stored. Prefer not to use WhatsApp? Write to us at Moroccotourshub1@gmail.com instead.

Do you need my passport details?

Not to book. We need each traveller's name as written on the passport so hotels and camps can find your reservation. In Morocco, hotels, riads and desert camps check your passport at arrival, as the law requires them to register every guest. That happens directly between you and them.

For a few services, such as a domestic flight or some activity bookings, we may ask for a passport number. We will tell you why, use it only for that booking, and you never need to send a photo or scan of your passport.

How we use your information

  • To answer your questions and send you a tailor-made itinerary and quote.
  • To book your hotels, riads, desert camp, activities and driver-guide, and to run your tour.
  • To stay in touch before and during your trip (pick-up times, changes, help on the road).
  • To keep the booking and payment records Moroccan law asks every business to keep.

That's all. We don't use your details for advertising, we don't build profiles, and we only write to you after your trip to ask how it went.

Who sees your information

Only the people who make your trip happen, and only what each one needs:

  • Your driver-guide: names, number of travellers, pick-up place and time, and the needs that matter on the road.
  • Hotels, riads and desert camps: names, dates, room types and diet or access needs.
  • Activity partners (camel rides, balloon flights, quad biking, cooking classes): names, number of people and, when it matters for safety, ages or weights.
  • Public authorities: only when Moroccan law requires it.

We never sell, rent or trade your details, and we don't pass them to marketing companies.

Payments, and how to spot a fake "Morocco Tours Hub"

You pay a small deposit by bank transfer and the balance on arrival in cash or by card. Card payments go through the card machine's own secure system, so your card number never passes through our hands, our email or our website.

We will only ever ask you to pay to the account written in your booking confirmation. Our only WhatsApp number is +212 621 460 083 and our email is Moroccotourshub1@gmail.com. If someone contacts you in our name from another number or address, or asks for card details by message, don't pay and tell us straight away.

Cookies and our website

Our website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking or advertising tools, so there is no cookie banner to click. Like every website, a few files are loaded from outside services, which then see your device's IP address:

  • Google Fonts for our typefaces.
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs) and OpenStreetMap for the route maps on tour pages.
  • Our web host, which keeps short technical logs to keep the site safe and working.

Links to Tripadvisor, Facebook, Booking.com or Google Maps open those sites only if you click them, and their own policies then apply. If you leave us a review on Tripadvisor, it is published under Tripadvisor's rules.

Photos taken on your tour

Your driver-guide is happy to take photos of you with your phone. We only share photos of you or your family on our website or social media if you clearly say yes, and we remove them whenever you ask.

How long we keep your information

  • Enquiries that don't become a booking: deleted within 2 years.
  • Bookings and payments: kept as long as Moroccan accounting law requires, then deleted.
  • Health or diet notes: deleted after your trip if you ask.

How we keep it safe

Your details stay with our small team in Marrakech, on password-protected accounts. Partners receive only the part they need for your booking. No system is 100% secure, so please don't send documents we haven't asked for.

Your rights, and how to use them

Wherever you live, you can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you and send you a copy;
  • correct anything that's wrong;
  • delete your details (except the records the law makes us keep);
  • stop using them, or stop contacting you.

Just send one message by WhatsApp or email; no form or reason is needed. We answer within 30 days, usually much sooner, and it's free.

We follow Morocco's data protection law (Law 09-08). If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to Morocco's data protection authority, the CNDP. Travellers from the EU or UK also have their rights under the GDPR and can contact their own national data protection authority.

Children

We only collect children's names and ages from the parent, teacher or group leader who books the trip, so we can plan rooms, seats and activities that suit them. Trips for anyone under 18 are always arranged with that adult, never with the child directly.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information, we will update this page and the date at the top. If you have a booking with us and the change matters to you, we will tell you directly.
