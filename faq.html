Frequently Asked QuestionsEverything you need before you travel

Booking & payment

How do I book, and do I pay everything upfront?

No. Message us your dates and group size on WhatsApp or through our contact form and we’ll send a tailor-made itinerary with a fixed price. A 20–30% deposit by bank transfer secures your dates; the balance is paid on arrival in Morocco.

Which payment methods do you accept?

The deposit is paid by bank transfer. The balance is paid on arrival in Morocco, in cash (EUR, USD or MAD) or by card.

Are your prices per person or per group?

Every private tour is quoted per trip, based on your group size, accommodation level, season and itinerary. Your quote lists everything that is included: what we agree on is what you pay, with no hidden fees.

What is your cancellation policy?

If you need to cancel, tell us as soon as possible. Cancellation charges may apply to cover non-refundable costs already committed for you, such as hotels or desert camp reservations; the exact terms are confirmed in writing when you book. If we have to cancel, we offer a suitable alternative or a refund. See our terms & conditions.

Can I change my dates or itinerary after booking?

Yes. Because every tour is private, we always try to accommodate changes to your dates, route or group size. Some changes may affect the price or may not be possible close to your travel date, depending on hotel and camp availability.

Our private tours

Are your Morocco tours private or in a group?

All our tours are private by default. You travel only with your own party, in a private vehicle with your own driver-guide, and the itinerary is yours to shape. If you’d prefer to share and save, ask us about small-group options.

Can I customise my itinerary?

Absolutely. Every tour on this site is a starting point: we can shorten or extend it, add cities, change the pace, upgrade the hotels or design a completely new route around your dates and interests.

What is included in your private Morocco tours?

Your own air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking driver, fuel, hotel pick-up and drop-off, and accommodation with breakfast. On desert tours you also get dinners, a camel trek and a night in a Sahara camp. Lunches, drinks, monument entry fees, local site guides, tips and travel insurance are not included.

What vehicles do you use?

Air-conditioned 4×4 Land Cruisers for couples and small groups, minivans for families, and Sprinter minibuses for larger groups and luggage. For 50 guests or more we can arrange full-size coaches.

Do you pick us up from the airport or our hotel?

Yes. Your driver collects you from your hotel, riad or the airport and drops you off at the end of the tour, so you never have to find your own way to a meeting point.

Do your driver-guides speak English?

Yes. Our local driver-guides speak English and are with you throughout the trip. At sites such as Aït Ben Haddou or the Fes medina you can also hire a licensed local guide for a deeper visit.

Are your tours suitable for families with children?

Yes. Private tours suit families well because the pace, stops and driving times are planned around your children. Tell us their ages when you enquire, and see our family tours in Morocco.

The Sahara Desert

How far is the Sahara Desert from Marrakech?

Merzouga’s Erg Chebbi dunes are about 560 km (around 9 hours of driving) from Marrakech, so our Sahara desert tour from Marrakech takes 3 days, via Aït Ben Haddou, the Dades Valley and Todra Gorge, with a camel trek and a night in a desert camp.

Merzouga or Agafay: which desert should I choose?

Agafay is a stony desert under an hour from Marrakech, ideal for a day trip with a camel ride and sunset dinner. For the golden sand dunes most people picture, choose Merzouga (Erg Chebbi), which needs at least 3 days from Marrakech or Fes.

What is a night in a Sahara desert camp like?

You ride a camel into the dunes at sunset, have dinner at the camp, spend the evening under the stars and wake up for sunrise over the dunes. Ask us about upgrading to a luxury desert camp.

What should I bring for the desert?

Comfortable clothes, a warm layer for the evening, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat or scarf and your camera. Desert nights can drop to around 3–5 °C in winter, while summer days can pass 40 °C.

Travelling in Morocco

Is Morocco safe for tourists?

Yes. Morocco is Africa’s most visited country and is safe for tourists, families and solo women. Watch for pickpockets in busy medinas and avoid unofficial “guides”; on our private Morocco tours, your English-speaking driver-guide is with you every day.

When is the best time to visit Morocco?

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are the best time to visit Morocco and the Sahara Desert. Winter brings sunny days but cold desert nights, while July and August pass 40 °C inland, the best season for the Atlantic coast and Essaouira.

Do I need a visa to visit Morocco?

No visa is needed for US, UK, EU, Canadian, Australian and New Zealand citizens: you get up to 90 days free on arrival. Bring an undamaged passport valid for at least 6 months; other nationalities can apply online at acces-maroc.ma.

What currency is used in Morocco, and can I pay by card?

Morocco uses the dirham (MAD), a closed currency you get on arrival from ATMs or official exchange offices. Cards work in hotels and larger restaurants, but souks and small cafés are cash only, so keep some dirhams with you.

What should I wear in Morocco?

Light, loose clothing that covers shoulders and knees is respectful in medinas, villages and religious sites. Pack layers for cool evenings in the mountains and desert, comfortable walking shoes, and a light scarf for sun and dust.

Can I travel to Morocco during Ramadan?

Yes. Tours run as normal during Ramadan. Some cafés outside tourist areas close during the day and opening hours can change, so it is polite to eat and drink discreetly in public. Evenings are lively, and it’s a special time to visit.

How many days do I need in Morocco?

Three days is enough for a Sahara desert tour from Marrakech. Allow 7–10 days to combine the imperial cities with the desert, and 12 days or more for a grand tour including Chefchaouen and the coast.

Do I need travel insurance?

Yes, we strongly recommend comprehensive travel insurance covering medical expenses, repatriation, cancellation and personal belongings. Travel insurance is not included in our tours.
